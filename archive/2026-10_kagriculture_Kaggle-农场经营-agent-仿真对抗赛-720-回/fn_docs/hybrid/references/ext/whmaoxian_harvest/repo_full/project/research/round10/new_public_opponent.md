## What the notebook says the new decisions do

The notebook markdown describes three narrow interventions above a precomputed route portfolio:

1. **Same-day weed recovery:** when a weed obstructs a planned planting and a safe later idle slot exists, insert `DIG` and shift the remaining same-day worker commands one step. The idle slot absorbs the shift. The intent is to preserve the plant and its water action without changing the later route.
2. **Confirmed mirror sale ordering:** in a close, visibly similar farm race, compare bounded permutations of existing SELL orders under the official rounded price curve and simultaneous quotes. It preserves the exact order multiset and accepts a swap only with modeled gain of at least 25 coins, similarity at least 0.90, and cash lead at most 500. The opponent's private shed is **modeled**, not observed.
3. **Shop-conditioned livestock substitution:** around game day 6, when the first two shops both demand eggs and none demand milk, replace one two-cow purchase with two geese, then verify the purchase succeeded before changing pickup, coop-building and placement actions. The route's worker skeleton remains the same. This uses real current shop demand but is triggered by a particularly narrow two-shop regime, so it needs out-of-family validation before adoption.

The public search snapshot showed a score around **2678** for notebook version 7; other cached Kaggle listings show small fluctuations. That is the notebook's reported public score, **not** a local result or evidence that it is stronger than V9. The online rating changes with matches.

## Opponent diversity and adoption boundary

The 719-line chassis block at extracted `main.py` lines 2–720 exactly matches V9's lines 223–941 (`experiments/round9_market_slack.py`). The route payload and later wrappers differ, notably this source credits Yusuke Hayashi's Shop Router 0909 while V9's route payload credits Shop Router 0913. Therefore it is a useful **route and shop-policy variant**, but should not be counted as an independent algorithm family alongside V9. A win or loss against it cannot stand in for DSM/Vadim's private adaptive agents.

The weed-recovery branch is broadly mechanistic if its resynchronization proof holds. The market branch is explicitly mirror-gated. The livestock branch is a targeted response to two observed egg shops, with possible weakness if future shops or market gluts change the value. These can inspire isolated ablations, but importing all three directly into V10 would confound which mechanism helped. The current task only made the unexecuted opponent available; the root agent owns later scheduling of league games.
