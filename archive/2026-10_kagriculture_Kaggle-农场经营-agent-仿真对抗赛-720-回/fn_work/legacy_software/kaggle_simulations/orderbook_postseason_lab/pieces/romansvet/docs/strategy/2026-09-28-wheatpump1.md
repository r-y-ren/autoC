## 1. Mechanism, from the engine (`kaggle_environments/envs/kaggriculture/kaggriculture.py`, sha256 bc8a5487… = vendor/engine.lock.json)
- **One shared book per product.**
  - `_process_market` (L544-625) quotes a SELL at `market_price(item, inv)` and a BUY_PRODUCT (WHEAT/FERTILIZER only) at `market_price(item, inv − 1)` (L596-601). The comment there reads: *"Quote at post-buy inventory so a buy/sell round-trip against an unchanged market nets zero"*.
  - `_commit_unit` (L652-673) does SELL `inventory += 1` (not at the $1 floor) and BUY_PRODUCT `inventory −= 1`.
  - So **a BUY moves the same book the rival sells into**. It raises the rival's later sale quotes, and its later buy costs, until a sale returns the unit.
  - SELL and BUY touch only the shed, so a round trip costs **0 unit-turns**. Its only cost is shed room (RESALE1: −4..−10 u of other products).
- **Wheat curve** (L42, `market_price` L192): `25 + sqrt(10000 − inv)` below I0, and `25 − 0.834·ln(1 + inv − 10000)` above.
  - The book is steep below I0: 100 units under → 35, 500 under → 47.
  - It is almost flat above: +500 → 20.
- **Unlike melon, the wheat book is drained by the town.** `_town_consume` (L728-748, called L942 after every market phase) removes 1 unit per unlocked BAKERY / PIZZA / BRUNCH / ICE_CREAM / FARMERS_MARKET per 4 turns, plus 1/day from the town centre. On these boards that is about 1 / 7 / 18.5 / 30 u/day over d0-2 / d3-9 / d10-19 / d20-29.
  - Wheat does not ratchet like melon. The only exogenous profit on a held lot is this drain, which is RESALE1's result.
- **Instrument check.** 99.9 % of recorded prices equal `p(inv)` / `p(inv−1)` at commit. The misses are the lockstep second mover, which is off by one unit.
