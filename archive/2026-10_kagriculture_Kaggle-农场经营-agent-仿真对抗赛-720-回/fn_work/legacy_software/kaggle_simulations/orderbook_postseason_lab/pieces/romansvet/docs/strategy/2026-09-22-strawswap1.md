The switch is present as `STRAW_SWAP1_ON=False`. On d10–24 it can replace at most one lowest-value
selected wheat fertilizer target with one highest-value eligible, unselected strawberry target;
the selected count, fertilizer purchase and application count remain fixed.

The pre-run replay audit found a broad physical upper bound: 4,338 turns on all 107 corrected loss
boards had a selected wheat application and an unfertilized, unselected strawberry tile. That was
enough to proceed, but it deliberately lacked the planner's value/timing candidate mask. The exact
paired run showed that none of those physical tiles survived that mask as an executable swap on the
development panel: OFF and ON had **identical complete 720-step tapes on 100/100 boards** (replay IDs
alone differ). Thus executed swaps, extra harvested strawberry, extra sold strawberry, Δours and
Δtheirs are all zero. Both final purses are retained per game in `straw_swap1_dev_{off,on}.csv`.

Development was precommitted before the ON read: 100 games drawn from LIVE250 indices 0–149 with
50 original seat-0 and 50 original seat-1 boards. Heldout was frozen as indices 150–249. OFF also
reproduced the corrected incumbent purses byte-exact on all 100 games. The declared kill rule
`dev net < 3 or mean Δtheirs > 0` fires on net 0, so heldout and the incumbent-188 LIVE302 ship gate
remain unopened. No packaging or upload was performed.
