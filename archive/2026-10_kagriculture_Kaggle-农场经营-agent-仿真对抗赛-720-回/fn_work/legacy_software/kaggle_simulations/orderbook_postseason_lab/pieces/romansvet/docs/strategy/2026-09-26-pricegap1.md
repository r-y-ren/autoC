# PRICEGAP1 (2026-09-25T00:23Z–00:40Z): the live "price half" of the d10-19 gap is an accounting artefact of the split formula; the real price gap is ≈ 0 live AND offline. Verdict: none of (a)/(b)/(c); no lever.

Branch `pricegap1`. Scripts are in `S/pricegap1/` (`split.py`, `offline_census.py`, `table.py`) and outputs in `out/`. Live input: the LIVELOSS13 `census.tsv` (37 losses + 10 contrast wins). Offline input: an ECONCENSUS census of all 46 POOLCHECK1 vrp2 loss replays (31 pool + 15 v15stack, `out/census_offline.tsv`).

## Cause: the two docs split revenue with different formulas
- **MID** (LIVELOSS13 `ledger.py`): vol = (uo−ut)(po+pt)/2, price = (po−pt)(uo+ut)/2. When a seat sells 0 units in the phase its price is blank and gets read as 0. For a product only the opponent sells, this books **−pt·ut/2, half of their revenue, as "price"**.
- **LASP** (POOLCHECK1 `lossmap.py`): vol = (uo−ut)·pt, price = uo·(po−pt), with pt := po when ut = 0 and po := 0 when uo = 0. A product only one seat sells counts entirely as volume.
- In d10-19 that product is **MELON**. We sell 0 melon on d10-11; they dump about 56 units on d10 and 12 on d11, which is the known V melon plate. MID books half of that plate as a price gap.
