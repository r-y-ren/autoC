# CROPMIX1: the executor's carrot / tomato mix vs MMPQ

**Date:** 2026-09-30, 09:26Z to ~10:05Z. Opus, xhigh, time box 90 min.

**Question:** the deterministic MMPQ executor (crew tree cf1b9790, PROG_CREW_ON; HOLD20 0.847 of MMPQ's coins) plants 42 carrot and 13 tomato tiles a game where MMPQ plants 74 and 19-22, worth about -9.2k of sales a game. What is MMPQ's carrot / tomato emission rule, how does ours differ, and does fixing it close the gap to 0.90?

**Verdict: the emission rule difference is real and is now reproduced, but it is NOT a coins lever. NONE.**
- With the fitted MMPQ carrot + tomato targets (`cm_car` + `cm_tom`), the executor matches MMPQ's standing carrot and tomato per day. Plantings go from 42 / 13 to 65 / 19 (MMPQ 74 / 19).
- Carrot units sold rise 99 -> 137 and tomato 63 -> 99. Wheat sold falls 315 -> 221.
- Coins: HOLD20 0.855 (+0.0084, t 1.20, 13/20 up), TUNE20 -0.0060 (t -0.96). Pooled over 40 boards: +0.0012, t 0.25.
- Across all 19 mix cells on TUNE20, revenue is conserved (136.8k-137.8k against the control's 137.6k). The crew makes the same ~328 harvests a game whatever it plants. Every carrot or tomato unit gained comes out of wheat, at about 1:1 in coins.
- MMPQ gets its carrots and tomatoes ON TOP OF 580 wheat. The gap is output per unit of labour, not crop choice.

## (2) The rule difference

MMPQ plants no carrot before d10. Its standing carrot is keyed on the number k of carrot shops unlocked (fitted on 40 replays, logs/fit40.txt; value = h0 standing + that day's plantings):

| days | k ≤ 1 | k = 2 | k = 3 | k = 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| d10 | 0 | 3 | 23 | 23 |
| d11 | 0 | 9 | 30 | 30 |
| d12-14 | 1 | 6 | 28 | 30 |
| d15-17 | 1 | 3 | 20 | 28 |
| d18-20 | 2 | 2 | 17 | 22 |
| d21-22 | 6 | 9 | 18 | 30 |
| d23-24 | 8 | 15 | 22 | 36 |
| d25-27 | 14 | 23 | 28 | 40 |

- **Carrot shape:** the table rises through the back half and has a big early field only with 3+ shops. MMPQ plants on the far south rows in the afternoon, from seed lots of 6.
- **Our carrot rule** is flat: `[0,3,11,19,24,…][k]` on d8-14, 2k on d15-18 and 4.5k on d19-27.
  - It over-plants at k = 1 early (3 vs 0-1).
  - It under-plants late: 4.5k ≈ 9 standing vs MMPQ's 14-23 on d23-27.
  - It under-plants at k ≥ 3 on d10-14: 14-18 vs 23-30.
- **Tomato:** MMPQ's standing is 5 + 6t on d8-14 (it keeps topping up on d11-14 after the cash-tight land days d8-10) and 9 + 5.5t on d15-19, with no planting after d19.
  - Ours is 4 + 5t on d8-10 and d15-19 only.
  - The d8-10 block is cash-held (standing 5.2 vs a target of 9), and the window closes on d10, so the deficit is never made up.

## What the next builder should take

1. The crop-mix emission rule is fixed and faithful (`cm_car` + `cm_tom` reproduce MMPQ's per-day standing carrot and tomato within about 1 tile, d10-27). It is revenue-neutral. **Close the crop-mix axis.**
2. Under our crew, total harvests (~328 a game) are invariant to what is planted. Every crop added displaces wheat at about equal coins. The 0.90 bar needs about 90 more harvests a game, which is the labour/visit efficiency that CREWSCHED1, PLANTDEATH1, SEEDSTOCK1 and CREWBUNDLE1 all hit. It cannot come from which crop the tile gets.
3. If a future crew gets more harvests per day, switch on `cm_car` + `cm_tom` with it. On HOLD20 the MMPQ mix is +0.008 when the labour is spare.
