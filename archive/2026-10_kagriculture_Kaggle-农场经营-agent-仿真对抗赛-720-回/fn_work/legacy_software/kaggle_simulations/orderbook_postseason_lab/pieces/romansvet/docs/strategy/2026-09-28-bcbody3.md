## Rung 1: diagnosis (3 teacher boards, clone vs teacher per day d0-d10; `S/bcbody1/diag.py`, `diag/diag3.txt`, `diag/market3.txt`)
The collapse comes from closed-loop drift, not from model error. Under teacher forcing on d1-d6 (held-out teacher episodes) the unit accuracy is **0.95-0.98** and the HIRE head reproduces the teacher's per-day hires exactly (d1-d6 4/5/5/6/6/9).

Top 3 mechanical causes (d1-d10 sums, clone / teacher):
1. **Herd neglect (units).**
   - FEED on d2-d6 is 0-3 per day (teacher 3-10).
   - Animals at d5 h0: 0-3 (teacher 8-9). On decem_114310214 the herd went 5 -> 0: the animals escaped after two unfed days.
   - Herd at d10: 0-9 (teacher 20-23).
   - Units do not carry wheat (PICKUP_WHEAT at the shed at h1, then FEED), so animals go unfed.
2. **Early market over-ordering, then no investment (market).**
   - On d1 the clone orders 10-18 melon seeds (teacher 2-4), 8-28 wheat (teacher 6-8) and 8-17 HIRE (teacher 3-4).
   - Money at d2 h0: 3-22 (teacher 131-167).
   - It buys 0 cows or sheep on d2-d5 (teacher 4-9) and 0-1 BUY_LAND (teacher 3-7).
   - Once the guards fixed the units, the clone hoarded cash on d6-d10 (1-3.7k vs the teacher's 0.05-0.6k).
3. **No replant or expansion, wasted labour (units).**
   - Plantings on d1-d10: 97 vs 194. Productive tiles at d10: 32 vs 74.
   - MOVE is +30% (3,439 vs 2,655) and WATER -35% (445 vs 690).
   - 11 plants died unwatered (teacher 0). The teacher waters every other day; plants survive one dry day.

**Diagnostic X1** decides between the unit and market explanations. It feeds the teacher's own recorded market orders open-loop (by day/hour) and lets the clone play the units:
- Clone units alone: **0.416**, herd_d10 4.6. The units cannot run the teacher's economy.
- With the CARE=1 caretaker: **0.649**, herd 9.0.
- With CARE=2: 0.636, herd 11.6.
- With CARE=3: 0.686.
- Conclusion: **the units are the bottleneck.** The teacher's own market does no better than the clone's market once the guards are in (r2c 0.715).

## What moved and what did not
**Moved:**
- **The caretaker (+0.20 to +0.23 ratio).** Idle or wandering units are routed to the herd and to plants that would die tonight. MELON dours improved from -48k to -30k.
- **Data (+0.08 to +0.10 ratio).** Going from 874 to 1,349 seat-episodes raised the ratio from 0.72 to 0.82 and MELON dours from -27k to -20k.
  - Held-out accuracy by data size:

    | data | unit token | qty | market nonzero |
    |---|---|---|---|
    | 300 eps | 0.793 | 0.861 | 0.383 |
    | 874 eps | 0.837 | 0.894 | 0.452 |

  - By-day accuracy (1,349 eps): d2-d10 unit 0.86-0.97, market 0.61-0.89.

**Did not move:**
- Per-day market caps.
- The teacher median schedule (it breaks the opening or buys animals that nobody places).
- The invest targets. The herd still reaches only 9-11 by d10 against 20, because placement is too slow.
- The seed fill.
- The replant rule.
- The coordination features alone: r3 is 0.549, and teacher-forcing accuracy was already 0.95 on d1-d6.
- The MELON flip count: +1/-7 vs V56 on every rung, all on the same boards.

## Rung 5 (RL fine-tune) was not run
- The clone acts through the Python engine at about 40 s per game with 2 local workers.
- There is no batched or JAX environment for this 44-token multi-unit action space, so 20 minutes of PPO would be under 60 episodes.
- The fetch and the 1,349-episode retrain took the rung-5 time slot, and the data gain was the larger lever.
