## Answer

- **Real P48 (56 seats, exact).**
  - Productive tiles (planted, or holding an animal) are 23.0 at the end of d0, not 25. Q1 is full (25) from the end of d1 to the end of d5.
  - The seat then adds two quadrants, each in one afternoon:
    - **Q2 on d6:** 25.0 at the end of d5, 48.9 at the end of d6 (sd 1.6). Q2 holds 24.0 of 25 by the end of d6 on 55 of 56 seats.
    - **Q3 on d8:** Q3 holds 17.2 at the end of d8 and 24.6 at the end of d9.
  - d9 h12: 68.9 (sd 3.7) = wheat 17.9 / melon 12.0 / strawberry 20.6 / other 0.5 | cow 7.1 / sheep 4.5 / goose 6.4.
  - By quadrant at d9 h12: Q1 25.0 / Q2 24.8 / Q3 19.1.
- **Port on the same 16 boards.**
  - g4g d9 h12: 41.6 = 2.7 / 10.6 / 13.9 / 0 | 5.6 / 3.7 / 5.2. By quadrant: 24.1 / 14.4 / 3.0.
  - g4e d9 h12: 39.0 = 1.4 / 9.4 / 14.1 / 0 | 5.6 / 3.8 / 4.7.
  - The deficit against real is -27.3 (g4g). Planted tiles are -23.8 of it: wheat -15.2, strawberry -6.7, melon -1.4. Animals are -3.6.
- **PFS (88 live seats).** d9 h12: 48.0 (10.1 / 3.1 / 23.2 / 0.6 | 5.9 / 3.4 / 1.7). PFS buys Q2 on d5 and no Q3 by d9.
- **First divergence: d6.**
  - Up to the end of d5 the port is within 2 tiles of the real seat. On d6 it is > 4 behind on 10/16 boards at h12 and on 6/16 more by the end of the day (every board by the end of d6).
  - The mean gap goes from 0.0 at the end of d5 to 19.7 at the end of d6.
  - **The cause is that the port's schedule never asks for the plantings.**
    - Money is not short: g4g buys Q2 (1,000) and all of d6's seeds (M3 S11.5 W3 = 1,420 coins, real 1,506) in one step at h6, filled after the h6 wool sale.
    - Land is not missing (Q2 bought at h6), tiles are free (19.4 empty Q2 tiles at the end of d6), and the crew is there (9.3 vs real 9.6).
    - Yet the plant count does not rise once between h6 and h20, and the seeds sit in stock.
    - The same holds with P48GRAPH3's `P48G_PLANT_NOW=6` (g1f). There the stock is planted the NEXT day (d7 h9-h21, 16.1 tiles), because that repair uses only units whose whole remaining day is PASS. No unit qualifies on d6.
  - The d6 backlog then eats d8. The port plants the d6 stock in Q2 on d7-9 while Q3 gets 2.6 tiles on d8 (real 17.2), with 650-770 coins idle all afternoon.
- **Money is not the constraint for planting.**
  - Over d1-9 P48's plant side needs seeds 3,049 + land 3,000 = 6,049 coins. The herd side takes animals 5,241 + feed wheat 2,285 + hires 455.
  - All of it comes from d1-9 revenue of 14,450 (+156 on d0 = the 14.6k).
  - On d6 alone the seat spends 2,543 on the plant side and 1,777 on the herd side, from d6 revenue of 4,319 + a dawn purse of 378. The day ends near zero.
  - The port spends the same on d6 (seeds 1,420 + land 1,000, herd 1,844, revenue 4,379). It just does not plant.

## Caveats

- **Port ledgers.** The plantings are h0 / h12 / next-h0 layout diffs, which miss a harvest and replant within one half-day. The plant hours are the steps where the plant count rises (net of harvests). The seed coins are orders x list price (an order the purse cannot fill still counts). The port's unit actions (PLANT / WATER / idle) are not logged.
- **Real seats** are exact per-step tile diffs of the engine replay, with every market fill and land purchase from the engine's commit (S/plantgap1/pg.py, 144 games, 0 failures). The daily coin flow uses S/portgap1/res/led.jsonl unchanged.
