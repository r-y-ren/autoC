## 3. GATE2, the proposed ship gate
**Legs.**
- BAND, now the 142 banked band seats on the orig seat. Grow it to ≥ 284 (both seats) and toward ~400 (BANDBANK2).
- The five V legs as run today: dev100, held100, FRESH300, tapes50, faithful59.
- Every leg is paired per board against the live package.

**Metric.** The soft win of each board:
- w(m) = 1 / (1 + e^(−m/3,000)), where m = ours − theirs.
- Δw = w(arm) − w(base).
- It is the flip count smoothed over the margin: unbiased for Δwin-rate where the margin density is flat within ±s, and it uses the near-miss boards that a flip count discards.
- Both seats of one opponent form one cluster.

**Pass iff all three hold:**
1. **Either branch.**
   - **A.** The BAND family soft Δθ is ≥ 1.96 × its SE. It is the BANDLEG1 Part C family formula fed per-family mean Δw; the SE is a seat bootstrap within family. This is the MELON-cell path.
   - **B.** The pooled soft-win t is ≥ 2 over all legs, BAND included, **and** the BAND family soft Δθ is ≥ 0.
2. **Guard.** No leg with n ≥ 20 has soft-win t ≤ −2.
3. **Gift rule.** It fails only if both hold, pooled over the reacting legs dev/held/FRESH/pool: Δtheirs t ≥ +2 and soft-win t < 2. A rival gain that our wins do not pay back fails.

**Reported without a threshold:** hard flips (± bootstrap SE), hard family Δθ and Δmargin.

**Operating characteristics:**

| arm | BAND142 | BAND284 |
|---|---|---|
| null | 0.038 | 0.044 |
| +2 flips/100, all legs | 0.93 | 0.97 |
| transfer fail | 0.075 | 0.017 |
| +2 BAND-only | 0.42 | 0.61 (0.76 at 426) |

**Adoption.** GATE2 replaces the per-leg flip bar in TRAINING-PROTOCOL §1.7 once the user adopts it. Until then that section still defines the operative bar. Its definition is written into docs/PIPELINE.md §3 "The bars".

## 5. Caveats
- **Model assumptions.** The power model assumes uniform shifts and no rival response. Denial or transfer arms can differ; the gift rule is not simulated.
- **Zero-SE legs.** The bootstrap SE of net flips is 0 on legs with 0 discordant boards (e.g. vrp7 dev/held). The model null sd per leg is the real floor, in flips: dev 2.0, held 1.9, FRESH 4.3, tapes 1.4, faithful 1.9, BAND 2.9 (4.2 at 284).
- **Branch B's BAND condition is only a sign test** on an SE ~3 θ estimate. It lets V-heavy arms through when BAND is flat (transfer-fail pass 0.075 at 142).
- **Stale bases.** Every reject was judged on an older base (vrp5-vrp10). A PASS* is a reason to re-judge, not to ship.
