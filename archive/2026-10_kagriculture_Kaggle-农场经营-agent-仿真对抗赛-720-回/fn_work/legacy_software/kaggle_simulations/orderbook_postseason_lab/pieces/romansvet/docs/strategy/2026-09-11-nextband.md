## 4. Verdict against the pre-registered bar

Bar (strategist doc §1): **B > hr at |t| ≥ 1.5 ⇒ the judge is band-invariant and NEXT30 becomes a
free 5th leg; hr ≥ B at t ≥ 1.5 ⇒ band-dependent, re-make the incumbent and seed choices; neither
⇒ underpowered, extend to 60 boards once.**

**B > hr at t = +1.93 on NEXT30 (and +1.94 on the 44-board supplementary read, 7 win flips to 1).
The judge is band-invariant at 2550-2750. VERDICT: bar cleared, first clause.**

Consequences, in order of what they cost:

1. **The incumbent stands.** B is better than hr on the band neither of them has ever played, by
   the same statistic and roughly the same size as on LIVE-C H30B (+1,028 coins, t 2.02 in the
   `AUTOJUDGE-vsB` line) — so the choice of B over hr was not an artefact of measuring only
   ≤ 2500 opponents. No re-make of the incumbent is warranted.
2. **The seed choice stands.** flow209 is seeded from B and flow210 from hr; §92's worry was that
   this was decided on the wrong opponents. It was not. Keep flow209 as the primary arm; nothing
   here argues for re-seeding flow210.
3. **NEXT30 is now a 5th leg** and costs ~5 minutes at WORKERS=4 (the base csv exists, §2).
   Adding it to the promotion rule buys the first measurement at the rating we are actually
   climbing into, for less wall clock than the TOPB2 leg.
4. **One caveat, and it is the interesting one.** B's edge is *not uniform inside the band*: it is
   +1,377 (t 2.20) at 2550-2650 and **+102 (t 0.29) at 2650-2750** — level. On the 44-board read
   the same shape holds (+799 lower, +336 upper). So the judge's *ordering* is band-invariant, but
   the *size* of B's edge decays as the opponent's rating rises, and by 2700 it is gone. That is
   consistent with the TOPB2 reading (B +7.5 win points there) being a smaller, noisier version of
   the same thing, and with §92's core claim that we have no evidence at all above 2750. It is an
   argument for the strategist's experiment #3 (re-point the rungs at the band we face), not
   against the incumbent.
5. **Absolute win rates are the usual tape-judge optimism** (open-loop opponents cannot react to
   our prices) and must not be read as a ladder projection — 63-70 % here is the instrument's
   scale, exactly as 63-83 % is on LIVE-C. Only the paired column decides anything.

## 5. Running it on any theta

```
WORKERS=4 bash S/nextband/run.sh <name> /mnt/e/_work/kaggriculture3/.claude/worktrees/arms-next \
    <theta.npy> "OPEN_PUMP_ON=True,TAIL_FILL_ON=True,BANK_BEFORE_LOT_ON=True,HIRE_ROW_ON=True"
.venv/bin/python S/bank/paired.py S/lossflip/nextband_hr.csv S/lossflip/nextband_<name>.csv | grep '^ALL'
```

(`S/nextband/pair.py` prints the same paired line plus raw win rates; `S/nextband/split.py
<base csvs> --vs <cand csvs>` prints the per-board table and the two rating halves. To run the
queued extension at the seeds it will draw once appended:
`WORKERS=4 FIRST=30 IDS=S/nextband/ids_ext.txt bash S/nextband/run.sh <name>_ext …`.)

The 44 new town rows live in `S/nextband/town_schedules.json`; `S/nextband/town_append.sh` merges
them into `S/band2100p/town_schedules.json` (atomic replace, prior rows asserted equal) whenever
no judge leg is running. It was deliberately **not** run today: two autojudge legs were using that
registry, and NEXT30 does not need it.
