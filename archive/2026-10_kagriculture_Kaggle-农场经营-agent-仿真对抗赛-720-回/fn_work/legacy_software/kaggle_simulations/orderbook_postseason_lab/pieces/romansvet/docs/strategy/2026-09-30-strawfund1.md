## 3. The switch (plan.py, final pass `ask_fill` only; OFF = master)
- **Re-order.** On days `STRAW_FUND_D0..D1` (4..7), when the grant leaves the strawberry want unfunded:
  1. The lists named in `STRAW_FUND_KEEP` keep their OFF grant.
  2. Strawberry seed is bought next, up to the planner's own tile-clipped want (positive-value prefix, at most `STRAW_FUND_MAX` over OFF).
  3. Every other list is re-granted on what is left, and never above its OFF grant. The switch only defers; it never adds.
- **Herd deferral.** `STRAW_FUND_DEFER_COWS` / `STRAW_FUND_DEFER_SHEEP` hold that species' herd at want-1 on d0-5. On d6-9 those heads are bought behind the strawberry ask.
- **Land deferral.** `STRAW_FUND_LAND_DEFER` (S9/S10) defers the d5-7 quadrant when two things hold:
  - the strawberry ask is unfunded under the land grant;
  - the board without the quadrant still holds the day's whole planting ask.
- **S5 latch.** S5 uses a runtime d2h0 latch: the rival has 1-10 MELON plantings. This is the LATCHHERD1 / RESERVEFIX1 R4 rule.
