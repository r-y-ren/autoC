## 3. Hypotheses

**H1 SEATFLIP — we lose about 0.8k margin whenever we sit in seat 1. The data is consistent on all four legs (diagnostic first; a lever only if the cause is on the agent side)**
- **Observable.** Margin as P1 minus margin as P0: dev100 −0.87k (P1 lost 10/56 vs P0 3/44), held100 −0.78k (9/53 vs 5/47), FRESH300 −0.73k (27/159 vs 23/141), live 406 rated games −0.95k (46/194 vs 43/212). Offline pooled loss rate is 17.2 % for P1 vs 13.4 % for P0.
- **Mechanism.** The engine is seat-symmetric except in three places: P0's empty tiles draw the weed rng first; within a market lockstep round P0 commits first (this only matters when a commit fails on money or shed); and atomic HIRE/BUY_LAND resolve in player order. Candidates:
  - head_940 was PPO-trained with `learner_seat=0` only (S/actionrl/head.py:424, flow257 self-play);
  - V56's own code;
  - a board-set confound. The seat labels come from our live episodes, so this cannot be ruled out.
- **Lever.** None is known yet. Whatever the paired flip isolates: fine-tune the head as P1, or fix a seat bug.
- **Prediction.** If the cause is agent-side and fixable, closing the whole offline P1 excess (3.8 pp on the ~55 P1 boards per 100) is about +2 flips per 100, with no gift risk because it is a symmetry repair. If the flip shows nothing, the effect is board composition and the item closes.
- **Test (≤ 1.5 h).**
  1. Run dev100 twice: the base, and the same manifest with `our_seat → 1 − our_seat` (same seed, same pinned town, SAFETY_S=1e9 both). Pair per board: P1-minus-P0 margin on identical boards.
  2. If mean ≤ −300 at t ≥ 2, bisect with 2 more flipped runs: plain planner without the head (ZERO_BIAS/greedy-off), and ROUTE_VRP off.

**H2 CONTEST — price the rival's visible strawberry wave with a denial term instead of ceding it (a new condition on OPP_MIX/OPP_SUPPLY: opposite sign, margin objective)**
- **Observable.** V plants 11.3 strawberries on its Q3 day (d11) against our 1.3. The wave is public on their tiles from d11. They sell 135 strawberries on d21-29 vs our 110. The dawn quote runs 146 (d20) → 39 (d24) → 90 (d29).
- **Mechanism.** Above I0 the strawberry curve is linear at 1.92 coins/unit, and an extra unit stays in the inventory until the shops drain it. So one extra unit of ours sold on d21 is worth:
  - own: about −55 (quote ~60 minus 1.92 × our ~60 later units);
  - to them: 1.92 × their ~100 later units ≈ −190;
  - margin ≈ +135/unit.
  The planner prices only its own purse. OPP_MIX/OPP_SUPPLY pushed the other way (cede contested books), and both were refused two-purse on 09-16 against tapes, before VRP.
- **Lever.** A small code change: a signed OPP_MIX with weight 1 + k·share for STRAWBERRY only, d10-12, in `_candidates` v_seed. The current clip ceiling of 1× blocks k > 0.
- **Prediction.** Own −0.3..−0.5k/board, theirs −0.6..−1.0k, margin +0.3..+0.5k on V boards.
- **Gift risk.** V9_RACE pre-sells strawberry up to 40-48 turns ahead of our observed lots (v56leg/main.py:3729) and can turn the denial into our own crash. The wheat tiles given up are on a log book (about zero gift). The labour cost is 11.35 vs 4.64 turns per planting. The prior is low (standing law).
- **Test (≤ 2.5 h).** Complete grid k ∈ {0.5, 1, 2} × start day ∈ {10, 12}, 6 cells, dev100 exact sim vs V56, SAFETY_S=1e9. Ship bar: net flips ≥ +1 and dtheirs < 0. Then held100.

**H3 LIVECLOCK — VRP-era arms are judged with an unbounded search, but live runs a 0.65-0.75 s deadline (new condition: every gate since ROUTEOPT1 set SAFETY_S=1e9)**
- **Observable.** The local apply time is p99 0.50-0.67 s, max 0.705, against the 0.65-0.75 s deadline (VRPDEADLINE1 §2). The Kaggle CPU ratio has never been measured. Live, vrp3 is measured weaker than vrp2 (theta 2,598 vs 2,818, Δ220 ± 78) and loses 8/61 to opponents rated < 2,300 against vrp2's 1/22. vrp3 differs from vrp2 by one switch that gained +1..+7 offline.
- **Mechanism.** Heavy dawns truncate live. vrp2/vrp3 fall back to the planner table; vrp4 falls back to a checkpoint. The NEEDS_FIX trims interact with that truncation.
- **Lever.** Existing: base choice (vrp2 / vrp3 / vrp4), RR_ITERS, SAFETY_S/RESERVE_S. Small change: a time-scaled `_CLOCK`.
- **Prediction.** If truncation is material, the arm order under a ×1.5-2 clock differs from the SAFETY_S=1e9 order. That decides the Oct 1 final base. There is no gift channel.
- **Test (≤ 2 h).**
  1. Replay our seat on 10 live replays with SAFETY_S=1e9 and diff the day tables against the live actions; mismatched dawns = live truncation.
  2. dev100 for vrp2/vrp3/vrp4 with `route_vrp._CLOCK` scaled ×1.5 and ×2, SAFETY_S 0.75 (6 runs, 2 procs); count fired deadlines and flips against the unbounded rows already banked.
