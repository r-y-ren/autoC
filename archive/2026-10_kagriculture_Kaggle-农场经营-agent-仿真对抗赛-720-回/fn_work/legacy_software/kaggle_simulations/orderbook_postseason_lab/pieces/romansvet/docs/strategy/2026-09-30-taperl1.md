clones are pushovers (PFS 95-99 % vs clones, 27 % live). P48TAPE103 (brainstorm7) replays the real P48 side from its recorded
orders; P48 reacts only through its wool gate, so the tape is faithful except there.

## Trainer (`S/taperl1/train.py`)
PPO on the v1 residual head (18 slots / 92 logits / 64 feats), init = copy of head_940 (md5 769ff15e), learner JAX on GPU0,
2 spawn CPU rollout workers. Reward = ACTIONRL10 paired CRN baseline `A = (sign m - sign m0) + (m - m0)/1e4`, m0 = the identity
margin on the same tape (exact, so the baseline is noise-free per board), batch-centred. T = 0.5, batch 16 games, 4 epochs x 4
minibatches with a KL stop at 0.01, clip 0.2, Adam 3e-4 linear decay over 40 planned updates, grad clip 0.5, entropy 0.001.
Train = TUNE only (`S/genetape1/tune.txt`, 51 games); HOLD (52) never seen.
