## (5) Cash at purchase time (coordinator follow-up; `buys.py` -> res/buys.jsonl, `buyagg.py` -> res/buys.md)
Every successful animal or land purchase on d0-9 was logged with four things:
- the cash at the start of that step (before any sale);
- the sale fills committed earlier in the same step;
- the cash after the purchase;
- the sellable stock at the step start: carried by units, and deposited in the shed (value at that step's prices).

The engine replay and seats are the same as `led.py`.

**Hypothesis confirmed for P48: it buys in the same step as the sale.**
- **Q2** is bought on d6 at h3. In **100 %** of seats the step-start cash (387) is below the cost. The wool sold in that step (+1,264) pays for it, and the units still carry 2.8k of stock at that step.
- **Q3** is bought on d8 at h6. In **95 %** of seats the step-start cash (1,624) is short. The milk and other goods sold in that step (+979) pay for it.
- **Animals** are bought at h4-h9 (the modal hours), not at dawn.
- **Overall: 52 % of P48's d1-9 herd and land coins** (4.3k of 8.2k per seat) go through steps whose starting cash is below the price.
- The coordinator's example is reproduced exactly (ep 115010875 seat 1):
  - d2 h8: 480 + 193 of fertilizer - 400 for a cow = 273.
  - d6 h3: 144 + 1,271 of wool - 1,000 for Q2 = 415. Three more sheep follow at h4-h11.
- **The funding sequence is confirmed:**
  - d1-5: fertilizer at 5-7 u a day @99-90.
  - d6: wool, 18 u @196 = 3,533, pays for Q2.
  - d8: milk, 12 u @182 = 2,186, pays for Q3.
  - d9: wool, 12 u @147 = 1,782, pays for the d9 animals.

**PFS never does this.**
- Every PFS purchase is at h1 (land at h3), and **0 %** need a same-step sale.
- It holds 0.9-2.0k of cash at its purchase steps and still 1.1-1.9k afterwards, so its cash at purchase time is never short. The herd it buys is small by choice, not for lack of funds:
  - d1-5: 0.6 animals per seat vs P48's 3.8.
  - d6-7: 0.7 vs 5.3.
- It also keeps 1.1-2.3k of sellable stock in the shed at those steps.

**The port** logs only h12 state, so its cash at purchase time cannot be read without a game (this stream runs none).
- Its h12 cash is higher than P48's, yet Q3 waits until d10. That fits a buyer that needs the purchase money at the start of a step, as PFS's h1 market does.
- P48 instead lands Q3 in the d8 h6 step, out of the milk sold in that same step.

**This sharpens (4).**
- **The port fix:** buy herd and land in the same mid-morning step that sells the carried wool and milk (h3-h9), and keep no overnight reserve.
- **For PFS:** the same move applies to its own d5-9. It sits on the idle +1.12k per night plus the 1-2k of shed stock, and neither touches the d0 purse.
