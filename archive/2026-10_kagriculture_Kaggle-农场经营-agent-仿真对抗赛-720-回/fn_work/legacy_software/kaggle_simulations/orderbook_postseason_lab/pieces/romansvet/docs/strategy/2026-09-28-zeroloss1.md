Both ship the NONV2 zero-melon latch: `ENGINE_GATE_SET = MELON_PLATE_TILES=20;MELON_PLATE_DAY=1;NONV_PLATE_LAST=6`, latched at the d1 dawn when the rival shows 0 melon and at least 1 planting.
The work is analysis only: 15 recorded live games re-applied through the pinned engine (ECONCENSUS1 `measure()`, ledger valid 30/30 seats), and no agent was run.

## Verdict

- **The live ZERO class is 12-3** (vrp12 6-3 at 80 games, vrp10 6-0). The plate earns us ~30.8k of melon per game in 13/15 games, while the rival's d18-19 melons sell for ~0.5k.
- **The plate performed normally in 2 of the 3 losses.** Boesen made 30.9k and quantara L 30.8k, the same as the 30.3k wins mean.
- **Separator.** In all 3 losses:
  - the town drew **>= 2 milk shops by d12** (PIZZA / ICE_CREAM / SMOOTHIE consume MILK, engine L104-111);
  - **and** the rival ran a **9-12 cow herd** (ours was 4-5).
  - The milk price held at 181-229 through d15-20, and the rival banked 47.7-82.6k of milk.
  - The 12 wins have neither: milk-rich draw with rival cows >= 9 is **0/12**.
  - Milk price >= 180 at d15 is a looser split: 3 W (+0.5k, +8.3k, +8.5k) against 3 L, and 9-0 elsewhere.
- **A d1 latch rule cannot separate the losses.** Boesen and quantara L are byte-identical to 10 wins through d3: rival money 109/166/417, 19 wheat, 1 quadrant, 5 animals, 0 hands. Only Ebi (d1 money 39) and masayoshi (13, a +10.8k WIN) differ.
- **Candidate cell R1: d5-dawn plate cut.** If the rival has >= 5 melons on the board at the d5 dawn, end the plate at d4 (NONV_PLATE_LAST=4).
  - It fires on 1/15 live ZERO games (Ebi, 9 melons planted d4). masayoshi planted d6, so R1 does not fire there, and it touches 0 wins.
  - Expected value on Ebi is **about +3k (range −5k..+10k), 0 flips**. The game needs +71.8k.
  - R1 is **not worth a paired grid**: the ceiling is 0 flips, and it fires on none of the 7 BAND142 ZERO seats (standard opener, rival melon d5 = 0).
- **Rejected rules.**
  - A rival d1 wheat count gate: all rivals hold 19-20 wheat.
  - A land-purchase gate: rival land spend is 3,000 in all 15 games. Ebi and masayoshi buy Q2/Q3 early, masayoshi at 6/6 and a win.
  - A d1-money gate: fires on Ebi L and masayoshi W; it saves 0 and risks −1 flip.
- **The only lever seen is not on the latch.** It is a milk-draw × herd cell: the rival turns a milk-rich shop draw into 9-12 cows × ~220 milk. Boesen (−9.7k) and quantara (−25.1k) are within one herd response of flipping. The herd axis has a gift history (HERDTILT, HERD_FIRST), so this is a follow-up question, not a recommendation (see §5).

## 5. Named follow-up (not run; outside the latch): MILKDRAW

- **What the losses show.** All 3 losses are milk-rich shop draws where the rival holds 9-12 cows while we hold 4-5.
- **The observable.** The draw is visible at dawn: `town.unlocked_shops`, 2 milk shops by d9-12.
- **The candidate.** A gated cow add, "milk shops >= 2 at dawn → +k cows". Two effects:
  - our milk volume, which is also denial on the shared milk book the rival sells into;
  - flips possible in Boesen (−9.7k) and quantara L (−25.1k), but not Ebi.
- **Why it is only a candidate.**
  - The herd family's history is gifts: HERDTILT two-sided −5.2k, HERD_FIRST kills the herd gift, ENGHERD1.
  - The gate applies to all classes, not just ZERO.
  - It would need a BAND142 grid stratified by milk draw, before any cost estimate beyond "1-2 flips of 3 live ZERO losses".
- Recorded here as the only lever this stream found. It is not a recommendation to ship.
