# LIVEGATE — abstaining per-action gate

## Verdict

**FAIL.** All six action-specific models maximised board-grouped five-fold CV
net flips by always abstaining.  Their selected thresholds are `a=1.01` and
`b=0.00`, giving zero fires, zero flips, and mean delta-theirs 0.0.  This meets
the explicit early-stop condition (CV net <= 0 for every edit), so complete
train/dev rollouts were not run, sealed was not opened, and no
`live_gate.npz` was saved.

The negative result is informative: across all three dawns, isolated edits
produced 24 loss-to-win labels and 174 win-to-loss labels.  A small state-only
logistic gate could not separate those rescues from regressions out of board.

## Exact labels

`live_gate_labels.py` replayed the chronological 150-board training panel
(109 wins, 41 losses) using the exact seed, original seat, pinned town, taped
opponent, and frozen `head_940`.  The 150 incumbent trajectories matched their
recorded final purses exactly.  It then ran one exact real-engine continuation
for each of 150 boards x three dawns x six edits = 2,700 continuations.

The feature vector is the incumbent dawn's existing 64-value head vector plus
explicit copies of its normalized day and own purse.  Episode, seed, and game
index are metadata only and never model inputs.  Actual opponent purse was
not added: under the shipped switches `view.opp_money` is the head's zero
default, and exposing the observed purse would require the prohibited
production parsing change.

## Board-grouped CV

For each edit, two class-balanced L2 logistic heads predict loss-to-win and
win-to-loss.  Folds are `game_index mod 5`, so all three dawns from a board
remain in one fold.  Thresholds use out-of-fold predictions only.  The rule is
`P(flip) >= a` and `P(break) <= b`; among eligible edits runtime would select
the largest `P(flip)-P(break)` and change at most one slot per dawn.

The denominators behind combined CV are 41 loss boards / 109 win boards
(123 loss-dawns / 327 win-dawns).  The gate fires zero times on training CV;
there is therefore no first-divergence day.  Dev fire count and first
divergence are **not run**, as required by the early-stop rule.

## Runtime identity

`live_gate_policy.py` is a NumPy-only experimental wrapper around the frozen
head.  Outside d10--12, below threshold, or when an edit target already equals
the incumbent action, it returns the incumbent categorical action bytes.  If
armed with a passing model it can alter at most one slot at each target dawn.
No production parser or planner file was changed.
