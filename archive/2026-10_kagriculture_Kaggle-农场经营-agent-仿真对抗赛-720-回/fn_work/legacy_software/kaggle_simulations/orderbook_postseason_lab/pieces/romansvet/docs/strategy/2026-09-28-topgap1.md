# TOPGAP1 — top-5 capacity programme ported into the V56 body after handoff (2026-09-28)

Stream TOPGAP1 (Opus xhigh, 180-min box, 2 sim workers). Question: TOPOPEN1 measured that the top-5 finish +20.6k over V56 on the
same board, via capacity (3rd quadrant d8-9, 12 vs 9 hands at d12) feeding a d10-29 second wave (tomato +7.4k, eggs +5.3k,
carrot +3.9k). Ported into V56's body after the KERNEL2 handoff, as switches, does that capacity win MELON seats, paired?

Every override is logic in one appended layer, `_tg_agent` (the last callable, wrapping the pre-TOPGAP1 agent). Crew hands take
the actor indices after the tape's roster, work only SE tiles (geese nearest the shed, then tomato, then carrot), drop
products at the shed before midnight, and every crew DROP appends a SELL of what it carried. OFF (all `V56_*` None) is
byte-identical: tests/test_topgap1.py (3 boards x 96 steps vs the kernel source without the TOPGAP1 block; the wrapper's parent
is the pre-TOPGAP1 last callable). The grid's base column reproduces the V56TUNE1 base rows exactly on all 20 seats.

**The farm is full, and cash is short until d10.** On the V56 body (seat 1 replay, S/topgap1/occ.txt): NW is fully planted d0-5,
NE is planted on d6 the day it is bought, SW on d11 the day it is bought. There is no empty owned tile d6-d29. Cash is 0.6k-2.3k
at the end of every day d5-d9, and the melon income arrives on d10 (18k). So:
- the 3rd quadrant cannot be bought on d8 in the V56 economy. G1 never fired by d9 on any of the 20 seats (quads_d9 = 2 on all).
  It buys at d10 (the first affordable hour) instead of the tape's d11 h1. The tape plants SW only from d11, so one extra empty day.
- the only capacity to add is the 4th quadrant (SE, 4,000), from d10 at the earliest.
- every extra hand is the 10th-14th hire of the day: fib(9..13) = 55-377 coins per hand-day (engine `_hire_cost`), on top of V56's 9-11.

## 3. Why every crew arm loses coins (seat 1, istinetz_114301085, same board, S/topgap1/flow_seat1.txt)

| | base | G2 geese | G3 tomato | G4 carrot |
|---|---|---|---|---|

| programme product sales | egg 4,883 | egg 10,894 (+6.0k) | tomato 4,853 | carrot 4,928 (+0.8k) |
| land | 2 quadrants tape | + SE 4,000 | + SE 4,000 | + SE 4,000 |

| other | | wheat bought +5.8k (feed), wheat sold +1.9k, 8 geese 2.4k, milk -1.8k | 12 seeds 0.6k, fertilizer +1.2k | 21 seeds 0.4k, milk -1.8k |

- Tomato: the engine caps an ongoing plant at 4 productions (`max_yield` 4 = production count; +2 per watered+fertilized day).
  A d12 wave gives at most 4-8 units per tile on d20-23. 12 tiles returned 4.9k coins against 4k land + 1.8k seed/fertilizer +
  2.2k hires.
- Geese: 7-9 geese standing from d12-13 (the gap's capacity is reproduced, geese_d12 8.7 vs 2.7). But each goose-day costs about
  one bought wheat (~40) plus a share of 2 marginal hands, and returns about 2 eggs. Eggs +6k do not cover feed + hands + land.
  The rival also gains (dtheirs +3.1k, t 2.0, GIFT on G2/G5), and 6 of 9 base wins are lost.
- Carrot: SE is bought only on d21 (4,000) for about 2 waves of 3-4 units per tile. That leaves +0.8k product against 4k land.
- The capacity columns move the way TOPOPEN1 measured for the top-5 (hands 12 vs 9.2 at d12, geese 8.7 vs 2.7, d10-29 sale coins
  up to +9.2k). The P&L does not follow, because V56 gets capacity at the wrong price. Land comes after d10 at 4,000, and hands
  are the 10th-14th hire of the day.
- G1: land at d8 is cash-infeasible in V56 (quads_d9 = 2 on 19/20 seats; the fire day is d9-10). The game is byte-identical on 11
  of 20 seats. Two seats move +16k/+24k and +13k/+23k (ours/theirs), a market cascade, and one of them (thunderthunder) flips a
  win into a loss.

## 4. Verdict: NO SHIP

On the 20 forced-handoff MELON seats, paired, no part of the top-5 capacity programme wins games once it is ported into the
V56 body. G1 -1 flip, G3 -4, G4 -5, G2/G5/G6/G7 -6. ours -3.9k (G3) to -17.3k (G7) per seat, all t <= -8.8 except G1.
The top-5's +20.6k is not "V56 + more capacity". Their capacity comes early and cheap: land when they have cash on d8-9,
geese from d6, hands inside a smaller roster, and it is financed by their own d1-9 economy (strawberry/staged melons). V56's
body has a full farm and less than 2.3k cash until the d10 melon income, and it already hires 9-11 hands a day. Bolting on SE
land plus marginal hands after d10 costs more than the second wave returns, even before the rival's reaction. Adding the 4k land
back (free land, arithmetic only, not measured) still leaves G2 -6.5k and G4 -0.8k; G3 would be about +0.1k/seat, and 0 flips
would come back on this prefix.
The 9 `V56_*` TOPGAP1 items stay None (inert). vrp15/v4g: unchanged. The capacity gap can only be closed by a different d0-d9
economy (the top-5 opening), not by the V56 body after handoff.
