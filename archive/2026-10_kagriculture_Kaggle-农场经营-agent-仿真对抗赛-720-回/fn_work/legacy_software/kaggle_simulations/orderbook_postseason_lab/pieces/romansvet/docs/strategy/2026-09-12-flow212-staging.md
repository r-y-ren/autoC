## 3. `--rung-weight 0`: it costs an episode, and it does not touch `rank_normalise`

**(a) A weight-0 pinned rung is still played.** `Trainer.pinned_block`
(`src/kagg3/es/train.py:4413-4429`) puts *every* pinned rung in the block and settles this case in
its own docstring: *"Every pinned rung is in the block, including one at `--rung-weight 0` … the
weight is applied in the aggregation (`pinned_weights`), where a zero still means 'not in the
objective'. The alternative — dropping weight-0 rungs to save an episode — would make the block's
size depend on the weights, which is exactly the coupling the flag removes."* `pinned_keep`
(`:4432-4443`) gives each pinned rung exactly one episode and never truncates. So flow212 plays
**196 pinned + 4 residual = 200 episodes/gen**, unchanged, and **39 of those 200 (19.5 % of the wall
clock) buy nothing but a free read** of the zeroed families.

That is deliberate. Keeping them pinned keeps the episode composition, the `--pinned-fixed-seed`
board set, the seat rotation, `n_pairs` and the abs-probe shape **identical to flow211**, so
flow211 vs flow212 under the shared `--seed 309` CRN draw is a pure **weight** A/B. Removing them
from `--tape-actions` instead would buy back ~19 % of the generation but change the rung count
(200 → 161), the abs-probe shape, the residual allocation and the self-play tail at once — three
confounds to save 19 %. (It is also the free read: the zeroed families are still *measured* every
generation in the gen line, which is exactly what a transfer hypothesis wants to watch.)

**(b) A weight-0 episode never enters the rank normalisation.** `rank_normalise` (`:509-526`) ranks
over the **population** axis, not the episode axis, and `shaped_advantage` (`:584-668`) collapses the
episode axis **first**:

```
w = ep_weight / sum(ep_weight)
rel, own = (rel * w).sum(axis=1), (own * w).sum(axis=1)
return abs_weight * rank_normalise(own) + (1 - abs_weight) * rank_normalise(rel)
```

A weight-0 episode contributes exactly `0.0` to every candidate's collapsed `rel` and `own`
**before any ranking happens**. It therefore cannot shift a rank, cannot create or break a tie in
`rank_normalise`'s tied-rank averaging, and cannot dilute the normalisation — the rank denominator is
`pop − 1`, untouched. `sum(w) = 1292 > 0`, so `w / sum(w)` is well defined (only an all-zero weight
vector would divide by zero, and flow212 is nowhere near it). **Net: weight 0 = "played, read,
ignored"; the gradient is identical to one computed from a 161-episode objective that never rolled
those boards out.**

**(c) `mean_win` is even less readable than flow211's.** It is an *unweighted* episode mean
(`:5009`); `ep_weight` reaches the gradient only, by design (`:5007-5008`). For flow212, **39 of the
200 logged episodes (19.5 %) are outside the objective entirely**, and NEXT30 is 15 % of the logged
number while carrying 47.4 % of it. Read `best`, the gate RECORD lines and the held-out judge legs;
never flow212's `mean_win` level against flow211's or flow209's.

## 7. Reading rule

**flow212 vs flow211 is the clean pair**: same recipe, same 196 boards, same CRN seed (309), weights
only. flow212 vs flow209 adds the NEXT30 family on top of the weight change — read that pair second.
Both arms' band column is the held-out **NEXT14**, never NEXT30.

* flow212 gains on the band legs where flow211 does not → the §107 transfer was the whole story and
  the fix is the objective, not the interface.
* both flat → the band was never the constraint; the answer is upstream of the rung list (§85's
  market-row cadence, §60/§66/§76's lattice).
* flow212 gains on the band and **loses TOPB2** → the transfer running in reverse (band margin bought
  with top-tier margin). A finding, not a promotion: TOPB2 vetoes.

47 % of flow212's objective has **zero** representation in the 124-game real gate, so a quiet gate
log is not evidence of failure — and 19.5 % of its wall clock is a free read of the three families it
has stopped optimising, which is the cheapest available check on whether the trade is symmetric.

**Staged, not launched. Which arm it replaces — and whether flow211 or flow212 goes first — is the
user's call.** flow211 remains the more conservative member of the pair (top-ten at 10 %, not 0).
