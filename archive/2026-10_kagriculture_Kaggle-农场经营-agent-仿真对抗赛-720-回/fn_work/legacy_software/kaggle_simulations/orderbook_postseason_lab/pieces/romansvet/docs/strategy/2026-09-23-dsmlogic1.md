# DSMLOGIC1: the decision logic of rank-1 DSM (sub 56463942, 128-3), mined from 131 replays (2026-09-23)

Branch `dsmlogic1`, READ-ONLY (no src change). Harness `S/dsmlogic1/` (`run_all.sh`):
- `extract.py` compresses each DSM tape (dsmfull1 worktree, `S/dsmtapes_0923`) and each OURS seat-swap replay (`S/dsmfull1/replays/OURS`, same 131 boards, our master stack in DSM's seat) into per-step pickles.
- The mining scripts write `routine.tsv` (phase x hour x role action shares), `rules.tsv` (rule, reads, fidelity), `exceptions.tsv`, `crewtime.tsv` (per unit-day, DSM vs OURS), `water.tsv`, `animals.tsv`, `sales.tsv`, `layout.tsv`, `days.tsv` and `hires.tsv`. Printed results are in `logs/`.

Fidelity is the share of DSM's decisions of that type the one-line rule reproduces across all 131 games (d10-27 unless noted).

## 0. Architecture (what kind of program DSM is)
- **Deterministic function of the full observation**: grouping steps by the full observation gives identical DSM actions in 2,737 of 2,738 groups (d0-11, `oppdep2.py`).
- **A per-unit job dispatcher on top of a day-scripted economy.** Each hand walks straight to the nearest open job tile and completes every due op on that tile before it leaves:
  - nearest 90.7 %, within +1 98.5 %, direct path 99.8 %;
  - assignment is dynamic, not zoned (the same hand's tile set overlaps day to day with Jaccard 0.155; ours is 0.381).
- **Jobs are classified MANDATORY or FILLER.** Filler absorbs every spare turn, so a hand never PASSes after d9.
- **Market orders**: a fixed hire and land calendar, JIT 1-unit seed orders, and two sale modes (an evening flush and a shop-tick drip).

## 1. DSM PROGRAM SPEC (numbered rules, by phase and hour; fidelity in brackets)
**Opening d0-5 (scripted) [d0 99-100 %, d1 72 %, d2 88 %, d3 90 %, d4-5 91-100 %]**
1. d0 h1: HIRE 4. BUY_ANIMAL 2 COW + 3 SHEEP. BUY_PRODUCT 9 WHEAT (feed). BUILD_PASTURE x5 on the tiles next to the shed. PLANT 9 WHEAT (NW column x0-2) + 6 MELON (x1-4, y0-4). Seeds are bought as the planting happens, and the purse ends near 5 coins.
2. d1: 8 HIRE orders (the purse caps them at 4 hands) and +4 MELON, so the plate is 10. d2: 6 hands, 4 STRAWBERRY. d3: 6 STRAWBERRY (NW 3x4 block). d4-5: 6 hands and no planting (the purse is empty; this is the 206 mid-day PASS).
**Expansion d6-12**
3. Land [Q2 131/131, Q3 131/131, Q4 125/131]:
   - Q2 at d6 h5.
   - Q3 at d9 (h3 70 %, else the next tick h5/h1/h7).
   - Q4: BUY_LAND is re-issued every turn from d10 ~h12 and fills at the first turn the purse reaches 4,000 (d10 h12-14 69 %, else d11).
4. Herd, d6 after Q2: BUY_ANIMAL at h6/8/10/12/14 with a **price-conditioned mix**. The mean is +4.2 COW, +1.9 GOOSE, +1.3 SHEEP. SHEEP are bought only when WOOL ≥ ~200 (corr 0.70), cow count tracks the MILK price (0.77) and geese the EGG price (0.55). On d9-12 geese are topped up to 6.6. From d13 there are no more buys.
5. Feed wheat: BUY_PRODUCT WHEAT d0 9, d1 12, d6-8 9-18/day, d10 82, then 1-5/day. After d10 the wheat relay feeds the herd.
**Crew (every day)**
6. Hands per day [exact 75.5 %, ±1 98.2 %; d0-9 exact 85-100 %]:

   | day | d0 | d1 | d2-5 | d6 | d7-8 | d9 | d10 | d11-13 | d14-24 | d25-28 | d29 |
   |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
   | hands | 4 | 4 | 6 | 8 | 9 | 10 | 16 orders, filled to the purse (10-14) | 11 | 12 (11 on ~45 % of days) | 11 | 10 |

   Hires are issued as 8 HIRE at h0 plus the rest at h1 (the 10-order cap per turn). The 12th hand is not a function of day-start state (best single rule: animals ≥ 22, 64 %).
7. New hands spawn at the shed. h1-2: PICKUP WHEAT (feed) and, d20+, PICKUP FERTILIZER. From h2 every hand works; the last op is at h22-23. PASS is 0.02 per hand-day on d10-29 [PASS 0.4 hand-turns per game].
**Tile jobs (dispatcher; each unit takes the nearest open job, rule C2)**
8. ANIMAL tile, h2-h10 block, done as the triple FEED → CARE → COLLECT_FERTILIZER on one stop:
   - COLLECT every animal every day [97-100 %];
   - FEED+CARE are MANDATORY on production nights and after an unfed day [COW 98.6 %, SHEEP 94.5 %, GOOSE 85 %], and FILLER otherwise [COW 56 %, SHEEP 75 %];
   - HARVEST an animal when its yield is > 0;
   - sheep and cows are let go late (sheep 6.9 → 1.0 by d29).
9. WATER a plant iff one of these holds: planted today; consecutive_unwatered ≥ 1 (death guard); a one-shot crop in its window [(maxday+1)//2 .. maxday] with yield < max; or an ongoing crop that is fertilized and produces tonight.
   - These mandatory classes run at 95.0-96.5 %, and the rule overall at 84.3 %.
   - Everything else is FILLER: ongoing tiles with cu0 are watered at 51.8 %, mean h18. This filler is where DSM's idle turns go.
10. FERTILIZE (fert carried from COLLECT, no shed trip):
    - WHEAT and CARROT at age 2, the first window day, before WATER, so the waters on ages 2-4 add +2 [wheat age-2 tiles 62 %, 39 % of all FERTILIZE];
    - STRAWBERRY at ages 9/12/15 and TOMATO at 7/10, just before production nights.
11. HARVEST:
    - WHEAT at age 4 (6 u), or at age 3 when fertilized (after the water, 5 u) [81.7 %];
    - CARROT at age 3 [91.1 %];
    - MELON at age 10 [100 %].
    - The exception is fertilized wheat at age 3, which waits one more day 41 % of the time.
12. **Relay:** a one-shot harvest is followed by the SAME unit PLANTing on the next turn and WATERing (the plant-day water is mandatory) [next turn 90.3 %, same unit 91.8 %]. That is 175 replants per game d10-27 (ours 74).
13. Crop choice for a free tile [tree depth 4 = 80.4 %, majority 61 %]:
    - replant the harvested crop (W→W, C→C; same crop 70.4 %);
    - STRAWBERRY only d2-15, TOMATO d9-19 on the x ≤ 1 corner tiles (y 0-1 and 6-9);
    - CARROT takes the inner tiles: 9 % of plantings d10 → 57 % d26 (a 3-day cycle fits the tail);
    - WHEAT everywhere else, including the edges.
    - Plantings per day d10-27: 10-16.
14. Seeds: 1-unit BUY_SEED orders through h4-h20 (carrot in 6-packs), so stock is ≥ 5 at 63 % of plantings. No unit ever waits for seed, and there is no h1 seed row.
**Sales**
15. FLUSH at h22 (residue h23): sell the whole shed stock of WHEAT, EGG, TOMATO, CARROT and FERTILIZER [P(sell | stock) 0.92 / 0.98 / 1.00 / 0.89 / 0.98].
16. MORNING h0-2:
    - a partial WHEAT sale (42 % of wheat units) that keeps about 27 u for feed;
    - the FERTILIZER surplus (85 % of fert units; 112 u on d10-19).
17. DRIP: STRAWBERRY, MILK and WOOL are sold in 1-2 unit lots at the town-shop ticks h1/5/9/13/17/21, right after the book drops.
    - Share of sale orders on a tick: 61 % / 86 % / 78 %.
    - P(sell | tick) 0.58 / 0.44 / 0.58, against 0.13 / 0.03 / 0.06 off-tick.
    - Price level is NOT the trigger: marginal price / base ranges over 0.2-1.8.
**Endgame d28-29**
18. No planting from d28 (0.35 per game), then harvest everything. From d29 h14 the hands walk to the shed and DROP (0.85 per hand-day), and the stock is flushed at h22.

## 2. Crew time: where DSM's +2.6 ops per unit-day come from (hands, per hand-day, `crewtime.tsv`)
| | DSM d10-19 | OURS d10-19 | DSM d20-27 | OURS d20-27 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| turns | 22.68 | 22.81 | 22.67 | 22.78 |
| work ops | **13.65** | 11.43 | **14.22** | 11.24 |
| moves | 9.01 | 9.54 | 8.43 | 9.82 |
| PASS (h0-2 / h3-20 / h21-23) | **0.02** (0/0/0.01) | 1.85 (0.77/0.20/0.87) | 0.02 | 1.72 (0.74/0.12/0.86) |
| shed ops (PICKUP/PLACE/DROP) | 0.82 | 1.24 | 0.62 | 1.18 |
| moves per stop / ops per stop | **1.24 / 2.05** | 1.57 / 1.88 | **1.17 / 2.11** | 1.60 / 1.83 |
| first / last work hour | 2.2 / 22.2 | 2.7 / 22.0 | 2.0 / 22.4 | 2.9 / 22.0 |

- **Mechanism, with numbers.**
  - d10-19: the +2.22 ops = +1.83 from zero PASS (82 %) + 0.53 from fewer moves − 0.13 from turns.
  - d20-27: the +2.98 ops = +1.70 PASS + 1.39 moves − 0.11.
  - Over d10-27 that is +648 hand work ops per game (2,882 vs 2,234).
- **PASS → work** needs three things:
  - (a) no h0-2 wait: seed is already in stock (rule 14), and the h1 PICKUP is only feed wheat or fertilizer;
  - (b) no dusk stop: FILLER jobs (optional water on ongoing tiles, off-production feed/care: ≈ 350 optional ops per game, estimated from water.tsv and animals.tsv) keep every hand busy to h23;
  - (c) fewer shed ops (−0.42 to −0.56 per hand-day): fertilizer goes COLLECT → FERTILIZE in the field, and feed wheat comes from one h1 PICKUP.
- **Fewer moves:** jobs are denser. All 100 tiles are active, and the relay puts HARVEST-PLANT-WATER on one stop, as do FEED-CARE-COLLECT, so a stop carries 2.05-2.11 ops and costs 1.17-1.24 moves (ours 1.57-1.60).
  - Routing itself is NOT better: both sides take the nearest job about 90 % of the time, with mean trip lengths of 1.23 vs 1.26.
- **The extra ops by type** (per hand-day, d10-19 / d20-27): WATER +1.36/+1.91, PLANT +0.55/+0.55, HARVEST +0.46/+0.71, FERTILIZE +0.14/+0.49, PICKUP −0.58/−0.70.

## 3. Opponent dependence
- DSM is **market-reactive, not opponent-board-reactive**. When the own state is identical (same tiles, purse, seeds, shed, unit positions and inventories), actions still diverge in 268 of 3,013 step groups (8.9 %).
  - Adding the market book (prices + inventory) resolves 240 of them.
  - Only 27 more are resolved by adding the opponent board or town.
- Even unit moves shift with a 1-unit change in the wheat book (d0 h21-22), which suggests the jobs are valued at market prices.
- The concrete channels are:
  - (i) the d6 herd mix follows WOOL/MILK/EGG prices, which the rival's sales move (rule 4);
  - (ii) the drip sells fire on book drops (dinv < 0), which includes the rival's buys (rule 17);
  - (iii) partial lot sizes.
- There is no evidence of reading the opponent's tiles. A port must feed DSM's price-conditioned choices from the live book and must not freeze them to one table.
