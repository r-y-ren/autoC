Definitions:
- Standing tiles and animals are an hour-23 snapshot. Cash is taken at hour 0.
- Hands = the maximum number of hands during the day.
- Wheat and fertilizer bought = BUY_PRODUCT quantity ordered.
- Sale units per step = clip(holdings[t-1] + bought + same-step harvest − holdings[t], 0, SELL qty), where holdings = shed + unit inventories. Coins = units × pre-trade price. On SELL-only steps the estimate is **0.988×** the realised cash (43.7M vs 44.2M). Estimators that looked only at the shed were off by 0.75–1.25×; see `salecheck*.txt`.
- Plants lost = a plant that disappeared with no HARVEST or DIG on its tile (it died), plus plants that expired while ripe.
- The **paired** block compares the same episode (same board, same market): TOP5 seat vs V56F seat, n = 49 (DSM 28, M&M&P&Q 18, Vadim 3).

## 4. The 5 largest V56 → top-5 gaps a KERNEL2 body could copy (coin terms, same-board paired)

1. **Tomato second wave, d12–d24: +7.4k coins d10–29 (t 3.8).** TOP5 stands 9 / 15 / 15 / 10 tomato tiles at d15 / d18 / d21 / d24. V56 stands 1 / 6 / 7 / 6.
2. **Geese from d6, 7 standing by d12: +5.3k egg coins d10–29 (t 9.7).** Geese standing at d6 / d9 / d12: TOP5 1 / 2 / 7, V56 0 / 0 / 2. V56 buys its first geese at d10 and holds 2–3 geese all game.
3. **Earlier expansion: 3rd quadrant at d8–d9 instead of d11; +21 / +25 productive tiles at d9 / d10 (t 26 / 35); hands held at 11–12 through d12–d20 instead of 9–10.** This is the capacity behind gaps 1, 2 and 4. d10–29 sales are +11.8k in total (t 4.0). Cash is below V56 until d15 (−7.9k at d11 because V56's melon money arrives at d10), cross between d15 and d20, then +7.0k at d20 and +13.6k at d25.
4. **Carrot late wave, d21–d24: +3.9k coins d10–29 (t 5.0).** TOP5 stands 8 / 15 carrot tiles at d21 / d24; V56 stands 3 / 7.
5. **Early strawberries instead of the extra melon, d2–d4: +2.0k strawberry coins (t 2.3).** TOP5 stands 3 / 9 / 18 strawberry tiles at d2 / d4 / d6; V56 stands 0 / 0 / 12. This is paid for by standing 6 (then 10) melon tiles instead of 12, which costs −3.3k melon coins, so the melon cut alone is **not** worth copying. The strawberry tiles and the d9 land are what the cut buys.
   - Also in V56's favour: fertilizer. V56 buys +63 units (−2.2k coins) and sells +3.3k more fertilizer (net about +1k).
   - Hands at d0: TOP5 hires 4, V56 hires 5.

For the requested items that are **not** gaps: d0 hands (4–5 for everyone; V56 is not a 1-hire opener in the replays), herd (cows+sheep 11–14 at d9 for TOP5, 12–13 for V56), wheat bought d0–9 (TOP5 excl. Boey 72–101 units, V56F 80), water/care per unit (3–4 / about 1 for both), and plant losses (median 0 for all groups in d0–9; 5 vs 3 over the game).

## 5. One programme or several?

Between-team share of the d0–d9 variance is **0.47**. k-means on per-seat d0–d9 vectors (`S/topopen1/clusters.txt`) finds one core programme and two variants:

- **Core programme, all teams except Boey.** d0: 6 melon, 12 wheat, 2 cows + 3 sheep, 4–5 hires. Strawberries from d2. Geese from d6–d9. 3rd quadrant d8–d9. 75 productive tiles and 10 hands at d9. Melon harvest at d10, part of it staggered.
- **Variant a (d1 top-up):** 6 melon, then 10 at d1. This is DSM 41/77 and Vadim 41/76. DSM and Vadim split almost identically, which suggests the same code.
- **Variant b:** 6 → 8 at d1, then → 12 at d6–d9. This is DECEM 43/43, M&M&P&Q 68/77, and the rest of DSM/Vadim.
- **Boey is a separate programme (27/27):** a wheat relay (buy 58 / 113 wheat units at d0 / d1, 1,523 units d0–d9, 136k gross wheat coins over the game), otherwise the same body.
- V56F and V56K all fall nearest variant a, at 2.6× the TOP5 within-cluster distance.
- PFS is the farthest (6.5×).

## 6. Missing / limits

- **No V56 per-day trajectory from the judge.** `S/judgeall1/runs/mh4v2/rp/` is empty and its CSV rows hold only final money and h1 cash. `S/bcbody1/res_*.csv` rows hold the clone's d0–d2 and d10 summary only.
- **So V56 = 126 V-family rival seats plus our 3 live fired games.** The identical d1–d9 medians make the family match certain, but the exact V56 build is not verified per seat.
- The V56F seats are mostly losers (9/126 W), because most face top-5 teams. The paired block controls the board, not the rival.
- Obs carry no hire, seed or animal prices, so the cost side is only wheat and fertilizer (qty × pre-trade price).
- Boey n = 27, and its relay dominates the pooled wheat means (use the medians).

## Scripts (all under `S/topopen1/`)

- `mklist.py` → `eps.tsv` (463 episodes).
- `census.py eps.tsv` → `days.tsv` (JSON line per episode/seat/day, both seats) and `seats.tsv`. One process, about 9 min.
- `agg.py` → `long.tsv` (group × day × metric: n / median / IQR / mean) and `tables.md` (all tables A–E below).
- `paired.py` → `paired.md`.
- `cluster.py` → `clusters.txt`.
- `salecheck*.py` → estimator validation.
- The shed-only sale estimators were superseded; their runs were deleted. `days.tsv` is kept on disk as `days.tsv.gz` (3 MB) and agg.py reads either. S/ data files (tsv/txt/gz) are gitignored, so only the scripts and the .md tables are in git; clusters.txt is copied into the appendix below.
