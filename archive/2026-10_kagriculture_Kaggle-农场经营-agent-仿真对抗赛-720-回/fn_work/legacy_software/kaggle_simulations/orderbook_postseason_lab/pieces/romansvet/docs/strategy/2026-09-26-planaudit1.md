## 1. Map: planning horizon of the shipped agent (read from code; file:line in the worktree)
- **One-day planner.** `build_day` rebuilds the whole day at h0 from that morning's obs and keeps no plan for tomorrow (plan.py:11551). Hires come from an argmax
  over today's admitted task value (plan.py:11867-11935). `_prestock` (buy for tomorrow) is OFF (`PRESTOCK_ON=False`, plan.py:3495).
- **Lookahead is valuation only.** `board_forecasts` projects output at +1/+3/+7 days (`FCAST_HORIZONS`, brain.py:299) and `forward_value` values it at TODAY's price, with
  no price forecast by design (brain.py:482-485). `expected_drain` counts town demand to the end of the game (brain.py:208-226). Survival water is priced at the
  crop's remaining stream to pay_day (valuation.py:214). `can_mature`/`mature` gates stop plantings that cannot pay (brain.py:1230, plan.py:8178). The hire scan
  may count tasks up to `macro.forward_days` 0-6 ahead (plan.py:11914). The daily Macro (plant_target, animal_want, hire_bias, hold/press, ...) is one net output per dawn.
- **Open-loop tables:** `PROGRAM_ANIMALS/SEEDS/HANDS/LAND` (runtime.py:23-51) are fixed per-day schedules and are OFF in the shipped agent (`PROGRAM_ENGINE_ON=False`).
  sell.py `hold`/`press` are the value of keeping stock one more day and the within-day delay cost; neither reads prices beyond today's lots. `SELL_SPREAD_ON=False`.
- **Route-dependent? No.** `build_day` runs first, then `route_vrp.apply` only drops hires (runtime.py:685-698). `ROUTE_VRP_SHADOW_ON` hides `vrp_saved` from every
  later observation (runtime.py:617-620), so the planner plans exactly as with the router OFF. The spare capacity (`vrp_fill`) reaches no ask (`ROUTE_FILL_MODE="ii"`),
  and the Q4 release is OFF. Router-to-planner feedback is 0 by design.
- Already tried: MPCFEAS rollout planner NO-GO (the shop draw re-rolls, ranking = lottery); PLANSELECT oracle +3.2k but not selectable; PLANNER3 list
  scheduler NO-GO (no reachable work); PLANNER4 PARKED (Macro already creates the work); VOLUMEHI (no lever clears the cap); RELAYFILL1/CREWRELAY1 (d20-29 volume
  CLOSED); TOMATOFILL1 (tomato fills gift V56 +0.8-2.3k).

## 2. Headroom: offline planting oracles (opponent-free, rival sales FROZEN = upper bound on the market side)
Model (`oracle.py`). A programme is a set of plantings (crop, day). Each planting uses our own pooled empirical template: units by age, crop ops by age,
tile-days and death rate (wheat 4.78 u / 6.6 ops / 4 d; carrot 3.0/5.2/3; tomato 4.5/11.0/10.3; strawberry 7.5/15.9/17.7; melon 6.0/10.6/10).
Extra or missing units are sold or withheld on the harvest day and move the product's market inventory for every later sale; the inventory never resets.
The SELL rows are clipped to dawn shed + that day's harvest, which reproduces 86 % of units and 91 % of sale revenue. Tiles per day = empty + weed + crop tiles at dawn. Seeds are unbounded at list price.
Herd, land, cash and animal sales are left as shipped.
Arms: A = labour-capped (crop ops/day <= shipped); B = extra ops priced at the day's marginal fib hire (hand throughput = shipped work ops/hand), with dropped ops saving wages; glob = hindsight, any order;
B(H) = rolling day by day, prices known through d+H-1 and frozen beyond. 'scratch' = rebuild the whole programme; 'add' = only add to the shipped plantings.
Value = crop sale revenue − seeds − Δhire bill; ship = 58,576/game. The d0-9 melon plate is CLOSED (MELONVRP1/MELONGENES1: V56 takes it back −15..−30k, and it
is purse-capped at d0), so the second row forbids d0-9 melon adds.

(wheat −4.1k, carrot −2.9k). Glob adds a little strawberry and melon. It is a crop-MIX reallocation, not added volume. Adding to the shipped programme is worth ≤ +1.0k even with hindsight,
and ≈ 0 at a 1-3 day horizon.
**Horizon:** the reallocation needs hindsight. With the market known only 3 days ahead, the rolling rebuild LOSES 8.5k/game. It plants strawberry d10-19

What 30-day hindsight knows is the rival's future volume and the shop draw, which is exactly MPCFEAS's lottery.
Late wheat/carrot CUT alone (`cut.py`): best cell is wheat+carrot d>=24, 25 % latest first, +180/game (t 17, model); every other cell is ≤ +114 or negative.
