## 1. Diagnosis (OFF, dev100 861 u, held-out 922 u)
All hour-23 night transfer, shed EMPTY at dusk: the crew (up to 13 units) works until hour 23 and carries the whole
day's harvest into the transfer; hands never visit the shed. d14 is the wheat harvest-and-replant day
(HARVEST, PLANT WHEAT, WATER until dusk): the plan CARRIES the d14 harvest overnight, it never deposits or sells it
before replanting. Excess per event is small (10–20 u over a crew load of ~110–120).
Engine rules used for pricing (sim/units.py, eod.py, engine l.434-782): a tile is watered once a day; one-time crops
gain yield only on in-window waters; two dry days kill; one-time crops rot from hour 0 of day planted+max_day+1
(1 u / 2 steps); fertilizer availability resets nightly (uncollected = lost); EOD destroys the LAST units' cargo.

## 2. Fix — `plan.OVERFLOW_GUARD_V3=False` (runtime, after V2; OFF = master byte for byte)
`agent/overflow.py:guard_v3` replays the day (`_trace`) and, while projected night stock > 100, picks the best of
per unit/hour: A = walk to the shed, DROP/PLACE, SELL cheapest-marginal (displaces ops t..23);
B = detour and resume (displaces the day's last 2d+1 ops, d ≤ 3, never shifts a shed op);
C = cut a harvest into destruction (crop left ripe, sold a day later).
Displaced jobs priced by `_job_cost`: WATER 0 if already watered/emptied, INF if planted today or dry ≥ 1,
bonus×price in the one-time window, 0.25×price otherwise; HARVEST 0+one-day cycle delay if the crop keeps,
n×price if it would rot; COLLECT_FERT = fert price; PLANT = one cycle-day (full yield if it can't mature);
CARE ½ unit; FEED/FERTILIZE/shed/DIG/BUILD = INF. Taken only if saved − displaced > MIN_NET=20 coins;
units left on the tile weigh DEFER_W=0.5. Trips on dev: C 191, A 46, B 43; displaced HARVEST 207, WATER 82, COLLECT 27.
