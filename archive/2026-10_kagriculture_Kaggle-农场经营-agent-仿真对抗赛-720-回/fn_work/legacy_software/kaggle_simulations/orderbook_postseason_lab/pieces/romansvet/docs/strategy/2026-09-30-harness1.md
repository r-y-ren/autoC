# HARNESS1 — a tape-anchored reacting P48/PQ4 rival (2026-09-30, 14:06-15:30Z)

**Question.** The P48/PQ4 tape judges replay the rival's recorded orders literally. When our play changes, the replayed rival can collapse: it keeps sending orders for items it never received. Today's examples:

So the question: can a harness keep the rival faithful when our play changes, and what does the vrp26 vs vrp24 read look like under it, next to the gate-reactive (G25) judge? This is Q4 of astra brainstorm17 and item 2 of the BRAINSTORM7 Oct plan.

- **The collapse games are gone.** Under the harness, vrp26 leaves the rival within 3.2 % of live on all 6 collapse games, where G25 had it at -37 % to -82 %. Replaying vrp24 on 115787355 gives a rival of 110.7k against 110.5k live; G25 gave 62.5k.

  - G25 judge on the same runner and games: +4,685 (t 2.42), net flips +9.
- **G25 overstates the gain by about 3.4k per game and 4 net flips.** It disagrees with the harness on 8 W/L outcomes, and on 7 of those 8 G25 is the one that flatters vrp26. The cause is the same tape-collapse artefact.

No master, shipped or dist files were touched. No src worktree was needed: the harness lives in S/harness1 with its own engine build.

## 1. Design

The rival replays its recorded game step by step, and its **unit actions stay taped**. Only its **market list** is rewritten, at the points where the recorded order is infeasible, or depends on price or stock, in the counterfactual game.

A **live twin** is a second engine that replays both recorded seats. It reproduces the live game exactly: at every step its rival money is checked against the recorded money, and `twin_ok` holds on 174/174 tapes. The twin tells the harness what the live rival actually achieved at each step.

**Engine hooks.** The engine is S/reactclone1/fastenv plus 3 C API additions; the rules themselves are unchanged. Copy in `S/harness1/fastenv`.
- `kag_bought`: cumulative acquisitions per farm (products and animals, seeds per crop, hires, land).
- `kag_last_exec`: units executed per market-order slot in the last step.
- `kag_add_money`: the loan hook.

**Rules**, in `S/harness1/harness.py` `Harness.order` / `post`:

1. **Acquisition ledger (live-anchored timing).** For every recorded buy order (seed, wheat/fertiliser, animal, HIRE, BUY_LAND), the quantity becomes what that order slot executed live (from `last_exec`), plus any counterfactual deficit still pending.
   - The ledger updates `pending += live acquired − counterfactual acquired` after every step.
   - **Deficit:** the order is re-issued on the next steps until bought. This follows MMPQ rule 1.4 and the P48 land retries.
   - **Surplus:** a richer counterfactual rival does not buy early. The recorded attempts that failed live, which are the programme's "retry until affordable" orders, are trimmed.
   - A trimmed order keeps its list slot as a quantity-0 no-op. The engine resolves slot i of both seats in lockstep, so shifting later orders forward would change the price interleaving; the first cut of this rule broke identity on 8/149 tapes until the slot was kept.
   - Hire deficits expire at dawn. Other deficits expire after 48 steps.
2. **Stock-follow sales.** At a step where the tape sells product P (a non-sentinel lot) and the counterfactual rival holds more P at the market phase than live did, the lot becomes the live units sold plus that surplus. This follows P48 rules 5.7/5.8 and MMPQ 5.2: the lot is the stock in hand, not a price decision.
3. **Wool price gate** (P48 rule 5.4, d10-28, G25): the RSTUNE1R rule on the counterfactual price. PQ4/MMPQ tapes run without a gate (MMPQ 5.2: no price gate). In H mode a withheld sale keeps its slot as well.
4. **Purse-edge loan.** When the live rival acquired something this step and the counterfactual purse is short by at most 50 coins, the harness lends the shortfall.
   - It takes the loan back as soon as the counterfactual purse is above live, and any balance left at the end is deducted from the final rival money.
   - Why it is needed: the programmes spend to about 0 coins on d0-2. In 115271256, a 1-coin wheat price move from our d1 h20 buy cancelled the rival's d2 h0 hire. The missing hand then never collected or sold its fertiliser, and the rival stalled at 27.6k even with the retry rule.
   - The real programmes are cash-limited on the wheat count (MMPQ rulebook: "only the d0 wheat count depends on the opponent"), so their timetable goes on.
   - A second failure mode was surplus-driven. In 114962331 a 34-coin richer counterfactual rival bought a 500-coin sheep 1 step early and starved its wheat and feed. That is what rule 1's live-anchored timing fixes.

**Modes** (`HARNESS_MODE`):
- `H`: the harness.
- `G`: retry, stock-follow and loan all off. This is the RSTUNE1R/CONFIRM1 G25 judge; it matches CONFIRM1's rows byte for byte (46/46 for both vrp26 and vrp24 on P48 new46).
- `T`: taped.

The runner `hrun.py` uses the STACK1 lrun format: `TREE SW OUT EPS`, the same agent setup and the same game dirs.

## 3. What remains
- **Unit actions are taped.** If a purchase fails beyond the 50-coin loan and the 48-step retry window, a tile falls off the live trajectory. This shows as tile_div, and occurs on 1/149 games here. The fix would be a unit-level repair (re-plant or re-place when the late seed or animal arrives). Not built.
- **The timetable is live-anchored.** A richer counterfactual rival never buys earlier or more than live, and a poorer one re-buys and borrows up to 50 coins. The real programme's cash rule sits between the two. The loan cap and the expiry are modelling choices, not measured rules.
- **Milk's soft gate (P48 5.5) is not modelled.** Strawberry lot sizes follow the live lot plus surplus.
- **One recorded rival game per tape.** The harness removes collapse, not tape sampling. It also covers only programme rivals: P48 and PQ4. V56 remains the reacting bank agent, and OTH tapes are still literal.
- **Promotion.** Before H replaces G as the tape leg of the gate, 1-2 more candidates should be read in both modes. The JC1 "rival < 50 %" drop is then unnecessary in H.
