## 2. PFS yield machinery (plan.py, master 8d670dad)
- **Fertilizer:** `fert_cand = is_plant & t_fert < day & fert_val > fert_bar`.
  - `fert_val` = `VAL.fert_marginal_value`: the exact clipped marginal units at today's price, which is 0 for a crop that saturates anyway.
  - `fert_bar` = the fertilizer's own quote (OFF `FERT_VOLUME`).
  - `FERT_TIMING_ON=True` (2-day look-ahead) holds an application while a later day buys more.
  - The rest is SOLD (`PROGRAM_FERT_SELL=True`).
  - Off genes: `FERT_VOLUME_ON` (bar multiple), `FERT_FLOOR_ON` / `FERT_RESERVE_ON` (sale holds), `FERT_DUMP_ON`, `SAME_DAY_FERT_ON`, `CLIP_FERT_SKIP_ON`.
- **Water:** every planted tile, daily (`SURVIVAL_WATER` tier in SWITCH_GENES). Water per tile-day is 0.820 vs the programme's 0.824.
- **Harvest:** non-ongoing crops are harvested on their max-yield day (wheat age 4.00, carrot 3.00, melon 10 at 6/6 units). Ongoing crops never hit the 4-unit cap. No harvest-timing gene exists or is needed.
- **Replant:** `REPLANT_SAME_TURN_ON=False` (window `REPLANT_FROM_DAY` 0 .. `REPLANT_TO_DAY` 24) keeps the rotation whole, with a seed floor funded from the leftover purse.
- **No shipped gene is a fertilizer cadence floor**, so the switch below was added.

- **Mechanism of the fertilizer floor.** Every unit the floor applies is a unit PFS no longer sells.
  - PFS's value-ranked site (`fert_val > quote`) already applies every unit that beats its own sale.
  - The forced units buy +1..2 wheat / carrot, or nothing on a saturating tile, which is worth less than the sale they replace.
  - The fertilizer market keeps its price: fertilizer is the one product the town never drains. So the RIVAL's own fertilizer sales earn more (+1.2k to +2.5k per game).
  - The extra FERTILIZE ops also take crew hours from wool / milk in d10-17.
  - Result: +3 to -66 own coins and -91 to -208 margin per extra unit, with a rival gift at every dose.
- **Mechanism of the replant.** `REPLANT_SAME_TURN` (shipped code, OFF) replants a harvested tile with its own crop on the same turn, with a seed floor funded from the leftover purse.
  - Own sales rise +1.6k on m40, from wheat / carrot in d0-17, melon, egg and wool.
  - Against the bar rival that is +1.0k own coins at t 2.1-2.2.
  - On the 27 live27 seats the margin is flat: the rival earns as much more as we do (C1 dmargin -292, C2 -1).
  - This is the same ~+0.5k "empty owned tile-days" slack the census named (section 1g, M7). It is a fill lever, not a yield lever.
