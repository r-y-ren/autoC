## Verdict

- **NO SHIP.** All 8 cells lose our own coins: dours −5.1k..−11.7k per game (t −3.1..−7.0; faithful seats −2.6k..−10.1k).
- **No cell gains a flip.** Net flips range −1..−10, so no cell reaches GATE2 entry (flips ≥ +2 with dtheirs t < 2). GATE2 was not run, and the 15 live ZERO boards were not run. S/zeroloss1 holds ledgers only, not leg boards.
- **The rival does not lose.** The rival's coin drop (dtheirs −1.1k..−3.8k) is 74-125 % tape breakage. On faithful seats (S/judgeclean1/pair_faith.py) dtheirs is −1.0k..+1.6k, with every |t| ≤ 1.4. So extra cows deny the rival nothing measurable.
- **Premise check.** ZEROLOSS1's separator was "≥ 2 milk shops by d12 AND rival cows ≥ 9". The milk-shop half alone is common: it fires on 73/142 BAND seats (MIN 2, d12), where our base wins 39-34 live. It is not a loss marker by itself. Answering a milk draw with our own herd costs more than the extra milk earns.

- **Seats where it does not fire:** identical by construction, since the `where` never selects. This was not re-measured.

## 4. Mechanism

- **The extra cows do get bought.** Cows at d10 rise +0.6..+2.5 and our milk sold rises +19..+34 units per game (base-replay subset).
- **They do not pay.** Under MIN 2 our melon plate is unchanged. Under MIN 1 (which fires from the first d3 milk shop) melon d7 falls 0.3-0.5 tiles and melon sold d10-19 falls 1.2-1.6 units, a d2-7 purse displacement. Sheep at d10 fall 0.2-0.7.
- **Where the loss comes from.** Our loss is spread across most seats (45/51 negative in cell 1; median −4.8k). It is not a few breakage seats.
  - The added milk sells into the same milk book that the rival and the town drain, so the price falls.
  - The cows and their feed are bought at d2-12, when the purse competes with crops.
  - The un-gated cow lane buys cows that the spot gate had priced as losing.
- **The rival's milk earnings are not denied** on faithful seats.
- **Size of the loss.** The own cost (−2.6k..−10.1k per game on faithful seats) is larger than anything the 3 live ZERO losses needed from a herd response. Boesen needed −9.7k.
- **Why the rival's herd works and ours does not.** The rival's 9-12 cow herd earns because it is the only large herd in its games. Ours joins a shared, saturating milk book.

## 5. What is closed

- **Closed:** the shop-reactive additive cow ask, in both the {+2, +4} × MIN {1, 2} × DAY1 {12, 20} dose and window.
- **Unchanged:** the herd axis gift and cost history (HERDTILT, GEESE1, NONV4-a2, PLACEFEED).
- **Not tested:** a gate on the rival's herd size (the second half of the ZEROLOSS1 separator, i.e. rival cows ≥ 9 observed on the board). The own-cost figures above bound it: any cell has to recover ≥ 2.6k/game of self-inflicted loss before it can flip.
