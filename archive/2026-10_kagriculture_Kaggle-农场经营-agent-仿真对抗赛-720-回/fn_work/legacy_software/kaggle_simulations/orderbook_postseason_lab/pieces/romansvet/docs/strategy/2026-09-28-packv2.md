- **Step 0 is the first call of the file agent.** It includes the cold import of the package (main.py, kagg3, theta, route_vrp_c.so), PFS's real h0, and now the V56 source exec (PRELOAD).
  - Live vrp12 already pays 0.74 s on this seat. vrp14 adds up to about 0.8 s on this loaded box; V56PACK1 measured the preload alone at about 0.17 s unloaded.
  - One step-0 turn in 3 games went over 1 s and drew 0.55 s from the 60 s per-game overage bank. That is not a timeout: it forfeits only when the bank is exhausted.
- **Every later turn is under 0.52 s.**
- **Optional hardening, not done:** move the preload to step 1 on fired seats only. That would change the fired-seat step-1 cost (+0.17 s) and needs re-proof.
