## Root cause (the mechanism measured)
The wool price curve above I0 is `200 - 0.058 (inv - I0)^2`. It crosses 30 -> 1 within ~4.5 units of market inventory, and a sale at 1 coin adds no supply.

The control's 21 latched TUNE seats split into two regimes:

| regime | seats | hours d12-28 with price < 25 | P48 wool d12-29 | our wool d12-29 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| saturated | 12 | 71 % | 50 u / **1,728 coins** | 1,856 coins |
| unsaturated (yarn demand) | 9 | 11 % | 117 u / **18,989 coins** | 28,022 coins |

- **Saturated seats:** the price is already 1 from ~d17 (e.g. 115477195), so P48's gate is already shut and nothing is left to deny. The plan clears our wool at 1 coin. The rule holds it instead, so wool piles up in the shed: max shed wool 23 vs 10 in D12F16L4. That displaces crop drops and sales, and own falls 1.3-3.1k/game with ±7 coins of wool revenue on either side.
- **Unsaturated seats:** our ~6 u/day cannot push the price below 25 against the consumption. Early small lots only sell our own wool cheaper. D12F16L4: our wool revenue -1,297, P48's -611, so every coin denied costs us about 2.
- **F is inert for wool.** F14/16/18 give byte-equal results because the price jumps from >= 25 straight to <= 11. The MILK variant loses a further ~1.3k of our milk revenue (P48 milk -0.2..-0.5k).
