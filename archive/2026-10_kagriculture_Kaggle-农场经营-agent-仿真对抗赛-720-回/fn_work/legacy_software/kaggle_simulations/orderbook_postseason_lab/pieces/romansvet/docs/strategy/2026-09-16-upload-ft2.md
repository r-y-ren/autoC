## What it does
A FERTILIZE covers day..day+2, so its yield depends on where in the tile's cycle it lands. The 2953+
engine class lands 91 % of its applications on the tile's best day; we landed 29 %. The gate is one
line at the fertilizer site: apply today only if today's marginal value is at least the best of the
next two days' (`fert_cand &= fert_val >= max(fert_val over day+1..day+2)`).
