## 3. Why the turns do not become tiles
1. **The idle is task exhaustion, not a blocked start.** H23 credits +2 turns/unit to admission and 35/100 dev boards are byte-identical, the rest move ±0 idle: on d10-29 the admit stage is almost never labour-bound — the day's task list (plantings × water/harvest chain) runs out before the crew does. H1 frees 10 dawn turns and 3 dusk turns per game and ops/unit-day stays 11.35 → 11.38: the freed turns are re-spent as later PASS, exactly EVE_STOCK/PRESTOCK_V2/IDLE_TAIL_HOPS (−33 / −326 / −213).
2. **The dawn wait is owed PICKUPs of today's BUY row, not seed.** H1's seed half (`seedfree`) almost never fires: seeds are private stock (never picked up) and a PLANT opener with no walk is rare (H1WAIT: SEED 0.3 % of lead). What H1 does free (held-kind pickups at h1) moves the feed/fert run a turn earlier and gifts: Δtheirs +301 t 2.9 on dev.
3. **EVENING_SEED is a pure cost when nothing plants earlier.** Seed prices are fixed, so no book move; the −439 (t −6.3) is the overnight purse: tomorrow opens with seeds instead of coins (forecast = today's crops, not tomorrow's), which trims the next day's land/animal/crop purchases (plantings −0.05/day, 5 W→L).
4. What DSM has that we lack is **work, not turns**: 305 vs 183 plantings, 62 vs 38 planted tile-days d20-29, 11.4 empty tiles/day we never ask for. Every lever that creates that work is the ask (PLANT_ASK/ASK_FILL/PLANT_FILL_LATE/WHEATLATE1 — all rejected as gifts). A crew fill has nothing to fill until the ask changes; the turns are free once it does (≈ 200 dusk + 200 dawn unit-turns).
