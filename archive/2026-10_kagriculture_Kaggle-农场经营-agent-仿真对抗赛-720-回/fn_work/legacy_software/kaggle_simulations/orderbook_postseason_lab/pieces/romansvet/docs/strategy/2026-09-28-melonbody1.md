**Verdict.**
- **Most of V56's 31-20 against PFS's 14-37 on MELON seats is open-loop tape breakage, not denial.**
  - The rival's −20.5k in SELL coins splits into a price part of **+2.2k (t 1.0)** and a volume part of **−22.7k (t −4.3)**. The price part has the wrong sign for denial.
  - The volume part is the tape rival failing its own scripted actions. Its buys are the same under both bodies, but its hires fail at 0-5 coins, and when we sit in seat 0 its weed draws change with our empty tiles.
  - Its farm diverges before d10 in **40/51 seats**, 16 of them by d1.
  - Against its own **live** purse, the tape rival ends **−15.1k (t −4.1)** under V56 and 11/51 seats fall below 75 %. Under PFS it ends +4.2k, with 1/51 below 75 %.
- **The clean subset.** On the 11 seats where the rival's farm stays identical until ≥ d10, V56 goes 5-0 against PFS 0-11.
  - Δours is −0.7k and Δtheirs is **−10.9k (t −4.4)**. That is real price denial: MELON −5.5k (the d0 plate booking d10 ahead, unreachable from a d2 latch, CLOSED) and WOOL −6.8k (earlier sheep and a longer wool book, the herd lever MELONHERD1 is measuring).
- **No new lever is worth a paired MELON-51 grid after MELONHERD1.** Every other body difference sits in a closed family or has the wrong sign on our purse or the rival's.
- **If MELONHERD1 fails, the V56 body holds nothing that can be reached from a d2 latch.**

- **Sheep/wool is the only latch-reachable lever with denial of the right sign.** Its size:

  |---|---|
  | own, d0-9 wool net of sheep | about +1.8k |

  - That is exactly MELONHERD1's grid, `{+2s, +2c+2s, +4c+4s} × {d5, d9}` under the d2 latch, so it is not duplicated here.

  - MELON −4.2k is the d0 plate and is CLOSED.
  - The −22.7k volume part is tape breakage.
- **Consequence for judging.** BAND142 MELON Δtheirs for any arm that changes our d0-1 or our empty-tile count in seat 0 carries this artefact.
  - V56's tape rival ends 15.1k below its live purse. PFS's ends 4.2k above.
