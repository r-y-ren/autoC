# LATELOSS1 (2026-09-29): is the d20-29 edge loss variant-specific and product-specific?

**Verdict: NONE.** The d20-29 edge loss is spread over variants and books. It is not concentrated on the new Q4-tomato rivals. It is our own late tomato and milk volume and price, not the rival's tomato denial. Selling our tomatoes before the rival's first tomato fill keeps at most +134/game on the losses and +255/game on the Q4TOMATO losses. The bar is +2k.

## Data and method
- **Games.** All 117 vrp20 (56652418) live games (73-44).
  - 90 use the CREWLOSS1/2 census ledgers (S/crewloss2/res/ledger_rerun.json + ledger_new.json).
  - The other 27 (wins against rivals < 2340 that CREWLOSS1/2 left unparsed) were measured here with the UNCHANGED S/crewloss1/ledger.py code: S/lateloss1/ledger_extra.py -> res/ledger_extra.json, 27/27 valid.
  - Also the 70 unfired vrp19w seats (res/ledger_vrp19w.json).
- **Windows.**
  - Recent = the CREWLOSS2 last 40 (24 of the 44 losses).
  - Earlier = the other 77 (20 losses).
  - The losses reproduce CREWLOSS2 exactly: d20-29 net edge +8,537 -> +3,209, t -2.5; margin -3,910 -> -10,544, t -3.1.
- **Rival variant, from its own ledger.**
  - Q4TOMATO: 4th quadrant by d15, OR >= 10 tomato plantings d10-17 AND >= 75 tomato units harvested d18-29.
  - Otherwise PROGRAMME: CREWLOSS1 fam MELON, i.e. a d2 dawn melon plate.
  - V: fam V.
  - OTHER: ZERO/OTHER.
- **Per-game tags** are in S/lateloss1/res/per_game.tsv, both seats (_O = ours, _R = rival). Each game has:
  - Q4/Q3 day.
  - Tomato plantings d10-17 and d18-29; tomato harvested, sold and revenue d18-29; first tomato sale and harvest day.
  - Revenue and units sold for all 9 books in d0-9, d10-19 and d20-29.
  - Net revenue, product spend, purse at d20, final purse.
- **Edges.**
  - Edge = O minus R, in coins.
  - Books = census `revenue_<X>` summed over the window.
  - WHEAT and FERTILIZER are shown net of product purchases: wheat buy-and-resell makes the gross wheat book +-40k noise. With that netting the books sum exactly to net revenue.
  - t = Welch t, recent vs earlier, within the cell.
- Scripts: S/lateloss1/feat.py, tab.py, cf.py, shopdraw.py. Full tables: res/tables_vrp20.md, res/tables_vrp19w.md, res/cf.md, res/shopdraw.md.

## 5. Counterfactual: sell our d18-29 tomatoes before the rival's first tomato fill (two-purse, day-level)
- **Definitions.**
  - fill = the rival's first tomato sale day >= 18.
  - p_pre = our realised tomato price from d10 to fill-1.
  - Our keep = our units sold after fill x max(0, p_pre - p_after). This is an upper bound: it ignores the price drop our own dump would cause.
  - The rival's loss is bounded by the same gap.

| group | games with a rival fill | our tomato units d18-29 | ours after fill | rival units d18-29 | fill day (median) | p_pre | p_after | keep ours / game | rival loss bound / game |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| all games | 50 | 48 | 42 | 83 | 21 | 98 | 103 | +404 | 338 |
| losses | 26 | 42 | 40 | 91 | 21 | 83 | 90 | **+134** | 134 |
| Q4TOMATO losses | 12 | 32 | 31 | 123 | 20 | 74 | 70 | **+255** | 255 |
| recent losses | 16 | 30 | 26 | 89 | 21 | 76 | 80 | +79 | 79 |

- **Tomato recovers, so there is no first-seller rent.**
  - Our after-fill price is not below our pre-fill price.
  - The rival's 90-120 units set the level, and we have only 30-40 units to move.
- **Affected share.** Q4TOMATO rivals are 12 of 44 losses (27 %), which clears the 20 % gate. But the read is +255/game against the +2k bar, so the gate fails.
- **Strawberry and milk.** Milk is our volume and price, both seats hit (-2.0k, t -1.9). Strawberry is -241 in losses (t -0.2).
