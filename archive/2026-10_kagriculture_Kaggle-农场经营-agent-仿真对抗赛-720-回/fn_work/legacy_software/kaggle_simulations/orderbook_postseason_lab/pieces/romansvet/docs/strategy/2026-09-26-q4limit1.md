## 4. Verdict: which component loses the most Q4 coins
- **Router: nothing.** It keeps 100 % of the planned Q4 PLANT and WATER, leaves 0 optional stops on Q4, and executes 99.5 %. It uses 0.5-1.0 moves per extra Q4 op.
- **Sales: nothing.** Overflow is +0.5..+5.9 u/g (≤ 200 coins). Unsold stock at d29 is ≤ 0.2 u. Wheat sells above the dawn quote (35.9 vs 34.8).
- **Head: nothing for Q4** (F−E −74, t −0.4; D−C +65, t 0.25). The head is still worth +286/g (t 3.3, −3 flips) without Q4. **Keep head_940.**
- **Theta land gene: it is the gate, and it is right.** head[1] saturates land_bias at −4,000. Bypassing it (E) costs −2,419/g (t −5.1) and −10 flips.
- **Planner, the biggest Q4 loss.** Once Q4 is owned, the fixed-fraction ask (brain.py:1141-1142, aux[2] < 0) plus placement across all free
  tiles (plan.py:8250 `_place_split`) moves plantings from Q1-3 to Q4. About 127 of the 215 Q4 units are displaced Q1-3 output (≈ −4.2k gross at
  ~33/u), plus −56 egg/milk/fert units sold. Next come labour (+2,851, 18 hand-days at fib 142 = the 11th-12th hand) and land (3,120/g).
  E's ledger: +4,343 revenue − 3,120 land − 2,851 hires − ~790 seeds/other = −2,419.
- **Concrete fix (planner): make Q4 ADDITIVE.** With Q4 owned, size the Q1-3 ask on Q1-3 free tiles exactly as without Q4. Give Q4 its own
  wheat relay placed only on Q4 tiles: reuse `Q4_RELAY_ON`'s split at plan.py:10414 (Q4 tiles out of the planner's asks) with the `Q4_OWN` d11 buy.
  Price the relay hands through the planner's own hire scan, with no FQ crew floor (the floor is what cost fib 194 vs 142 in C).
  Upper bound from E's ledger: recovering the 127 displaced units is worth +4.2k gross, minus their labour of ~12 hand-days at fib 144-233 (~2k),
  which gives Δours ≈ −0.5k..+1.3k/g (the top of the range also recovers the −56 animal-product units). That is **break-even at best, with no flips expected (Δtheirs −0.3..−0.5k, t < 1)**.
  Q4 costs 4,000 on d11 and needs ~120 extra wheat units just to repay itself. Our Q1-3 farm is already full (1-2 free tiles at dawn d12-20), so Q4
  only pays with volume the rival loses (DSM's denial). Neither E nor C shows that denial (Δtheirs −113..−511, |t| < 1).
- **Status: Q4 CLOSED at the current planner (6th Q4 study).** No arm reaches the dev bar. Nothing ships. Q4_OWN_ON stays OFF.
  RELAY12 (level crew) is the only open Q4 cell. If it runs, it must use the additive split above, or it repeats E's displacement.
