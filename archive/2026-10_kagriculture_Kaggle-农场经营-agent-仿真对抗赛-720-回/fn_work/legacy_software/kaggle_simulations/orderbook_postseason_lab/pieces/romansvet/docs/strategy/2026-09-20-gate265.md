## 3. Mechanism — win-only is blind to the gift it creates

The win reward is measured against byte-exact tape opponents, whose purse is fixed because their controls
cannot react to our changed use of the shared pot. The other 80% of draws are self-play against theta-only
clones. In `ppo.py:609-620`, that self-play draw replaces the hard-board tape controls with `[-1, 0]`, so
the hard-board weighting applies only to the 20% tape share. Sim `mwin 0.92` is therefore principally the
self-play share, not evidence of performance on the re-planning BAND2 field.

That objective admits a direct exploit: increase shared-pot throughput enough to look winning against a
fixed tape or theta-only clone, without credit for how much more a reacting opponent can collect. The gate
measures the missing counterfactual. At u900 essentially none of the extra purse is ours (`dOURS −4` versus
the incumbent), while **+2,729** goes to theirs; at u1380 our +1,203 is dominated by **+4,538** theirs. The
apparent simulated win improvement is pot inflation, and re-planning opponents turn the gift into 28–39
net lost boards.

`dmargin t < 3`, and net flips < 0. `head_940` remains the banked and shipped head. Do not continue

The Astra review ranked (1) win-only continuation of `head_940`, (2) paired win credit with a gift
