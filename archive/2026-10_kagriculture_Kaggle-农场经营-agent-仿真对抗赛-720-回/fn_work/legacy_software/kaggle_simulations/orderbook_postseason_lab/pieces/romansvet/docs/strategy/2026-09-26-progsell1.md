## Verdict: STILL LOSES (0-100 vs reacting V56 under VRP; the gift is +20.4k, the same as PROGFIX2's +20.2k)
The sale side of the port is faithful in volume: it sells what it harvests. The gap is **production**. The port has too little carrot in d20-29 (seed capped by free cells: no relay dig-out of spent crops), too little strawberry in d0-9 (the d2-9 purse), and too little animal yield (care bonus, 3.75 vs 4.45 wool per op). Every missing unit on the steep wool, milk and strawberry curves becomes the opponent's price. Closing the gap needs DSMLOGIC1 port item 1: a per-unit dispatcher with MANDATORY/FILLER jobs (daily FEED+CARE filler, relay dig-and-replant), which turns the port's 167-490 idle unit-turns into harvest volume. Neither the sale rule nor any existing knob does this. ENGINE port sale-side: CLOSED. PROGRAM_ENGINE_ON stays False.
