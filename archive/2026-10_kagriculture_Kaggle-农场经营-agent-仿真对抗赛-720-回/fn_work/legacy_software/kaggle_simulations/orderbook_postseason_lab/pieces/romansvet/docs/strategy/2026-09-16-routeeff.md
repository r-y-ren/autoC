**Why it missed, measured, not guessed: the development-compaction lever is
already 92.5 % spent.** `ROUTE_EFF_COMPACT = 4` (half the scale) is **byte-for-

## 2. Instrument — every unit step, both seats, real engine

`S/routeeff/probe.py`. Market orders (SELL / BUY_* / HIRE / BUY_LAND) are a
**separate action channel** (`kaggriculture.py:555-612`) and cost **no unit
step**, so every unit step is exactly one of MOVE / PASS / PROD and, per
unit-day,

    prod = present − move − pass        (exactly, no residual)

The probe hooks `_apply_unit_action` and records `(turn, class, op, x, y)` for
every (seat, day, unit idx) — hands are cleared nightly (`:881`), so `idx` is a
stable identity inside a day. Derived per unit-day: the pass and move split
into **dawn** (before the first prod op), **mid** (between first and last) and
**tail** (after the last); unreached (0 prod ops); distinct tiles touched; and
the mean pairwise Manhattan distance between them (`clust`). 22 + 30 boards,
both seats, ~6 min at `WORKERS=3`.

## 5. The switch — `ROUTE_EFF_ON`, built and KILLED

## 6. Standing

* **`mid_move` is the largest term of the d20-29 volume gap and it is REAL**
  (+0.749 ops/unit-day, t 6.05 on ENG22; +0.650, t 5.90 on the top 10), worth
  **+4,290 / +3,784** a board at spot.
* **It is planner-reachable but NOT by a module constant.** The one constant
  lever (development compaction) is 92.5 % spent and its remainder is a −111
  t −0.21 REJECT. The next arm on this term would have to reorder a block's
  own visiting sequence — nearest-neighbour instead of serpentine — through
  the `harvest_first` plumbing in `_routes`, priced against the same
  `bud − lead` wall. That is a mechanism, not a switch, and the lead decides.
* `dawn_pass` (ENG22 +3,356, TOPLEG +5,257) remains the second-largest term
  and is the turn-1 BUY-row law; both of its arms are already measured and
  dead (IDLEOPS §DAWN).
* Nothing here is promoted. `ROUTE_EFF_ON` ships **OFF** and is pinned
  byte-identical to `f32e919` by `tests/test_routeeff.py`.
