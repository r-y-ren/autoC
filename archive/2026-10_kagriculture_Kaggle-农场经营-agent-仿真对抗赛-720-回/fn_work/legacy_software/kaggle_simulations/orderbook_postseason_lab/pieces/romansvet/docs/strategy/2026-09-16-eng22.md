## 4. Per-product / per-phase shape (the E-C test)

Tool chain (all CPU, sim, CRN action-replay seat, both seats):
`S/eng22/mkboards.py` copies (episode, seed, seat) out of the ENGINE leg csvs →
`S/eng22/ledger.py` (byte-copy of `S/topledger3/ledger.py`, HERE retargeted) records
per-product sale **and purchase** receipts at 31 day snapshots → `S/eng22/shape.py` prints
the tables. Band-clone baselines TOPB2 (40 games) and LIVE-C (28) are the existing
`S/melon_decomp/raw_B.npz`, same theta, same four switches (`S/melon_decomp/B.log:1`).

Every number is **ours − theirs and net of market purchases**:
`net(p) = (our sold[p] − our bought[p]) − (their sold[p] − their bought[p])`, the definition
of `docs/strategy/2026-09-14-top5-ledger.md` §2-§3, so buy/re-sell churn cannot inflate a
line. Boards whose tape desyncs in the engine leg (retention < 0.85: 107466019 in TOPB2,
108042307 / 108042425 in NEXTHIGH) are dropped from every set.

## 5. Verdict on E-C: the shapes **DIFFER**, and not the way the archive predicted

**The 2953+ engine class is a second opponent class.** The two tests agree on the sign of
9 of 10 lines (only MILK disagrees: controlled +3,011, cross-set −1,786), and the two most
robust lines point in opposite directions:

1. **FERTILIZER +6,294 (t +5.51) / +4,028 (t +4.86) in OUR favour.** Against the clone we
   lose −4.7k…−6.9k on fertilizer, because the clone *sells* 337 units into the market
   (NEXTHIGH profile, §4.0) and we compete at its price. The engine *applies* 171 units and sells only 219, so the
   line nearly vanishes (−627 / −665). This is the single most reliable class discriminator
   in the data and it says the engine's fertilizer is **not** a coin line we lose to — it is
   yield it converts elsewhere.
2. **TOMATO −5,039 (t −2.59), a sign flip** (+2,026 → −3,013; cross-set +898 → −1,710).
   The engine plants **10.8 tomato tiles by d29 against the clone's 2.3**; tomato is sold
   only in d15-29 and at ~100-116 coins/unit it is the priciest crop line after melon. This
   is where their applied fertilizer reappears.
3. **STRAWBERRY −6,279 (t −2.58) — halved, not flipped** (+9,949 → +3,671). It stays our
   largest surplus against both classes. **This contradicts `2026-09-14-top5-ledger.md` §3**,
   which found strawberry flipping to −3,131 against the top five; that was **ymg_aq**, which
   `2026-09-14-top5-profile.md:95` classifies as *neither* cluster (a strawberry-first third
   build). On the true cluster-1 class strawberry does not flip.
4. **WOOL is NOT the differing line.** The ymg_aq read had wool −5,659, "1.2-3x larger, the
   same sign against both classes". Against the engine class wool is the *smallest* deficit
   we have (−882 / −155, vs −3,761 / −1,767 / −4,725 against the clone).
5. **CARROT −2,179 / −3,057 (t −2.73 cross-set)** — the clone edge disappears (+1.6k/+2.3k → −0.6k/−0.8k).
6. **WHEAT −3,580 / −2,299 (t −1.10 / −1.29)** — same direction as the ymg_aq −6,619 but
   **not significant on this set**; report it as a trend, not a finding.

**So the 2750-2950 gap does not collapse into the existing legs, and ENG22 is the missing
rung** — with one correction to how the census framed it: the 2780-2950 band itself is
30/30 clone (NEXTHIGH already covers it and B is +2,045 there). The uncovered population is
**2953+ and half of it is this class**, where B is −3,228 to −6,932. The rung that was
missing is not a rating band, it is a **build class**.

**What the leg says to aim at (not yet tested, no lever is claimed here):** the coins are in
**d15-29 tomato and carrot production** against an opponent that fertilises rather than
dumps, not in the d10-14 melon pot (lighter against this class) and not in fertilizer
arbitrage (already retracted, `2026-09-12-fertilizer-premise-correction.md`). Note the
engine's own fertilizer line is nearly neutral for us, so a "beat their fertilizer" lever is
aimed at a line worth ~600 coins; the tomato line is worth ~5,000.

## 8. Caveats

* **Board confounding.** ENG22 vs NEXTHIGH is not a controlled comparison (different boards,
  different ratings); the TOPB2 cluster split is controlled but is n = 10 vs 9. Where the two
  disagree — strawberry — trust neither without more boards. Both are reported.
* **The ledger is the CRN sim**, not the engine. Justified here by §4.1 (mean board
  difference 2 coins on ENG22), not assumed.
* **ENG22 is informational and must stay so**: 10 of its boards are TOPB2 boards and 1 is a
  NEXT30 board, so pooling it into §115/§115b would double-count. `pooled_band.py` /
  `pooled_band180.py` were not touched.
* **One board below 2,953** (kwa, 2,577) is kept because membership is the §118 classifier,
  not a rating cut; dropping it moves the leg from −3,228 to −3,508.
* `S/eng22/retention.py` uses `money_d29` from `S/nexthigh/anatomy_opp.csv` as the tape's
  source coins; it reproduces the published NEXTHIGH control (mean 0.99, median 1.01, 2/30
  below 0.85) exactly, which is the only validation offered for that definition.
* No `src/` change, no commit, no training. CPU only, WORKERS=4 throughout.
