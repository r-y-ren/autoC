## 1. Sale mechanics on the 27 live27 replays (both sides real, same board, same market)
Script `S/saleside1/mech.py` -> `res/mech_rows.json` (16,055 SELL orders), `tab.py` -> `res/sale_mech.md` (24 TOP5-cluster seats, main) and
`res/sale_mech_all27.md` (all 27). Ours = the vrp15 body unfired = PFS; riv = the live programme rival on the same board.

**The engine has no order book.** A SELL is `["SELL", product, qty]`; it fills unit by unit from the shed at `market_price(inventory)`, and each
unit sold raises the market inventory by 1 (fastenv `sim.hpp` `process_market`/`commit_unit`; both seats interleave per unit, and the town drains
the inventory every 4 steps). So "ask vs the book" is measured as (a) **quote/base** = the posted price at the order / the price at I0 (how drained
the curve is when we sell = timing) and (b) **real/quote** = realised average / posted quote (lot slippage = size). Fill is immediate; the only
unfilled units are orders above the shed stock ("sell everything" rows). Both sides end the game with ~0 units unsold (ours 0.0, rival <= 1.4 wheat).

## Why it fails (mechanism)
- **One shared price curve.** Each unit sold raises the inventory by one for both seats, and the town drains it every 4 steps.
  - Our 8-12-unit dawn and dusk lots walk the curve down. That costs us slippage, but it also lowers the price the rival sells into for the next hours.
  - When we hold units back (lot cap or ask floor), the rival's next sales land on a higher curve. Rival sale coins d18-29: +0.9k, +1.2k and +1.9k in the three arms.
  - The held units come back later on a curve the rival has walked down, or they sit in the shed and displace our own harvest: wheat -13 to -21 and carrot -5 to -9 units sold d18-29.
- **The top 5 can afford small lots for a different reason.** Their volume arrives 8 days earlier (the d10 melon, the d11-14 wool and milk wave). Against us they are the ones walking a curve that we keep drained.
  - On the same board, their per-unit price is not better than ours: -0.7k per game over all our units.
- **The switch family is closed.** Lot cap, ask floor, and both, across windows, all lose closed loop: sale mechanics = CLOSED. PFS's big lots stay; they are denial.
  The income gap is output volume and timing (capacity), which only the clone body carries.
