## 2. Hypotheses
- **(a) co-adaptation basin — SUPPORTED, but the basin is the planner's three coupled caps, not theta's weights.** Every coordinate is a local optimum:
  KNOBVRP1 20/20 ≤ 0, KNOBV56 83/83, ESNEW1 216 cells (17 > 0, none clean), ESLOSS1 94 % losing perturbations, and `hire_bias` ±100 is INERT on 20/20
  boards d0-4 and moves only 8-16 boards for −35/−335 on d5-9 (ESNEW1 rows 8, 26). The caps: (i) the ask `n_dev = dev_frac × n_free`, `plant_total = n_dev − animal_want` (brain.py:1141-1142, 1179;
  collapses to 0-4 tiles after d12, brain.py:823; VOLUMEHI asks 9/1/4/0 against 26-38 developable); (ii) the hire scan = argmax over h of admitted task
  value − fib bill (plan.py:11936-12146), so hands follow tasks and a bias cannot add them; (iii) `forward_value` at today's quote with the rival's
  sales given (brain.py:460-485). DSM's regime on the same boards = margin +15.3k held (own −4.4k, denial +10.9k; COSTAUDIT: spend +11.9k, revenue
  +10.2k). Its payoff is nearly all DENIAL, which needs the whole added volume on the book; every partial step pays the own-purse cost first. Convex in
  the dose along the right direction, negative in every single coordinate: a basin.
- **(b) displacement — SUPPORTED; it is the gift mechanism.** Labour and purse are fixed by (i)-(ii), so an added input takes from another product,
  the book for that product empties, and the rival (V56 sells all 9 products) gets its price: TOMATOFILL1 +2.3 tomato = −1.6 wheat −0.4 straw −0.7
  melon, Δtheirs +0.8-2.3k t 4-7, hires flat 234.1 (the fill path cannot hire); HERDVRP1 d13 herd total 17.4 → 17.4 (mix only), hires −2..−18 hand-days,
  Δtheirs +0.9-12.9k; COSTAUDIT sheep cap −wool → Δtheirs +4.8k; Q4PROG1 Q4 +263 u, Q1-3 −236 u; CREWRELAY1 displaces melon/straw/herd timing.
  The one arm that added work OUTSIDE the asks with its own hands (Q4RELAY1 FQ) is gift-free: Δtheirs −113. Additive volume does not gift; displacement does.
- **(c) the planner never creates the work — SUPPORTED, root of (a)/(b).** Q4VRP1: +3,765 hires bought, +82 units, "the idle tail does not tend Q4";
  unshadowed planner buys Q4 on 0/20 (veto = `wants_land − wants_pre = 0`, plan.py:10787-10803); RELAYFILL1 asks 72 of 150 free slots d20-27 with
  purse/seed never binding; PLANAUDIT1 router→planner feedback = 0 by design. Hands, land and cash are all downstream of the ask.
- **(d) capital timing — SYMPTOM, not cause.** Dawn purse d10 5.4k vs 0.4k, but DSM is ahead from d12 (6.7k vs 4.3k) and d15 22.4k vs 8.5k; its d0-9
  extra 4.8k (heads +7 ≈ 3.5k, +29 hand-days = 391 coins, Q3 d9) returns +21k revenue d10-19. Yet each d0-9 lever alone is a CLOSED gift (melon plate,
  herd, wool-first: paid out of the opening purse = displacement). After d10 cash never binds (purse 3.8-8.5k d11-15 covers Q4 4,000 + a 12-crew at 376/day).
  Idle cash is what an ask of zero looks like.
- **(e) yield per planting — KILLED.** Wheat u/planting OURS 5.16, DSM 4.22, FQ 3.6; per op OURS 0.72 u vs FQ 0.65; Q4RELAY1 relay 4.55 u/planting at
  "min" water; coins per crop tile-day 65.4 vs 63.2. Volume = planting COUNT × tile-days (Q4 +460 tile-days, wheat 143 vs 74 plantings), never yield.
- **(f) NEW: labour PRICE and LOAD.** Fib convexity makes a level crew of ≤ 12 cheap (DSM 3-4 coins/op) and a jagged crew plus top-up hands dear
  (Q4RELAY1 FQ 21-26 coins/op). A hand pays for wheat at 27 only if it is ≤ the 12th hand AND loaded ≥ 13 ops (2.6 plantings, ~9 units, ~250 coins a day);
  the 13th (233) is marginal, the 14th (377) never. DSM/FQ run exactly 11.8-11.9 on d10-19 and 10.8-11.6 on d20-29: the Fibonacci break-even crew.
  Our 27-34 PASS/day is the 1.2 hand-days DSM converts into the relay's water.
