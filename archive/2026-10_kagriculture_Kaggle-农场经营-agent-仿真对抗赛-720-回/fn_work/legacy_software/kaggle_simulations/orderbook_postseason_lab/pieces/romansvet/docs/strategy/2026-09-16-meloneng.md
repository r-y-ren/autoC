## Question

TOPLOSS and LOSSMAP2 §3/§7 both name the same largest line of every loss:
SELL MELON in d10-19 (−11,500 on ENG22, −14,612 on the losing top-10 boards).
The top 10 plant melon d0-9 (11.8 tiles vs our 2.8) and sell d10-19 at ~222/u;
we plant d10-19 and sell into the d27 floor at ~152/u. The planner already owns
the recorded top-tier opening as a switch (`plan.MELON_OPEN_ON`,
`MELON_OPEN_TILES=12`, `MELON_OPEN_DAY=0`, `MELON_OPEN_LOT=24`). It was measured
at **−17,554 on the BAND** (clone class) in 2026-09-11/14 and its tile sweep
(M12/M8/M4) only ever ran in SIM. It had **never** been read in the engine
against the *engine* class on the shipped FT2 package. This box does that.

## Verdict — hard REJECT on every arm, plate CLOSED in the engine too

* **C1 is null** (+78, t 0.33): `BANK_BEFORE_LOT_ON` is worth nothing on ENG22,
  so none of the melon loss is the control's.
* **The damage is monotone in tiles and it is not our purse — it is theirs.**
  At 12 tiles our own coins move −676 while the opponent gains **+15,547**. The
  plate is a *price gift*: the day-0 melon supply we add collapses the melon
  quote for the whole board, and the engine class — which plants melon d0-9
  anyway — is the seat positioned to sell into the curve we flatten. This is the
  2026-09-14 decomposition's "(a) displacement −26,647, 113 % of the loss,
  −21,156 handed to the clone as price" mechanism reproduced *against the engine
  class*, at the same sign and within the same order of magnitude.
* Shrinking the plate does not find a profitable corner: 3 tiles still loses
  −5,563 at t −5.8, and there the loss has *shifted into our own purse*
  (−3,168 ours vs +2,394 theirs) — too few tiles to win the race, still enough
  to pay the land.
* So the d10-19 melon line that TOPLOSS/LOSSMAP2 measure is **first-mover rent
  we cannot buy with this switch**. Copying the top-10 opening reproduces their
  supply without their board position, and every unit we add pays them.

**MELON_OPEN family CLOSED in the engine against the engine class.** No TOPLEG
leg was run (the gate requires a passing arm; none passed). No src change; the
switch stays OFF and unmodified.
