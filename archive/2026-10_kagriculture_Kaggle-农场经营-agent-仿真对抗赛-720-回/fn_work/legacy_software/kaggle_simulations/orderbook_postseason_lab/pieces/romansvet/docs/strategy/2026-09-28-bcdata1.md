## 2. Extraction (remote CPU; counts)
- **Pipeline:** `fetch.sh` (remote, `curl --compressed`, 8 parallel) -> `rp/<ep>.json.gz` -> `exloop.sh`/`exrem.py` (3 CPU workers) -> `data_prog/<ep>_s<seat>.npz`, and the replay is deleted after extraction.
  - The npz codec is byte-for-byte BCBODY's `extract.py`: same `feats.py` and same keys, `meta = [ep, seat, seed, final_own, final_opp]`.
  - `curl --compressed` moves about 0.3 MB per replay over the wire. The alternatives were much slower:
    - The remote link manages about 650 KB/s.
    - Python urllib with gzip moves 1.9 MB per replay.
    - The local fetchers ran 0.15-0.26 eps/s on the saturated box.
- **Output:** **3,379 npz, 492 MB**, in `~/stage_bcdata1/data_prog/`. 3,377 remain after dedupe; `dedupe_drop.txt` lists the 2 already held by BCBODY4. No replays are kept (`rp/` is empty).
  - The largest teams are kigasudayooo 235, KawattaTaido 222, Densike 207, Arda Ceylan 193, Artem 186, 吃白饭的大肥鱼 176, Luca 170, TheEggman 168, unreal 167, Just A game 161, yuto083 158 and 有辣条有权 148. The rest come from 72 other teams.
- **Labels:** `labels.tsv` (local and remote) has one row per seat: key, team, rating, seat_score, win (margin > 0), margin, opp_team, opp_score, opp_prog, teacher_opp, and the d0 fingerprint.

## 3. Training recipe (not run by this stream)
- **Trainer:** `S/bcdata1/train3w.py` is a copy of `S/bcbody1/train3h.py` with two changes:
  - `BC_WEIGHTS` multiplies the unit and market sampling probability of every step by its episode weight. Episodes with w = 0 are not loaded.
  - PR/PV/mkt are kept int8/int16 in host RAM and cast per batch, which saves about 4 GB at 6.5k eps.
  - A CPU smoke on the remote passed: 24 eps, weights parsed, w0 dropped, warm start from params_r5a2 at hold tok 0.83, 4 steps.
- **Data:** `data_mix/` = symlinks to `~/stage_bcbody1/data/*.npz` (3,152 top-10, read-only) + `data_prog/*.npz` (3,377), with BCBODY4's `holdout.txt` (36 teacher eps).
  - Held-out accuracy therefore stays comparable with r4c/r5a2/r5a3.
- **Settings:** the same as BCBODY4's last train3h run (r5a3): 30k steps, cosine 1e-3 (built in), W210=3, eval every 2,500, `XLA_PYTHON_CLIENT_MEM_FRACTION=.60`.
- **Warm start:** `~/stage_bcbody1/out5a3/params_best.npz` (r5a3, newest), with fallback `params_r5a2.npz` (md5 59339f23).
- **Command:** `bash ~/stage_bcdata1/launch_recipe.sh l015` (then `l0`).
  - Output: `out_l015/params_best.npz` -> `S/bcdata1/params_bcdata1_l015.npz`.
  - Expect about 7 min of load (about 4.7M steps and 47M unit rows, roughly 25 GB host RAM) and about 14 min of training.
