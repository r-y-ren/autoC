- Once fired, `engine_gate_apply(True)` writes `ENGINE_GATE_SET = "MELON_PLATE_TILES=20;MELON_PLATE_DAY=1;NONV_PLATE_LAST=6"`. This is the MELONGENES plate rewrite: the day mix on d1..d6 moves onto MELON, 20 tiles in all (ours at d7 dawn: 23-31 melon tiles against 0-2 OFF), and the plate sells 138-186 melon on d10-19. On d0 the latch is idle, so d0 is byte-identical between ON and OFF.
