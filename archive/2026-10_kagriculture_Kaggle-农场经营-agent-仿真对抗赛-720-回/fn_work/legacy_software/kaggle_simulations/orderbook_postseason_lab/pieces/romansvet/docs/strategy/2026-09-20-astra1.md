Purpose: RL-head plan review after GATE263.

**Run a controlled, win-only continuation of `head_940` first.** None of the measured results supports promising +300 rating. That requires a substantial, replicated increase in wins against the target field; hundreds of extra margin coins or one net flip do not establish it.

The remaining credible experiments concern **objective, credit assignment, and opponent distribution**. I would not reopen production or planner families.

All implementation changes below are proposals; nothing was modified.

**Common gate for every experiment**

Run `N=233 LEG=band2 WORKERS=6 LABEL=<candidate> bash S/actionrl/gate.sh <checkpoint>`, then compare with `report_band2.py <candidate> a8_257_940b2`. Require all three: **Δtheirs ≤ 0, paired Δmargin t ≥ 3, net board-win flips > 0**.

That is a screening gate, not sufficient evidence for +300. Select checkpoints on a separate development panel, then confirm the finalist on fresh, representative 2,400–3,000 boards, counting paired win discordances and their uncertainty. Include wins and losses; average mirrored seats before counting independent boards. BAND2 has already been consulted repeatedly.

1. **Win-only PPO, starting from the actual incumbent**

   **Mechanism:** learn which existing coordinated deviations flip outcomes. This is an objective experiment, not another quantity knob. [ACTIONRL12, “Launch”](/mnt/e/_work/kaggriculture3/docs/strategy/2026-09-20-actionrl12.md) changes reward, layout, initialization, and sampling together. Its wide head starts from scratch; [GATE263 §3](/mnt/e/_work/kaggriculture3/docs/strategy/2026-09-20-gate263.md) already says the wider space mostly buys unprofitable commitments. A clean incumbent continuation remains untested in those records.

   **Implementation:** existing `S/actionrl/ppo.py`, `KAGG3_HEAD_LAYOUT=v1`, `--resume .../head_940.npz --margin-weight 0 --hard-boards 0 --lr 1e-4 --ent 0.001 --batch 512 --updates 2000`; retain the existing six-member supplied pool and `--selfplay-frac 0.8`. Two seeds. Add approximately 80 lines across `head.py`/`ppo.py` for a greedy evaluation rollout and fixed development-panel reporting every 100 updates.

   **Judgment:** common gate; sampled `mwin` cannot select the winner.

   **Budget/kill:** **8–12 GPU-hours**, estimated from ACTIONRL12 throughput including evaluation. Stop at 500 updates if neither seed improves greedy development wins; stop the experiment if both final candidates fail the common gate.

2. **Paired win credit with an explicit gift constraint**

   **Mechanism:** remove board luck without paying for surplus margin. Train on `win_candidate − win_940` under identical weather, shops, and opponent. The reference is independent of sampled candidate actions, so subtraction preserves the expected win objective. [GATE261 §3](/mnt/e/_work/kaggriculture3/docs/strategy/2026-09-20-gate261.md) establishes that gift pricing changes behavior, but its coin-dominated reward sacrifices our purse. This tests different credit assignment and constraint handling.

   **Implementation:** approximately 150–220 lines in `ppo.py`: generalize `make_baseline` to fly greedy `head_940`; add proposed `--win-reference`, `--gift-budget 0`, and adaptive nonnegative gift multiplier. Reward:
   `Δwin − η·Δtheirs/10000`.
   Update η from rolling paired gift violation. Use existing `--lam 1 --gamma 1`; train the critic on fixed-scale returns, normalize advantages only. Compare against experiment 1 with identical seeds and opponent draws.

   **Judgment:** common gate; log paired purses during training, including when η is zero.

   **Budget/kill:** **14–20 GPU-hours** for two seeds. Kill if gift suppression again removes development win gains, or no greedy improvement appears by 750 updates. No λ sweep.

3. **Train against the deployed policy and relevant reacting opponents**

   **Mechanism:** fix the opponent distribution. [Pipeline §(i)](/mnt/e/_work/kaggriculture3/S/pipeline/60_actionrl.md:135) documents theta-only opponents; `head.jax_fn` explicitly returns no-op for the opponent. Thus training never faces the deployed incumbent. This is opponent coverage, not a new melon strategy.

   **Implementation:** approximately 200–300 lines in `head.py`/`ppo.py`: proposed `--opponent-heads`, explicit opponent weights, and learner-seat randomization. Freeze opponent snapshots within each run. Allocate 80% to existing reacting BAND proxies and 20% to incumbent/recent head opponents; verify proxy behavior against recorded BAND traces before scaling. Replace tape-rung hard weights with matchup sampling evaluated against `head_940`, retaining an unbiased evaluation panel.

   **Judgment:** common gate plus fresh reacting-opponent checks; tape gains alone cannot validate this experiment.

   **Budget/kill:** **12–24 GPU-hours**, conditional on experiment 1 showing signal. Kill if gains concentrate on head opponents while target BAND development wins remain flat.

**What is wrong today**

Hard weighting does **nothing to the 80% self-play portion**: `ppo.py` replaces the weighted tape control with `[-1,0]`. Four head-off weather draws also cannot establish that a board is unflippable.

The margin-t requirement is misaligned with wins-only ranking; retain it as requested, but do not call it win significance. The ES loss-only gate cannot measure preservation of ordinary wins, lacks a gift criterion, and reacting opponents receive only **4.4% of fitness weight** ([ESLIVE1 §§1–2](/mnt/e/_work/kaggriculture3/docs/strategy/2026-09-20-eslive1.md)).

Frozen theta is not yet the demonstrated bottleneck. Unfreezing thousands of parameters before fixing these issues would spend the remaining week without isolating a cause.
