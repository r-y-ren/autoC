## 2. What the switch does (`src/kagg3/core/plan.py`, BOEY2 block next to the BOEY1 switches)
While ON, `boey_pkg_sync()` runs at the top of `build_day` and writes the existing switches of the package. It restores them once on an ON→OFF transition. No existing switch's code was duplicated. The switches it writes:
- `BOEY_PKG_SET`:
  - BOEY1: `BOEY_NO_TOMATO_ON`, `BOEY_Q2_DAY=6`
  - NONV2: `NONV_Q2_DAYS=6:9` (Q2 on the first covered day, no reserve)
  - WHEATMIX1: `Q3_EARLY_ON`, `Q3_EARLY_DAYS=8:9`
  - NONV4: `NONV_MELON_SKIP=10:29`, `NONV_MELON_TO=WHEAT` (no melon planted from d10; this rewrite runs after the learned residual)
- `BOEY_PKG_PLATE_SET` (plate component): BOEY1 `BOEY_EARLY_WHEAT_DAYS=0:9` (no carrot d0-9).
- `BOEY_PKG_HERD_SET` (herd component):
  - WOOLFIRST1: `WOOL_FIRST_ON` with 3 cows + 2 sheep on d0-2, geese 0 on d0-2, `WOOL_FIRST_SELL=False` so our sale side is unchanged
  - EGG: `GEESE_TARGET=7`, `GEESE_FIRST_DAY=3`. Boey stands 0 geese on d0-2 and 7.2 by d12.

Four sites exist only under BOEY2:
1. **d0 plate (`_boey_plate`)**, the last mix rewrite, after the residual:
   - d0 plant target = 10 melon + 13 wheat, nothing else.
   - The two seed lists are served first on d0 (`VALUE_CAP`).
   - The d0 seed clip takes melon and wheat first (the `open_board` rank).
   - Without that clip rank, melon, as the last crop, got 0 tiles once the herd had claimed its tiles.
2. **No crew reserve before d10.**
3. **Never Q4.**
4. **Crew floor** `BOEY_PKG_CREW`: the largest affordable crew up to 7 hands on d3-9 and 11 hands on d10-28.

**Overrides and collisions:**
- **ENGINE_GATE (M20z zero-melon plate) and MELON_COUNTER:** `runtime.py` skips both latches while `BOEY_PKG_ON`, because the package owns the opening. This is per process. A live agent runs one seat per process, so it applies only to the seat that runs the switch.
  - In the sim leg neither latch runs (guard mode has no runtime latch), and neither would fire against V anyway (V always plates melon on d0).
- **NONV_\* switches:** written by the package set above. The ENGINE_GATE_SET values (`MELON_PLATE_TILES=20; MELON_PLATE_DAY=1; NONV_PLATE_LAST=6`) can therefore never land on top of the Boey plate.

**Where the body could not follow Boey** (fidelity table):
1. **The Q1 tile limit.** 25 free tiles cannot hold 23 plantings plus 5 animals on d0. The plate therefore plants 10 melon + 9-10 wheat, and the herd gets 3 cows + 1 sheep.
2. **The d0 purse** (3,000 against 3,130 of plate plus herd). The second sheep and the cow build-up are starved: cows are 3.7 at d12 against base 7.2 and Boey 7.8.
3. **The crew floor is not realized.**

   - Our planner has no work for 7 hands on d3-9. Boey's hands do herd CARE/FEED/COLLECT at 13 ops a day.
4. **"Cash to ~0" is not reached.** Dawn money on d6 is 1.6k against base 0.9k and Boey 21, because there is nothing left to buy: the tiles are full and the herd lanes are value-gated after d2.
