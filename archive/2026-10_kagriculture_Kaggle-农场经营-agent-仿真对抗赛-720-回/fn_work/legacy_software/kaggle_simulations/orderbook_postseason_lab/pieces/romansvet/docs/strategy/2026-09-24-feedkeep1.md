## 1. Mechanism (file:line)
`plan.py:10108-10165` (`_derive`): a hungry animal (`must_feed = t_cons >= 1`) is fed iff
`feed_value = min(animal_value − stock_today, ANIMAL_COST) + bank_val + care_val > feed_price` (wheat: hour-0 spot for shed wheat,
the buy curve for bought wheat). `animal_value` (`valuation.py:101-124`) = remaining fires × `price[product]` + fert-days × `price[FERT]`,
and `price` is `view.price` = today's hour-0 SPOT quote. So on a milk/wool trough the whole remaining stream is priced at the trough.
ON replaces the product quote (egg/milk/wool only) with `max(spot, price_table[inv_at_day(K) (+ _pipeline_units)])`, the
`CARE_HOLD_ON` / `ANIMAL_BUY_FWD_ON` forward read (no new estimator; fert, bank and wheat side stay spot).
