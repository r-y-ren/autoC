## 2. What sets our tomato tile count, and what theta can move

The chain, in order, with the line numbers:

1. **`brain.decide`, the one shared crop softmax** — `src/kagg3/core/brain.py:1036-1064`.
   `crop_logits = grow[:5] * (1 + sig(head[7])*4)` (+ `PLANT_MIX_DRAIN_GAIN*drain` under a
   switch), then `w = softmax(crop_logits + clip(CROP_MIX_GAIN * out.crop_mix, ...))` with
   `CROP_MIX_GAIN = 256.0` (`brain.py:802`). **Theta-movable.**
2. **The `absorb` gate** — `brain.py:1122-1124`:
   `absorb = (drain[:5,1] + DRAIN_CLIP*tanh(out.sat) > -DRAIN_CLIP)`, `w = w * absorb`.
   `drain[:,1]` is `residual_drain`'s `share` column (`brain.py:239-290`), and that function
   is **deliberately opponent-inclusive**: `supply = (mkt_inv − I0) + shed + (own + opp) *
   RATE * grow_days`. So the engine's 10.6 committed tomato tiles are **already** subtracted
   from the town's remaining tomato appetite before the mix is chosen. **Theta-movable
   threshold** (`out.sat`), and the observed response is the right shape but the wrong sign
   for this class: we raise tomato where they raise it (r = +0.509), because the town term
   dominates the opponent term.
3. **Quantisation** — `plant_target = _largest_remainder(w, plant_total, 5)`
   (`brain.py:1134`, helper `brain.py:917`), with
   `plant_total = n_dev − sum(animal_want)` and
   `n_dev = _qfloor(dev_frac * n_free)` (`brain.py:1009-1034`). **Theta-movable in total,
   capped by `n_free`** — `2026-09-14-macro-extract.md` §3 measures the residual as a
   *capacity* wall (4.03 tiles/day unclosed at no sigma limit).
4. **The seed side** — `plan._wants` (`plan.py:5537-5546`) takes
   `want_raw = max(plant_target − view.seeds, 0)`; `plan._candidates`
   (`plan.py:5587-5670`) prices each seed's remaining unit stream with `_stream_rev`
   (`plan.py:4467`) and ranks by `grow_mult`/seed cost/`RATIO_SHIFT`. **This is never what
   zeroes tomato**: `2026-09-13-tomato-maturity-economics.md` reconstructed seven dawns of a
   real episode and found `plant_target == 0` removes tomato from the day's wants *before*
   `_candidates` reads the seed list. The budget walk can only ever buy fewer seeds than the
   mix asked for.
5. **Fixed planner logic, not movable by theta:** `spec.CROP_WINDOW_START`
   (`spec.py:56`) is `(CROP_MAX_YIELD_DAY+1)//2` off the engine's own crop table — a spec
   constant, not a knob; `CROP_FIRST_YIELD_DAY[TOMATO] = 8` with `interval 1`, `max_yield 4`,
   `ongoing 1` (`spec.py:36`) is why tomato is a d15-29-only product and why a tile planted
   after d21 banks nothing; and the four mix-rewrite switches (`_melon_open`, `_wheat_mix`,
   `_late_straw_cap`, `_endgame_tomato`, `plan.py:6789-6807`) are all default-OFF.

**Summary for the 6,789-float theta:** it can move the *share* of the day's tiles that go to
tomato (softmax + `crop_mix` + the `absorb` threshold) and the *total* (`dev_frac`), and it
cannot express a **day-indexed** crop programme at all — one shared softmax, measured at no
sigma limit in `2026-09-14-macro-extract.md` §3, collapses carrot/strawberry/melon to ~0 when
fitted to a real block-structured programme. Fertiliser **application** has no decoder
channel whatever (`macro-extract` §4.2): it is a planner decision off the shed.

### 2.1 What applied fertiliser actually does (engine rules)

From the bit-exact port, and it is **two different rules**:

* **Ongoing crops (TOMATO, STRAWBERRY)** — `sim/eod.py:refresh_plants`:
  `fires = is_plant & ~died & (ongoing==1) & (dsf >= 0) & (dsf % interval == 0) & (pcount <= mxy)`,
  `fertilized = was_watered & (t_fert >= day)`,
  `new_yield = min(mxy, t_yield + where(fertilized, 2, 1))`. So a fertilised **and watered**
  tomato tile banks **+1 extra unit per night fire**, up to 4 fires (ages 8-11).
  At the ENG22 realised 112.9/unit that is worth up to ~450 coins a tile.
* **One-time crops (WHEAT, CARROT, MELON)** — `sim/units.py:112-115`: the in-window WATER
  adds `water_bonus = 2 if t_fert >= day else 1`, clipped at `CROP_MAX_YIELD`. Wheat
  (window 2-4, max 6, seeded 1) goes 4 → 6 units = +2 at ~38 coins. **Carrot** (window 2-3,
  max 4, seeded 1) goes 3 → 4 = **+1 unit at ~47 coins**, i.e. it barely clears the
  fertiliser unit it burns. **Melon** saturates without fertiliser (`spec.CROP_SATURATE_AGE`)
  and the gain is **0 units** — only two days earlier.
* `FERTILIZE` burns one shed unit and sets `t_fert = max(prev, day+2)`, so one application
  covers three days (`sim/units.py:119,159-160`).

**Do we apply it?** Yes, and more than the engine class does: the 2026-09-11 census
(`docs/strategy/2026-09-11-fertilizer-engine.md`) measures **190 applications a game for us
against the clone's 62**, and `S/nexthigh/anatomy_opp.csv` puts the ENG22 class at **170.7**.
The allocation is already value-ranked by exactly the arithmetic above:
`valuation.fert_marginal_value` (`src/kagg3/core/valuation.py:127-180`) prices the ongoing
branch as fires-in-window × crop price and the one-time branch as the clipped water gain, and
`plan.py:6029-6031` builds `fert_cand`/`fert_rank` off it. So "apply the collected fertiliser
to tomato instead of selling it" is **already the shipped behaviour**, it is already ranked
tomato-first by value, and the *volume* half of that trade is closed twice
(`FERT_VOLUME_ON`, 2026-09-04 and 2026-09-06; the 2026-09-09 census values our 179
applications at +16.8k net a game, ahead of the clone's +12.4k). Applying it to **carrot** is
what the rules say **not** to do.

## 4. The switch

`plan.CARROT_EARLY_HOLD_ON` — `src/kagg3/core/plan.py:4042-4101` (the block),
applied in `plan._sell_hold` (`plan.py:4288-4321`), reached from the two existing call sites
`plan.py:6071` (the provisional land-valuation allocation) and `plan.py:7615` (the real
sale). `_sell_hold` gains one keyword argument, `day=None`, read by this switch alone.

```python
CARROT_EARLY_HOLD_ON  = False   # default OFF
CARROT_EARLY_HOLD_DAY = 15      # the reservation is released here
CARROT_EARLY_HOLD_NUM = 5       # floor = NUM/DEN of this game's own carrot base
CARROT_EARLY_HOLD_DEN = 4       #       = 43 coins vs a d0-9 realised 33.5
```

It is a **reservation**, not a new decision: `sell.allocate` already sells a unit only when
its adjusted marginal clears `hold`, so raising `hold[I_CARROT]` stops the early lot exactly
where the quote stops paying, the shed-overflow forced sale stays the safety valve, and the
terminal law is untouched (`xp.where(terminal, hold, gated)` — day 29 sells regardless
[LAW, 0.4]). The floor is read off the **price table** (`price_table[I_CARROT, _I0_COL]`),
not `spec.DEFAULT_MARKET_PARAMS`, so it is this game's own base under the training
randomisation — the reason `FERT_FLOOR` reads it there.

**OFF path:** `_sell_hold` returns its argument object unchanged and builds no array, so a
theta trained before the switch decodes byte for byte.
