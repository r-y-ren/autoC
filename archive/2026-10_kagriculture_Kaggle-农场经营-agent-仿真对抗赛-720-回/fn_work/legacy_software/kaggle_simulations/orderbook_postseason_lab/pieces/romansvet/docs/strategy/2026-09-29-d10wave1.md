## 4. Why (per-product sale coins, cell minus control, paired, `S/d10wave1/prodd.py`)
- **A (sale timing) has no lever.** PFS already sells every melon as it ripens, and none ripens before d15 (planted d5+). A release day in d10-14
  moves 0 units; the only way to own d10-14 melon is to plant by d0-4, which is B.
- **B (own d0-1 wave) takes the window and pays for it twice.**
  - B8 sells 78 melons in d10-14 at 205 (+16.0k), so our d10-14 cash rises +7.8k (t 33).
  - It is paid out of the d0-9 herd/fert purse: our d0-9 sales -7.7k (fert -2.4k, milk -2.0k, wool -1.2k, wheat/carrot -1.5k), cash -3.3k.
  - The melon curve does not refill: after 78 + 45 units walk the d10-14 book, our late melons sell at 53 instead of 169 (d15-29 melon -12.4k).
  - **The rival's wave is not denied:** it sells first in the day, so its d10-14 melon falls only -0.9k (45 units, price 251 -> 233), and its
    d10-14 cash is +0.9k (t 2.9) because our herd products are missing from that book too.
  - **The gift is late:** rival d15-29 +16.7k (t 17): milk +7.4k on the same units (price 82 -> 135), strawberry +4.5k, wool +3.3k, egg +1.1k.
    PFS's early herd volume is what holds the rival's late milk/wool/strawberry prices down (PROGRAMME1's "the late volume PFS keeps holds the
    rival's prices down", now from the d0-9 side). Our own late strawberry/milk/wool gain the same price (+12.6k) but it does not cover the melon and
    wheat loss. Net: own +0.4k, rival +17.5k, margin -17.1k (t -13), 22 wins lost.
- **C (FQ tail, Q4 from d10) is a purse loss, not a denial.** Q4 land + hands + seed cost +7.2k of spend (d10-14 +2.3k, d15-29 +5.0k) for
  +4.0k of Q4 crops (tomato +2.0k, strawberry +1.4k, melon +0.7k, wool +1.2k) minus eggs -1.4k and fert -0.8k (fert moved onto Q4). The rival
  gains +3.5k (milk +2.1k: our milk -14 units, the herd loses care hands). Same verdict as Q4DIG1 (own Q4 -1.4k margin at best) and BEATPROG1
  (FQ's own d18-29 is -6.2k vs a programme seat): the denial FQ shows (-9.5k .. -12.9k of the programme's d18-29) comes from FQ's whole body
  (2-3k BUY_PRODUCT orders, 95 productive tiles at d18), not from a Q4 bolted onto PFS.

## 5. Verdict: NONE. The d10-14 window cannot be taken back by PFS switches
- **CANDIDATE: none.** No cell keeps own coins within 1k of the control with a better margin. Closest non-null cell: **C** (Q4 program from d10):
  own 109,602 (-5,567, t -14.6), rival 93,743 (+3,526, t 4.7), margin +15,858 vs +24,952 (-9,094, t -9.3), 65/80 W (-10).
  A10 is the control to the coin (no lever). B4 -15.3k, B8 -17.1k, B4+C -22.7k margin.
- **Answer to the question.** PFS can own a d10-14 melon wave (B8: 78 units, +7.8k window cash, t 33) but not without losing d15-29: the plate
  is bought with the d0-9 herd purse, the melon curve does not refill for PFS's late melons, and the rival's late milk/wool/strawberry/egg prices
  recover (+9.3k to +16.7k rival d15-29). It cannot deny the rival's wave: the rival sells first and loses only 0.3-0.9k of melon income while its
  herd products gain in the same window. The FQ tail on PFS is a -5.6k purse loss. The window gap is the programme's d0-9 skeleton (crew,
  herd and melon together), consistent with PROGRAMME1 / BEATPROG1: only a new body carries it, and PFS's d15-29 lead is its denial.
- **Flood rival read of A10 (coordinator request; JUDGERIVAL2 `flood`, 20f3cfa1, via `rcrw_fl.py`; first 10 boards_m40 x 2 seats = 20 games):**
  A10 = the control to the coin on 20/20 (PFS 119,429 / rival 75,093 / +44,336, W 20/20; PFS melon 0 u d10-14, 90 u d15-29 at 192; rival melon
  43.2 u at 252 in d10-14). With the late prices flooded, PFS's melon reservation still does not bind: A is inert on both rivals.
