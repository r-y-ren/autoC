# BAND180 — widening the §115 band from 90 to 169 boards (se 173 → ~133, bar t ≥ 3)

2026-09-15/16, judge-infrastructure leg. Problem statement: `docs/strategy/2026-09-16-es-plateau.md`
§Q3 — one read of the 90-board POOLED BAND has a board-level **se of 182** (this run measures 173
on flowq2), so §115's "+450 at t ≥ 2" is a *single 2.48 σ test*: P(a true-zero candidate reads
≥ +450) = 0.0066, and over 30 candidates **0.181**. The fix costed there (row 2 of the
recommendation table) is a ≥180-board pool: se 129, false-promotion rate 0.007.

This leg builds as much of that as the corpus allows: **79 new held-out pinned-town boards**, not
90 — the disk is exhausted (see §1). Pool = 90 + 79 = **169 boards**, se ≈ 182·√(90/169) ≈ **133**,
and at the new bar (**d ≥ +450 AND t ≥ 3**) the binding constraint is +450 = 3.38 σ →
P(one) ≈ 3.6e-4, **P(≥1 of 30) ≈ 0.011** — within a rounding error of the 180-board target.

### Caveats
* **Fewer than 90.** The corpus is exhausted at 79. The remaining 11+ can only come from the
  download/cut pipeline (`S/nextband/pick.py` → `dl.sh` → `cut_all.sh` → `build_set.py`; ~1.5 GB of
  replays for 48 boards, hours, and a Kaggle leaderboard snapshot).
* **Population is not LIVEC/NEXT30-pure.** 36 of 79 (tiers A, C) sit at or above NEXT30's band,
  12 more in LIVE-C's; the 31 `E_live_low` boards are the LIVE62 population, one band below.
  Pooling them into the band statistic changes the estimand slightly — it now averages a wider
  slice of the ladder than the old 90. Read the in-band-48 line when that matters.
* **Disjoint by episode, not by team.** Several `C_live_top` teams also appear in TOPB2/TOPB3, and
  the 2200-2600 band is one open-loop clone family. New boards and new towns, not new opponent
  *policies*: the variance reduction is real, the independence partial.
* **Append-only, order load-bearing.** seed(board *i*) = `SB0 + 1000003·i`, `SB0 = 1000780001`
  (= 777001 + 1000003·1000, clear of LIVE-C 0-102, TOPB2 777001, NEXT30 200777601, BAND40
  400778201, NEXTHIGH 600778801, TOPB3 800779401). Never reorder/renumber/delete an id.

### Finding vs the ES-PLATEAU prediction

The prediction from the shrinkage model (`2026-09-16-es-plateau.md` §Q3: EB true effect **−97**
for flow218_g110, **−98** for flowq2_g150) was that both would read near −100 on fresh boards.
They did **not**: +249 (se 199) and +234 (se 273). Each is ~1.2-1.7 σ above its EB point estimate,
so neither *falsifies* the shrinkage — with se ≈ 200-270 a read of +240 is entirely compatible
with a true effect anywhere in −150 … +600 — but the two independent fresh reads are both
positive, which the τ = 47 prior did not expect. Two readings of the same size, however, is still
*n = 2*: the honest statement is that the new boards have **not** reproduced the records' +300 and
have **not** reproduced the predicted −100 either; they have shrunk both toward a common ≈ +250
with a t that is still far under 3.

The decomposition says where flow218's remaining number comes from: **+590 on the 31 low-band
boards and +5 on the 48 in-band boards** — i.e. the trade that `2026-09-12-objective-gate.md`
already named (the ES step buys low-band/LOSS10 margin and sells band margin), now visible inside
one leg. flowq2 is flatter across tiers but reads **+3 on the 14 top-tier boards**, the population
that actually decides top-10 entry.

**Consequence for §115b.** On this evidence the +450/t ≥ 3 bar is doing exactly what it was built
for: the two best candidates in the whole ES pool read t +2.10 and +1.54 on 169 boards and are
rejected without ambiguity, where the old 90-board read put them at +295/+302 — one lucky draw
away from the +450 line. Keep `pooled_band180.py` as the promotion statistic and
`pooled_band.py` for the unchanged TOPB2/LIVE62 vetoes; run `run_band180.sh` as a 9th leg
(~7 min) on every candidate that clears +300 on the old band.
