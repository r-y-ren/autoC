## What the rungs showed
1. **Herd drift is a market problem, not a placement problem.** Source: `herddiag.py`, `diag/herd_r4c.txt`.
   - The r4c clone places every animal it owns (shed animals ~0, free pastures ~0).
   - It buys too few: by d12 COW 4.4 vs 9.3, GOOSE 3.7 vs 8.6; herd 11.8 vs 20.8; quadrants 2.7 vs 3.2; plants at d10 38 vs 54.
   - It hoards cash on d6-d10: 0.7-2.6k vs 0.2-0.7k.
   - Forcing the teacher targets (INV) buys the herd on d10-d15, too late to pay back: ratio 0.714.
   - INV on d1-9 only lifts the ratio (0.846 / 0.840) but raises the rival's income (dtheirs +15k) and costs 1-2 MELON flips.
2. **The V56 sell layer does not carry the flips.**
   - Its WHEAT sells starve the herd (sale1: herd_d10 6-9 vs 10-12).
   - Limited to cash products (sale3) it adds +0.011 ratio and +1.2k dours, with dtheirs unchanged.
3. **dtheirs is price denial by production volume, not sell timing.** Sources: `rivdiag.py`, `diag/riv_v56_vs_clone.txt`, and the V56 kernel as a file agent in `v56main/main.py`, which reproduces bandfamily1 `v56.csv` exactly.
   - In the V56 games the rival's big late products are crushed:
     - aguacates STRAWBERRY at d20-28 sells for 22-41 (clone game 170-192), town inventory +90;
     - navierstokes MILK at d18-28 falls 82 -> 1 (clone game 154-189), inventory +110.
   - V56 simply has more of those products. On aguacates it sold 78 strawberries on d16-19; the clone sold 42.
   - Three clone overlays tried to reproduce this and failed:
     - dumping our own stock (crush16): theirs +-1k, ours -2 to -11k;
     - swapping the filler seed orders to the rival's crop (mix8): our cash drops (dours -20.6k) and the rival barely moves;
     - handing the farm to V56 at d18 (hand18): the kernel cannot run a clone-built farm without INHERIT (-6 flips).
4. **Data is still the only lever that moves the MELON margin.**
   - Median per seat: -28.5k (r4c, 1,349 eps) -> -21.9k (r5a2, 3,025 eps).
   - dtheirs +11.8k -> +6.0k; held-out token accuracy 0.853 -> 0.862; market-nonzero accuracy 0.476 -> 0.507.
   - But the flips did not move (the same 2 wins and 6 losses vs V56), and the teacher ratio is flat within the 10-board noise (0.80-0.84).
   - The V56 body's median MELON margin is -0.4k, so the clone remains ~20k per seat behind it on the flip leg.

New agent switches in `S/bcbody1/agent/main.py`: all are env-gated and off by default.

| switch | effect |
|---|---|
| BCB_INV=1 (+BCB_INV_DMAX, BCB_INV_RES) | teacher-median herd / geese / quadrant targets (targets.json): land first, then one animal per step |
| BCB_D1HIRE=n | day-1 HIRE cap |
| BCB_SALE=1/2/3 | shadow V56 kernel (S/v56tune1/v56kernel_orig.py), one call per step, fresh namespace per game. 1: its SELL rows replace ours. 2: union. 3: cash products only (MELON/MILK/EGG/WOOL/CARROT/TOMATO/STRAWBERRY); the clone keeps its WHEAT/FERT sells |
| BCB_CRUSH=D (+BCB_CRUSH_TGT, BCB_CRUSH_ITEMS) | from day D, sell the full shed stock of the cash products every step |
| BCB_HAND=D | from day D the shadow V56 kernel plays every turn |
| BCB_MIX=D (+BCB_MIX_END) | d D..18: the filler seed orders become the rival's top late crop |
