### 3.3 "Same-turn sale race" — what it actually is

Confirmed from the engine's own price function, which the notebook embeds
verbatim from `kaggle_environments 1.32.7`:

```
price(item, I) = base ± amp * shape(|I - I0|, T),  I0 = 10000, floor 1
  WHEAT base 25 T 400 sqrt/log        MELON base 250 T 300 log/sq  (above_target 3.6)
  CARROT 35 T 450 hinge/sqrt          EGG 50 / MILK 160 / WOOL 200 / STRAWBERRY 120
  hinge(u) = u + 8.0*max(0,u-1)^2     FERTILIZER 100 T 200 linear/linear
```

and from `_r37_quote_priority`, which prices an order as
`Σ_{j<qty} price(I+j) − Σ_{j<qty} price(I+batch+j)` with
`batch = clamp(rival's visible standing ripe yield, 8, 24)`. So:

* **the walk is per unit off the cumulative shared inventory** (`off = +j`) —
  identical to `sim/market.py::_quotes` and to `2026-09-16-pricevol.md` §4.3;
* **orders resolve by queue index across both seats against one pre-commit
  inventory** — the same lockstep our `plan.py:1209` block documents;
* therefore **"winning the race" = having your units earlier in the interleaved
  queue than the rival's units of the same product.**

Three layers exploit it, in escalating order:

| layer | what it does | gate |
|---|---|---|
| `_r37_reorder_sales` | within each contiguous SELL block, sorts by `quote_priority` desc (quantities unchanged, buy barriers preserved) | always |
| `_r36_reserve` | reads its **own future tape**, sums planned SELL of each product over the next `H` turns, issues them **now** as one order, and records a "debt" that `_apply_suppression` subtracts from the future turn | steps 216–695, `H` default **2**, clipped to the 72-step block end; skips WHEAT/FERTILIZER, prices < 2, and any product with a PICKUP/BUY in the window |
| `_RACE` (V44) | raises `H` to **8** while the rival is a detected clone, and to **24** permanently once a lost race is observed | `_race_clone`: rival `hands` list **and** `farmer` position exactly equal for ≥4 of the last 6 turns **and** `_r37_similarity ≥ 0.95` on occupied-tile (crop, animal) pairs; `_race_lost`: rival's market inventory of a race product rose beyond modelled town consumption at the turn our own shed stock of it rose and we sold none |

`_race_town` models the town drain explicitly: every shop's items drain 1/tick
(2 if the shop is single-item) on `step % 4 == 0`, plus 1 of every race item at
dawn. That is the same `SHOP_SELL_INTERVAL = 4` our projector uses.

**The gate never fires against B.** It needs our worker *positions* to equal
theirs. pop-shift puts B at distance 0.79 from cluster-0 on aggregate build
statistics, but B's crew geometry is its own. So V44's horizon stays at the
native 2 against us: **V44's +155 rating is clone-on-clone displacement.**

### 3.4 "First-turn wheat round trip" (V45) — our OPEN_PUMP, re-derived

The layer is 26 lines. If the parent's step-0 market is exactly
`[BUY_PRODUCT WHEAT 5, BUY_PRODUCT WHEAT 10, SELL WHEAT 60]` it is replaced by
`[BUY_PRODUCT WHEAT 70, SELL WHEAT 70]`. Their own stated reasoning:

> "the shared per-unit market quoting makes that second purchase cost about sixty
> more, which their strictly funded first-day plan cannot absorb: they plant one
> melon fewer. The same mechanism was used against us by two live rivals
> [who] used `[BUY_PRODUCT WHEAT 78, SELL WHEAT 78]`."

and the disclosed trade-off: against "the older Two Coins opening" (which *sells*
wheat before buying again — the V35–V41 tape's `[BUY 13, SELL 13, BUY 13]`) the
round trip **costs them** the melon instead.

This is `OPEN_PUMP` (`plan.py:1209-1311`, 53 units at hour 0, sell-back as slot 0
of the hour-1 BUY row, ON since 2026-09-04). Our block already states the
mechanism to the coin (26 coins of denial refusing their second 500-coin SHEEP,
`5*(sqrt(n)-1) >= 25` ⇒ n ≥ 36), the paired panel (+20,509/game t 12.71 on
panel24) **and the regression** (−4,640 t −6.22 vs top-10 tapes that already pump
30–43 units). V45 is the third independent discovery of the same lever
(boatlee V13-R3 August → our OPEN_PUMP 09-04 → V45 09-15).

### 3.5 Three engine quirks the layers exist to service (check ours)

* **Dawn shed overflow destroys an ordered suffix.** `_r148_overflow` runs at
  `step % 24 == 23`: worker inventories deposit into the 100-cap shed **in
  order**, and the tail past 100 is destroyed. It sells exactly enough shed stock
  that the discarded suffix fits, and asserts the post-dawn shed vector equals
  the unpatched one. (V43's headline "Recovering Lost Harvests".)
* **PLANT is all-or-nothing per crop.** `_r148_atomic`: if the number of PLANT
  commands for a crop exceeds seeds held, the engine cancels **every** PLANT of
  that crop. They demote the physically invalid ones to PASS to keep the rest.
  `_r148_seed_prefund` buys next turn's shortfall in advance for the same reason.
* **Animals starve on day 1 if nothing FEEDs them.** nathanjacob's v40 autopsy:
  3 of 4 losses were 2 cows dead on day 1, each worth **$12k–32k** of milk;
  `_r85/_r86` and prvsiyan's V226A wheat top-up are the fix.

### 3.6 Terminal handling

At step **711** a private `copy.copy(chassis)` shadow replays steps 712–718;
step 718 is `_parent_liquidate` — every worker adjacent to the shed DROPs,
then `SELL <item> <projected shed qty>` for all 9 products **sorted by
`price × qty` descending**. The shadow is accepted only if each of 712–717
emits exactly 9 SELL orders of ≥100 units and the planner's own simulation
reproduces the action. nathanjacob independently: "**85 % of close losses happen
in the final 7 game steps**"; optimising them took his hardest matchup 66 % → 83 %.

## 6. What changes our next experiment

### 6.1 `OPEN_PUMP_TELL_KEEP0`'s tell is blind to V45 (the one actionable defect)

`plan.py:1363-1398`: the tell reads `10000 - inv - OPEN_PUMP_UNITS` at **hour 1**
to recover the other seat's hour-0 draw — "0 against a class-A tape, 30-43
against a top-10 one". **V45 buys 70 and sells 70 inside step 0**, so its net
hour-0 draw is **zero** and the pot at hour 1 reads exactly like a class-A tape.
Meanwhile the *interleaving* at hour 0 is 70 units deep: our 53-unit ladder is
quoted behind theirs, which is the same −4,640 (t −6.22) regression the block
already records for the top-10 tapes, now arriving in the *band*.

Two cheap, paired, sim-first probes, in this order:

1. **Re-price `OPEN_PUMP` against a V45-shaped opening.** The band tapes our judge
   replays were recorded before 09-15, so they carry `[BUY 5, BUY 10, SELL 60]`.
   Synthesising one seat whose step 0 is `[BUY WHEAT 70, SELL WHEAT 70]` is a
   two-row edit to a taped opponent — no training, no new gene — and it answers
   whether `OPEN_PUMP_UNITS = 53` and `OPEN_PUMP_KEEP = 5` are still right, and
   whether `OPEN_PUMP_SLOT0_ON`'s interleave still holds, against the build the
   band will be running by tomorrow.
2. **Make the tell read the turn, not the pot.** Their round trip is visible
   *within* step 0 only to a seat that draws at hour 0 (we do). If the hour-1 pot
   is restored but our own hour-0 fill price was walked, that is the V45
   signature and the one bit `OPEN_PUMP_TELL_MIN = 40` cannot see.

### 6.2 The judge's blind spot, stated plainly

From 2026-09-15 the band carries a **reactive** market layer. Our band/ENG22/
TOP5NOW opponent seats are byte-exact action replays, which prvsiyan states the
same limitation for ("public raw-action controls reproduce observed rival actions
but cannot replan in response to our changes"). Two consequences, opposite in
sign, and both real:

* `_RACE`'s clone gate needs our worker positions to equal theirs, so **it will
  not fire against B** — the replay is faithful *for this layer* and V44's rating
  gain is not aimed at us. Good news, and it is why §115b's bar should not be
  loosened on the strength of the band's drift.
* `_OPEN`'s round trip fires on **any** standard game — it is unconditional. So
  the replay tapes *do* understate the band on the opening, and §6.1 is the fix.

### 6.3 Two free CPU checks worth an hour each

`_r148_overflow` (dawn destroys the deposit suffix past shed 100) and
`_r148_atomic` (a seed shortfall cancels every PLANT of that crop). Both are
engine quirks, not opponent behaviour, so a sim probe on B's own boards prices
them without any opponent model. If B never overflows and never over-requests
seed, both close in an hour; if it does, these are the cheapest coins on the
board, because the fix costs no crew-turn.

### 6.4 What this closes

* **"Copy the band" is dead for the last time.** The band's file is public and we
  now have it; it ends at 2810 and it improves by pointing its market layer at
  other copies of itself. `kaggle-clone-family-2026-09-05` was right and is now
  proven from the source side.
* **The top five are private.** 81 % of the top 50 is the V-series; the 8 slots
  above it are singletons at 35-54 % similarity, and nathanjacob's own conclusion
  is "convergence won't get there — it requires innovation". Our TOP-NEW /
  SINGLETON profiles are the only description of them that exists anywhere,
  public or otherwise.
* **The action interface remains the bottleneck**, exactly as
  `2026-09-16-es-plateau.md` and `2026-09-11-codex-blind-review` said. Their
  advantage is not a better plan — their tape is worse than ours on coins per
  unit (`pricevol.md`: B 85.5 coins/unit vs TOP-NEW 85.2) — it is that they can
  put a market row on any of 24 turns and we can put one on six.
