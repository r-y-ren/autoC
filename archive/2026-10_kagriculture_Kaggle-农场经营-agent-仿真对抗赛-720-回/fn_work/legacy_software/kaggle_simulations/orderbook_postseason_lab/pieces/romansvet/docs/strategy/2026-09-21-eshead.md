# ESHEAD — flow267 greedy output-bias ES launch

Launched 2026-09-20 23:37Z on GPU1 as pid 1847989. `flow267_eshead` directly optimises the greedy v1
policy flown by deployment.

## Design

- Freeze `head_940`'s theta, trunk, and output weights; search only the per-slot mean-free part of `b3`:
  92 logits minus 18 slot means = 74 effective dimensions.
- Use mirrored seats and common random numbers for every antithetic `+/-` perturbation pair.
- Run `--gens 50 --pairs 32 --episodes 256 --tape-frac 0.8 --gift-w 1.0 --lr 0.05`; the remaining
  20% use a reacting frozen v1 `head_940` opponent. Checkpoint every five generations.
- Calibrate one fixed `--sigma auto` on 4,320 recorded slot decisions, targeting about 2% greedy
  decision changes. Launch calibration selected sigma 0.133 at 1.94%.
- Fitness is paired win improvement plus a clipped 1e-6 margin tie-break, minus
  `gift_w*max(mean_gift, 0)/1e4`. Convert candidate fitness to centred, variance-normalised ranks and
  update the centre with Adam ascent.
- Evaluate the centre every ten generations on fixed held-out 128-episode CRNs. At about 2.5 min/gen,
  kill at generation 20 if held-out centre win improvement is below +3 percentage points.
