## 0. VERDICT

**No. Nothing new arrived at B's rating, on 09-15 or on any other day.** Of the 92 of B's
games profiled here (09-12 to 09-16), **91 opponents are the same §118 cluster-0 band
clone** (mean distance 1.8 in
the frozen z-space of `S/nexthigh/anatomy.py`, where the 100 tapes the split was fitted on
run out to 12.8); the 92nd is a single game against the #5 team's newest file. The
`>= 56250000` submissions the brief flagged are not a class — submission ids advance
~22,000 per day (max id met: 56171389 / 56192535 / 56214572 / 56239079 / 56263351 /
56263779 on 09-11…09-16), so "id ≥ 56.25 M" is a synonym for "uploaded on 09-15", and B
goes **18-19 (48.6 %) against them**, no worse than the 42.2 % it manages against the
09-13/14 wave.

Three things, none of them a new class at B's level, produce the fall:

1. **The placement overshoot unwound.** B entered on 09-11 at rating 600, climbed to 2510
   in 133 games at a mean |ΔR| of **21.4 per game** (max 136), and from 09-12 onwards the
   step is a flat **4.4-4.5 per game**. The 2700 print on 09-13 was made against a mean
   opponent of 2237-2614 during that high-step phase; the 2546 print is 400 games of the
   converged step. No sigma effect is left to unwind — the rest is real.
2. **The ladder is getting absolutely stronger while B is a frozen file.** Regressing B's
   per-game coin margin on opponent rating **and** calendar day over all 628 games:
   **day = −969 coins/day (t −3.39)**; restricted to the 2500-2800 opponents B actually
   lives among, **−1,633 coins/day (t −4.87)**. Same regression with opponent submission
   id in place of day: **−24,600 coins per million ids (t −3.19)** ≈ −540 coins/day. At a
   *fixed displayed opponent rating* B's margin went +3,902 → +1,454 → +30 → +120 over
   09-12…09-15. That is the whole 2697 → 2546.
3. **The clone class is drifting toward the fertilizer engine, inside the class.** Across
   the submission waves the opponents' `fert_applied` median goes **79.5 → 85.5 → 93.0**
   (Spearman ρ +0.429, p < 1e-4, n = 92; weighted regression +234 fert-ops per million
   submission ids, t +2.74), `fert_sold` **353.5 → 348.5 → 343.0** (ρ −0.309, p 0.003) and
   wheat sold after d15 **248 → 282.5 → 289.5** (ρ +0.502, p < 1e-5). Cluster-1's centre is
   fert_applied 170.7 / fert_sold 218.8 — the population is walking that line.

**A third class does exist — 480-650 rating points above B.** A k = 3 refit on the 117
profiled seats (49.0 % of the build variance) separates B's world from the top of the board
without being told which is which: 94 seats at distance **0.79** from the frozen clone
centre (91 of them B's opponents), **18 seats at 6.87 from the clone centre and 8.27 from
the engine centre** (17 of them the current top five), and **5 seats at 15.24/16.55 from
both** — M & M & P & Q, rank 2, alone. The top five are **not one class**, and B has met
that population **7 times in 628 games**, once against the current-day build (§5).

**Therefore:** the current top five are a class our judge has never scored, so §5 cuts the
25 tapes (**TOP5NOW**, `S/popshift/ids.txt`, seed base 1400781201, 25/25 verified
byte-exact). It is **not** registered in the judge — retention is unmeasured, three of the
five teams already own TOPB3/ENG22 tapes of *other* submissions, and that has to be an
explicit decision, not a side effect of this study.

## 7. What this changes

1. **Stop reading the 09-15 submission wave as an event.** The id bucket is a date. The
   measured population term is a steady −1.0 to −1.6 k coins/day at matched rating, running
   since at least 09-12 — B is a frozen file on a moving ladder, and roughly a third of the
   fall is the placement overshoot besides.
2. **The band legs still describe B's real opponents.** NEXTHIGH is 30/30 cluster 0 and
   NEXT30 29/30 (`S/nexthigh/cluster.md`), and cluster 0 is 91 of the 92 games profiled. They are not stale — but they
   are cut from *older* clones, and the live clone's fertilizer is drifting up at t +2.74,
   so the band sets have a shelf life and should be re-cut rather than re-read.
3. **Nothing our judge owns covers the top of the board as it stands today.** ENG22 is the
   2953+ *engine*; TOPB3R9 is 09-14's Majkel/ymg_aq/DSM at 9 usable boards; ymg_aq is no
   longer in the top five. TOP5NOW (§5) is cut and waiting for a decision.
4. **The one build lever the census points at** is the market itself: the class holding
   ranks 1-5 uses **421 selling turns and 1,214 sell orders at a 2.2 % idle rate**, against
   B's **56 turns, 147 orders, 10.2 % idle**, and buys the third quadrant by d10 to plant
   44 more tiles (77 of them carrot). That is an action-interface statement, which is where
   `docs/strategy/2026-09-16-es-plateau.md` already pointed after the ES plateau.
