# CREW-PUSH: size the crew-target push off the hand's own fib bill (census #5)

**Verdict: CLOSED.** The ceiling is real and the switch removes it *at the
enumeration's intent* -- `crew_target > n_hire` goes 42/300 -> **0/300** board-days
-- but the intent is not what the day buys. `HIRE_ROW_ON` (shipped **True**) trims
the HIRE row to the hands `_routes` actually loaded, so 42 moved intents become
**2 moved HIRE rows** and the whole plan changes on **4/300 board-days**. Paired
band margin **+53 (t +0.57)** on 68 boards, against the §115b bar of +450 at t >= 2.
No judge leg, no promotion. The opposite sign (`exact`, the bill alone -- a
*smaller* push) is a clean loss, **-2,766 band margin at t -8.21**, -68.7 hands a
season: the direction is settled and B sits against a ceiling, not on a slope.
The mechanism below says why no *bigger* push helps either: the crew near B is
task-limited, not push-limited.

`crew_push` becomes a **cumulative** table indexed by the same `min(h,

(`HIRE_COST[k-1]` = the k-th hand's own marginal fib price, 1,1,2,3,5,8,13,21,34,

| mode | per-hand push | cumulative at m=13 | reads |
|---|---|---:|---|
| OFF | `400` | 5,200 | shipped |
| `sum` | `400 + HIRE_COST[k-1]` | 5,809 | bill refunded inside the target, tilt kept -- **upward**, the census's read |
| `max` | `max(400, HIRE_COST[k-1])` | 5,200 | only the 15th/16th hand move; **inert on B** (`crew_target` maxes at 13) |
| `exact` | `HIRE_COST[k-1]` | 609 | the bill alone -- **downward**, never measured in this shape |

Tests (`.venv/bin/python -m pytest tests/test_crewpush.py tests/test_lot4.py -q`
-> **21 passed**): OFF is the default and `crew_push_cum() == 400 * m` in every
mode; whole-plan sha256 digests on **five** boards (`rich`, `poor`, `deep`,
`noramp`, `taxed`) equal a pristine `git archive HEAD src` subprocess's; each
mode's table shape; monotone + zero at 0; unknown mode raises; a zero
`crew_target` is inert in every mode; `sum` never books fewer hands than OFF and
`max` books exactly OFF's; `sum` books the hand the flat push refused on the
census's board shape; affordability (`bills[h] + cash_reserve <= money`) is
untouched; past the target the push is flat.

**The mechanism.** `plan.py:7484-7500`: with `HIRE_ROW_ON` (shipped True)
`n_hire_row = min(n_hire, n_act)`, where `n_act` is the last hand `_routes` gave
a non-PASS route. The census itself measured that clamp biting on **150/300 =
50 %** of board-days, mean 1.43 hands. So the flat-400 ceiling sits *upstream of
the binding constraint*: the day already enumerates more hands than its task set
can load, and paying the 13th hand its fib price buys an intent the route drops.
Measured directly on the 42 moves: **40 of 42 were eaten by the clamp**
(`n_hire_row < n_hire` after the move), and the row's own census figure
reproduces to the decimal -- `HIRE row < n_hire` on **153/300 = 51.0 %, mean
1.43 hands** (census: 150/300, 1.43). Mean HIRE row over the 300 days: 8.723
(OFF) -> 8.730 (ON).

This is FORWARD_ADMIT's lesson again (`2026-09-09-forced-opening-ramp`): near B
the crew is **task-limited, not push-limited**, which is also why a larger
constant cannot work -- `max` is inert by construction and `sum` is the largest
bill-shaped push there is.

## 6. Verdict

**CLOSED, with the mechanism.** Census direction #5 is now measured in both
signs and the build exists (default-OFF, `CREW_PUSH_COST_ON`), so the row can be
struck from the open list rather than carried as "never measured". The upward
direction is +53 (t +0.57) on the band and the downward one is **-2,766 (t
-8.21)**: an asymmetry that is itself the finding.

* The **ceiling is real** and the fix removes it exactly where the census said
  it binds: `crew_target > n_hire` 42/300 -> 0/300, all 42 moves upward, the
  refusals on d11-d20 at 12.7-vs-11.6 hands.
* **It does not reach the board.** `HIRE_ROW_ON`'s route trim
  (`plan.py:7484-7500`, `n_hire_row = min(n_hire, n_act)`) eats 40 of the 42
  moves; the HIRE row moves on 2/300 board-days and the whole plan on 4/300.
  Paired: **band margin +53 (t +0.57)**, our coins +68 (t +1.12), 0 flips, against
  a promotion bar of +450 at t >= 2. No judge leg.
* The 12-board ymg_aq read (+256 margin, t +2.12) is **not** promotable evidence:
  it is 12 boards off the same 4-in-300 plan changes, and the 68-board band --
  the §115b surface -- reads +53. Treating the ymg figure as the estimate would
  be exactly the winner's-curse read the archive keeps re-closing.
* **No larger push can work either.** `max` is inert by construction on B
  (`crew_target` maxes at 13, where 400 already beats fib 233), and `sum` is the
  largest bill-shaped push that exists; past the target every mode is flat by
  design. The binding constraint near B is the day's **task set**, not the
  push's size -- the same wall FORWARD_ADMIT and LABOUR-DOES-NOT-COMPOUND hit.
  A crew lever that pays would have to create the work first (the MACRO_EXEC
  site-5 path, which excludes this trim), not price the hand.

**Keep the switch** (default OFF, byte-identical, 12 tests): it is the honest
instrument for any future crew work and it makes P3's "never measured" row false.
