The score, per lot row and product, is

    batch[p] = clip(rival's visible ripe yield of p, 8, 24)
    score[p] = sum_{j < q[p]} price(p, inv[p] + j) - price(p, inv[p] + batch[p] + j)

with `inv` = `sell.lot_inventories`'s own projection for **that lot's turn** (the shelf the
row will actually meet) and `price` our existing `tables.price` table — no engine parameter
is inlined. Rows are ranked by `score` descending, ties to the lower product index; every
key is distinct by construction so numpy's unstable `argsort` and JAX's stable one agree.
Half (a) moves the block ahead of the day's purchases only when no product is both bought
and sold that turn (`B_WHEAT`/`B_FERT` are the row's only `BUY_PRODUCT` slots) and never on
`OPEN_PUMP`'s day, which owns index 0 of turn 1.
