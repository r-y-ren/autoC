## Pipeline (S/dagger1)
- `dg.py` (run through `dgr.py` = the rcr.py wrapper, `--rival g0capsfix`). Our seat, per `--mode`:
  - `expert`: PFS executes. This is rc.py's tree path bit for bit, used for round-0 data and control rows.
  - `codec`: `decode(encode(PFS action))`, i.e. PFS through the clone's action codec (the ceiling).
  - `student`: the JAX student executes (kh._decide greedy, `--ourcfg`). PFS runs in shadow on the same observations every step.
    - `--dayfrac` (TRAINREVIEW1 note): PFS executes WHOLE DAYS at that share, and labels are kept only on PFS days, so every label is consistent with PFS's own dawn plan.
    - `--mix mkt|unit`: decomposition. Student units with PFS's market, or PFS's units with the student's market.
  - Every mode writes one npz per seat-episode in the xrc.py / extract.py format (PFS's action is the label). Rows = rc.py columns + `agree` (student-vs-PFS unit tokens) + `magree` (market exact step).
- **Trainer:** `train3h10.py` (the BCBODY10 recipe, model3, BC_W210=3, BC_EVAL 2000, params_best by hold score). One addition, `BC_SELLALL` (default on):
  - A SELL label above the observed shed-stock bin goes to the 500 class, meaning "sell all available"; the engine fills unit by unit.
  - That covers 21 % of PFS's SELL orders: stock plus units placing the same step. Separately, 17 % of all SELLs are orders on an empty shed.
  - 55 % of PFS's SELLs equal the stock bin and 24 % are smaller lots. Both are left as they are.
- **Warm start: r5a3.** 128 episodes are 3.9 % of core4's 3,317. From scratch, model3 cannot learn the game's structure from that little data; the r5a3 representation carries it.
  - The market head still memorises: train lm 0.000 by step 6k, hold mkt_nz 0.49.
- **Boards.**
  - Training data comes only from synthetic boards `boards_syn_*.json`: random seeds, towns sampled slot by slot from the m116 towns.
  - m76 (152 games) and m40 (80) are held out.
  - The hold set is 6 fixed round-0 syn episodes (`holdout_syn.txt`).
- **Lanes.**
  - GPU1: one generator at a time, N 64. JUDGEGPU1's fwdserver also sits on that card; it is not mine.
  - GPU0: the trainer, and the m76 judge between trainer runs, never together.
  - CPU: at most 2 workers after the 09:54Z TRAINREVIEW1 cap.
  - Every GPU launch runs under `flock -o /home/user/gpu_launch.lock` + sleep 90.
- **Throughput** (the binding constraint). PFS labels cost 15-19 s per game on the contended CPU, single-threaded:
  - GPU1 generator: 64 games in 1,434-1,557 s.
  - CPU worker: 16 games in 650 s.
  - One DAgger round = about 26 min of generation + 7 min of training + 13 min of m76 judging.
