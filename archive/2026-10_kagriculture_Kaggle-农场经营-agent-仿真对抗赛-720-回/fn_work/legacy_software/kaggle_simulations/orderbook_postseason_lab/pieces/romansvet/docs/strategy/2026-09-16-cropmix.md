# CROPMIX — the crop mix is not the edge

2026-09-16, 16:05-16:40Z. Tools `S/cropmix/`, code + tests on branch `cropmix` (NOT merged). Instruments: 20
M & M & P & Q replays (both seats) + 184 `S/rotband` band replays + the engine-replayed `S/mmpq/quads*.csv`
ledgers.

## 0. VERDICT

**The price signal the lead hypothesised is REAL and the mechanism that uses it LOSES.**

* The dawn quote *does* forecast the quote on the day the tile sells — `r(now,fut)` 0.95 WHEAT
  / 0.98 CARROT / 0.84 TOMATO on 184 band replays — and the mean moves too: **MELON is planted
  at 0.76 of base and harvested at 0.46**; STRAWBERRY's dawn level forecasts nothing.
* But that signal **already reaches the plant-time choice at three places** (§1), one a hard
  glut veto, and the crop mix is worth **at most +1,020 coins a game** (§3, an upper bound with
  no glut charge; the tile-holding version of the same bound is **−11,396**).
* `CROP_SCARCE_ON`, built here, closes the one real gap — the mix never reads the forward quote
  the budget already computes. **Three arms, 52 boards: −425 (t −0.81), −69 (t −0.16), −749
  (t −1.40); zero row flips for, five against, win rate down on every leg.** REJECT, and **the
  plant-time crop-mix family is CLOSED**: last untried signal, after `PLANT_MIX_DRAIN` (drain
  share), `CROP_DAY` (calendar), `OPP_MIX`/`OPP_SUPPLY` (other seat) and the ES crop-mix arm.

## 1. Q1 — where the crop is chosen, and what price it sees

| stage | file:line | what it does | price it sees |
|---|---|---|---|
| mix | `core/brain.py:1048-1064`, `:1134` | `w = softmax(grow[:5]·(1+σ(head[7])·4) + clip(crop_mix))`, then `plant_target = largest_remainder(w, plant_total)` | **the dawn quote, learned**: the head's per-product features are `(inv − I0)/T` and `price/base`, hour 0, today (`brain.py:552-553`) |
| maturity gate | `core/brain.py:1080` | `w *= can_mature` | none (calendar) |
| **glut veto** | `core/brain.py:1122-1124` | `w *= (drain.share + 4·tanh(sat) > −DRAIN_CLIP)` | **none — a QUANTITY test.** "unclaimed town appetite", opponent-inclusive; it is the melon veto and it is a supply count, not a price |
| seed budget | `core/plan.py:6919-6935` | `inv_h = inv_at_day(mkt_inv, shops, _FIRST_P, day) + _pipeline_units(...)`; `v_seed[c] = grow_mult·_stream_rev(price_table[c], inv_h[c], u_new[c])` | **the forward quote**, the k-th planting's units walked down the curve at that crop's own first-yield day |

**The gap, exactly.** The planner already knows what a tile will fetch on the day it sells —
`_candidates` prices it — but that number reaches only the **budget**, capped by the mix (`:6836`). The
budget can **refuse** a crop; it can never **substitute** one. A per-product veto does exist (`absorb`) and
it is a supply count: **there is no price veto and no price bonus anywhere in the mix.**

Rule "plant the dearest live crop": hit **63.9 %** (MMPQ) / **58.7 %** (band) against 20 % chance, the
pick's level at its own harvest day **+0.28 / +0.30 of base** over the average live crop's. **The signal is
real — and real where the mix is least free.** WHEAT and CARROT persist because their markets absorb (`T`
400/450, `log`/`sqrt` above-curves); STRAWBERRY (`T` 100, `linear` 1.60) and MELON (`sq`, 3.6×) are the two
no forecast helps with, because their price is set by what both seats are about to dump — the sign even
flips between populations (strawberry +0.11 MMPQ, −0.25 band). The rival's share of that is the term no
forward projection of ours may use: `OPP_SUPPLY_ON` tried and lost 192 boards at t −3.80
(`plan.py:1810-1829`).

**BOUND.** Hold our 813 crop units, re-share them in **their** unit proportions, price each at
**our own realised** price (no glut charge — deliberately generous): **+1,020 coins a game.**
Hold our 186 **tiles** instead, with their tile shares and their units/tile: **−11,396.** The 19,308-coin
crop-revenue gap is **volume** (974 units to 813, 271 tiles to 186) and realised price, not share allocation
— and our TOMATO realises **123.2** to their 78.4 *because* we sell 23 units to their 122, so "sell more
tomato" is a walk down a `T = 200` curve, not a free +6.8k.

`S/cropmix/tileday.py` (tile-days counted off the replays) says why: coins per **tile-day**, MMPQ / their
opponents — WHEAT 27.8 / 34.9, CARROT 46.8 / 43.0, TOMATO 49.3 / 53.7, STRAWBERRY 81.4 / 84.6, MELON 94.2 /
130.8. Every build at the top of the ladder puts half its tiles on the worst of these, because wheat is the
one channel that **absorbs** (332 units move it 1.40 → 1.43) and the one whose tile comes back in 3.2 days.

## 4. Q4 — `plan.CROP_SCARCE_ON` (built, default OFF, REJECTED)

mix rewrite: one bounded re-share of `macro.plant_target` by the **forward quote over the spot quote**, per
crop — the number the budget computes over the number the head saw:

`inv_h = inv_at_day(mkt_inv, shops, _FIRST_P, day) + _pipeline_units(...)` (town drain + our own pipeline,
the expression `ANIMAL_BUY_FWD_ON` already reads), `ratio = clip(price_table[inv_h] * 64 // spot, 48, 85)`

trains on is not double-counted; the ±33 % clip is the lesson of `PLANT_MIX_DRAIN_ON` (−11,906 at t −15.4
for a raw logit of order 1). **A constant, not a gene**: the layout is frozen and an appended coordinate is
worth its slope only if an ES arm will train it. Effect on the pinned boards: 1-2 tiles of 20 a day.

## 5. CLOSED / what remains

**CLOSED.** The plant-time crop-mix family on all four signals it has — drain share
(`PLANT_MIX_DRAIN`), calendar (`CROP_DAY`), the other seat (`OPP_MIX`/`OPP_SUPPLY`) and now the
**forward price**; the mix already carries a learned dawn-price response and a hard glut veto.
Also closed: "their crop mix is the edge" (+1,020 upper bound, negative on tiles).

**REMAINS, and it is not mix.** Our 186 plantings / 10.4 % idle against their 271 / 5.1 %
(`2026-09-16-mmpq.md` §4) is a **volume** statement, and tiles + hands together already lost −4,835
(`2026-09-16-jointlift.md`). The one thing §2 leaves open is **absorption**, not level: wheat and carrot
persist (r 0.95/0.98) because their curves absorb, strawberry and melon do not because both seats dump into
a `T = 100` / `sq` curve — a claim about *how much of one crop to sell*, and sell-cadence is closed already
(`SPREAD6`, −1,802).
