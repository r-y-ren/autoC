### The V56 kernel breaks the clone rival tapes
The gated body breaks **45 of the 68** base-faithful rival tapes. On those seats the rival's units sold fall **-29 % to -49 %** vs live (one seat -98 %), and its purse ends at 0.65-0.93x live.

- Their tape and our V56 kernel play the same d0 script, down to the same `BUY_SEED WHEAT 1` at d0 h19.
- On **36/45** broken seats the rival's farm first diverges at **step 20 (d0 h19)**. Its cash there is 6-9 coins live and **0** in our game.
- From that point the open-loop tape plants seeds it does not have, and its sales collapse.
- A live rival would re-plan. That makes the +40 flips on these seats a **tape-breakage artefact**, not denial. It is the same failure MELONSWAP1 flagged, only much stronger: PFS broke 92/160 tapes, while V56, a sibling clone, breaks almost every clone tape.

- It sits **far below the top teams**, who win 16 of the 23. Gated is -18.4k margin vs top (t -11.0) and 0 upsets vs 12 top-only wins.
- Bias: faithfulness is decided after the swap, so JC1 drops seats where the arm squeezed the rival. On this bed, though, the dropped seats are dropped by the d0-seed collision itself.
