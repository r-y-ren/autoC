## 2. The binding quantity is the ASK — `macro.plant_target`; the quad is downstream of it

`110480465`. The planner's OWN developable-tile count (dawn free **plus** what today's harvests release)
against its ask: d10 `n_free` 38 / ask 9 · d12 27/1 · d14 30/4 · d16 26/**0** · d18 27/1 · d19 28/2,
purse 3.5k→53k. The dawn "1.1 free tiles" is a *consequence*: the planner sees 26-38 plantable tiles a
day and asks for 0-9. `land_reach` is saturated (`n_eff = 25 = LAND_TILES`) and the purse clears
`LAND_PRICES[2]` from d13, so the 4th quadrant is refused for exactly one reason —
`wants_land − wants_pre = 0` → `land_value = 0` → `buy_land` False (`plan.py:8794`). **A quadrant is
worth nothing because we would not plant on it.** `plant_total = n_dev − Σanimal_want`,
`n_dev = _qfloor(dev_frac·n_free)` (`brain.py:1061-1094`) is the hard cap; `_wants` clips the seed want
to it and `plant_eff` clips the PLANT ops to it.
