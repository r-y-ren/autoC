## 5. Risks
1. **Cost.** The 1-generation smoke ran **309 s/gen** vs flow220_sw's 92 s (52 rungs, whole theta).
   A useful read is days, not hours; abs checkpoints every 10 gens — judge early, do not wait.
2. **52 boards for a 6,636-coordinate search** is the memorisation risk the hold-out exists to
   catch. V45LEG2 falling while ENG22 rises is the arm cheating, not a lever.
3. **Tape fidelity.** TOPLEG retains **0.83** mean, 11/30 below 0.85 — a third of the training
   signal is a mispriced collapse the arm can farm. Read candidates on the retention-gated subset.
4. The seat is **today's** top 10; re-cut TOPLEG every 24–48 h (the NB-INTEL staleness defect).
