# WINRATE — retune ES from mean margin to board wins (recipe only, nothing launched)

`S/winrate/launch_remote.sh`, `MODE=a|b`, a copy of `S/dual234/launch_remote.sh` MODE=ft2. Branch `winrate` off
master `4be2450`. No arg = print; `--stage` once (either mode); `--smoke` = 1 gen into `artifacts/<RUN>_smoke`;
`--go` nohups. Two arms, identical but for GPU and `--margin-scale`; **same `--seed 336`** = CRN across the pair.

## 1. The objective change

GOALAUDIT, 56 gated engine boards: board win **32.1 %**, median **−4,124**, 18 wins averaging **+21,509** against 38
losses at median **−8,356** — and a **mean of +1,243**. Mean margin is therefore *already satisfied*: the blowouts
pay for the tail and ES is never paid to fix the 38 losses. Bradley-Terry pays only for the tail.

The lever is `--margin-scale`: the relative term is `sigmoid((mine − theirs)/margin_scale)` (`train.py:589`), and
`--tape-score` stays at default `margin`, so the 52 tape rungs read that and not `own_coin_score`. Sigmoid slope as
a fraction of its maximum, at GOALAUDIT's two boards:

| scale | loss −8,356 | win +21,509 | release | depth −8.4k→−16.7k | |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 3,000 | 0.22 | 0.003 | 71 : 1 | 0.054 | flow222 — collapsed −18,661 |
| **4,000** | 0.40 | 0.018 | 22 : 1 | 0.104 | **arm b, GPU1** |
| **8,000** | 0.77 | 0.238 | 3.2 : 1 | **0.150** | **arm a, GPU0** |
| 100,000 | 1.00 | 0.989 | 1.0 : 1 | 0.021 | flow235_ft2 — POOLED169 −128 t −1.14 |

**8,000** gives the loss tail 77 % of the gradient there is, releases an already-won board 3.2 : 1, and is the
**argmax of loss-depth resolution** — the one scale at which the objective still knows a bad loss from a worse one.
**4,000** reads the *sharp* side, because ESJUDGE10 has already killed the flat one; with 3,000 and 100,000 measured
the pair puts four points on the axis and spans 7× in release (a ×2 arm at 16,000 would span 1.6×).
Selection is `--select-metric score` = the rung-weighted mean of `sigmoid(margin/margin_scale)` over all 52 pinned
rungs (`train.py:5659,5666`) — at these scales that *is* the block-wide soft win rate. **Not `softwin:<rung>:<tau>`**:
`select_spec` (`train.py:1464-1507`) → `_select_value` (`train.py:5867`) returns `rep.softwin[i]`, **one rung**, and
the only rung names this ladder has are `tape_act_<episode_id>` (`tape_actions.py:96-100`) — no aggregate name
exists, so that metric would be board-selection, not a win rate.

## 2. Why it dodges the flow222 scar (ESFIX), and the n = 60 gate

flow222 was `--margin-scale 3000` **+ `--arch-frac 0.5`**. (a) 3,000 is a win **bit**: a won board reads 0.3 % of
peak slope and the loss tail is *also* over the shoulder — both tails dead, which is why "its fitness saw nothing";
neither arm here has a dead tail. (b) `--arch-frac 0.5` let it raise a saturating bit by beating its past selves —
**0.9 is kept**, 90 % of episodes stay on the 52 fixed ENGINE tapes, archetypes at weight 0. (c) It had no gate big
enough to catch it. ESJUDGE10: a 22-game gate cannot select an effect the pool measures at se ≈ 101 — and those 22
were **11 boards twice**, since with the town pinned both seats return the same coins (`scripts/train.py:2347-2358`).
The gate is now `--real-gate-pinned-seats 1` over `S/winrate/gate_ids.txt` = **60 independent held-out boards**, the
original 11 first so it is a superset of flow235's; se × √(11/60) = **0.43**. 60 is patience, not supply — 517
pinned × packaged × non-training boards exist — and costs 1 MB staged, ~2 min per gate on 8 workers against ~30 min
of training between them. `--real-gate-metric win`, `--real-gate-every 10`.

## 4. JUDGE BAR for any candidate

Two-purse, paired CRN, `S/judgekit/judge.sh <label> <theta>` with `LEGS=abce` = the 28 gated engine boards. Report
**four** numbers, not one: **board-win %**, **median**, **mean**, **d_theirs**.
**PASS** = board win **≥ 38 %** (32.1 + 6 pts) **AND** median lift **> 0** **AND** mean d **≥ 0** with **d_theirs not
significantly up** (MELONGIFT: judge on the pair). A candidate that raises the mean while the median falls is the old
objective wearing a new label — **REJECT**. Then the gift check, **BAND250** (`S/carrotflat3/run_band250.sh` on the
`carrotflat3` worktree; ≥ 250 band boards per sign, ~27 min at 4 workers), must not be negative. Only then §115b
(POOLED180 ≥ +450 **and** t ≥ 3) decides shipping.
