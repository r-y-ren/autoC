## 1. Where our milk shortfall comes from
- **It is not a loss/win discriminator on the bed.** Our own d20-27 milk is *higher* in the losses (7.95k vs 2.59k). The milk price level is a board property: board 14 sells at 122-128 a unit, while boards 19 and 21 sell at 1-7. Live LOSSMAP17 compares different boards, so its "−3.1k milk in losses vs wins" is mostly board price level.
- **Within a game, our milk is short of the rival's by a care-rate gap.** Cow-days are equal (65 vs 67), and the price is not lower (107 vs 100 a unit, unit-weighted). We make fewer units: 77.8 vs 89.5, and 11 of the 11.7 missing units are care bonus (45 vs 56).
  - The V rival CAREs 100 % of its cow-days. We care 73 % in losses and 50 % in wins.
  - The per-day trace (`perday.py`) shows the rule behind this: on milk troughs (quote 7-45 against wheat 40, d20-24) we feed cows on the every-other-day survival cadence and refuse the CARE, because `care_pays` (spot milk > spot wheat) fails. That happens even on a fire night whose feed is already bought for survival. Example, cha22@9: d21 5 cows, 3 fed, 0 cared; the rival had 6/3/6.
- **Ruled out:**
  - Fewer cows: no (equal cow-days, 0 escapes).
  - Unfed cows as such: fed 83 % vs 89 %, a small part of the gap.
  - Sale slots: 0 MILK units ordered past the 10-order cap in any game.
  - Rival selling first: no. The unit lockstep quotes both seats the same price per unit. Per-game averages differ only by sale day (36 vs 52 on herd_saf@9, 104 vs 97 on cha22@20), and over the losses ours is the higher: 107 vs 100 a unit.
  - Milk held unsold: shed MILK at d29 dusk is 0 in every game.
- **The bigger own-side gaps in the losses are WOOL and TOMATO.**
  - Wool −3.3k vs the rival in losses, against +2.1k in wins. On equal sheep-days we make 36 wool vs 69: sheep fed 55 % vs 79 %, and 3 escapes per loss game at wool troughs of 1-11. FEEDKEEP1/FEEDNIGHT1 closed that axis.
  - Tomato: 0.8k in losses vs 2.3k in wins.

## 3. Fix: `CARE_RIDE_ON` (plan.py, OFF = byte-identical)
- **What it changes.** A CARE rides a survival feed. On a `must_feed` tile, the wheat is spent whatever the care does. So when the next fire's eod falls on the every-other-day feed cadence (`(h_next − 1 − day) % 2 == 0`, a cow on its fire night), the care is admitted at the one-coin floor of `v_care`, even when spot milk < spot wheat.
- **What it leaves alone.** Only `want_care` sees the widened mask. `feed_value`, `care_val` and the mandatory tier are unchanged, so it buys no wheat and changes no feed.
- **Tests.** `tests/test_care_ride.py`: 5 passed. OFF digests over the 12 `test_route_early` cases are equal to the pre-switch tree (ef581fd3); `test_route_early.PIN` itself is stale at HEAD, and `test_care_hold`'s identity test fails on the unpatched tree too. The behavioural tests are: OFF refuses the care; ON cares the cow on its fire night; ON skips a care whose fire is off the cadence.
