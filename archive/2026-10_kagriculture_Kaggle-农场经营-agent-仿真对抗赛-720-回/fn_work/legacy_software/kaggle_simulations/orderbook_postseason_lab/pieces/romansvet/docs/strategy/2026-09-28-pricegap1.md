## 3. Why no mix lever pays, and the gaps that need a new switch
- **The judge cannot see the strawberry flood.** Both reacting rivals sell about 64-70 strawberries in d18-29. The live programme sells 147.
  - ctrl: 64.4 u on live27, 65.8 u on m40. g0capsfix: 69.2 u on m40.
  - So in the closed loop, our d18-29 strawberry sells at 151 (m40) or 134 (live27), against 87 live. A strawberry cut can only lose its own units there. The flood it would dodge is not in the judge.
  - **g0capsfix does reproduce the MILK flood.** It sells 127 milk in d18-29 (live 121, ctrl 92). Against it, PFS's d18-29 milk sells at 94 vs 148 (-6.0k), which is most of the 9.9k it takes from PFS's sales.
- **PFS's own levers move the share onto the other flooded crops.** With LATE_STRAW_CAP the freed strawberry share goes to melon and tomato. There is no master switch that sends the share to WHEAT or EGG instead.
  - The explicit redirect levers that do exist (WHEAT_LATE_ASK, the goose floor) are neutral to negative. Their revenue is paid for by the herd, the feed and the milk they displace.
- **Gaps (no switch in master; expected coins are the live PRICE terms they would target: upper bounds from section 1, not measurements):**
  1. **d10-14 output wave / earlier cash crops.** Live prices hold in d10-17: strawberry 176-192 live vs 187-195 closed, melon d10-14 247, wool d15-17 163 vs 161. The flood is d18-29.
     - Target: the d18-29 PRICE loss of the top 3 flooded products, **strawberry 7.4k + melon 3.2k + milk 2.5k = 13.1k per game** at most.
     - This is PROGRAMME1's scope (the programme's d10-14 wave), and TOPMECH1's OWN term, +25.1k d10-17.
  2. **A day-indexed strawberry schedule** (plant d0-5, sell d10-21, and stop, instead of LATE_STRAW_CAP's "move to the brain's other crops").
     - The CROP_DAY genes exist in theta but are untrained, and ES on the shipped genes is closed.
     - Target <= 7.4k (strawberry d18-29 PRICE). It must not hand the share to melon or tomato: sf shows that gives back 4.9k of melon price.
  3. **A crop-share destination switch** ("redistribute to WHEAT/CARROT only").
     - Today LATE_STRAW_CAP, MELON_VETO_FLOOD and OPP_MIX all re-share onto the brain's own choice.
     - Target: the cut's own units. sf lost 45 strawberry units that sold at 151 closed and 87 live. The live value of the cut is at most the melon and tomato price damage it avoids, about 1-5k.
  4. **A judge rival that floods strawberry d18-29** (JUDGERIVAL1 scope). This is the prerequisite for measuring 1-3 closed loop. Without it, every strawberry lever reads as a loss.
