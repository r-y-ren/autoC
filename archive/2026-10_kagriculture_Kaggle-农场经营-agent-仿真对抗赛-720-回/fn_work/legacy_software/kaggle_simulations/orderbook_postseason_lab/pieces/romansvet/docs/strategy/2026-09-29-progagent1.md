## Design (what was built)
- **Template = a real P48 seat's own per-step action rows.** Every farm starts identical and the farm is deterministic except weeds and
  the market, so a positional tape transfers across seeds (the engine clips a SELL to shed stock and refuses an unaffordable buy
  silently). `S/progagent1/mktape.py EP SEAT` extracts row i = the action the seat submitted at step i (replay `steps[i+1][seat].action`)
  from the TOPAUDIT1 cache replay into `src/kagg3/prog/tapes/p48_<ep>_<seat>.json` (720 rows, ~130 KB).
- **Executor** `src/kagg3/prog/agent.py` `ProgAgent(cfg).act(obs)`: for days < handover it returns the tape row for `obs.step` behind
  two observation guards: (1) roster: the hands list is cut / PASS-padded to the live hand count (a refused HIRE shortens the crew);
  (2) the engine's atomic PLANT rule (a turn asking more PLANTs of a crop than seeds held drops EVERY PLANT of that crop): keep the
  first `held`, PASS the rest. Market rows are the template's own, in order (sale rows fund same-step buys, as P48 does: Q2 at d6 h3
  from the h3 wool sale, Q3 at d8 h6 from the h1/h6 milk sales). From the handover day it returns None and the runtime's own planner
  (PFS, the OFF string) plays, rebuilding its day plan at h0 from the live observation.
- **Wiring** (`S/progagent1/patch_rt.py`): `plan.PROG_AGENT_ON = False` (the one switch; True = defaults, int D = handover day D,
  "D:label" = handover D + tape label), `runtime.Runtime.act` calls the executor first when the switch is on; the prog module is
  imported eagerly at runtime import (the harness drops `kagg3` from `sys.modules` mid-game, so a lazy import fails).
- **Not used: the router.** The tape is P48's own crew routing (10.6 units at d9), so nothing is handed to `route_vrp`; the router
  still runs from the handover day inside PFS. No call into `core.plan`'s planner before the handover.
- **V gate** (added 19:00Z after the V56 read): `PROG_AGENT_ON="D:label:vN"`: at dawn d1 (step 24) a rival holding >= N MELON tiles
  hands the game to the planner at once. The V family plants its 12-tile melon plate on d0 (8/8 live V rivals of VBAND1's v21 list:
  12 at d1 h0 and d2 h0; the V56 bank agent 12 at d2 h0 on 20/20), the programme 6 (56/56 real P48 seats at the end of d0), PFS 0.

## Verdict: NONE (no package, no _pin row)
- The executor does what the brief asked for d0-9: the exact P48 timetable on 16/16 boards (gate 1 PASS in 29 min, no planner call).
- Head-to-head vs PFS it wins (+5.5k..+6.7k, 13-14/16 at H 8-11). Against the two faithful rivals it loses the PFS edge: V56 m40
  dmargin -9.9k (18/40 vs 38/40), v21 -6.9k (7/21 vs 13/21), p48c -2.7k (73/80 vs 78/80). Our own coins rise (+1.0k / +4.2k / +4.5k);
  the rival's rise more (+10.9k / +11.1k / +7.2k): the programme build gives away our late milk / wool / melon denial.
- A V-gated deployment (programme opening only against non-V seats) needs a PFS-compatible hand-back at d1; there is none: PFS cannot
  run the programme's zero-cash d0 farm (W 0/40 vs V56). Handing back at d7-9 keeps the full +10-11k rival gain.
- Not run: the Q4-on-d10 / DSM-tail late template (TOPAUDIT3 R1-R3, TOP1WATCH1). Its measured value is +2.0-2.2k/game among
  programme seats; it cannot close a -9.9k V56 hole that is fixed by the d0-6 build, and the late template would replace PFS's late
  game, which is the part that still denies V56.
