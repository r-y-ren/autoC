## 1. What every closed family has in common

**All ~60 closed families are REALLOCATIONS at a fixed factor input.** Each moves output that already exists — in time (`-sellrace`,
`-sellday`, `-planttiming`, `-caretiming`, `-actscan`), in space (`-routeorder`, `-routecut`, `-tilealloc`, `-routeeff`), across products
(`-cropmix`, `-woolprice`) or on a signal (`-planselect`, `-boardselect`, `-slotmirror`, `-rivalrank`, `-townadapt`) — while the crew, the
land and the worked-turn budget stay exactly what the one-day greedy produces. In a shared persistent pot a reallocation is zero-sum before
costs, and every *cut* gifts price: 93 % of MELONGIFT's +15.4k is the quote lift on the eight products we stopped supplying. The three §115b
passes are not reallocations — FERT_TIMING (+2,845), LOT4@17 (+861), SLOTPRIO (+459) each raise the coins extracted from units we already
produce, without cutting supply.

**The axis never read as a composition is the factor input itself: worked crew-turns** — also the only two-purse- positive axis, since by
MELONGIFT's arithmetic cutting our output credits their purse, so adding output debits it. `2026-09-14-turns.md` sized it on B: we work
**5,986 turns/game vs ymg_aq's 6,681, −695 (−10.4 %)**, worth **12,150 coins at our own realised 17.52/worked-turn**, against a −13,722
margin — *the whole top-five gap is inside the crew- turn budget*, with our per-turn economy already level (4.89 turns per unit sold vs
5.03). It splits: **266 turns PASS at hours 0-1** (morning market-row wait), **351 turns of smaller crew**, 86 tail PASS.

**Every single-switch attempt on that axis was rejected for a reason that names another gate in the same chain**: EVE_STOCK freed dawn steps
and they fell into the tail *because `n_adm` is fixed at dawn* (−33); ADMIT_SLACK raised admission with no turns to spend it (ENG22 +357 t
2.59, POOLED180 +41); CREW_PUSH moved 42/300 hire intents and `HIRE_ROW_ON` trimmed them to **2 rows**; JOINTLIFT's +19 tiles were never
watered (value_dropped 514 → 5,810/game, 5,418 in d20-29); FORWARD_ADMIT double- counted because `hire_bias` is trained for today-only
admission. SWITCHVEC's additivity verdict does **not** cover this set: its two 2^5 cubes are five market/route switches, and the labour
switches are either OUT as hard rejects alone (`FORWARD_ADMIT`, `MELON_OPEN`, `MACRO_EXEC`) or sat in the resolution- III PB where two-
factor interactions are aliased [`S/switchvec/factors.md`]. Gate compositions are unmeasured **by construction**.

## 3. Killed here

* **Rolling-horizon / rollout planner** — 20+ h; its ceiling is what SCHEDSEARCH reads for 6-10 h, since a searched schedule *is* a
  lookahead plan with perfect knowledge. Not before #4.
* **Rival classifier / per-class opening** — closed five ways (`-planselect` t ≤ 1.32, `-boardselect` day-0 has zero bits, `-rivaltell-arm`,
  `-slotmirror`, `-rivalrank`); at our rating the rival is one class and plants melon d0 on 110/112 boards.
* **A new ES arm from FT2** — closed on every mask, σ and fitness [`-esjudge9`].
* **Upload lottery as the path to 3,066** — a 20-0 start lands r@20 2,323-2,404 [`-ratingpath` §2]; the plateau follows win rate, ours is 50
  % in the band → 2,600-2,800. It buys landing, not strength.
* **Herd scale for free fertilizer** — ~+26 coins/animal-day for one COLLECT turn vs 33.6 coins/op on the best land [`-woolprice`, `-mmpq`]:
  it spends the scarce turn, i.e. direction #1 at a worse rate.
