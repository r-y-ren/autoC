## 3. Exact pre-emption bound (`bound.py`, engine melon curve of `S/gametheory1/melonorder.py`)
The bound is computed per game from the fill ledger:
- Take our first plate lot (Q units, sold at step s1).
- Put it, ideally, at **d13 h0, queue index 0**. That is earlier than any harvest could make possible: the plate is on the vine at d13 h0.
- Every rival melon unit filled in [d13 h0, s1) is then quoted Q units deeper on the curve (its inventory is inferred from its own fill price).
- Our lot is quoted correspondingly shallower. The move is zero-sum; ignoring the town drain makes it an upper bound.
