### Why land_value is 0: the brain land gene

The brain computes `land_bias = price * qfloor(tanh(head[1] + aux[1]*afford) * 256) / 256`:
- At nquad = 3, head[1] is between -3.92 and -4.50, so the tanh input sits between -4.2 and -9.7. That is saturated, so land_bias = -4,000 exactly on every day d11-29 (`S/gameread2/gene_115820870.md`).
- aux[1] turns negative from d16. Being richer therefore pushes it further down.

The same bias sets brain `land_ok = land_bias > -price` to False. Then:
- The brain sizes development without the new tiles, so the planner's `wants_land == wants_pre` and `land_value = 0`.
- land_value > 0 on exactly the three days land_ok was True: d0 (361), d5 and d10.

**One sentence:** in this game Q4 was blocked by the brain land gene (theta head[1] ~ -4.2 at nquad 3, saturated tanh -> land_bias = -4,000 and land_ok = False -> the planner values the quadrant at 0, and the `land_value + land_bias > 0` test fails). It needed 4,000 coins, against a post-reserve purse of 646-1,839 on d11-15 (cash gates also fail) and 6,643-85,209 from d16 on (cash gates pass; the gene alone blocks). On d10, when they bought, we had 5,242 after hires and needed 6,000 for SW + SE, because our SW came one day later than theirs and we had no d10 melon harvest.

Q4_PROG_ON, Q4_VRP_ON, PLANT_ASK_QUAD and MACRO_EXEC_ON are all False in vrp26. No switch bypasses the gene.
