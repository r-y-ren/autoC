## 1. Rules that set the model (RULES1, official pages 09-25)
- Evaluation: "Games will continue to run for approximately two weeks to continue to reduce uncertainty, especially for new agents. **A final Bradley-Terry tournament will be run on those episodes to produce the final leaderboard.**"
- Evaluation: "only the latest 2 submissions are tracked. The latest 2 submissions are also used for final leaderboard evaluation." / "only your best-scoring bot will be shown". Team = the best of the 2 subs.
- RULES1 §2: "no text describes a rating reset. The live Elo carries on, but it does not decide the final ranking: the BT fit does." Whether pre-deadline episodes enter the fit was asked on the forum on 09-15 and is still **unanswered**.
- **Why BT and not Elo.** The rules name BT. Live Elo has a K floor of 8.9 from game 70 (RATINGPATH2), so it weights recent games and depends on the path. BT is one unweighted MLE over all window games. We fit Elo/400-logistic BT for each sub on its October games, with opponents fixed at their rating. For a settled sub the BT θ equals the live μ within 2 θ (PFS 2,683 vs 2,683). The two diverge only when the window contains a cold-start climb (§5).

## 8. Caveats
- The October field is unseen: 16 of the top-20 re-uploaded on 09-26/27, and final uploads land 09-28..30. The LB top is selected for high draws (winner's curse), and a σ = 27 noise term stands in for it.
- The ≥ 2,800 families rest on 16 labelled teams (TOPLOSS1 plus our games). The LB-vs-GW gap at 2,600-2,700 (MELON 0.58 vs 0.77) is the main model uncertainty, and it is worth ~15 ranks at today's strength.
- Each family has one Elo curve at every rating. MELON θ comes from 51 band seats (SE 55) and V θ from 81 (SE 57). There are no seat, board or town-draw effects. Games between our two subs are ignored.
- The game-rate law is fitted on 09-24..27 upload-heavy traffic and extrapolated to 10-19-day-old subs. The brief's 75-game case brackets it from below.
