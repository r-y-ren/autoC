## 4. What the grid says
1. **The plate already makes the trade.** With the shipped herd ask and an 8-tile plate (c4p8 = D10WAVE1 B8) PFS's own budget buys
   MELON 8 + CARROT 3 + COW 2 + GOOSE 1 and NO sheep at h1 (3 hires, not 4), then MELON 5 + COW 1 on d1 (diag, 3 boards,
   `res_local/diag_c4p8_w0_act.jsonl`): cows at dawn d9 3.1 vs 5.9. The 6-tile plate does the same (cows d9 3.5-3.8). So `H1_COWS=3`
   never binds under a plate: **c3p8 == c4p8 == B8 bit-for-bit (40/40 g0capsfix + 20/20 flood) and c3p6 == c4p6 (40/40 + 20/20)**.
   The "fewer h1 cows for melon tiles" move is what D10WAVE1 B8 and MELONTRIAL1's vrp21_melon already play.
2. **Forcing 2 cows shrinks the plate.** `H1_COWS=2` + plate keeps the h1 sheep and 4 hires, and loses the d1 melon top-up: 7 seeds
   instead of 13, melon tile-days 64 vs 113, d10-14 melons 42 vs 78, dawn-d2 cash 525 idle. Own coins -3.6k (t -4.3), rival +10.9k,
   margin -14.5k (t -10.0) on g0capsfix; same sign on flood / big / live27.
3. **Cutting cows without a plate gifts the rival milk.** c2p0 / c3p0: the freed 400-800 coins sit idle (dawn d2 cash 945 / 540), own
   coins -1.3k / -0.8k, rival +10.3k / +5.0k. The rival's gain is its MILK price: rival milk coins +6.7k on -6 units (c2p0), +7.5k on
   -5 units (B8), +10.0k on +11 units (c2p8), plus eggs/fertilizer +1.5k/+1.7k. Our h1 cows hold the shared milk price down for the whole
   game; every cow we drop is paid by the rival's price, not by our own coins.
4. Every cell raises the rival (+3.1k .. +17.8k on every read) and lowers the margin (every read, t -0.84 .. -15.3; the closest cell c2p8 on big
   is -2.0k, t -0.84, but -10.7k on flood, -14.5k on g0capsfix and -12.8k on live27).
