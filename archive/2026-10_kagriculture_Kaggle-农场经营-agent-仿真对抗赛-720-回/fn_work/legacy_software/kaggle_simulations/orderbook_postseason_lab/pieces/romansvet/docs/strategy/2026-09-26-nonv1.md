## Verdict
**E1 passes the bar as written.** Net +2 (orig), gift-free with Δtheirs t −3.35, and the tell never fires on V56 dev 0-99. I do **not** recommend ship legs, for three reasons:
- Its mechanism is absent: hands d2-9 +0.05.
- Its flips are the −29 coin-flip board plus the board every perturbation flips.
- Its Δours is negative (−837). The margin comes from Δtheirs on open-loop tapes, which cannot react the way a live bot would.

Every other cell is at +1 or below. The early-crew, Q3-d9 and melon-plate recipes cannot be reached from a d2 latch: they are d0-1 opening decisions, while ours are fixed until the latch. The next step, if any, is a d1 latch plus a gated d1 opening. The V opening holds 12 melon from d1 (ENGGATE1).
