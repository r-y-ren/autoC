## 3. Verdict: NO SHIP; the recipe does not port as specified
- No cell reaches net ≥ +2 on dev 0-49. The best is the relay alone (g/g21/g0) at −1.
- So dev 50-99, held-out100 and FRESH300 were not run, and neither were the EMPTY1 extension cells (they were conditional on a dev pass).
- The switches stay OFF and there is no package.
- The recipe's parts fail for three separate reasons:
  - Q3 by d8 is paid for with our herd (a milk/wool gift).
  - "No late melon" removes our only melon.
  - The wheat relay does deny the rival's wheat (−1.1..−4.9k), but on our farm it is paid for with hires and displaced carrot, straw and tomato.
- Of the four teams' levers, the only one left open is the volume of the free-tile wheat relay itself. At about 22 relay plantings per game it nets 0.
  - More volume needs more free tiles, and freeing melon tiles costs 11.9k.
