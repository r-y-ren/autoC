# Q4RELAY1: low-labour Q4 wheat relay with its own hands, priced by an ROI gate (CLOSED)

**Question (USER 09-26).** Can we make a 4th quadrant pay the way the rank-7 team does (FOURTHQ1)? Their Q4 is a dense wheat relay with little labour: no fertiliser, survival water only, and a crew of about 12. The relay adds wheat plantings AND the hands they need together. Each addition must pay ROI × (Fibonacci hire bill + seed) at today's wheat quote. Q4VRP1 tried hands without plantings, and Q4PROG1 tried plantings without hands. Both lost.

**Switch family.** `Q4_RELAY_ON` and `Q4_RELAY_{BUY_DAY,ROI,WATER,FERT,SCHED,HOLD,...}` in `src/kagg3/core/plan.py`. Default is OFF, and OFF is byte-identical (tests/test_q4_relay.py and tests/test_route_vrp_opt.py pass, 14 tests). The planner's asks stay off Q4. The relay buys Q4 in the window d10-13 when the purse covers it and a relay size T<=25 passes the gate. It then plants wheat each dawn and pays for the extra hands that day.

**Why it is closed.**
1. **The damage grows with the relay's size, and no relay size is clean.** Lowering the gate or removing it adds relay plantings (0 → 1.3 → 4.4 → 8.7 plant-days per game) and loses more flips (−1, −7, −17, −41 per 100). At the default gate the relay is inert: it bought on 1 of 50 boards and planted 0.
2. **Hands cost more than the Q4 units earn.** At our crew size (h ≈ 8-10 by d11), each extra relay hand bills at Fibonacci 89-377 per day. FQ gets 180 Q4 units per game (101 wheat), about 3-4k gross at a wheat quote of ~20-35. It pays +6.5k in extra hires, and Δours is −6.5k. Their crew of 12 pays because they carry no other labour. On our farm the relay hands are added on top of the planner's crew.
3. **No denial.** Δtheirs is never negative with t < −1 (−113 to +443). The expected signature, a falling rival wheat quote and purse, does not appear against the reacting V56. Our extra wheat does not take their sales. It only costs us hires.
4. This matches Q4VRP1 (hands) and Q4PROG1 (plantings). Coupling the two does not change the sign. On this farm and crew, Q4 is limited by labour cost. It is not limited by cash, and it is not limited by the missing coupling.

**Verdict: CLOSED (5th Q4 study).** No cell reaches the dev bar (net ≥ +2), so held-out100 and FRESH300 were not run. The switch stays OFF and there is no package. Any follow-up must level the crew instead of adding hands on top of it (a level crew cap plus the buy day; the coordinator has sent this to a new agent).
