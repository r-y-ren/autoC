## 1. Engine rule (kaggle_environments/envs/kaggriculture/kaggriculture.py)
- `_process_market` l.602-603: a BUY_SEED unit is quoted at `CROPS[item]["seed"]` (WHEAT 10, CARROT 20, TOMATO 50,
  MELON 80, STRAWBERRY 100) — a CONSTANT. It never reads `market["inventory"]`.
- `_commit_unit` l.673-678: pay the constant, `private["seeds"][item] += 1`. The market book is NOT touched (unlike
  BUY_PRODUCT l.671 / SELL l.660), so seed buys cannot move any price, ours or the rival's, within or across calls/draws.
- Lot failure: an order stops at the first unit the purse cannot pay (l.674, l.623). Seeds persist across days (no reset).
- Step order: unit actions (PLANT consumes seed, l.912-934) run BEFORE `_process_market` (l.941), so a seed bought on step t
  is plantable from step t+1 whatever the lot size.
- **Verdict: a split buy can never be cheaper than one bulk call.** Price per unit is identical; splitting only uses market
  order slots (cap 10/turn) and defers cash. The only non-price effect is purse timing (cash held a few hours longer),
  which matters only when the purse binds on other same-day orders — and seeds have no carrying cost, so a bulk dawn buy
  loses nothing except when the purse is short (then DSM's own late orders simply fail, see below).
