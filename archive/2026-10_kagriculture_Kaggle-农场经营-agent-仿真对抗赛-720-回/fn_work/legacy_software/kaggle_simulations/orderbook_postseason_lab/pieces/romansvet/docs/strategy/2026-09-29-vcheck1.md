- The V56 agent sells a fixed late book (208 strawberries d18-29 in every cell, 72 melons, fertilizer 270u), so every coin it gains is price.
- PFS sells about 50 strawberries on d15-17, ahead of the V's d18-29 wave. That drains the curve: the V gets @89 (v21) / @71 (m40).
- vrp21_clsearch sells only 21 of them on d15-17 and moves them into d18-29. The V's 208 late strawberries then sell @135 / @106:
  rival strawberry +9.9k (t 9.8) on v21 and +7.7k (t 10.0) on m40. Our fertilizer volume (+54u / +44u) takes -2.0k / -1.7k off the V's
  fertilizer book, but that pays back only a fraction.
- Our own books rise: eggs +1.9k / +2.3k, fertilizer +1.4k / +1.0k, strawberry +2.5k / +1.3k (fewer units at better prices).
  Milk and wool barely move (-0.7k..+0.7k). The d9 herd is larger (C/S/G 7.0/4.3/3.3 vs 5.8/3.8/2.0 on v21).
- bs2lp keeps about 30 strawberries on d15-17 (vs 21 and 51), so the V's late price rises only to @109 / @85: rival strawberry +4.3k / +3.2k.
  That is why it passes the W bar where clsearch fails. Our egg gain (+1.0k / +1.6k) is smaller, and the margin is still negative.
- This matches VBAND1's rule ("our volume is denial"): against V, any d1-9 change that delays our first strawberry lot hands the V its late price.
