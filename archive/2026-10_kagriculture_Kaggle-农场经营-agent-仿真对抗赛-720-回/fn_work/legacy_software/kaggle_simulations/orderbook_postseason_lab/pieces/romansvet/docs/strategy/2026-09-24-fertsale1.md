## 3. Mechanism (plan.py)
- Apply-vs-sell is one bar: `fert_cand = is_plant & (t_fert < day) & (fert_val > fert_bar)` (`src/kagg3/core/plan.py:10237`),
  `fert_val = VAL.fert_marginal_value(...)` (plan.py:10207; valuation.py:127) = exact clipped marginal units x today's price;
  `fert_bar` = today's fertilizer quote (plan.py:10219) — a unit is applied only if the crop it buys beats its sale price.
  Every unit not applied is sold via `SELL.allocate` (no reservation; FERT_RESERVE_ON/FERT_FLOOR_ON OFF, plan.py:6311/6362).
- Knobs: `FERT_VOLUME_ON` (plan.py:6581, multiplies the bar; KNOBV56 dev −390 t −3.6 net 0), `FERT_DUMP_ON/DAY/BONUS`
  (plan.py:6643-6652, adds a denial bonus to the bar from DAY = sell instead of apply; FERTDENIAL 09-16 REJECT vs ENG22/V45LEG),
  `FERT_TIMING_ON` (shipped ON, plan.py:6691), `PROGRAM_FERT_SELL` (plan.py:1569, programme only), CLIP_FERT_SKIP_ON (:776).
- Hypothesis: we do not leak fertilizer (0 unsold, 0 overflow, same collection); we APPLY ~80 units they sell, because the
  bar says the crop is worth more than the quote. Selling them instead lowers the shared quote for both (denial +, not a
  gift) but costs our own crop. Whether that trade is net positive vs the reacting V56 is what the grid measures.
