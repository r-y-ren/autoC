# SELLSTEP1 (2026-09-29 18:17Z-18:40Z): same-day lot splitting of late sales - verdict NONE (mechanism step, no build)

**Lead (TOPAUDIT3 §3).** PFS sells strawberry d14-29 in 17 steps of 12.8 units (62 % at h12-17). The top bodies sell 3-4 units per step, and their late strawberry price on the same boards is 104 against our 89. Question: does splitting one day's sale into smaller lots across the same day's steps earn more per unit?

**Mechanism (exact).**
- The engine (`_process_market`, verified in the vendored kaggriculture.py) quotes every unit at the current market inventory. `sim/market.py` transcribes this bit-exactly.
- Within one hour the orders run slot by slot in lockstep, and no drain happens between slots. The town drains only in `town_consume`, which runs at `step % 4 == 0` after the market: shops every 4 hours, the centre once a day.
- So within an hour, the revenue of N solo units is the same however they are split: the walk is path-independent.
- Across hours, a split gains only the drain ticks that fall between lots, which is less than delaying the whole lump to the last lot's hour.
- If the rival sells in the same hour, it can only cut in ahead of our later lots.

**Measurement.** `S/sellstep1/mech.py`, `mech_sum.py` -> `res/mech_events.tsv`, `res/mech.md`, `res/drain.tsv`.

**Verdict: NONE (below the 3 coins/unit strawberry bar; no build, no judge).**
- Strawberry, same-hour split: +0.00 coins/unit (an exact identity).
- Strawberry, hourly lots of 3/4/6: +1.29/+1.02/+0.70 coins/unit. In h12-17, where 62 % of our strawberry sells, the gain is +0.12/+0.10/+0.01.
- All of that is partial delay: the whole lump sold at the last lot's hour gets +2.36/+1.93/+1.39.
- Against a rival that sells 10 units right after our first lot, splitting costs us 6-7 coins/unit and pays the rival +11..+15 coins/unit.
- Wool's hourly-lot gain of +4.2 coins/unit is also a delay effect. With the rival cutting in it turns into -12.2 coins/unit for us and +19.5 for the rival, which breaks the two-purse rule.

**What this says about the 104-vs-89 gap.** Within a day the lot size does not set the price. The 3-4 units per step of the top bodies is a harvest-as-you-go cadence, not a pricing lever. The price gap comes from the inventory level each seat sells into (which seat sells first after each drain tick, and total supply). Over our 30 newest games it is already nil: 89 vs 86. Lot size is a dead axis for PFS.
