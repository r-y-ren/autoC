flipped **9/25** remaining training losses and **13/41** cumulatively, but the
state-only classifier did not transfer that oracle signal to complete held-out

selection rule includes the incumbent as the safe checkpoint, so a failed
distillation cannot silently replace it.

## Distillation data and loss

`live_expert_head.py` records only the existing 64-value head feature vector,
the incumbent head action, and the final program action at each dawn.  It does
not add an episode id, seed, game index, or other lookup feature.  Exact-engine
replay reproduced every incumbent win purse and every expert-program final
purse before accepting a trace.

| population | states | slot labels | coefficient |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| committed expert cells | 1,230 expert dawns | 153 | 1.0 |
| other expert-program cells | 1,230 expert dawns | 21,987 | 0.1 |
| incumbent actions on 109 wins | 3,270 dawns | 58,860 | 1.0 |

The objective was `mean CE(overrides) + 0.1 mean CE(other expert) + mean
CE(win rehearsal)`.  Each population was independently sampled into a
256-row balanced minibatch and independently normalised.  Training initialized
all weights from `head_940`, used Adam at 1e-3, and ran 100 epochs.
