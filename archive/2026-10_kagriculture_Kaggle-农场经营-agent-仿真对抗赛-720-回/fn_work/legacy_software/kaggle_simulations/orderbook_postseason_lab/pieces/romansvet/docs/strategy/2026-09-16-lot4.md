## 4. WHAT THE ROW ACTUALLY BOUGHT — a price, and the opponent a bigger one

Band, our seat: volume is flat (−0.10 units a game-seat, t −0.44) and the
whole-book realised price rises **91.45 → 91.70 (+0.25/unit)**. That is the
lot-depth effect LOT-DEPTH looked for, delivered: 1,404 units × 0.25 ≈ +350,
which is the +308 the ledger shows.

And it is smaller than the externality it creates. Their whole-book price rises
**84.22 → 84.95** on the band (theirs +1,139, t +9.05) and **67.96 → 68.34** on
ymg (+956, t +5.47) on **identical volume** — 1,562.3 and 2,501.2 units, the
same to the tenth. Selling our dawn shed three turns later leaves that much more
on the shared shelf through hours 19-22, and the action-replay seat, which
trades on all 24 hours, is the one that meets it. On LIVE-C — the subset whose
tapes trade latest — the effect is worth +1,757 to them and −132 to us.

This is the shape ROUTE-FREEFIRST §3 and SHED-DEFICIT §2 both ended on, now
with the largest coefficient yet: **a change in OUR market timing moves the tape
opponent's purse by 3.7x what it moves ours.**

## 5. THE COST, priced

Each extra resolved market turn is ~6 % of simulator throughput
(`ops.SELL_TURNS`' own measured comment). **These legs paid none of it** — the
band tapes present a market row on all 24 hours, so `rollout` already resolves
every turn when a tape is in the seat (`sim/rollout.py:395-410`) and the fourth
row only fills slots on a turn that was resolved anyway. The 6 % is the price a
**training** run would pay, where only our own market turns are resolved. So the
promotion bar for this switch was never +450: it was +450 *plus* 6 % of every
future ES generation. It delivers +308 on our purse and −831 on the margin.

**So the 21.6 units a game-seat are not recoverable from the SELL side at
all.** Both doors (the forced sale's size, and the number of rows) are closed.
Anything that reaches them has to stop the units existing or stop them being
stranded: SHED-DEFICIT §5's second item — **do not RESERVE what the route will
never reach** (`want_feed` / `n_fert_eff`, `plan.py:7509-7519`) — is the only
untouched lever in the family, and it is an admission/reservation question, not
a sale one.

**One live read to keep, already half-measured.** The price channel is real and
it is mis-signed for us: a three-turn delay is worth +0.25/unit to us and
+0.73/unit to them on frozen volume. §2b ran the opposite direction and the
whole ledger flips sign — their coins −83 (t −3.37), margin +112 (t +2.96) — so
our sell timing IS a lever on their purse, and the useful direction is EARLIER.
But +112 on the band is a quarter of the bar, and `EARLY_SELL_MODE` already owns
that axis with three unmeasured modes ("B", "A21", "Z"). **If anyone re-opens
the sell clock, open it there, not with a fourth row**: the row costs 6 % of
training throughput to buy the same 0.1 % of purse the mode switches move for
free.
