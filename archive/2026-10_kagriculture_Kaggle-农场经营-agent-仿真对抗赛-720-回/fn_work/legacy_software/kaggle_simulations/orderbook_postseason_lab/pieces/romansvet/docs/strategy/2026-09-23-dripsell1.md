## 5. Verdict: NO SHIP, flag OFF, CLOSED
The drip is a pure price loss for us: same units, sold into the book before the later draws have thinned it. The draw
consumes the same units whether ours arrived at turn 5 or 17, so splitting cannot "meet a freshly thinned book" more often —
turn 17 (after the most draws since the dawn lot) is the best quote the day offers, as V15LOSS1's single-lot sweep found.
DSM's drip fits its own routine (no dusk lot, stock arriving through the day); for ours it only moves units earlier.
