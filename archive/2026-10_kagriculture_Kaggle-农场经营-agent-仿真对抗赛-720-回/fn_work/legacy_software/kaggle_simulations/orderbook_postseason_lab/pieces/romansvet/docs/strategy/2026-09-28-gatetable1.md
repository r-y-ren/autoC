## Verdict
- **The evidence gate is a 4-value whitelist: fire V56 only when the rival's h1 cash is 33, 55, 2,339 or 2,467** (rule E4/+1: faithful n >= 4 and faithful
  net flips >= +1). It fires on **10.4 % of our live games** and is worth **+1.7 net flips per 100 live games** (bootstrap 10-90 % **+0.9 to +2.6**; split-half
  cross-validated +1.1 [+0.5, +1.8]). On the full live field (V56WEAK1 footing, including top-team games) it is +2.1 [+1.0, +3.5] at 17.4 % fired.
- **The candidate R0 (cash <= 2,550) is the worst rule on the evidence**: +0.7 [-0.5, +1.9] per 100 of our games with 6.7 % of games fired on vectors
  that have NO faithful measurement; with those unmeasured vectors at the reacting-rival rate (-0.25/seat) it is **-1.0**.
- R1/R2 (ZERO_CASH exclusions) look better in-sample (+2.3 [+1.3, +3.4]) only because unmeasured and n = 1-3 vectors count as 0 or as their lucky
  single seats; with unmeasured vectors at the reacting rate they drop to **+0.6**. The evidence whitelist keeps **+1.7** under both footings (it fires on no
  unmeasured vector).
- **The existing switches cannot express the whitelist** (`KERNEL2_CASH_MAX` + the `KERNEL2_ZERO_CASH` exclusion list; no whitelist form exists on
  zerogate1 / pfsh0merge1 / ship_vrp13_v56gate). A new switch is specified in §3.
- **Unseen vector (a new bot) default: do NOT fire (PFS).** Every reacting-rival measurement we have is negative (pool+self n28: +0/-7, -0.25/seat), the
  fresh TOP46 hold-out is -0.10/seat, and everything outside the whitelist is -0.11/seat (n83). Only rare band-tape vectors are positive (+0.17/seat, n36).
- **Robustness caveat: the whole +1.7 is tape evidence** (0 reacting seats on any whitelisted vector), and +0.9 of it (cash 33 and 55) rests on band-276
  alone. The gain is small: about 1.7 games per 100, i.e. far from the top-5 lever (TOPV56-1).

## 4. Recommended switch (for the build agent; NOT implemented)
The existing knobs cannot say "fire only on these values": `KERNEL2_CASH_MAX` is an upper bound and `KERNEL2_ZERO_CASH` is an exclusion list (runtime.py
~668: v56 iff cash <= CASH_MAX and cash not in ZERO_CASH). Expressing the whitelist through them would need ~2,460 excluded values.

**Spec: `KERNEL2_FIRE_CASH = ""` (str, default empty = OFF, legacy CASH_MAX/ZERO_CASH behaviour byte-identical).** When non-empty it is a
`|`-separated list of exact rival h1 cash values (same parsing as ZERO_CASH; `|` because the gene block is comma-split). At the step-1 latch in
`runtime.py`, if FIRE_CASH is non-empty the kernel is `v56` iff int(rival cash at our step 1) is in the list, else `pfs`; CASH_MAX and ZERO_CASH are
ignored while FIRE_CASH is set. Every other value (including a never-seen bot) goes to PFS. **Recommended value: `KERNEL2_FIRE_CASH=33|55|2339|2467`**,
together with `KERNEL2_ON=True` and `KERNEL2_PFS_H0MERGE=True` (the whitelist leaves ~90 % of games to PFS, so the idle-hour shift must be off:
TOPRIVAL1 measured the shifted PFS at -3 on 62 faithful unfired V seats, which alone would cost ~-4/100 and wipe out the +1.7). Gene block:
`KERNEL2_ON=True,KERNEL2_PFS_H0MERGE=True,KERNEL2_FIRE_CASH=33|55|2339|2467`. Conservative variant (E6/+2): `55|2339|2467`.
