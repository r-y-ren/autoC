## 4. Verdict
- **Diagnosis.** 82 % of this game's empty tile-days (84 of 103) come from the planner's share rule. The dawn ask is
  `qfloor(dev_frac · n_free)`. Late in the season dev_frac falls to 0.25-0.6 as n_free rises, which gives 67 tile-days on d21-27.
  The floor rounding leaves one tile every day, which gives 17 tile-days on d3-20. Placement by `DIST_SHED` rank makes the
  leftover tiles the far corners. Seeds, cash, labour admission, VRP drops and water cause 0-1 tile-days. The opponent had 1.6 % empty.
  On V56 dev our empty share is 6.8 % against V56's 3.8 %, so we are already inside the ≤ 10 % target. The gap is d20-27 (11.1 %).
- **The fill works mechanically.** rl takes empties from 6.8 % to 2.5 % (d20-27 from 11.1 % to 1.7 %), which is below V56. It does not pay.
  Each late fill buys about 11.5 hires a game (+1.3k bill), and the extra work displaces about 0.9k of other product sales, because the crew is the
  binding resource on d20-27 (LABOUR1: the added plantings do not ride the idle PASS turns). The revenue from filling (+1.0-1.2k)
  is roughly equal to the hires and seeds it costs. Rounding-only fills (r1/r2) are small, +0.1-0.2k own, and they are -2 on flips.
- **NO SHIP. No cell reaches net ≥ +1 on dev 0-49**, so no held, FRESH, tapes or faithful legs were run and no vrp8 was
  packaged. Every switch is OFF on `gapfix1`, and OFF is byte-identical (master digest test).
- Closed: filling the empty tiles by asking for them, at any dose (1-2 tile rounding, full late fill, carrot or wheat, d20-26 or d23-26).
  Together with LABOUR1's (e) floor 0.8 (-1), this closes the ask side of the gap. The opponent's full farm is paid for by a crew that does
  more work per hand (DSM/FQ 0-1 PASS/day against our 25-28), not by the ask. Any remaining lever is crew packing (RRDEPTH1, which is
  bound by the clock). It is not the planner.
