3. **Q3 — No intrinsic diversification bonus under the stated pooled-team Bradley–Terry scoring.**

   - With identical opponent/seat exposure and upload share \(\alpha\), pair win probability is
     \[
     p_{\rm pair}(x)=\alpha p_A(x)+(1-\alpha)p_B(x).
     \]
     The scorer receives every win and loss; it does not select whichever upload would win each matchup.
   - At **50:50** exposure, replacing one A with B captures **half B’s aggregate win-rate advantage over A**. Disjoint winning seats do not add a bonus. Under fixed rival strengths and common exposure, the mixture’s expected fitted strength lies between the two homogeneous endpoints.
