## Verdict
1. **Rivals.** All 11 losses are to plain V-family engines. There are 11 distinct submissions from 10 teams, with no hybrid and no stronger version among them.
   - Every one of them plays the same opening, the same one we beat 34 times:
     - h1 row: COW 2 + SHEEP 2 + HIRE x5.
     - 12 melon seeds on d0, and the d2 signature is 12m/7-8w.
     - Q2 on d6, Q3 on d11.
     - The d10-11 melon wave: 72 melons at 242 = 17.5k. This is identical in all 45 games vs rivals >= 2,340.
     - A fixed late book of about 240 strawberries, 150-175 milk and about 115 wool in d15-29.
   - They differ only in the h0 wheat trade, which sets the h1 cash: 2867 x4, 2630 x2, and one each of 2854 / 2864 / 2802 / 2911 / 2869.
   - Our record against the main keys at >= 2,340: 2867 11-4, 2854 12-1, 2630 1-2. Rival rating: LV 2,416 vs WV 2,409 (t +0.39).
2. **Ledger.** Our income is HIGHER in the V losses than in the V wins: final 110.4k vs 100.6k (+9.9k, t +1.77).
   - The rival's is higher by twice as much: 113.7k vs 94.0k (+19.7k, t +3.44).
   - Almost all of it is d15-29: rival +18.2k (t +3.43), ours +9.1k.
   - In the losses the town drew strawberry/milk/wool shops: shop demand d15-29 829 vs 665 units (t +3.08), PET_CAFE by d18 0.45 vs 1.21.
   - Both late books re-price upward, the rival's more. Rival strawberry 240 u at 125 vs 235 at 85, milk 174 at 100 vs 153 at 76, wool 115 at 134 vs 112 at 109.
   - Our strawberry/milk gain matches the rival's (+10.7k / +5.9k vs +9.9k / +5.7k).
   - Where the margin goes (d15-29): wool -4.2k, carrot -2.5k, tomato -2.3k, melon -1.5k, wheat -0.9k.
   - The d10-14 window is the same structural hole in wins and losses: margin -21.5k vs -20.1k.
3. **Mechanism: the same d10-14 melon wave as MELON, but that is a constant.** What separates V losses from V wins is the town draw, with the OPPOSITE sign to MELON.
   - V margin vs S+M+W demand: r = -0.59 (n 45). MELON: r = +0.22 (n 35). MELON losses sit in POOR towns (716 vs 870).
   - Record by S+M+W-demand tercile: 14-1 / 12-3 / 8-7. 7 of the 11 losses are in the top third, 1 in the bottom.
   - Not the cause: a bigger herd (rival d18 cows/sheep/geese 8.1/6.3/2.8 vs 7.5/7.0/2.9 in wins), a Q4 (2/11 losses vs 6/34 wins, all on d18), or a wheat engine (the relay rivals are the weak ones: 13-1).
   - Not a timeout or error either: 720/720 steps in all 115 games, 0 non-ACTIVE statuses, our remainingOverageTime never below 59.80 of 60 s, ledger valid on both seats.
   - The losses are close: median -2.0k, 7 of 11 within -2.7k. **Board luck (the shop draw) sitting on a constant -20k d10-14 deficit.**
4. **Lever.** None new. The only draw-coupled lines we steer are our wool/carrot/tomato mix.
   - Wool is the biggest line (-4.2k). Our bound is <= ~2.1k per LV game: we sold 11.7 fewer wool than in the wins (99.5 vs 111) and got 6/u less than the rival.
   - That would flip at most 5/11, and only if it came free.
   - It was already tested, all against the programme clone, and every bet was a transfer to the rival:
     - RISKWIN1 (docs/strategy/2026-09-29-riskwin1.md): wool hold hpw margin -2.2k; sheep bet sb33 rival +14.9k; var25p +9.7k; draw-react tomato drt12 own -2.2k.
     - HERD1: s2 (+2 sheep) -15.5k.
   - The draw is only half-visible by d12: with >= 6 S+M+W consumer shops at d12 the record is 4-4, with <= 5 it is 30-7.
   - The structural d10-14 melon hole is the MELON lever, closed by D10WAVE1 / MELONTRIAL1 / MELONSHIFT1 / COMBO1 / PLATENOLAND1. **CANDIDATE: none.**

## Data and method
- **Games.** All 115 vrp20 (56652418) games from the CREWLOSS1 ListEpisodes JSON (`S/crewloss1/logs/eps_56652418_now.json`, 73-42, to 08:39Z).
  - Replays came from the local cache: 43 in `S/livewatch23/gz`, 72 in `S/crewloss1/gz`. None was missing, so there was no Kaggle call.
  - Families use lw23 `fam()` on the rival's d2 dawn crop tiles (V = 11-13 melon + 6-9 wheat).
  - V games: **11 losses (LV, all vs >= 2,340), 34 wins vs >= 2,340 (WV), 21 wins vs < 2,340**, of which the 20 most recent form WVlo.
  - vrp20 vs V overall: 55-11. Below 2,340: 21-0.
- **Ledger.** The 88 games vs >= 2,340 use the CREWLOSS1 engine-replayed ECONCENSUS ledger (`S/crewloss1/res/ledger.json`, both seats, valid 88/88).
  - The 21 low V games were measured here the same way: `measure_low.py` -> `res/ledger_low.json`, ledger_valid 21/21, 0 cash mismatches.
  - Window cash is exact from the purse: d0-9 = dawn d10 - 3,000, d10-14 = dawn d15 - dawn d10, d15-29 = reward - dawn d15.
  - `decomp.py` splits each window into the net revenue of each product (revenue minus that product's market buys) and the seed/animal/hire/land spend.
  - The lines sum exactly to the purse delta in every window.
- **Rival identity and draw.** `extract.py` reads each replay once and writes `res/light.json`:
  - h1 cash, the h0/h1/h2 market rows, d0-1 orders, dawn snapshots per day (money, quadrants, hands, animals, crop tiles), shop unlock days, dawn quotes, our overage and statuses.
  - Shop demand is from the engine SHOP table (spec.py): each unlocked instance eats 1 unit (2 for single-product shops) per product every 4 steps, and the town centre eats 1/day.
- Tables: `res/agg.md` (per-game rival table, version keys, window ledgers, programme shape, town draw), `res/draw.md` (tercile / relay splits),
  `res/decomp.md` (line decomposition), `res/stats.md` (LV per-game windows, t-tests, d12 visibility, MELON contrast, flip bound), `res/per_game.tsv`.

## 4. Lever, bound, and what already tested it
- **The one line a body could own is wool (-4.2k of the -9.1k d15-29 margin gap).**
  - Our wool shortfall in LV is 11.7 u (99.5 vs 111) plus 6/u under the rival's price. That is <= ~2.1k per LV game gross, before the animal cost, the cows it displaces (our milk line is the one that DID keep pace, +5.9k) and the rival's price response.
  - A uniform +2k flips 5/11 (and puts 3/34 wins within reach of the reverse); +3k flips 7/11.
- **Tested and closed, all against the programme clone, not the V rival:**
  - RISKWIN1 (`docs/strategy/2026-09-29-riskwin1.md`): hpw wool hold margin -2.2k (t -2.61); sb33 sheep bet rival +14.9k (W 38 -> 26 of 40); var25p rival +9.7k; drt12 draw-react tomato own -2.2k. CANDIDATE NONE, because a draw bet in a shared market is a transfer.
  - HERD1 (`docs/strategy/2026-09-29-herd1.md`): s2 (+2 sheep by d9) -15.5k.
  - Carrot/tomato: our carrot plantings already fall with the draw (36 vs 54), and ENDGAME_TOMATO (drt12) crashes our tomato hinge.
- **Timing.** A conditional lever could fire by d12 at the earliest.
  - With >= 6 S+M+W consumer shops at d12 the record is 4-4; with <= 5 it is 30-7. By d18 (8 consumers) it is 1-3.
  - RISKWIN1 section 2: a bet must swing ~10k by d18-21 to flip a typical loss.
- **The constant hole is the d10-14 melon wave (-20k in every V game).** It is the MELON lever: D10WAVE1, MELONTRIAL1 (+12..18k to the rival), MELONSHIFT1, COMBO1, PLATENOLAND1, all closed.
- **CANDIDATE: none. PROMISING: none.** The V losses are shop-draw luck on top of a structural -20k d10-14 deficit that PFS repays in poor and mid towns.
