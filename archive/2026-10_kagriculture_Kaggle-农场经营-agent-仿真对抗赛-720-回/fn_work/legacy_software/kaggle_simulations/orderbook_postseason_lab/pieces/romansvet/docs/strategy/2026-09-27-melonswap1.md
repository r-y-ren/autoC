## Reading
1. **Our own purse is close to the top-10 bodies on their boards.**
   - Δours is +3.3k on faithful seats, and the mean own purse is 106.4k (top) vs 103.2k (PFS).
   - The +20k/game in BUILDREVIEW1 §9 cannot come from out-producing the rival. The best MELON body out-produces PFS by ~3k.
2. **The margin they have and we lack is denial, and it is timing, not volume.**
   - Melon: the top body sells its 72 melon units on d10-19, the same window as the rival's plate. That takes 2.7k from the rival's melon.
   - PFS sells 78 melon units on d20-29, after the rival's plate has cleared. It earns the same melon money but denies nothing.
   - Wool, milk and fert: the top body's sales in d10-29 overlap the rival's lines and take a further 9.5k.
3. **The wheat pump explains the unit gap.** Book wheat is bought and resold: product spend is +20.0k against wheat revenue +20.1k. Its own-purse net is 0.
   - Its effect on the rival's wheat is −2.2k (d20-29).
   - A volume arm that copies the unit count without the denial timing buys nothing. This matches RELAYFILL1, CREWRELAY1 and TOMATOFILL1.
4. **How far this transfers.**
   - These boards are top-10 vs top-10 (rival median 2,938). Our band losses are to MELON rivals at about 2,670.
   - The rivals are tapes. A live rival reacts, and denial against a reacting rival was a gift in every prior probe (MELONVRP1, P10, HERDVRP1).
   - So +14.6k is an upper bound on a body's headroom in our seat.

**Answer:** the reachable headroom in our seat is **≈ +15k margin/game at most (upper bound), of which ≈ +3k is own purse and ≈ 11k is the rival's purse.**
- The mechanism is same-window selling into the rival's books: melon d10-19, wool/milk/fert d10-29 and wheat d20-29.
- There is no own-volume +20k. The MELON-cell gap is a denial problem. A counter must move our melon and animal-product sale timing into the rival's d10-19 window without the plate gift that the P10/MELONVRP1 probes paid.
