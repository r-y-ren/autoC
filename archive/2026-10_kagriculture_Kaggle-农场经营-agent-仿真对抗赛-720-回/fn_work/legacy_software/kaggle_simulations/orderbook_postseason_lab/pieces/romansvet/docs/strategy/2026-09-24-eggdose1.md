## 1. Bug (two layers) and fix
1. As DSMGAP2 found: with `GEESE_TARGET > 0` the goose lane in `_wants` was `max(a_want - a_have, 0)` from GEESE_DAY0, i.e. the
   WHOLE decoded goose want skipped the `acquire_ok` value gate.
2. Deeper (new): `animal_want` is the day's ACQUISITION and `a_have` is the SHED (`plan.py` `a_have = view.shed[GOOSE..]`,
   ~0), not the standing herd. So `GEESE_TARGET=k` was a *daily* purchase floor of k birds, never a stock target. The
   literal fix asked for (cap the bypass at `GEESE_TARGET - a_have`) still bought ~1 goose/day at target 1: smoke 3 boards
   geese 4.7 -> 10.2 mean d12-29 (14 max). That is what GEESE1/KNOBV56 measured.
- Fix (`src/kagg3/core/plan.py`): `_geese_deficit = GEESE_TARGET - geese standing on coops`; `_geese_floor` floors the goose
  acquisition at `max(deficit, 0)` from the first day; in `_wants` only `max(min(a_want, deficit) - a_have, 0)` bypasses the
  gate, the rest of the goose want is gated like every lane. New `GEESE_FIRST_DAY` (None = GEESE_DAY0 = 2, shipped behaviour).
- Tests `tests/test_egg_dose.py` (target 1 with 3 geese standing -> lane 0 gate closed; stock floor 5-3-1 = 1; first day;
  slow OFF-parity test behind EGGDOSE1_SLOW) + `tests/test_geese_floor.py` view fixture; 12 passed, 1 skipped.
- OFF parity: master src (3dcf0d62; its src diff vs 9f6d1290 is default-OFF only) vs eggdose1 OFF, exact V56 sim dev boards 0-2,
  local: byte-identical 3/3 (`out/parity_master_0.tsv` = `out/parity_off_0.tsv` = salepin1 `v2dev_0.tsv`).
- GEESE_TARGET=1 (KNOBV56 cell) after the fix: 3/3 byte-identical to OFF locally; dev100 **90/100 byte-identical**, 84-16 vs 85-15
  (-1 flip, Δ +9/-6 noise): the 10 differing boards have < 1 goose standing on some day, so the floor bites. KNOBV56's
  -26 flips were the daily-floor bug, not target 1.
