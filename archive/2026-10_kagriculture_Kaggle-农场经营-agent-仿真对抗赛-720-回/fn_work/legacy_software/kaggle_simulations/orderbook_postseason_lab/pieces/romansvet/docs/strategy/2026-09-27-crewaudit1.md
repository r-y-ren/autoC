## CREWAUDIT1b (2026-09-27 10:48Z–11:00Z): plant care — CORRECT, no next-day bug (same bed, `out/plantcare.tsv`)
User question: "what's the status for plants? do plants care correct or we have same next day bug?"

**Engine rules** (`.venv/.../kaggriculture.py`)
- **A planting must be watered on its planting day.**
  - The planting day counts as unwatered (`consecutive_unwatered: 1`, L222).
  - At the nightly refresh an unwatered plant gets +1 (L777-781), and at ≥ 2 it becomes a WEED (L783-784).
  - A plant left dry on its planting day therefore dies that night. A "first watered next day" state cannot exist.
- **Watering never grows a plant on its planting day.** Growth comes only from WATER inside the window `(max_yield_day+1)//2 .. max_yield_day` (L440-442):
  - wheat and carrot: ages 2..;
  - melon: ages 6..12;
  - ongoing crops (tomato, strawberry) produce on the `planted_day` clock (L789-795), whatever the watering hour.
  - So the timing of the planting-day watering neither delays nor reduces the first yield. It only decides survival.
- **A missed water day on an older plant:**
  - non-ongoing crop: loses that window unit, 1 or 2 if fertilised (L441-442);
  - any crop: advances the dry counter, and 2 missed days in a row kill it;
  - an ongoing crop also loses its fertiliser bonus on that production night (L799).
- **Fertiliser** covers days `day..day+2` (L481). Fertilising a wheat or carrot on its planting day would cover only window age 2, so the right move is to skip the planting day.
