## 1. Theta geometry: g150 is *not* "more g50"

`|g150−init| = 2.90` vs `|g50−init| = 1.83`. The projection of `g150−init` on the
`g50−init` direction is **1.10×** — the g50 step is kept almost exactly at unit scale —
but `cos(g50−init, g150−g50) = **0.085**`. The g50→g150 half of the walk is a
**near-orthogonal second change of comparable size** (`|g150−g50| = 2.11`), not more of
the same. The behavioural diff below says the same thing.

## 4. Two-purse

| comparison | our purse | their purse | d-margin | t | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| g50 − init | **−1,925** | **−2,137** | +212 | 0.25 | **denial** (both down, ours worse) |
| g150 − init | **−266** | **−1,147** | +881 | 1.02 | mostly denial, but our purse is back to level |
| **g150 − g50** | **+1,659** | **+990** | **+669** | 0.71 | **growth** — ours up, and 60 % of their price loss handed back |

The opponent is an action tape: it sells the identical **1,647.8 units over 260.4 rows**
under all three thetas, so every "their purse" move is a realised-price effect. g50 cut
their revenue 133,642 → 131,115 by flooding carrot/milk/egg and paid for it with
−2,976 of its own gross. **g150 gives 1,353 of that price denial back (131,115 →
132,468) and still nets more margin, because its own gross rises +2,064.** That is the
opposite signature: the g50→g150 increment is *our purse growing*, and it is the first
step in this run that is not a two-purse denial artefact.

## 7. What could not be measured

* **loss12 / fresh-loss tapes were not run** — the 60-game budget went to the three
  thetas on the family. The sim is not coin-exact on fresh-loss tapes anyway, so the
  remote engine's +1,879/game fresh-loss read cannot be attributed to a mechanism here.
* **Hands per day are not observable.** `State.nhands` is 0 at plan time and cleared by
  `end_of_day`, so the labour reading is the *ordered* HIRE quantity (261.4 / 260.5 /
  266.8) and the *planned* op histogram over all `MAX_UNITS` slots. The PASS share
  quoted (54 %) is therefore over plan slots, not live unit-turns, and is **not**
  comparable to the census's 8 % live-unit PASS.
* **Per-product coins are ordered qty × quote**, not executed revenue; the sale ledger
  is per-seat totals only. Season realised totals (`sold_n` / `sold_rev`) are exact and
  quoted separately.
* n = 10 boards. Neither comparison's d-margin clears t = 1.1. The concentration in
  §5 means the sim cannot separate "g150 fixed a g50 bug on one board" from
  "g150 is 669/game better".
* No engine leg spent; all numbers are the pinned-town action-seat sim, which on these
  boards is the engine to ~85 coins.
