(`src/kagg3/es/train.py`:605, used at `scripts/train.py`:5079) is
`0.6*rank_normalise(mean_e log1p(OURS))` (:674) `+ 0.4*rank_normalise(mean_e sigmoid(margin/1e5))` (:675).
