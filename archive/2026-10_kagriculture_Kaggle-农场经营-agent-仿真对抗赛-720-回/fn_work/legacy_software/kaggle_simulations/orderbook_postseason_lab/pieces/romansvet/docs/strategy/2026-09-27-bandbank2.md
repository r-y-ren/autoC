## 3. Judge a candidate on the enlarged leg
```

BL_BOARDS=../bandbank2/boards_band2.json KAGG3_SRC=<candidate tree>/src [BL_HEAD=<head.npz>] [BL_THETA=<theta.npy>] \
  nice -n 10 ../../.venv/bin/python leg.py <arm> "PLACEFEED_ON=True,PF_PUMPSAFE_ON=True[,<switches>]" ../bandbank2/res/<arm>.csv band <workers>
python3 ../bandbank2/pair_band2.py ../bandbank2/res/<arm>.csv            # flips, Δours/Δtheirs (t) per family / set (BAND142, HOLD104, NEW2, HOLD+NEW) / sub vs res/pfs_band2.csv

```
- **Candidate trees:**
  - ES (eswork_theta) candidates: copy the base tree and drop the genes into `kagg3/core/eswork_theta.npy`.
  - Head or theta candidates: use BL_HEAD / BL_THETA.
- **Cost:** ~45 s/game at 1 worker on the loaded box (276 games ≈ 3.5 h). At 3 workers it is ~20 s/game (≈ 1.5 h).
  - Subsets: `BL_FAMS=MELON`, `BL_SUBS=vrp12`, `BL_ONLY=<labels>`.
- **Read:**
  - Ship on the paired Δθ at **GW 2,600+** and at the **Oct fixed point**, with Δtheirs ≤ 0.
  - For band trainers (ESBAND*, PPO band arm), use the **HOLD+NEW** row: BAND142 is their train/accept set.
- **Hold-out law:** trainers must never read boards_band2.json or the POOL7-BANDHOLD / POOL8-BAND2 rows. They keep reading boards_all.json / POOL6-BAND.
- **Daily grow + re-weight:** `bash S/bandbank2/build.sh`.
  - It chains api.py eps + lb → select.py → build.py → run.sh pfs v10 fid (1 worker, resumable) → merge.py → bank_rows.py → mixtrack.py.
  - It needs V12/V10 source trees from `git archive 942b46cb src` and `git archive 3fbf79d0 src`; the scratchpad defaults are in run.sh.
