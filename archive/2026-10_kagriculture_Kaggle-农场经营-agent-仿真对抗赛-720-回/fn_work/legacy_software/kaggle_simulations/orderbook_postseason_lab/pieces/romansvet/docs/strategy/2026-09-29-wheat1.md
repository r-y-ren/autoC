(d10-19, turn-gated), the relay cadence is a numpy file, and the sale is the learned reservation. Hence one new switch
(worktree `kagg3_wt_wheat1`, branch `wheat1`):

`WHEAT_ENGINE="<tiles>|<d_from>|<d_to>|<keep>"` (default `""` = OFF, two Python-false branches):
- **tiles**: on d_from .. d_to-2 the ESWORK relay plants `tiles // 4` extra wheat a day on top of the shipped cadence (a wheat tile turns over in ~4 days:
  plant, water, harvest at age 2-4, replant, so the rate holds `tiles` extra tiles in wheat), through the relay's own tile room, seed walk and hands;
- **keep**: on d_from .. d_to the dawn sale sells the shed wheat above max(today's feed, keep) regardless of the reservation value
  (hold = `SELL.LIQUIDATE` for WHEAT only).
- Default-off identity: 6/6 games (first 3 m40 boards x 2 seats vs g0capsfix) equal to the banked control `S/judgerival1/res/g0capsfix/pfs.csv`
  on ours / theirs / h1 / t240 / t432. (The control tree `S/firebank1/tree/src` differs from master only in the default `KERNEL2_FIRE_CASH`,
  which the OFF string overrides to 99999.)

## 4. Deviations from the dispatch, caveats
- **Grid depth.** All 12 cells were run, but vs `big` on a 16-game subset (first 8 m40 boards x 2 seats), not 80 games; vs `g0capsfix` only the 2 best screen
  cells ran the 80-game read, and 4 cells the 27-game live27 read. The full 12 x (big 80 + g0capsfix 80) grid (~1,900 games) does not fit a 120-minute box
  at the 2-worker remote cap. The 6 d10-19 cells collapse to 2 (the tile axis is inert there), so the grid has 8 distinct cells, not 12.
- **Seat duplicates.** On the m40 controls about half the labels give identical rows for both seat orders (g0capsfix 19/40, big 18/40, flood 16/40),
  so the 80-game t overstates the independent evidence by up to ~sqrt(2); the verdict does not depend on it.
- **Live seat = vrp15_k2fire**, the PFS family with the fire list, not the pure-PFS body the judge plays.
- The screen's dmargin is positive in all 13 cells because the rival side of the 8-board subset moves (-0.7k .. -1.9k in every cell, including the
  sale-only control); the 80-game bar read of the best screen cell is +73 (t 0.19).
