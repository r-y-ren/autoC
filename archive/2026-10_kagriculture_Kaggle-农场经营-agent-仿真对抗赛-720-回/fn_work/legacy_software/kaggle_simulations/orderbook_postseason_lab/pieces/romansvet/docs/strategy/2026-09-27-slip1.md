## 1. Premise
BOEY1 (2026-09-26-boey1.md l.36) prices each lot on the exact engine walk (`S/boey1/churn.py`). On that basis we lose 5.0k/game to "walk inside our own lots" and Boey loses 0.6k. Boey sells lots of 7 units or fewer every hour (R12). Our shipped program sells one lot per product per day.

## 2. Market model (engine `kaggle_environments/envs/kaggriculture/kaggriculture.py`)
- The price is a monotone decreasing function of the market inventory: `market_price` l.192-206.
- A SELL unit adds +1 to the shared inventory for good: `_commit_unit` l.656-660. The only exception is a $1 sale.
- The walk does not decay. Nothing lowers the inventory except rival BUY_PRODUCT (l.671) and the town draw.
- The town draw is `_town_consume` l.728-747. It runs at every `step % 4 == 0`, after the market phase (l.941-942). Each unlocked shop takes 1 unit, or 2 for a single-product shop. The draw is unconditional: the inventory can go below 0 and nothing is refunded.
- So the units drained between two sale turns are fixed. They do not depend on our lot structure.
- Recovery between hours is exactly zero on the 3 turns of each 4-turn block that have no draw. At the draw turns it is c units per draw, and that happens whatever we do.
- Consequence: every unit we sell is priced at `I_day + (all units sold before it) − (draws before it) × c`. Total revenue depends only on how many draws each unit sells after. Lot size does not enter.
- Splitting a lot moves some units earlier, in front of fewer draws. That is weakly worse, never better. Selling later is the only direction that gains, and only across a draw.
- The "forfeit" is computed as Σ units × (first-unit quote − realised). Splitting shrinks it by relabelling which unit counts as "first", while the first-unit quote of each later lot falls by the same amount.

## 4. Why Boey reads 0.6k and we read 5.0k
The statistic measures lot size, not coins. Boey's small lots sell in front of fewer later draws, so they are priced lower, which is the Boey column of the demo. Boey's d20-29 price edge is board-level: BOEY1 found the straw quote at 134 vs 70, and the V rival gains the same +8.3k in those games. It is not lot-level.

## 5. Verdict: COVERED. No switch, no screen, SALE-LOT axis stays CLOSED
The 5.0k is SPREAD6's "forfeit" under a new name. Under the engine's permanent cumulative inventory it cannot be recovered by splitting. Splitting within the day only moves units in front of fewer draws. The one real lever is selling after a draw, and turn 17 is already the post-draw row (LOT4/LOT5).
