## 3. Diagnosis of EVE_STOCK_ON

### What the switch does
`EVE_STOCK_ON` combines two halves.
- **Evening row.** `_prestock` places a row at `TURN_PRESTOCK` = 20 that buys tomorrow's feed wheat and fertilizer. It is sized to what today realised, net of tonight's shed. It is clipped by shed room against the conservative-low `proj_eod`, then by `purse_left`.
- **Route half.** Tomorrow, the `ROUTE_FREEFIRST` per-kind test reads the zero `wheat_buy`/`fert_bought`. A block that owes only those pickups then starts at `TURN_BUY` instead of `ROUTE_BASE`.
- **Where play diverges.** The first divergence is d0 h20, where EVE buys BUY_PRODUCT WHEAT 4.

### Strawberry -0.44 u (d15-17)
- **SWITCHSWEEP1R rows (114 matched games):** d -0.49 u (t -1.81).
  - 38 games lose, for -133 u in total, and 24 gain, for +77 u.
  - Most moves are ±4-8 u, in the sale-lot quantum.
  - End-of-season strawberry totals are equal or higher on almost every loser.
- **Per-day ledger:** `S/evestock1/remote/esrun.py` = srun.py plus, at every dawn, both farms' sales, money, census and shed, and our per-day op counts. Run on 20 games (17 straw losers and 4 collapse games) for OFF and EVE, with OFF exact against live. Output is in `res/led1_full.txt`.
  - **Tiles:** EVE has **more** strawberry tiles, +16..+29 in total over d12-19 (about +1.2 per game). Planting and water were not displaced.
  - **Harvest ops** fall on d13-16: -12, -26, -15, -39. Sales follow: d15 -22, d16 +10, **d17 -74**, **d18 +41**.
  - So the loss is **harvest timing**: the d16 harvest slips to d17-18, and those units are sold on d18 instead of d17.
  - **Shed:** OFF's dawn shed is already 95-97 of 100 on d15-17. EVE's prestocked wheat adds +3 to +7 per game at those dawns (d17 wheat +3.1, strawberry -3.3; d18 wheat +7.2).
- **Fix cells.** I tried three, all P48-103 reactive vs ctl162 plus V56 on tree2, the knob diff in `res/tree2_plan.diff`. Each is compared with EVE on the same games.

### The 5 tape-collapse games
115554227, 115271256, 115560503 and 114962331 (P48/PQ4) are matched here, plus one more in SWITCHSWEEP1R's count.
- **Rival money:** the taped rival ends at -30 to -107k against control.
- **Timeline** (per-day ledger, 115271256 / 115291280 / 115560503):
  - Rival money and units sold diverge from d1-d3. At the d9 dawn the rival has 237 vs 1,693 coins, and 5 vs 1,646.
  - Our d0-d2 turn-20 wheat buys take the market stock the rival's replayed tape buys a turn later.
  - The tape then keeps playing actions for items it never received, so its farm stalls for the whole season.
- **Verdict: tape artefact.** A live rival would re-buy a turn later at a slightly higher price. The reacting V56 bounds the real denial: rival -346 (m40) and -248 (v21), with no collapse.
