## 2. Why the turns are idle — and the switch

`drop_turns` / `turn_budget` end the terminal day at `O.SELL_TURNS[-1] + 1`
(turn 19): before ENDROUTE existed, a DROP later than turn 18 had no lot to sell
into, so the last six turns were worth nothing and the admit stage was told not
to spend them. 53.4 PASS ≈ (23 − 18) × the crew — exactly that slack.

`ENDROUTE_ON` is a lot later than turn 18. `ENDROUTE2_ON` therefore moves the
same cut to `ENDROUTE_TURN + 1`: **+4 turns for every unit, terminal day only**,
in the two places the budget is written (the hire enumeration's `per_unit` and
`turn_budget`). No new op, no new row, no new task, no new value term — the
extra budget admits a longer prefix of the value order the planner already
built, and the DROP still lands before its own turn's market phase (units act
first, `interpreter`). The gate is `terminal`, not `drop_day`, so
`MIDDAY_DROP_ON`'s day 28 keeps the budget it had. The switch asserts it cannot
stand without `ENDROUTE_ON`, whose row is the only thing the extra turns bank
into. OFF, both expressions are the ones they were and the champion decodes byte
for byte against a pristine `git archive 516e6dc src` subprocess.

## 4. Why it is not an arm

1. **Under the bar.** +227 on the gated 28 is below +300, so `run_band180.sh`
   was not run (§115b needs ≥ +450 and t ≥ 3, and this leg cannot reach it).
2. **Not significant.** ENDROUTE2's own delta is +110 with se 158, t +0.70, and
   the two halves of the line disagree in sign (topleg2 −35, topleg3 +528).
3. **It spends the one thing ENDROUTE had.** ENDROUTE's whole point was
   `theirs +0` — the first gift-free arm of the season. Extending the route
   puts 265 coins of extra produce into the shared pot and hands the rival
   **+155** of it back: the variance goes from sd 143 to sd 858 and the arm
   rejoins the `MELONGIFT` family. A ROW on the last turn reaches nobody; a
   ROUTE that sells more into the same market does.

**CLOSED.** The terminal-day residue is 141 coins a board, the route that
reaches it is not free, and 116 + 141 could never have cleared +300. The
end-of-game family is exhausted: `ENDROUTE_ON` stays OFF as the best measured
member (+116, gift-free, under the bar) and `ENDROUTE2_ON` stays OFF beside it.

## 5. Where the rival's +155 comes from — traced, and it is NOT a leak

`S/endroute2/trace` rig (full per-turn action / money / price dump of one judged
board, both arms, real engine, same seed as the judge row). Two boards, the two

|---|---:|---|---|---|---:|

| 109826302 | 1551579386 | **d29 h0** | d29 h1 | d29 h19 | +452 |

Every one of the 696 engine steps before d29 h0 is **byte-identical** (the
`json.dumps(act, sort_keys=True)` of the farmer/hands/market action dicts, both
farms' money, and the full price vector). `terminal = day > O.LAST_SHED_DAY` is
day 29 alone and the switch does not leak. **No bug, nothing to fix.**

The cause is **our own price depression, moved off their sell hour**. On
109831304 the ENDROUTE arm dumps the terminal lot at **h19** (+4,314 coins),
which cuts STRAWBERRY 103 → 87 and WHEAT 44 → 42. ENDROUTE2's four extra turns
push the same dump to **h22-23**, so at h19 the quote only goes 103 → 99 and
wheat does not move at all. The tape's replayed sells fire at **h21 and h22**,
into a market we have not yet crushed: STRAWBERRY 97 → 108 and 51 → 62, and
their fixed quantities bank +46 then +441. 109826302 is the same sentence
(CARROT +1, STRAWBERRY +7, WOOL +19..29 at their sell hours; +82 then +398).

So `theirs +155` is not extra produce in the shared pot — it is the **price
denial we used to hand them for free** by selling before they did. Selling
later is worth +265 to us and costs the denial; the two are of the same order,
which is exactly why the net is small and noisy. The channel is the `LOSS
ANATOMY` d15-29 price-denial channel, seen from the other side, on one day.

## 8. ENDROUTE2_SPLIT — two shed trips on day 29, the first one on time

§5 said the +155 is not produce, it is **our own price denial moved off their
sell hour**. So do not move it: split day 29's route into TWO trips instead of
pushing its one trip four turns later.

`plan.ENDROUTE2_SPLIT_ON` (default **OFF**, byte-exact OFF, `e2split` branch):

1. the **first** trip is the `BANK_BEFORE_LOT_ON` mid-block excursion that
   already ships — walk to the nearest shed access, DROP, walk back — fired on
   the TERMINAL day (where `bank_day` was `~terminal & ~drop_day`) with its
   deadline re-cut from `O.SELL_TURNS[BANK_LOT]` to `ENDROUTE2_SPLIT_TURN` =
   `O.SELL_TURNS[-1]` = turn 18. **That is the h19 dump, kept at its hour.**
2. the block then spends `ENDROUTE2_ON`'s four extra turns on the ripe tiles
   behind it and ends in its ordinary return leg, whose DROP lands at turn 22 —
   `ENDROUTE_ON`'s row, i.e. a **second sell of the late harvest alone**.

No new op, no new row, no new task, no new value term: the deposit is the
shipped excursion and the second sell is ENDROUTE's own row. `DROP_ON` already
adds the block's whole banked yield to the day's LAST lot (`gain`), so the h19
row already offers what the first trip deposits; `BANK_BEFORE_LOT`'s own
`bl_gain` (which widens `BANK_LOT`, turn 10) is gated OFF on the terminal day,
where it would only pull the dawn shed onto an earlier row (`ENDSELL` −68).

The excursion pays a **round** trip where the return leg pays one way, so the
prefix in front of the last lot is the same or SHORTER than ENDROUTE alone's —
never later, which is the one direction that would hand the denial back. That
is variant (b) of the ask, and it is the only variant needed.

Knobs for the sweeps: `ENDROUTE2_SPLIT_TURN` (the first dump's hour),
`ENDROUTE2_SPLIT_MIN_VALUE` / `ENDROUTE2_SPLIT_MAX_TURNS` (`BANK_MIN_VALUE` /
`BANK_MAX_TURNS` on the terminal day alone). Tools `S/e2split/run_all.sh`
(28-board line) and `S/e2split/run_pool.sh` (POOLED180); pins in

### 8.4 Verdict

`ENDROUTE2_SPLIT_ON` is **15× ENDROUTE alone and 2.8× E + E2 on the same pool,
at t +8.74**, and it dominates both of them board by board: +356 over E (t
+8.09) and +245 over E + E2 (t +7.80) with the rival's purse **down 42** against
E + E2. The §5 diagnosis was right and the fix is the right shape — the coins E2
was buying were real, and what it was paying for them was the h19 dump, which a
second trip buys back.

Against the stated ship rule the arm is **3 of 4**: pooled d over ENDROUTE alone
is positive and large, t vs FT2 is 8.74 ≫ 3, no leg is significantly negative —
but `theirs` is **+120, not ≈ 0**. It is not the gift-free arm ENDROUTE alone is;
the units whose blocks cannot afford the round trip still make one late dump and
they carry the residue. d = +381 also sits just under §115b's +450 POOLED180 bar
while clearing its t ≥ 3.

So: **the best member of the end-of-game family by a wide margin, and the one
worth the ship decision** — but it is a two-purse trade, not a free coin, and the
default stays **OFF** until that decision is made. Open, cheap, and untried: the
`ENDROUTE2_SPLIT_TURN` / `_MIN_VALUE` / `_MAX_TURNS` sweep (§8.1 says relaxing
the gates is worse; tightening them, and moving the first dump to lot 2, are not
yet measured) and `ENDROUTE_TURN` 19/21 for the units that never split.
