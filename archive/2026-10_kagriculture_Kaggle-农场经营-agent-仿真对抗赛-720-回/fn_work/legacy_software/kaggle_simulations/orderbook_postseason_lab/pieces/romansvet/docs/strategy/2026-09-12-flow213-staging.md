## 3. The arithmetic the extra family forces

**(a) `--episodes` 200 → 240, +20 % wall clock.** 166 + 30 + 40 = **236** pinned rungs.
`Trainer.pinned_block` (`train.py:4413-4429`) returns every pinned rung — weight 0 included — and
`pinned_keep` (`:4432-4443`) gives each exactly one episode and never truncates; the residual is
`n_pairs = max((episodes - n_pinned) // 2, 1)` (`:4926`). Leaving `--episodes 200` against 236 pinned
would clamp to 1 pair and **silently play 238 episodes/gen** while the banner warned — the same trap
flow211 documented at 196/179. 240 = 236 + 2 pairs exactly: no clamp, the self-play tail intact at 4
episodes, banner `pinned-once: 236 pinned rungs x 1 episode + 4 episodes carried`.
**Cost: 240 / 200 = +20.0 % rollouts per generation — 4096 × 240 = 983,040 against flow212's
4096 × 200 = 819,200.** The refusal threshold moves with `--episodes` (238 = 240 − 2), so the file
still refuses rather than overspends if either id list grows again.

**(b) `--abs-pairs` 54 → 45.** The abs probe is ONE unsplit jit call (`train.py:5510`,
`max(chunk, total)`) of `2 × --abs-pairs × (4 archetypes + n_tapes)` rows — `--chunk` does not bound
it (§100 D0). n_tapes goes 196 → 236, so the rung count in that product goes 200 → **240**:

```
21,760 / (2 × 240) = 45.33  ->  --abs-pairs 45
2 × 45 × 240 = 21,600 rows  <=  21,760   (flow209's shape, the largest these cards have run)
flow212's 54 would be 2 × 54 × 240 = 25,920 rows -- 19 % over the only proven shape.
```

**(c) the zero-weight families still cost their episodes.** 39 of 240 = **16.3 %** of the wall clock
(down from flow212's 19.5 %) is the top-ten / LOSS10 / top-50 free read: played, printed in the gen
line, and contributing exactly `0.0` to every candidate's collapsed score *before* any ranking

### Why — §117 and §114

§117 (`docs/strategy/2026-09-12-press-scale.md`) put candidate B's decoded `press` at a **margin
local optimum**: scaling it softer hands the opponent their price back, scaling it harder costs our
own purse. §114 showed the ES from B nevertheless drifts `press` **down** (flow209) or **up**
(flow210) — two arms, opposite directions — along an axis priced at ~zero, while the transfer that
actually moves margin happens elsewhere. A zero-priced axis is precisely what an ES random-walks and
what the §72/§73 dimension budget is being spent on. Recommendation adopted: pin it.

### Which genes `press` is

`press` is the sell-side patience knob (coins per lot of delay). Its decode path:

| step | expression | where |
|---|---|---|
| shared encoder hidden | `h = tanh((x @ w1 + b1) + drain @ dh + fcast @ fh)` | `core/policy.py:forward` |
| **press head** | `press = h @ w3 + b3`, `Outputs.gate = press[:, 0]` | `core/policy.py:forward` |
| decode | `press = qfloor(base * relu(tanh(out.gate)))` | `core/brain.py:1053` |

**`w3` (64×1, flat offset 4189) and `b3` (1, offset 4253) are the press head and nothing else** —
`w3` is the 64-wide read of the shared encoder hidden, `b3` the sell head's single global
lot-count bias (§93/§103). They are **whole `PO.SHAPES` blocks**, not a slice of a shared block, so
the pin is a `--train-only` change exactly as the §117 recommendation assumed.

No per-gene facility was needed, and none exists: `kagg3.es.train.train_mask`
(`src/kagg3/es/train.py:890-968`) accepts `all`, `biases`, `all-biases`, or a comma-separated list
of **block names**, and refuses anything else; there is no `--freeze`, no `--train-mask` file, and
no mask-file env var in the trainer. §67's `KAGG3_PIN_INTS` is a **decode-time** switch used by the
`S/onestep_pin/` screen, not a trainer gene mask — it cannot pin a coordinate during training.

### `hold` does NOT share the head

`hold = min(qfloor(0.8 · base · unit_ratio(sell)), COIN_CAP−1)` reads `sell = scores[:, 1]`, i.e.
`w2`/`b2` column 1 plus `ds`/`fs` column 1 — a different path entirely. Pinning `w3`/`b3` therefore
leaves the reservation price exactly as trainable as it was under flow212 (through `ds` and the
shared encoder). The pin costs the arm nothing on the hold axis.

### The residual, stated

`b1` and `dh` stay trained and still move `h`, so `press` is **not** frozen to the coin. What it can
no longer do is move *by itself*: any remaining press motion now comes with a matching change in
`grow`/`sell` out of the same hidden layer, which is a priced change, not the free axis §114
measured. `gp` stays trained and reads the press **channel** of the product summary
(`summary = [scores, press] / PROD_SUMMARY_SCALE`); with `w3`/`b3` held, that channel is a fixed
read-out of `h` rather than a second press knob.

### Cost check on the inherited step arithmetic

The flow209 header's step-length numbers survive at this size: the decay equilibrium
`lr·sqrt(d_train / 2·WD)` moves by `sqrt(1126/1191) = 0.973`, and `rho = P/(P + d_T(1+nu))` improves
slightly (fewer coordinates against the same pop 4096). Neither is worth re-tuning `lr` or `wd`.

### Judging the pin against the live arms

`S/snr/drift_check.py` decodes the §114 signature knobs from a run's g10 centre, and **`press` is
one of them**: `SIG_BASES = ("grow_mult", "press", "hire_bias", "dev_weight")`
(`S/snr/drift_check.py:95`; `forward_days` is the day-0-only fifth). So flow211's and flow212's g10
centres **can** be read for press drift right now, with no new tooling:

```
export JAX_PLATFORMS=cpu
python S/snr/drift_check.py flow211 10
python S/snr/drift_check.py flow212 10          # add --boards 8 (the default) or more
```

The rows to read are the per-product **`press[<product>]` rows of the SIGNATURE table** — the table
prints `knob / product / flow209 dir (% of board-days) / arm dir` and closes with "N of M signature
knobs drift the flow209 direction". Read them against the other three signature knobs on the same
boards:

* `press` rows moving the flow209 direction on most board-days while `grow_mult` / `hire_bias` /
  `dev_weight` do not → the zero-priced axis is still live at this exact recipe, and the pin is
  buying what §117 says it buys.
* `press` rows flat at g10 → the pin costs nothing and flow213 is a clean control either way; the
  dimension saved (65 of 1,191, 5.5 %) is the only effect.

Either reading is informative, which is why the pin is safe to stage before the drift read lands.
