# ENDWAVE1 (2026-09-26): a d23-27 wave of 2-day crops + a d27-29 crew hold — NO SHIP, END-WAVE AXIS CLOSED

**Question.** Live 113629359 (vrp7 vs Durian, V family) was lost by 1,251. Durian planted 63 wheat/carrot tiles on d23-27
(we planted 40) and kept 10-11 hands on d27-29 (we ran 8/7/7). Does a late wave sized to our crew, plus a crew hold, flip
the V end-game losses?

## Build (branch `endwave1`, one switch `END_WAVE_ON`, OFF = byte-identical)
- `plan._end_wave` runs on `END_WAVE_DAYS` (default d23-27). It takes every free owned tile the day's ask leaves empty
  (`n_free − ask`, the same count as `brain.n_free_slots`) and asks it as wheat or carrot, provided the crop still matures
  by `VAL.pay_day()`. The crop is picked per 4-tile chunk by forecast value:
  - yield at harvest × the marginal quote of the dawn town ladder, after our standing supply (growing tiles + ask + shed);
  - minus the seed cost.
  `END_WAVE_CROPS` = BEST / WHEAT / CARROT.
- **The crew** is the planner's own argmax on the non-wave task set. The wave never votes on the count. On a day the wave
  adds tiles, one extra hand is offered (`END_WAVE_PLUS` 1). The VRP drops any hand whose stops fit the others, so +1 is a
  cap, not a hire. Seeds are bought that morning by the planner's own seed row.
- **Hold.** `END_WAVE_HOLD` N is a crew floor at N on d27-29, clipped to the affordable prefix. The plan is jitted and
  stateless, so "the d26 level" is a count (10, and 11 in the episode arm).
- `END_WAVE_CAP` (extra, not in the dispatch) is a crew cap from the wave's first day through d29.
- **Engine bug found and fixed.** A lazy `from . import brain` inside a planner call raises on every turn under the engine
  harness's vendored-import context: our seat ends at 3,000, and the first run of both the w23 and w23h episode arms did
  exactly that. This is the same failure VLOSS1 saw for `LATE_ASK_FLOOR` on selfplay1 ("3,000 every game").
  `_ew_free` counts the free tiles inline (a test checks it against brain's count). **`LATE_ASK_FLOOR` still has the bug.**
- `tests/test_endwave1.py`: 17 cases. With `test_labour1`, 25 pass (local).
  - The defaults are OFF.
  - OFF is byte-identical to the master digest `142b226d` on 3 days, and so is ON outside the window.
  - Only the free tiles are added, only wheat or carrot, and only inside the window. The crop filter holds.
  - The free-tile count equals brain's.
  - On a wave day, the plan plants more than OFF.

## 3. Why
- **The wave's own days re-derive the crew.** The planner hires only for work, but the wave creates that work: watering in
  the d24-28 window, harvest and haul. The crew goes from ~9-10 to 11.5-12.6 hands on d23-27 and 11.8 on d29. Hands 11-14
  cost fib 89-377 a day each, so the bill is +1.8k a game. The +1 cap binds only on the planting day. Capping the crew at
  11 through d29 (the episode arm) plants the same tiles with too few hands, and the margin is −2,047.
- **Late 2-day tiles are low-value.**
  - A tile planted on d25-27 is harvested at age 2-3 (2-3 u).
  - The extra wheat sells at ~33/u, and carrot collapses to ~6/u (our own supply walks the ladder).
  - The other products lose 495 coins/game, because hands move off herd, fert and sales work.
  - This is the RELAYFILL1/CREWRELAY1 result again.
- **The hold is inert.** The VRP (`mode2`, crew bill first) drops every hand whose stops fit the others. A planner floor only
  offers hands, and d28 still runs 9.2. d29 has no VRP, and its HIRE rows follow the loaded hands. Idle turns d27-29 do not
  move with the hold (92.2 vs 92.3). They do rise with the wave (73.6 → 92-95): more hands, same packing residue.
- **Durian's end-game is not our lever.** The tape is open-loop, and its 11 hands and 63 tiles come with its own herd and
  crew. On our farm the same tile count costs 1.8k of hires, and returns ~0.7-1.0k.
