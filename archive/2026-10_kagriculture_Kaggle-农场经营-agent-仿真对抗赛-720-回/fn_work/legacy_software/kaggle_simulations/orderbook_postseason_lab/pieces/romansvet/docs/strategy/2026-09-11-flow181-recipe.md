# flow181: raise the top-ten tapes to ~40% of the episode budget

`launch_flow181.sh` = `launch_flow180.sh` with only the 20 `S/top20/ids.txt`
rung weights changed (3 -> 10.2 each) and every `flow180` renamed to
`flow181` (header included, mechanical rename only). `--arch-frac 0.9`,
init theta, gate, and every other rung weight are unchanged.

## Arithmetic

`opponent_slots()`/`largest_remainder()` (`src/kagg3/es/train.py`) divide
`k = round(arch_frac * n_pairs)` archetype pairs across rungs in proportion
to `--rung-weight`. flow180: `--episodes 160` -> `n_pairs = 80`, `k = 72`.
152 archetype rungs total weight 315 (127 tapes @2, 20 top-ten tapes @3 =
60, 4 hand archetypes @0, 1 unweighted tape @1). Top-ten share of the
archetype block = 60/315 = 19.0%, of the full 160-episode budget =
19.0% x 0.9 = **17.1%**.

Target: top-ten share of the *full* budget = 0.40, i.e. of the archetype
block = 0.40/0.9 = 4/9. Holding the other rungs' total weight fixed at 255
(unchanged individual weights => unchanged ratios), solve
`x/(x+255) = 4/9` => `x = 204`, i.e. **10.2 per tape** (204/20).

New split: total weight 459 (255 + 204); archetype-block share =
204/459 = 44.4%; full-budget share = 44.4% x 0.9 = **40.0%** exact.

## Slot counts per generation (72 archetype pairs, x2 for both seats)

| | pairs | episodes | % of 160 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| flow180 (w=3) | 72x60/315 = 13.7 | ~27 | 17.1% |
| flow181 (w=10.2) | 72x204/459 = 32.0 | 64 | 40.0% |

32 pairs over 20 tapes = ~1.6 pairs (~3.2 episodes) each per generation,
vs ~0.7 pairs (~1.4 episodes) each before -- roughly 2.3x more signal per
top-ten tape.

## Risk

Reweighting trades band-tier signal for top-tier signal; the pooled ES
gradient may now underfit the 2000-2400 band it still needs to clear before
top-10 matters. Do not judge flow181 on TOPB alone: gate it on **LIVE55 +
TOPB together** (`docs/strategy/2026-09-11-topb-leg.md`'s held-out 20 -- not
the in-sample TOP20 -- plus the live-board band read), and watch for band
win rate dropping alongside any top-tier gain.
