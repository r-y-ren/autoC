## 2. Why 9, and why these 9

A tape is an action replay indexed by the engine step counter, so it can desync against an opponent
it was not recorded against and then earn a fraction of what it earned live. The live check is

    TAPE RETENTION = coins the tape scores in our leg / coins it scored in its source episode

and the cut is retention ≥ 0.85 (NEXTHIGH control: 30 tapes, mean 0.99, 2/30 below the cut).
Retention is measured **under the repaired seat**, which is what changed the count:

| seat | Majkel1337 | ymg_aq | DSM | total ≥ 0.85 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| original packages (`artifacts/panel_opp_town/`) | 0/6 | 6/6 | 1/6 | **7** |
| + hire-sticky re-cut | 1/6 | 6/6 | 3/6 | 10 |
| + hire-sticky **and** `TAPE_MAP_FIX=1` (this leg) | 1/6 | 6/6 | 2/6 | **9** |

(`S/topb3/tapemap/retain3.txt`.) The map fix is not a regression from 10: two of the three DSM
"gains" hire-sticky showed were the `_MAP` one-place shift flattering the tape, and with the fix on
the engine leg is identical to the sim's hire-sticky ledger on 18/18 boards — retention to 2 d.p.
and B's margin to the coin. `TAPE_PURSE_FIX` is left OFF: measured a no-op on this set.

The 9 boards retain 0.859-1.065 (mean 1.00). The 9 dropped ones retain 0.28-0.83 and are not in
the leg at all — on those B beats a crippled replay, not the file.

## 4. Running a candidate

    WORKERS=4 bash S/topb3/run_r9.sh <name> /mnt/e/_work/kaggriculture3/.claude/worktrees/arms-next \
        /mnt/e/_work/kaggriculture3/artifacts/kagg2_games/thetas/<theta>.npy \
        "OPEN_PUMP_ON=True,TAIL_FILL_ON=True,BANK_BEFORE_LOT_ON=True,HIRE_ROW_ON=True"
    # -> S/lossflip/topb3r9_<name>.csv (18 rows) + a line in S/topb3/r9_chain.txt
    .venv/bin/python S/nexthigh/pair.py  S/lossflip/topb3r9_B.csv S/lossflip/topb3r9_<name>.csv
    .venv/bin/python S/topb3/retention.py S/lossflip/topb3r9_B.csv S/lossflip/topb3r9_<name>.csv

`run_r9.sh` exports `TAPE_MAP_FIX=1` (override by exporting it) and points at
`S/topb3/tapemap/tapes/`, where hire-sticky is baked in. **A leg run with the plain
`S/topb3/run.sh` tapes is not comparable to this base csv.** Always re-read retention on the
candidate too: a candidate that hires differently can desync a tape that B kept, and a board whose
retention falls below 0.85 under the candidate is not a board the pair may be read on.

Seeds: `seed(board) = seed_lists(800779401 + 1000003·idx)` with `idx` the board's index in the
**18-board** `S/topb3/ids.txt`, so the 9 boards keep their original seeds. They sit at indices
2 | 6-11 | 15,16 — three contiguous runs — so `run_r9.sh` is three `on2b.py` calls at `FIRST` =
2, 6, 15, merged into one csv. The chunker derives the runs from `ids.txt` at run time and refuses
anything it cannot place; `r9_ids.txt` must stay in `ids.txt` order. Preflight also refuses an
unpinned id or a missing hire-sticky tape.

## 6. Caveats

* **B vs Majkel1337 is n = 1 board.** The rank-1 file (3204.4) contributes one readable tape
  (108801472, retention 0.859); its other five still desync at 0.28-0.49 because Majkel1337
  out-hires its source in a way the map fix does not address (it never pins at all — the DSM
  mechanism is a different one). B's +23,359 there is one board with no spread and **must not** be
  quoted as "B beats the rank-1 file". The leg's statistic is the pooled 9-board margin.
* Nine boards is a small leg: at B's sd of 13,324 the 9-board standard error is ~4,400 coins, so it
  resolves a ~+9,000 coins/board difference at t ≈ 2. It is a **fidelity-clean informational leg
  against the top five**, not a promotion gate; promotion remains the four paired legs.
* Per-team splits inside it (1 / 2 / 6 boards) are descriptive only.
* Extending it means fixing the Majkel1337 and DSM desyncs (or re-cutting those episodes), then
  appending the recovered ids to `S/topb3/r9_ids.txt` **in `ids.txt` order** and rebuilding the
  baseline from `topb3_Bmapfix.csv` the same way.
