## The switch (worktree branch p48graph1_0929; built by S/p48graph1/apply_p48g.py, apply_p48g2.py, apply_p48g3.py)
- **`P48G_ON`** (plan.py, default False) turns on `ProgramEngineState.repair_fund` (runtime.py). It runs on d1..`P48G_LAST_DAY` (10) at hours `P48G_H0`..`P48G_H1`: step 1 used h3-h9, step 2 widened it to h3-h20 because the port's sale fills land at h8-h18.
  - **Cash estimate.** It prices this step's own SELL rows along the engine's quote walk: units DROP before the market resolves, and market slots resolve in list order.
  - **Deductions.** It nets out every purchase today's plan still owns, the day's feed for unfed animals, and tomorrow's hire bill.
  - **Orders.** It then appends orders after the sells: BUY_LAND (the quadrant calendar), BUY_SEED (today's plantings not covered by seed in hand) and BUY_ANIMAL (the herd deficit, one at a time round-robin; cows first on d1-5, geese first from d6).
- **`P48G_NORESERVE`** (default True): the dawn intent keeps only the crew bill, with no overnight feed reserve.
- **`P48G_PLACE_ON`** (default True under P48G_ON): `repair_place` sends idle units (plan suffix PASS) to place shed animals the same day. Each unit does PICKUP at the shed, BUILD_COOP or BUILD_PASTURE on the nearest unclaimed empty tile, then PLACE.
- **`PROGRAMME_SCHED="p48"`**: the median tables, with the herd replaced by PORTGAP1's real-seat end-of-day herd (S/C/G):
  - d0 3/2/0
  - d2-5: cows 3 -> 6
  - d6 4/6/3
  - d8 5/7/6
  - d9 5/7/8
- **OFF:** P48G_ON False is a Python-false branch at three call sites, plus two per-game dicts. Identity with RIVALP48's rows is 6/6 (`res/id0.md`).

## What this says (for the next build)
1. **Buying at the right hour does not fund the build.**
   - P48's same-step purchases work because its units DROP the day's wool and milk by h3-h6.
   - PFS's executor carries them until h8 (d6 wool) or h13-15 (d8 milk), and places bought animals only at the next dawn, about 1-2 a day.
   - A faithful port needs the executor's MORNING route: harvest the sheep and cows first, drop at h3-h6, and place the same day. A purchase rule alone cannot give it.
   - The same late-carry route explains the first melon sale at step 260 rather than 246 (d10 melons dropped at h16-20).
2. **The cheapest real gain found is placement.**
   - Same-day placement of shed animals (`P48G_PLACE_ON`, with P48G_LAST_DAY=0) takes the old port from 10.4 to 13.4 placed at d9 h12 and adds +1.0k of d0-9 fertilizer.
   - It is not tested on PFS: PFS buys at h1 and has idle overnight cash (PORTGAP1).
3. **The crew must survive the spend-down.** P48 hires 10-11 hands daily out of a 0-400 dawn purse (wages about 50 a day); the PROGRAM_ENGINE dawn grant cuts hands when the dawn purse is low.
4. The judge rival question is unchanged: no rival reproduces the d0-9 build, and `p48` is not wired.
