## 1. Design
- **Harness:** `S/esband2/esband2.py` = the ESBAND1 harness (`S/esband1/esband.py`) with the per-episode THETA as the candidate.
  - Kept from ESBAND1: band fitness, stratified halves, M20z `f_eng` gate fix, tape-guard sim.

- **Code pin:** src = master **942b46cb**, from `git archive` into `S/esband2/stage/src` (TREE cdfc1618 = master src).

  - Worker cfg line: `plan=.../S/esband2/stage/src/kagg3/core/plan.py`.
- **Genes:** the 6,779 live NN floats of theta7659 (table below), all at **σ 0.002**.
  - This is the ESV56/ESV57 live-block sigma. ESV56 PROBE1: 0.002 moves 10/10 boards, 0.005 gifts +1.4k.
  - These are exactly the NN genes ESV56/ESV57 perturbed.
  - Excluded:
    - the zero-init decode blocks (gb3, gb4, mh, ms, cm, cb, cd): PROBE1 found any sigma there loses 5/5;
    - the switch block sw/swb: code switches, covered by ABLATE2/BANDSTACK1, and swb is not in the 7,692-float theta;
    - the residual head and the eswork genes.
  - ESPOOL1's 9 integer + 27 switch-bit genes are layout.txt knobs, not theta floats: not used.
- **Fitness** (ESBAND1, unchanged): `F = Σ_f share_f · mean_f[1.5·flip + Δmargin/1000]`.
  - It is paired against theta7659 on the same seat. Shares: MELON 0.565, V 0.348, ZERO 0.087.
- **Batch:** K = 8 antithetic pairs = 16 candidates on one stratified 71-seat half (alternating A/B).
  - Centred-rank update `step = lr·σ·(w·ε)/Σ|w|·2`.
  - lr ∈ [0.125, **1.0**]. The ESBAND1 cap of 2 would give 1.6 σ per gene, past the 0.005 gift point.
  - sig_mult: ESSIGMA1 schedule after 3 rejects (×0.5..×2, so σ ≤ 0.004).
- **Accept:** the step candidate on the full band 142 plus dev 0-19 (V56 guard). It is accepted when all four hold:
  - `F > best`;
  - band Δtheirs t < 2 (gift-free);
  - band net ≥ 0;
  - dev20 net ≥ 0.
- **Saved candidates:** every step candidate with band net ≥ **+2** goes to `run1/cands/cand_gNNN.{npy,json}` together with
  `cand_gNNN_band.tsv` (per-seat base/cand ours/theirs/margin/flip). This happens whether or not it was accepted, and the json
  records the gift flag and the dev20 result.
- **Generation cost:**
  - 16 × 71 = 1,136 train eps plus 162 accept eps = 1,298 eps = 130 ten-episode jobs.
  - Measured locally, one job takes ~65 s per worker in steady state.
  - So a generation is ≈ **30-35 min at 5 workers** and ≈ 75 min at 2 workers.
- **Workers:**
  - `set_procs` runs before every run call. It uses 2 workers while `pgrep -c -f floorhold1` > 0, otherwise the cap of 5.
  - `run1/procs_max` overrides the cap.
  - Everything runs under `nice -n 10`.

## 4. How a candidate is judged
1. **Band gate.** A candidate in `run1/cands/` must show band net ≥ +2 with Δtheirs t < 2 and dev20 ≥ 0 (see its json).
   Re-run it with `run_local.sh eval <npy> <tag>` → `gates.tsv` plus a per-seat table.
2. **STACKJIT ship legs** (TRAINING-PROTOCOL §1.7, paired vs the live package, SAFETY_S 1e9 + REPAIR_MS 1e7), with the candidate as `theta.npy`:
   - dev100 ≥ +1, held100 ≥ +1, FRESH300 ≥ +3, tapes ≥ 0, faithful ≥ 0, Δtheirs t < 2.
3. **Reacting cross-check vs the pool kernels** (S/pool1/bank + S/pool2, POOLCHECK1 harness). Band tapes are **open-loop**: the
   rival replays its recorded actions and cannot react to our new mix. A theta that "wins" by, say, grabbing melon early can fail
   against the live kernel that reacts to the price. The candidate must hold pool net ≥ 0 and V-series net ≥ 0.
4. **BANDLEG1 `leg.py` in the real engine** re-confirms the harness band number.
5. **Ship path:** candidate → `submission/theta.npy` is NOT overwritten in place. The ship is a new package built by
   `scripts/package_submission.py` with the candidate theta, into `dist/`. Uploads are the user's.
