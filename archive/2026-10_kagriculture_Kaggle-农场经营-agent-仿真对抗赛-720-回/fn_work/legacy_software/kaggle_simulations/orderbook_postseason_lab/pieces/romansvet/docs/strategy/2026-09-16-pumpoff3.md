## Why
Turning the opening pump off is not a small local edit: the pump changes what is bought and
sold on turn 0, and the end-of-day shop draw is one RNG draw per EMPTY tile, so every later shop
in the game re-rolls (`shop-lottery` memory, +-25k per board). That shows up directly in the
numbers -- the increment's board-level sd is 1971 against 817 for the SELL_SLOT_PRIORITY
increment measured on the same 169 boards, i.e. the same statistic is 2.4x noisier for the same
sample. The +335 mean therefore sits inside the re-roll spread rather than on top of it, and it
is carried by ONE leg (BAND180 +797) while the two 30-board legs it was supposed to agree with
are level or negative. Consistent with the earlier reads of the same switch -- +410 t 2.4 alone,
+367 t 2.2 on LOT4@17 -- this is the third time pump-off has landed at +300..+400 with t ~ 2.2
and never reached t 3: the effect size is real but small, and it does not move outcomes at all
(0 net flips here, 2W/8L on the LOT4@17 read). Pump-off stays a coins-only, outcome-neutral
perturbation, so the pair ships alone and the family is closed at this bar.
