## 7. What the search says (mechanism)
- **Only one family moves own coins: REINVEST_DAILY (BRAINSTORM2).** Every rung-0 axis that is not REINVEST is inert or negative on
  the reacting rival: SHEEP_FIRST / WOOL_FIRST on d1-2 are EXACT no-ops (0/10 games differ: PFS's dawn-d1 purse is ~200 coins, the
  zero-slack invariant), LAND_DAY moves are no-ops or losses (q2d5/q2d6/q3d10/q3d11 identical; q3d8/q3d9 -1.4..-2.9k, as LAND1),
  YIELD replant +0.2k, HERD_PLAN floors -0.5..+1.2k, FEED_ALL alone -3.4k (t -2.4), and 10 of 12 head-bias cells are flat or lose up
  to -5.7k (t -5.4). No HEAD_WIN cell alone reaches +2k own.
- **The one joint effect: gb2[6] (animal share) +0.75 on d1-9 lifts REINVEST.** On the same 40 games: 4:600 +1,332 -> +3,253 margin,
  4:200+FEED +3,057 -> +5,258; the rival's gain from REINVEST (+2.5k) goes to ~0 (-0.2k / -1.3k): the extra animal share keeps PFS's
  own cows (herd d9 C5.2-5.6 vs C4.7-4.8), so the rival no longer takes our milk book. Every other head bias on top of REINVEST
  dilutes it (a2m, h6m, y_c1, hp_d5 pairs all below the anchor alone).
- **It does not survive the harder reads.** Boards 21-40 cut the pair's margin to ~0 (80 g: +1.2k / +1.8k), m76 held-out gives own
  +2.7k (t 3.6) but margin -0.9k, and against flood every REINVEST cell loses own coins (-2.1k .. -3.2k): the sheep-first herd
  crashes the wool price (our wool -2.8k on +6 u) and cedes milk (-21 u) to the flood rival (+3.5k milk, +3.6k strawberry).
  **The cows-first lane order fixes it for 4:200 + FEED (FCSG): flood own +849 (80 g) instead of -2,805**, and on m76 it is the
  strongest cell (own +4,118, margin +2,815); it does not rescue 4:600 + h6p (flood own -3,246). On the faithful live27 tapes every
  REINVEST body still loses the same seat (W 6 -> 5, rival +3.8..+6.0k): tapes cannot react, so this is context, not a verdict.
- **JUDGECAL1 exposure.** The REINVEST gains are strawberry (+2.3..+4.2k) and egg (+0.8..+3.9k) coins; melon is flat (+0..+0.6k).
  Strawberry is exactly the book the under-built g0capsfix rival leaves uncontested (half the real strawberries): the flood rival
  takes it back (our strawberry +0.3k, the rival's +3.6k), so read these own-coin gains as upper bounds.
