## Data and method
- **Games (`res/games.tsv`).**
  - The 7 live PFS games vs top teams (TOPAUDIT3 `logs/pfs_hits.json`; akmr, DSM x2, Fourth Quadrant x2, My second life, Mother-Goose; all lost, -28.7k mean).
  - The 10 newest TOPAUDIT3 `PQ4top-P48` games in which the PQ4 top seat bought Q4 by d12. All are DECEM, 5-5 vs DSM x5, Vadim x2, akmr, Mother-Goose, Anton.
- **Replay (`rep.py`)** = the IDLEOPS method (`S/idleops/idle.py` helpers imported unchanged):
  - Every unit's recorded action goes through the engine's `_apply_unit_action` on a copy of the previous state, in interpreter order, with the atomic-PLANT block.
  - Effective = the engine changed position, tile, inventory, seeds or shed. IDLE = PASS + no-op.
  - It reproduces TOPAUDIT3 exactly: PFS 691 d0-29 / 421 d14-29, top teams 264 / 74.
  - Per (game, seat, day, hour) it records: units; idle by IDLEOPS cause and unit-day position (dawn / gap / tail); effective ops by kind; HARVEST product units; units delivered to the shed (DROP / PLACE-shed); WATER yield increments; PLANT by crop; public prices; and a day-end herd/plant tally (fed / cared / unfed, unwatered in-window, fertilizer bonus lost).
  - SELL units and coins come from TOPAUDIT3's exact ledger logs (`led_*.jsonl`).
- **Values.** Harvest value = units x that day's mean public price. Ceilings use the next day's price (the product lands at the next production) and stop at d27, because later product is not sold.
- **Scripts:**
  - `agg.py` -> `res/hour_*.tsv`, `cause_*.tsv`, `theirops_{strict,excess}_*.tsv`, `crew_*.tsv`, `herdwater_*.tsv`.
  - `ceil.py` -> `res/ceiling.tsv`; `q4rev.py` -> `res/q4rev.tsv`; `lotprice.py` -> `res/lotprice.tsv`.
  - "strict" = hours where we idle > 0 and they idle 0; "excess" = hours where our idle > theirs.
