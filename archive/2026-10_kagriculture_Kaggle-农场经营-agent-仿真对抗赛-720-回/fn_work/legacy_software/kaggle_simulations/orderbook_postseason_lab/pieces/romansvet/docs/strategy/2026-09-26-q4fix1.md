# Q4FIX1: the Q4 relay with admission COUPLING, a d10 buy, density and mix (2026-09-25T17:40Z): CLOSED

**Question.** Q4ROOT1 found the Q4 relay hired its hands but never got its tasks admitted. It asked for the coupling: relay tasks admitted AND hands sized from those tasks. With that fixed, and Q4 bought on d10, does our Q4 reach the full dose (80 or more relay plantings), cut the rival's purse (Δtheirs < 0) and raise the margin?

**Answer: no.** The coupling works: 76-81 relay plantings and 286-324 Q4 units a game, a full FQ dose, and the rival's wheat falls by 2.3-3.3k. But every cell loses 23 to 38 flips out of 50, to the Fibonacci bill and to our lost animal sales. Branch `relay12`, on top of RELAY12 7cc424e0.

## 1. Build (all OFF by default; OFF is byte-identical)
- `Q4_RELAY_ADMIT` does three things:
  - It places relay plantings on Q4 only (RELAY12's "after" placement) and adds them to the mandatory tier.
  - It admits in two pools: relay tasks and all mandatory tasks first (group 3), then exactly the set the planner's own crew (`q4r_h0`) admits on its labour share (group 2), then the tail.
  - It sizes the relay's hands on top of `h_star`, with no cap. A second `_derive` at the planner's crew counts the relay's turns (ops + EST_MOVES on `_q4r_mask` tiles), and `e = ceil(turns / Q4_RELAY_TPU=16)`.
- Other switches:
  - `Q4_RELAY_SPARE` charges the relay's turns to the planner crew's spare admit labour first and hires only for the rest.
  - `Q4_RELAY_DENSITY`: "FQ" plants 13 / 10 / 6; "DSM" plants 8, then 4 a day.
  - `Q4_RELAY_CARROT`: percent of relay plantings sown as carrot (own seed walk, Q4 placement, survival water).
  - `Q4_MIX`: the planner's asks keep the Q4 slots.
  - Buy day: RELAY12's `Q3_DAY=9`, `NO_RESERVE` and `BUY_SLACK=1` give a Q4 buy on d10.3 (49/50 boards).

- Harness: `S/q4fix1/`. `measure.py` is RELAY12's census plus the dawn wheat quote, the rival's sold_n/sold_rev, per-product sales for BOTH seats (SELL rows capped by the pre-step shed, priced by the engine quote walk), and our work ops by type. `table.py` builds the tables (`grid.txt`). `basei` (instrumented base re-run) matches `base_0` on all 50 purses.

## 3. What the coupling fixed, and what it could not
1. **Density is fixed.** Relay plantings rise from 37.5 (FQ, RELAY12 `fqd`) to 60.6-81.0.
   - Admitted = post-VRP = done, and relay crops died 2 fewer per game. SPARE reaches 81 plantings and 324 Q4 units, the dose Q4ROOT1 said denial needs.
   - On d11-14 the ask of 12 / 10 becomes 1-6 plantings: after the 4k land the purse (dawn d12 2.2-3.2k vs 5.1k in base) funds few seeds and hands, and d10-14 hands fall from 8.8 to 7.9-8.8.
2. **The hands are the dear ones.** The relay adds 2.3-3.0 hands a day on d15-27 (base 9.7-10.3, relay 12-13.4, peak 15.6).
   - These are fib ranks 11-15 (144-610 coins each): +5.1..+9.0k a game, or 24-37 coins per Q4 unit against a wheat price of 33.
   - SPARE saves only ~1.3k, because the planner's own tasks usually fill its crew's admit labour. Our own P&L is −6.0..−10.0k in every cell.
3. **Denial is real and small, as Q4ROOT1 predicted.** The rival's wheat falls from 530 u @ 38.6 to 496-506 u @ 34.6-36.0, which is −2.3..−3.3k of its revenue, mostly after d19.
4. **Displacement hits our animal products, not our crops.**
   - Our fert falls 60-91 u a game, egg 22-51, milk 14-35 and wool 5-23. The rival sells the same volume into these thin markets at better prices: milk +0.6..+2.9k, wool +0.3..+2.2k, fert +1.4..+2.6k.
   - The gift follows the **buy day**, not the relay's size: the d11.1 buy (a) gifts +2.3k; the d10.3 buy with Q3 on d9 (b-f) gifts +5.7..+7.3k, even at 50 plantings (c).
   - We also cannibalise our own wheat: the planner's Q1-3 wheat falls 44-174 u as its wheat quote drops, so the relay's net wheat is +150..+200 u.
5. **PASS and idle (coordinator lesson 2): the relay does not use the idle turns.**
   - PASS/day goes from 25.5 to 25.6-27.4, h21-23 idle from 398 to 402-423 a game, and h0-2 idle from 55 to 77-85 (the extra hands' spawn wait at h1).
   - The idle comes in slivers: ~2.5 turns per hand-day, ~1.2 of it at h21-23. A Q4 stop costs its op plus a 3-8 turn walk.
   - The VRP puts the crew bill first (it drops only a WHOLE hand whose stops fit the others) and the sum of finish times second. So relay stops ride on the extra hands, and each extra hand brings its own sliver.
   - Nothing at end of day blocks h21-23: the `late` bound applies only to expiring harvests, and the route end is 24.
   - The top teams' ~0 PASS comes from more admitted work than their crew can do. That is a planner-supply property, not a scheduler constraint.

## 5. Verdict: CLOSED (6th Q4 study)
No cell reaches the dev bar (net ≥ +2); the best is (a) at −23. So dev 50-99, held-out100 and FRESH300 were not run, there is no package, and the switches stay OFF.

A full-dose Q4 on our farm costs 5-9k in hands at ranks 11-15 plus the 4k land, and gains 2.3-3.3k of wheat denial. The early buy that gives it the dose also gifts 5-7k of animal-product margin. Against reacting V56, all three terms point the same way at every density, mix and buy day we tried.

It could pay only with:
- a level crew of about 12 whose Q4 hands are ranks 2-11 (FQ's d0-9 crew is 6.8, with no late melon);
- a herd bought before the land, not after it;
- a planner that treats Q4 wheat as additive to its Q1-3 wheat. Each of these is a whole-farm redesign, not a Q4 switch.
