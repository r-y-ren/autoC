## Verdict

* **κ does not improve at any σ in a way that generalises.**  σ = 0.01 raises the
  in-sample κ from 0.003 to 0.030 (obj_w) / 0.058 (win_w) in both seeds, but the
  +d step's own gain is zero (+0.002 obj_w, −0.004/+0.012 win_w); the antisymmetry
  is the −d side losing, i.e. the small-σ estimate resolves a sharp, board-specific
  feature of the 147 pinned in-sample boards (the CRN "reproducible but
  board-specific" gradient of 2026-09-06), and it is gone held-out (κ 0.012 vs 0.008
  at 0.02, within one random sd).  σ = 0.04 is ≈ 0 with seed-dependent sign; σ = 0.08
  is **anti-aligned** in-sample in both seeds (win_w z −3.6/−2.8) and has the worst
  held-out win rate (−13 % / −7 %): at 0.08 the member spread is 11–12k coins,
  4–5× the 0.02 spread, and the estimate reads curvature, not slope.
* **Recommendation: no change — keep `--sigma 0.02` for flow191+.**  Do not
  raise it (0.04 neutral, 0.08 harmful).  0.01 is not worth an arm either: its
  only effect is in-sample, its gradient noise amplification is 2× (‖g‖ 107 vs
  41 for the same rank-normalised advantages) and the held-out +d loses.  Sigma
  joins pop (B) and margin_scale as a non-lever; the remaining ES levers are the
  fitness/rung composition and the held-out gate, not the estimator's knobs.

## Dead ends / caveats

* Two eps seeds; each z is one direction, so the seed agreement in sign (0.01 positive,
  0.08 negative, both metrics) is the evidence, not the pooled κ.  Boards are B's.
* σ = 0.02 reuses msab's rollouts, so its row equals the ms 3000 row of
  2026-09-11-margin-scale-AB.md.  GPU0 was contended (rollouts 160 s vs 74 s); XLA's
  three preallocation retries before settling at 13.4 GB are benign.
* ‖g‖·σ is scale-free under rank normalisation and says nothing about signal.
