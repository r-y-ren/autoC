## 1. What shapes the V56 programme (v56kernel.py, 6,751 lines, ~40 stacked layers)

**Route tapes (data, not scalars).** `_R108_DATA` holds 41 per-hour route tapes (farmer + hands + market rows). Route 0 plays
d0-5, then at d6 the router picks a route by the first two unlocked shops (`_R108_SHOP_ROUTES` / `_R110_OLD_SHOPS` / `_V92_TABLE`,
yarn-rival table `_V93_ROUTE_BY_RIVAL`), and route 2 from d27. Route 0 as installed (`_alt_install('HybridOpening')` + the CL

The promotion rule was net >= +4 flips with dtheirs faithful t >= -1. No arm met it (best = 0 flips), so no hold32 run was made.

## 5. Verdict: NO SHIP

V56's scalar body constants are not a lever on the MELON seats it is handed. Across the full named grid, 12 arms plus 2 pairs, on
20 paired seats, the result is 0 wins gained, and the only flips are -1 on a 3-coin seat. The purse effects (<= +154 ours/seat)
are about 1/35 of the median loss gap. Two of the six knobs are dead. The remaining MELON losses are decided by the route-tape
programme (melon plate, hires, the d6 shop route): those rows are hand-route data, rewritten at h0/h1 by INHERIT, not scalars. A
shortened 20-seat read cannot hide a +4-flip arm, because the per-seat movement is too small to cross the loss gaps (only the
3-coin seat ever crossed). vrp15 / V4 configs: keep the V56 defaults. The v56tune1 code (V56_* switches, all None) is inert and
kept only as a tool.

Side finding (not this stream's question): forcing the handoff on non-whitelist cash wins 5 of this prefix's 16 v3 losses: h1 1,
2046, 117 (already in V4MAX1 v4g), 163 and 76 (1 seat each).
