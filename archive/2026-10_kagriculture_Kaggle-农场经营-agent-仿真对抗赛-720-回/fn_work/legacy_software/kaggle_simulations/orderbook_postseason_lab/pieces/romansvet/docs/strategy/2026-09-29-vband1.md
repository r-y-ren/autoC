## 3. Reading
- **The rival's late book is fixed.** V56 sells 247.4-248.2 strawberries in every cell and on both board sets. The body changes only the PRICE it gets:
  - m40 strawberry 88 (control) -> 123 (B8) / 101 (P8d3) / 93 (WOOL) / 98 (P8W).
  - Milk 94 -> 106-134; wool 129 -> 101-149.
- **Its gain is almost all d15-29:**
  - B8: +21.8k of +23.5k.
  - P8d3: +7.5k of +7.6k.
  - WOOL: +1.5k; P8W: +6.3k.
- This is the programme-clone shape ("our volume is denial"), now against a scripted V engine with a fixed late book instead of the clone. So the effect is not a clone artefact.
- **No pre-emption.** The V's own 72 melons sell @ 242 on d10-11 in every cell (72.0u@242 in all 10 rows).
  - Our plate melons arrive later and sell into that drained curve: B8 78 @ 149 (vs 205 against the clone), P8d3/P8W 24-26 @ 201-203.
  - Their d10-14 cash (+2.5k to +5.7k) does not pay for the smaller d15-29 book (-4.8k to -9.0k).
- **The herd is the price.**
  - B8 d9 herd: 3.0 cows / 0.1 sheep / 0.6 geese (control 6.1 / 4.2 / 1.6).
  - P8d3 5.3 / 2.7 / 1.3; P8W 4.2 / 4.8 / 0.3.
  - WOOL swaps cows for sheep (5.0 / 6.9 / 1.1): d0-9 cash 975 vs 2,393, d10-14 +2.0k, late milk 121.6 u vs 129.7 u, our late wool price 116 vs 146. Own -610, rival +1.4k.
- **Against the V the bodies are WORSE than against the clone for our own purse.** B8 own -9.2k vs +0.4k; P8d3 -1.5k vs +3.2k.
  - The difference is the melon price our plate meets: the V's plate is 12 tiles and sells first, while the clone's d10-17 wave left the plate 205.
- Every W change is a loss (+0 on every row). PFS control remains the best body against both rival families.
