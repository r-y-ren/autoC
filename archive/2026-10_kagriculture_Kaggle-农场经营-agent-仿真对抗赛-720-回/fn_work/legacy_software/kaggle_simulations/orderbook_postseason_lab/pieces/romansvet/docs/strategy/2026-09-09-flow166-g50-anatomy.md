`S/simprobe/base_4d1adb3fef.csv` **byte-exactly on all 44 boards** (max abs diff 0.0),
so `day_metrics=True` and the extra plan call changed nothing. And the sim's family
d-margin, **+212/game**, matches the remote engine gate's **+203/game** to 9 coins.

## 2. Two-purse: the whole family gain is their purse down, and ours is down too

| set | our purse | their purse | margin |
|---|---|---|---|
| FAM (20 games / **10 boards**) | **−1,925** | **−2,137** | **+212** (t +0.25) |
| LOSS12 (24 games / 12 boards) | −838 | −844 | +6 (t +0.01) |

The opponent is an action tape, so its rows are fixed: it sells the **identical
1,647.9 units over the identical 260.4 SELL rows** in both runs, and its revenue still
falls **−2,527**. Every coin of "their purse down" is a **realised-price** effect —
our extra carrot/milk/egg volume floors the quote the tape sells into. Our own gross
falls in step (sold_rev 131,666 -> 128,690, −2,976, on 22 fewer units at 0.6 coins/unit
less). This is the two-purse **denial** signature of `counterfactuals-overstate`: the
margin moved, but our own purse is strictly worse and the net (+212 on n=10 boards,
t 0.25) is inside the noise.

## 5. What could not be measured

* **PASS share and the unit-op histogram are missing.** The dump masked unit ops by
  `State.nhands`, which is **0 at plan time** (hands are hired inside the day's market
  row and the crew is cleared by `end_of_day`), so `ophist` and `plant_by_crop` came
  back all-zero. Hires per season (261 -> 260) is the surviving labour reading.
  Recovering PASS costs another ~25-minute compile.
* **Per-product income is ordered, not executed.** The sale ledger
  (`sold_n`/`sold_rev`/`sold_rows`) is per-seat totals only, so the per-product coin
  column is ordered qty x that day's quote. Season realised totals are exact and are
  quoted separately.
* No engine leg was spent here (the local engine judge on this theta was running
  concurrently and was not touched). Everything above is the pinned-town sim, which on
  these boards is the engine to ~85 coins.
* n = 10 boards (family) and 12 (loss12). Neither d-margin is distinguishable from 0.
