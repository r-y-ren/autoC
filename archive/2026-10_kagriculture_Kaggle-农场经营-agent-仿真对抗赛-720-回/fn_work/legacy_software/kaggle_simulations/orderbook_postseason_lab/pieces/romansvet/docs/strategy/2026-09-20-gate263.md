## 3. Diagnosis — the wide ask/seed/hire space does move work, and the work is not worth its cost
Against **head-off** the wide head is a genuine, gift-free gain: `dOURS` **+98/+100 with dTHEIRS +2/+52**,
229 boards split 129/101 better, and mean `moves` rises 2,837 → **2,871/2,873** (+34) with `move_turns`
falling 598 → 594. So yes: the new `ask_fill` / `seed_buy` / wide-`d_hire` slots buy real extra volume —
about 35 extra acts a game packed into fewer turns, which is exactly the d10-19 under-request ENGTAIL
named — and unlike GATE260 the rival does not collect it (`dtheirs` t 0.03). That is the first clean
"volume without gift" result in the ACTIONRL line.

It is also not enough. The incumbent `head_940` reaches **mean ours 104,602** with only 2,859 moves;
the wide head reaches **104,400/104,402** with 2,871–2,873 moves. It spends ~14 more acts per game than
the incumbent and ends **~200 coins poorer** (t −2.4) — the marginal wide act is coin-negative, and
`unsold` is 0.00 everywhere, so nothing is being wasted at the sale end; the loss is upstream, in what the
ask/seed floors commit purse to. The wide action space is therefore not the binding constraint the
ACTIONRL11 premise assumed: v1's 92 logits already reach the productive part of this space, and the extra
33 logits mostly let PPO over-commit. Combined with GATE261 (λ=2 prices the gift but pays it from our own
purse) the pattern is now consistent across two independent axes: **every head that suppresses the gift
does so by spending our own coin**, and none has beaten `head_940` on margin.
