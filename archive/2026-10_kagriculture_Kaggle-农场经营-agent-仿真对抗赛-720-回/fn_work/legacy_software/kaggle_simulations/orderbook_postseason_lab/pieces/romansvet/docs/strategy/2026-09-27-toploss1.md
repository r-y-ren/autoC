## 4. Probe decision (step 5)
- No recurring action is both not-done and unmeasured, so no paired probe was run. I did not re-run any closed grid.
- The one context no one has tested is **late tomato gated on a PUMPER rival.** Build design:
  1. Gate on the rival's public product buys d0-2. Pumpers show product spend > 1k on d0 (15/28) or d2 (12/28), so this needs an opp-read in the planner; today DayView carries our board only.
  2. When the gate fires, set ENDGAME_TOMATO_ON with TILES 12, DAY 9, LAST_DAY 18, only in towns with a tomato buyer.
  3. Take the tiles from d10-19 wheat, which is what the winners do: wheat plantings p1 58 against 95 for the pumper.
  4. Judge on the reacting pool's pumper agents plus the 4 vrp10 pumper losses.
  5. Expected: ≤ +1 flip per 47 live games.
     - vrp10 is 6-4 vs pumpers (10/47 games); 3 of the 4 losses are within 5k (−1.9k, −4.4k, −5.0k).
     - The tomato mechanism already lost on the no-rival-tomato seed of 2026-09-04: +5.7k tomato, but −42.9k from wool and strawberry lost to crew displacement.
     - TOMATO15's realised price fell 112.9 → 85.3 at 12 tiles. Our tomato yield per planting is far below the winners' 7.4 u.
  - **Not recommended.**

## 5. Verdict: NONE reproducible
- The top-10's losses come from inside the MELON-plate family.
- Every winner action is either already ours (no pump, no Q4, strawberry, carrot ≤ 40) or an opening-package piece that was measured as a gift or an own loss on our ZERO opening (melon plate, early crew, sheep/wool, geese/eggs, Q3 d8, herd/fert, care, tomato).
- The winners' edge is a package, not a portable action: the d0 plate funds the d0-9 crew, the herd and Q3.
- Our own losses (opp − ours, n 11, +12.3k) are WOOL +5.9k, MELON +5.5k, FERT +3.0k, EGG +1.6k and MILK +1.6k, which are the same closed opening-package axes.
