# TURNS: where B's crew-turns go, against ymg_aq's

**VERDICT — there is NO turn sink in B.  B's turn *economy* matches ymg_aq's;
what B is short of is turns.**

Per grown unit sold B spends **4.89** worked crew-turns against ymg_aq's
**5.03**, and per worked turn B realises **17.52** net-market coins against
their **17.96** (−2.4 %).  Every op class is at or below their per-unit rate:
B moves 0.92 turns per task op to their 1.05, waters 965.8 times to their
1,254.9, and wastes 122.9 waters to their 351.9.  On the band set the same two
ratios are 4.86 vs 4.82 and 18.37 vs 18.10 — dead level.

The gap is the **denominator**: B works **5,986 turns/game to ymg_aq's 6,681**,
a **695-turn (−10.4 %) shortfall**.  At B's own realised 17.52 coins per worked
turn those turns are worth **12,150 coins/game**, against the measured sim
margin of −13,722 (`2026-09-14-top5-ledger.md` §1).  **The whole top-five gap
is inside the crew-turn budget.**

It splits into three, and the largest is one planner constant:

**ymg_aq PASSes ZERO turns at hours 0 and 1.**  Its farmer's hour-0 op and its
crew's hour-1 op are both **PICKUP** — off the previous night's shed, which
needs nothing from today's turn-1 BUY row.  B's schedule (`plan.py` §"the day",

pick up at turn 1, is **off**.

## 0. Method

`S/turns/instrument.py` is `S/replant/instrument.py`'s `sim.units.apply_units`
wrapper fused with `S/dev_slope/ledger.py`'s per-item `_market_turn`, with the
counters widened to the whole op alphabet and kept for **both physical seats**.
On a `--tape-actions` board `rollout.run_day` splices the recorded seat's 24
unit ops into `uop[seat]` *before* `apply_units` runs, so one wrapper observes
our planner's crew and ymg_aq's crew **inside the same game, through the same
`real` roster mask, at the same turn**.  No separate tape parse, no roster
guessing.  `src/` is read from a frozen `git archive HEAD src` (HEAD `d9b0ddb`)
in the scratchpad, because another agent is editing `plan.py`.

Three checks that the counting is sound:

* B's numbers reproduce the independently-measured REPLANT-SCOPE ones exactly:
  184.2 effective PLANTs/season (REPLANT-SCOPE 184.2), 141.2 one-time harvests
  (141.2), 687.0 PASS of 6,673.4 active unit-turns = 10.3 % (687.0 / 6,673.4).
* ymg_aq's op budget read off the *sim* matches the one read straight off the
  tape file (`S/turns/tape_ops.py`, roster from the tape's own HIRE rows) on
  every class but PASS: PLANT 240.4 vs 241.3, WATER 1,254.9 vs 1,260.5, HARVEST
  446.8 vs 446.8, MOVE 3,413.0 vs 3,413.0.  PASS differs by exactly the total
  (342.5 vs 702.5; 7,024.0 vs 7,384.0 active turns, both differences 360.0)
  because the file-side roster is "hires issued today" for all 24 hours, while
  the sim only counts a hand from the turn after it is hired — the 360 turns
  are slots at hours 0-2 whose hand does not exist yet.
* The market ledger closes against the top-five ledger: net market (sales −
  purchases) B 104,642 vs ymg_aq 120,158, difference **−15,515**, against
  `2026-09-14-top5-ledger.md` §2's independently measured **−15,483**.

Travel is the single largest op class for both seats and it is **not** the
sink: B walks 0.92 steps per task where ymg_aq walks 1.05.  Mechanism
(`plan._routes`, read not edited): `build_day` admits tiles by (mandatory tier,
value) against the day's turn budget, each costed at its ops plus `EST_MOVES`
and the walk out of the shed (`EST_LEAD`), then routes the admitted set as
**one serpentine sweep of priced-or-mandatory work followed by one of
zero-value work**; `_routes` cuts that single order into one contiguous block
per unit and charges the **exact Manhattan moves between consecutive tiles**.
Two groups, not four, because "a group of m scattered tiles spans the whole
board however small it is".  Shed round trips are charged explicitly: pickups
are `P_u` = 0-3 turns at the head of each block (`land_lead` is a floor under
them, not an addend), and `DROP_ON`'s return leg adds `DIST_SHED(e) + 1` to a
block ending at rank `e` on the one day it fires — a non-monotone tail, which
is why that cut takes the largest satisfying rank directly instead of the
`_count_le` walk.  B's 320.4 shed ops/game are 5.4 % of worked turns.

## 4. WATER — the rule, and who wastes it

**The sim's rule** (frozen HEAD `sim/eod.refresh_plants` + `sim/units`):

```
eod:   cons  = (t_water == 1) ? 0 : t_cons + 1
       died  = is_PLANT & (cons >= 2)        -> tile becomes WEED
plant: t_cons = 1, t_water = 0
water: t_water = 1;  yield += 1 (2 if fertilised) ONLY IF
       CROP_ONGOING == 0  and  CROP_WINDOW_START <= age <= CROP_MAX_YIELD_DAY
```

Two consequences, both deterministic:

1. **A tile planted today dies tonight unless it is watered today** (`t_cons`
   starts at 1).  After one watering a tile may skip exactly one day.  So the
   floor is *one water per two tile-days*, plus one on the planting day.
2. **Watering an ONGOING crop (tomato, strawberry) never adds a unit.**  It
   buys the day of life, and it is the gate on the fertiliser bonus
   (`fertilized = was_watered & (t_fert >= day)`).

Minimum crew-turn cost of one grown unit, straight from the table
(`S/turns/crop_turn_cost.md`): WHEAT 6 turns / 4 units = **1.50**, CARROT
5 / 3 = 1.67, MELON 10 / 6 = 1.67.  A unit **bought** costs **0** crew turns.

dies without them.  Only **16.8 wheat waters a game are strictly droppable**,
0.3 % of worked turns.  **Watering is not the sink.**

Hand-days: d0-9 B **44.5** vs 52.3, d10-19 B **110.1** vs 110.7 (parity),
d20-29 B **106.6** vs 114.7.  **The crew gap is d0-9 and d20-29, not d10-19.**

**What the extra hands do.**  ymg_aq's marginal hands are not doing anything B
cannot: their hour-by-hour op mix is B's mix (WATER on top from hour 7 to hour
23, MOVE 42-58 %).  They are doing it *earlier in the day* and *on more days*.

**Turn-limited or task-limited?**  Both, in different hours.  B's crew is
**fully booked from hour 4 to hour 12 (0-1 % PASS)** and idle only in the tail
— and the tail is where B and ymg_aq are identical (hours 20-23: B 304.0 PASS,
ymg_aq 311.5).  So a marginal hand at d10 buys 22 turns of which roughly 18 are
worked at the crew's prevailing rate; the crew is **turn-limited in the middle
of the day and task-limited only after hour 20**.  It is not a cash constraint
either: at d20-28 B holds 41k-78k coins and three more hands cost 371-1,094 a
day.  Three hands at d10 = 66 turns/day at 17.52 coins = **~1,150 coins/day of
net market against a 239-coin bill** — the fib wage is ~5x underpriced against
B's own realised turn value, so the binding thing is the **hire enumeration's
value model** (pass A prices the tile order once, scores each h as "value of
the prefix (h+1) units' turns can admit, less h's fib bill", ties to fewer
hands) plus the `cash_reserve` (tomorrow's bill one hand wider), not the wage.

**Caveat on the water classification.**  Each water is classified from the
state at the *start* of its turn, so a second unit acting on the same tile in
the same turn can be mis-binned.  The instrument counts those: **B 1.3 such
turns a game, ymg_aq 116.7** (1.7 % of their worked turns).  The direction of
the §4 result — B wastes less water than ymg_aq — is far larger than that
bound, but ymg_aq's 351.9 "strictly redundant" figure is an upper bound on
their waste, not a point estimate.

## 7. The one next experiment

**Start the crew's day at hour 0/1 instead of hour 2.**  266.3 unit-turns a
game — 4.0 % of B's active budget and 38 % of the whole 695-turn shortfall —
are PASS at hours 0 and 1 while ymg_aq's crew is already doing shed PICKUPs.
Two switches already exist and are off/partial:

1. `PRESTOCK_ON` (`plan.py:2075`, **False**) buys tomorrow's BUY_PRODUCT at
   turn 20 tonight so tomorrow's crew can PICKUP at turn 1 — exactly the op
   ymg_aq's hour-1 crew issues.  It was measured as a "+2.4-4.8k/game lever" in
   2026-08-30 and never landed.
2. The farmer alone holds hour 0 (30 active turns/game, **100 % PASS** for B,
   **0 % PASS** for ymg_aq).  The farmer is not hired, so no "a hand hired at
   turn t acts from t+1" law applies to it; `O.ROUTE_BASE` = 2 is a *schedule*
   constant, not an engine law, for that one unit.

Arm: `PRESTOCK_ON` on, plus a farmer block whose base is turn 0 when its first
op needs nothing from the BUY row, paired CRN on the 12 ymg + 68 band boards,
gate = LEG20 paired Δmargin at t ≥ 2.  Expected size if the turns convert at
B's own 17.52 coins/turn: **+4,700 coins/game**, i.e. a third of the −13,722.
