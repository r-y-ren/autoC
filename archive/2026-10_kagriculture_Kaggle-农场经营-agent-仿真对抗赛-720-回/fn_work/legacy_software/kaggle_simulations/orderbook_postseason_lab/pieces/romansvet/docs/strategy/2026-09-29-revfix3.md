## 1. Premise (code, cited)

**Where the episode ends.** `kaggriculture.json:8` sets `episodeSteps: 720`. `interpreter` (`kaggriculture.py:894-966`, kaggle-environments 1.32.7, source sha pinned in `vendor/engine.lock.json`) does the steps in this order:

1. `step = obs0.step`.
2. Unit actions: for each seat, the farmer then the hands (`:913-939`).
3. `_process_market` (`:941`), `_town_consume` (`:942`) and `_decay_plants`.
4. End-of-day, only when `(step+1) % 24 == 0`.
5. `if step >= cfg.episodeSteps - 2` (`:960`): every agent is set DONE with `reward = farms[player]["money"]` (`:963`).

So step 718 (d29 h22) is the last step the engine resolves, and h23 (step 719) is never interpreted. The JAX port has the same cut (`sim/rollout.py:600`, "the engine stops at step episodeSteps - 2 = 718"). The score is money only. Any unit still in a hand or in the shed after the step-718 market is worth 0.

**Where T acts.** The switch is `overflow.py:18`. The call is at `overflow.py:184-187`, inside V1's DROP loop, after `load > room`, and only when `obs['day'] == N_DAYS-1 (29)` and `hour == TURNS_PER_DAY-2 (22)`.
- `_terminal_deposit` (`:680-728`) writes only `uop/ua/uq[u, 22]` of the dropping unit. When a SELL row falls short it also writes that product's h22 SELL row (`_add_sale`). This never binds: the ENDROUTE row asks `ENDROUTE_ASK = 100` of every product at turn 22 (`plan.py:1029-1038`), and 100 is the shed cap.
- Nothing downstream can change the result:
  - V2 and V3 return on day 29 (`overflow.py:314`, `:555`).
  - The runtime calls V1 → V2 → V3 → `render.turn_action` (`runtime.py:1003-1009`), and `render.turn_action` is a direct row map (`render.py:57-66`).
  - `PROGRAM_ENGINE_ON`, `ROUTE_NN_ON` and `ROUTE_NN3_ON` are False (`plan.py:1552/7703/7708`).
- Result: T changes only our step-718 unit ops (and at most our step-718 SELL row). No later decision exists for either seat. Every step before 718 is byte-identical between the arms.

**What can still differ inside step 718 (the restriction).**
- **Our side.** The deposit fills our shed (`_apply_unit_action` DROP `:343-356`: cargo insertion order, the rest destroyed; PLACE `:377-410`: clipped to room, the rest kept in the hand). Our h22 SELL row then liquidates the shed.
- **The rival's side.** The market is per-unit lockstep across seats (`_process_market` `:544-628`). At each order index, both seats' current units are quoted from the same pre-commit inventory (`:612`) and then committed. Each sale above $1 adds 1 to the inventory (`_commit_unit` `:652-660`). So when our row sells a different mix, the quotes of the rival's same-turn sales of those products move. The rival's *actions* are fixed, but its *revenue* on step 718 can change.
- So "rival unaffected" holds for actions, not for coins. REVFIX2 saw -1 and this read sees +2. The read therefore does not assume d_theirs = 0. It replays step 718 through the engine for both arms and reports d_ours, d_theirs and d_margin = d_ours - d_theirs, all exact.

**Terminal value.** The final reward is money after the step-718 market. So:

delta = (money after step 718 with T's unit ops) - (money after step 718 with the recorded ops)

Each seat's money is summed over its h22 SELL commits at the engine's lockstep quotes. Kept, unsold units count for zero. There is no deposit valuation beyond the same-turn sale.

## 2. Method (`S/revfix3/tdelta.py`)

1. Load `steps[718]` (both seats' full observations, private included) and `steps[719]` (the actions taken at step 718, plus the final rewards) from the replay.
2. **Control.** Rebuild both seats' state and run `kaggriculture.interpreter` once with the recorded actions. The control must reproduce the recorded final money of both seats (`ctl_ok`).
3. **Pre-V1 plan.** Rebuild it from the record. A recorded PLACE of a product (or a PASS) by a unit on a shed-access tile counts as a converted DROP only if the worktree's own `overflow.guard()` with T OFF maps that DROP back to exactly the recorded op (fixed point over the units; `ident`). A MISMATCH would mean the reconstruction failed.
4. **T arm.** Run the same `guard()` with `TERMINAL_DEPOSIT_VALUE_ON = True` on the same plan. Substitute the changed unit ops (and the SELL row if it was topped up). Run the engine step again.
5. **Delta.** d_ours, d_theirs and d_margin come from the two engine runs.
6. **V1 early return.** V1 returns before its DROP loop when shed plus hands is 100 or less (`overflow.py:63-65`). T cannot fire in those games, and their delta is exactly 0.
