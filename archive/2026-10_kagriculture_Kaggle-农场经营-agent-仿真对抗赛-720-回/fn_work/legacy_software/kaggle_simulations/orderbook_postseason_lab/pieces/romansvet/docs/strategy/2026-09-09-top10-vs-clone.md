# 2026-09-09 — top ten vs the ~2000 clone: a paired census of recorded episodes

Measurement only. Nothing simulated, nothing run in the engine: every number below
comes from the recorded Kaggle episode jsons (`S/ep_<ep>.json`, both seats' full
state per step) and the pinned action tapes (`artifacts/tape_actions_town/<ep>.npz`).

Working files: `S/top10diff/` — `select.py`/`groups.json` (signature census),
`opening.py`/`opening.json` (day-0/1), `build.py`/`rows.csv`/`meta.json` (day ledger,
40 tapes), `an.py`/`an.txt`/`curves.json`/`diverge.json` (curves + divergence rank),
`shop.py`/`shop.txt`/`shopadapt.json` (shop-adaptivity), `rowsX.csv`/`metaX.json`
(control group).  `S = /tmp/claude-0/-mnt-e--work-kaggriculture3/8b388b22-9a4b-4bf0-8e3e-dc4aac1d5f04/scratchpad`.

Ledger machinery is `S/lossanat/ledger.py` reused verbatim (executed quantities from
recorded state deltas, engine price curve used only to price units that actually
moved).  Money residual is ≤1 % of gross on 19/20 CLONE tapes but 2–21 % on 8/20 TOP
tapes — the top seats put two-way rows on one item in one step far more often, which
the reconciler cannot split.  **Per-product income splits for TOP are therefore
indicative to ~±3 %; unit counts, tiles, herds and cash are exact.**

## 4. Does our planner already have these switches?

* **#1 tomato (+3.8k)** — yes: `ENDGAME_TOMATO_ON` (plan.py:1362, default **False**),
  with `ENDGAME_TOMATO_SHOPS = 2` gating on exactly the tomato-sink count this census
  finds the top ten reacting to. Verdict `2026-09-09-plateau-review-verdicts.md` §7:
  inert, and the late-tomato gap it was meant to close was **group selection** on
  hard boards, with the shop→mix response learned inside theta via
  `brain.residual_drain` (brain.py:208) rather than by a rule.
* **#2 carrot (+3.7k)** — no dedicated switch exists (`grep CARROT_` in plan.py returns
  nothing); the only mix-vs-town machinery is `OPP_MIX_ON` (plan.py:1572, **False**,
  with `OPP_MIX_SHOP = 32` giving penalty back per buying shop). Verdict §7: carrot
  late coins correlate +0.66 with carrot sinks, sink-matched the gap mostly vanishes,
  `OPP_MIX_ON` inert; a paired `PLANT_MIX_DRAIN` probe was queued, not landed.
* **#3 egg / goose (+2.8k)** — no switch: plan.py has no GOOSE or EGG lever at all
  (only `spec.I_GOOSE` indexing); herd composition is decided by the learned hiring/
  placement path. Verdict line 252 (herd composition) records the adjacent finding —
  revenue per animal-day tracks the season sink (wool +0.98, milk +0.89), we buy the
  animal of the *last* shop unlocked — and names `ANIMAL_MIX_DRAIN_ON` as a lever
  *under test*; no such constant exists in plan.py today.

## 5. What could not be measured

* Per-product income for 8/20 TOP tapes reconciles to 2–21 % of gross (two-way rows on
  one item in one step). Unit counts, tiles, herd, hands and cash are exact everywhere.
* The two groups do not share boards or opponents; only the CLONEX control isolates the
  opponent-supply effect, and it is a higher-rated set (2196–2737), so its carrot/tomato/
  egg baseline is if anything an over-estimate of the 2000 band's.
* Ratings come from the `S/kaggle/lb_now.json` snapshot, which is not the same date as
  the episodes; group membership by rating is approximate.
* The d10 melon dump *size* varies in the TOP group (sd 18 u) — whether that variation
  is a reaction to the board, the town or the opponent is not resolved here (n=20,
  every board different).
* Nothing here is a paired engine measurement: no coin size below is a promotion-grade
  estimate of what the same change would earn in *our* seat.
