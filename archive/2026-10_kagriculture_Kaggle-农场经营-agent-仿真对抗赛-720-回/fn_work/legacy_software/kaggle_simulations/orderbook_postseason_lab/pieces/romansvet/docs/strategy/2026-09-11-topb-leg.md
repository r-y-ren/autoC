## verdict

**Direction agrees, magnitude does not.** Both pairs beat the base and lift the
win rate, and their ordering is preserved (g170cpair ≥ g60pair). But the edge
halves against the in-sample TOP20 read — g60pair +5,424 t 4.84 (18/20) → +2,890
t 1.87 (15/20); g170cpair +6,067 t 5.46 (18/20) → +3,174 t 1.92 (12/20) — and
**both TOP20 PASSes become FAILs**: neither clears |t| ≥ 2, and g170cpair barely
clears the 11/20 positive-board floor. Roughly half of TOP20's measured top-tier
edge is training-set inflation. Treat TOP20 as a training diagnostic, not a
gate; TOPB is the top-tier judge. Both candidates lose the same two boards
(107247899 SpaTaro −15…−17k, 107246553 binghua −8k) — one shared failure mode.
