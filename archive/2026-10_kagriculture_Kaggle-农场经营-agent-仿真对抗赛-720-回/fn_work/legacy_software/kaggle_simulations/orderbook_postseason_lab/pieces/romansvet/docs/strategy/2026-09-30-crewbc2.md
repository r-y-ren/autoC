## A. Strawberry wall ledger (HOLD20, per game)

Script: `S/crewbc2/code/ledger.py`; tables in `S/crewbc2/ledger_v1.txt` and `tables.txt`.

**Mechanics, read from the sim (`sim.hpp` daily_refresh_plants).**
- A strawberry gets a yield tick every 2 days, starting at age 10, 4 ticks in all.
- A tick adds +1 unit, or +2 if the tile was watered AND fertilized that day. Fertilizer lasts day..day+2.
- Growth does not depend on water. The only water rule is that 2 dry days kill the plant.
- So the d15-17 supply = (tiles planted d4-d7) x (units per tick). Units per tick is set by fertilizer on the tick eve (d15 for the
  d6 wave). Harvesting at yield 1 loses nothing, as long as the pick lands inside the window.

**Verdict: NONE.** No v2 net beats m1:
- The two-head / weighted nets match m1 held out (per-step accuracy 0.95+ on the animal ops).
- Closed loop, they lose the animal chain the moment they drive it: bcani=3 costs 0.03-0.06, bcani=4 costs 0.18-0.26, with milk and
  wool halving.
- Per-step accuracy does not carry over to the closed loop. The chain errors compound (a missed feed = a lost yield day plus an unfed
  counter). BC on the teacher's states cannot see those states, so behaviour cloning alone cannot learn the animal chain.

## Spec for the Oct 1-15 crew learner (what the router must learn)
The body's gap to MMPQ is not routing accuracy. The per-hand BC router already matches MMPQ's next work tile 3 times in 4. The gap
is **three crew-split decisions that a per-step imitation cannot recover**:

1. **Strawberry wave and tick-eve care (the V56 invariant).**
   - Plant about 21 strawberry tiles by d7, from the d5-d6 cash, before animals. In the ST knobs this is emission, not learning.
   - On each tick eve (d15, then d19 = MMPQ's second pulse of 12.4), deliver about 15 fertilizer to the ripe strawberry tiles and water
     them that day.
   - Pick in the window (d15-17), not two days later.
   - Target: st15 >= 45 u. Our best is 33.9 (comboSFW), MMPQ 48.5, PFS 48-51.
   - The learner needs a fertilizer-carrier role: collect in the morning, hold, deliver on the tick eve. Today's dispatcher only
     fertilizes when a carrier happens to pass.
2. **Animal/build chain coverage.**
   - MMPQ per game: 378 feeds, 359 cares, 480 fertilizer collects, 153 animal harvests.
   - crewF4Sbc: 317 / 315 / 435 / 115. BC lam1.5: 342 / 336 / 429 / 204.
   - Cows on d6 are 3.2-3.5 vs 5.0, and geese on d12 are 5.8 vs 7.5.
   - Every body that lets a net drive this chain loses 25-60 % of milk and wool. The learner has to be trained **closed loop**
     (DAgger on its own states, or RL on the per-day unit value), with the chain's failure states (unfed counter, missed care)
     in its data.
3. **Wool herd and late animal volume.**
   - Wool 112-127 vs 154 and milk 122-142 vs 173 on HOLD20, with sheep and cows near MMPQ's by d12. So the loss is care coverage
     d10-29, not herd size.
   - crewF4Sbc + `feed_all_until=26` (PANELPREP1 0.920) is the only cell that moved it. It is the reference to beat.

Suggested objective for the learner: maximise, per day, the value of units that are sold with the V56-contested windows weighted up
(strawberry d15-17, milk/wool/egg d18-29). Hold the crop split fixed by the rulebook until the animal chain holds closed loop.
Gate: HOLD20 coins >= 0.90 AND value >= 0.90 AND st15 >= 40, then PANELPREP1's panel.
