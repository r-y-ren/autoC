## Rights gate

### A. Competition use allowed? YES, with constraints

Current rules state that Competition Data may be accessed and used for any purpose, including competition participation, research, and education. External data/tools are allowed when reasonably accessible to all participants. During evaluation, the submitted agent may not pull external information in or send information out, so derived strategy data must be packaged into the submission if used at inference time.

### B. Public Dataset redistribution allowed? SOURCE-SCOPED YES; direct Competition Data NO

The same competition rules prohibit transmitting, duplicating, publishing, or redistributing Competition Data to people who have not agreed to the rules. Therefore direct competition downloads are not a public release source.

Separately, Kaggle publishes daily `kaggle/kaggriculture-episodes-YYYY-MM-DD` datasets. Live metadata for `kaggle/kaggriculture-episodes-2026-08-31` states CC0-1.0 and describes JSON replays of completed simulation episodes. This separately licensed Kaggle Dataset is the preferred public provenance path. Public V1 should contain compact derived features/aggregates, not raw replay copies.

The broadly used `georgymamarin/kaggriculture-episodes` dataset is Apache-2.0 and useful for internal sampling/indexing. Before a public release derived from that source, retain its license/provenance and ensure the final release does not expose direct competition payloads or restricted information.

## Product hypothesis retained

Title: **Kaggriculture Strategy Meta & Agent Benchmark**

Subtitle: **Strategy families, openings, economy and matchup outcomes from public Kaggriculture episodes**

Target user: active Kaggriculture competitors who want to understand what strong agents do and what strategies counter other strategies.

## Why GO applies only to the narrowed V1

Demand is proven, rights have a defensible source-scoped path, and no exact duplicate was found. The categorical-family moat remains weak, so GO applies only after removing that claim from the core product:

1. `cow+strawberry` occupies 60/84 seats (71.4%), and score-band distributions are not usefully separated by the current label.
2. Only one real cross-family pair reaches five games; its Wilson interval remains wide even though both seat orientations are represented.
3. Seat 0 wins 64.3% overall and 74.1% in same-family controls, so seat/orientation is a material confounder.
4. Exact opening hashes are much more diverse than the categorical family labels, supporting a feature/fingerprint-first product shape.
5. A 46-column `strategy_meta.csv` candidate passed core schema QA on 96 official-CC0 rows and explicitly excludes team/submission/name identity fields.

## Pilot success criteria

- Recent episodes across low/mid/high rating bands parse reliably.
- Each seat yields meaningful opening/economy/action features with clear semantics.
- Engine-version handling is explicit.
- Deterministic pilot families are interpretable; no arbitrary clustering labels.
- Matchup rows can be computed with sample counts and confidence caveats.
- Derived V1 remains small and easy to load.
- No raw replay payloads enter Git/public release artifacts.

## Stop-loss

Stop or narrow the Dataset side if the pilot shows strategy labels are unstable/uninterpretable, matchup sample sizes cannot support claims, derived features add little beyond the existing episode feature table, or source/redistribution scope becomes unclear. Competition-side analysis may continue independently if compliant.
