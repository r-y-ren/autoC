* **Scratch centre** = `policy.init_theta(np.random.default_rng(0))` (the ES trainer's documented init, `src/kagg3/es/train.py:3632`:
  He-normal 2-D blocks, zero biases, zero sw/swb) with every block that is all-zero in theta7659 (gb3, gb4, mh, ms, cm, cb, cd, g12,

  flip at z ≥ 1.25 ≈ 2 flips/member); OVERFLOW_GUARD column frozen. 7,439 genes perturbed.
* **Pool (USER order)**: all 40 agents = POOL1 bank keep=1 (37, incl. abhinav0370_cha22_agent) + v56 (S/v56leg) + v57 + v15stack,
  official runner (`eval_vs_baselines._play`, actTimeout 600, fresh process per game, town pinned), boards LIVE250 idx 100-149 ×
  seat orig|flip (disjoint from dev 0-99 / held-out 150-249). Per gen 60 fresh triples (opponent, board, seat): every agent once

  (ravi, aurax7, reyhanksatria, web3cainiao, nusrati, andrewsokolovs, abhinav) → V ≈ 45 %, V+top ≈ 67 % of games.
  V56-in-sim (SIMGAP1) = the dev100 gate leg below (engine v56 is also in the pool).
* **Generation** = 6 antithetic pairs; pair p plays its own 10 triples with plus, minus AND the centre → **180 engine games/gen**,
  every member paired vs the centre on the same (opponent, board, seat). F = mean[win flip + clip(Δmargin, ±50k)/50k] (scratch
  margins are −40..−100k, so ±3k clips everything); centred ranks over the 12 members, w_p = r(+) − r(−), centre += lr·σ·Σw_p ε_p/‖w‖,
  **lr 1.0** (1 σ per gen). No gift term (scratch loses every game; margin carries it).
* **Throughput** (smoke 20:22Z, load 10-12 incl. knobvrp1): 35 s/game/worker → ~8.5 games/min at 5 workers → ~21 min/gen;
  gate every 3 gens (dev100 in-sim, shipped seat, VRP SAFETY_S 3.0 as ESV57, paired vs shipped theta7659 base rebuilt on this stage)
  ≈ 10 min → **~5-6 gens in my 3 h box**; the run continues after hand-back.
* **Expectation (stated up front)**: 7,439-dim ES from a random net at ~1,000 games/h cannot approach 86-14 in hours; the curve
  (pool W, mean margin, dev100) is the deliverable.

## 5. Read
From-scratch gene-block ES is feasible on the full reacting pool (40 agents, 0 voids, 10-20 g/min at 5 workers) but the signal per
generation is tiny: 6 pairs × 10 triples leave the 7,439-dim update ≈ noise, pool margin flat, dev margin +10k in 3 gens from −98k.
It cannot produce a shippable centre on any horizon we have before 09-30; its only value is as a curve. The GPU cannot speed it (CPU-bound).
