## (e) Cause proxy for our empty tile-days/game d0-27 (replay only, mapped to the GAPFIX1 classes)
- B = purchase day: the tile was locked at dusk d−1.
- T = turnover: the tile carried a crop at dusk d−1 and was harvested or died on day d. This is a 1-day gap, not a hole.
- The remaining classes are persistent: the tile was empty at dusk d−1 and d, so it was free at dawn and not planted. With P_d = the number of persistent tiles that day:
