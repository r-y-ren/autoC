## Mechanism (a vs b, day by day: `out/cmp_a_b.txt`)
1. **d14 h1, first divergence.** SLIVER adds one fill: BUY_SEED WHEAT 5 instead of 4, and PLANT W5 instead of W4.
   - SLIVER fires only twice in the whole game (d14 and d21; VRP stats `sliver 2`).
   - Every later difference is the cascade from this one fill: routes, sale lots, and a +6 wheat harvest on d19, which the planner sells the same day.
2. **d20-21: our shed is at the cap.** Shed totals are 98 / 100 in arm a against 96 / 97 in b. The shed holds strawberry 24, wool 25-29, fertilizer 23-24, melon 6 and wheat 12.
   - The planner's shed-room clip (`plan.py:10655`, `room = 100 − sum(shed)`, turn-1 LAW) grants the feed-wheat buy 2 / 0 in a against 4 / 3 in b (`wheat_avail = shed_wheat + wheat_buy`, `plan.py:11574`).
   - Planned feeds fall to 14 / 12 in a against 16 / 15 in b. This happens in the JIT planner itself, before the VRP router touches the plan.
3. **d21 eod.** Five cows and the goose are must-feed (t_cons 1), and so are 4 of the 18 sheep. The 12 wheat feed 11 sheep (7 of them *not* hungry: bank/care feeds ranked by `feed_value`, `plan.py:~10560`) and one cow.
   - 4 cows and the goose escape. The herd drops from G1 C5 S18 to G0 C1 S18.
   - b loses only the goose that night, to the same clip: room 3, buy 3.
4. **Result.** Milk sales fall from 152 u / 26,203 to 110 u / 17,640 (−8,563).
   - The other products roughly offset each other: wheat +1.7k, carrot +0.5k, strawberry +0.5k, melon −0.9k, wool −0.6k.
   - Net ours −7,347. Theirs +1,118 (the tape seat meets a thinner milk book).

## Is it systematic?
- **CARE_RIDE / SLIVER rules:** no misfire.
  - The SLIVER fill is legal and small: one extra wheat, and its harvest is sold.
  - The loss comes from how one 2-3 unit shift in shed occupancy lands on the base planner's shed-room cliff (must-feed wheat clipped by dawn room while the shed is jammed with low-value stock).
- **Prevalence on dev 0-9, both seats** (V56 reacting, a vs b; `out/starve_dev.txt`):
  - Room-bound escapes before d27 occur once per arm: one sheep on dev9 d22, identical in both arms. Every other escape is the d27-28 horizon value escape (shed wheat ≥ herd).
  - Stack A on these 20 games: W 20 → 20, Δours +9, Δtheirs −374.
- **Tape artefact?** No. The loss is entirely our own feed and shed decision. The tape seat's fixed actions play no role; its purse rises only through the market.

## Verdict: (1) BOARD NOISE
- One d14 SLIVER fill cascades into a base-planner cliff that already exists in vrp8_jit: b starves the goose on the same day by the same clip.
- It is not a rule of CARE_RIDE or SLIVER, so no fix switch was built.
- The vrp9_cs judgement (STACKJIT1/2) stands. The −7.3k counts twice in LOSSLEG1 because orig and flip are the same game.
- **Optional follow-up (base planner, not Stack A): FEEDROOM.** When `shed_wheat + room < must-feed count`, either:
  - add a post-lot-1 wheat buy row (turn ≥ 4, after the turn-3 lot frees room) for the must-feed shortfall, or
  - under a room-bound day, feed must-feed animals before bank/care-only feeds (7 sheep outranked 4 hungry cows here).
  - The upper bound on this board is about +8.5k milk. Prevalence before d27 on dev 0-9 is only 1 animal in 20 games, so check it first on herd-heavy boards (VLOSSBED).
