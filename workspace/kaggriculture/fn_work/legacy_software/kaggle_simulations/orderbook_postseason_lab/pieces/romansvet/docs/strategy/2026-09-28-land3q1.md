# LAND3Q1: why the top-5 clone never opens a 3rd quadrant by d9, and does forcing it win seats? (2026-09-28, 17:52Z-19:30Z)

Stream dir `S/land3q1/`. Clone = model3 + the BCBODY caretaker. The base cell is BCKNOB1's best, `c90_k1_w1_m1_f0` = GUARD=1 WATER1=1 CARE=1 CAP=90 END=1 M3=1.

## Verdict: CLOSED (bar not met)
- **The cause is (a), a literal cap.** The model does order the 3rd quadrant on d9 (p 0.79-1.00, rank 0), and the M1 per-day cap in `caps90.json` deletes all of those orders: BUY_LAND is 0 on d9.
- **Removing the cap works mechanically.** A LAND overlay opens the 3rd quadrant by d9 on 88-92 of 116 band seats (control: 10). Productive tiles at d9 rise from 49.6 to 57-58.
- **But it is worth coins, not flips.**
  - The T=70 cells gain **+0.5k..+1.2k per seat** (t 1.0-2.6) and **-2..+1 flips** vs the paired CARE=1 control.
  - Buying the 4th quadrant (T=90) costs **-3.1k..-4.6k** (t -7.6..-9.1).
  - On live27 (§B.2) the two best r5a2 cells (L7t70 and L8t70, identical there) win **1/14 faithful losses**, the same seat r4c won (satoshissss_114698801). They keep **1/4 faithful controls** (raw 2/6). Paired vs the CARE=1 control: +1/-0 faithful flips, dours +1,759 (t 1.50), dtheirs faithful -1,844 (t -2.37).
- **Mechanism.**
  - On the ~80 seats where the overlay fires, the clone's paired control loses by a median of **-30k..-33k**. Only 1 of those losses is within 2k.
  - Opening the 3rd quadrant 1-2 days early (the control opens it on d10 through the single d10 cap slot) adds ~11 tiles at d9 and **+1.2k..+1.9k** on those seats: about 1/20 of the loss.
  - The MELON gap is not land. BCKNOB1 and BCBODY4 measured it as sale pressure and production volume from d10 on (dtheirs vs V56 +10k..+17k). The land cap is a real bug and costs coins, but it is not the flip lever.
- **For BCBODY5:** fix the cap rather than train toward land. Either drop BUY_LAND from `caps90.json` (or set its d9 value to the teacher's), or charge the ledger at the real quadrant price. That is worth about +1k/seat at T=70. It is not a CANDIDATE: no cell reaches >= 2 faithful live27 wins with <= 1 control lost.

## A. Diagnosis: why the clone never opens a 3rd quadrant by d9 = (a) a literal cap
- **The model does ask for the 3rd quadrant, on day 9, and the caretaker's M1 market cap deletes every d9 order.**
  - The cap is `BCB_CAP=90` (`_cap_market`, `caps90.json`). Its BUY_LAND row allows orders only on 3 days: **d6: 2, d8: 5, d10: 1**. It is **0 on d7, d9 and d11+**.
  - Fast-env trace, r5a2 CARE=1, 5 MELON band loss seats, d4-d12 (`res/diag_ctrl.csv`, `res/diag_ctrl_sum.txt`):
    - **d9:** the market head orders land on 4-14 steps per seat. p(order) is 0.79-1.00 and "buy 1" ranks first (rank 0) on 5/5 seats. Max cash on d9 is 2,775-4,660, so the order is affordable. **0 orders survive the cap.**
    - **d10:** the single cap slot passes 1 order on 4/5 seats. The 3rd quadrant opens then, and the farm shows 3 quadrants at dawn d11. On the 5th seat (boominginging) the model does not order on d10 (p 0.47), so that seat **never** gets a 3rd quadrant.
    - **d8:** the model orders on 1/5 seats (2 steps). Cash crosses 2,000 on 3/5 seats, but only late (h16-h23).
  - Result: the clone holds 2 quadrants at dawn d10 on 106/116 band seats in the CARE=1 control, with **49.6 productive tiles at d9** (the rival has 57.1 on the band tapes and 68 on the live faithful seats).
- **Minor ledger bug in the same cap:** `_cap_market` debits every BUY_LAND order at 1,000, the 2nd-quadrant price. The 3rd quadrant costs 2,000, so an order placed with 1,000-1,999 cash burns a d8 slot without buying anything.
- **(b) is not the cause.**
  - Teacher data (`teacher_land.txt`; S/bcbody1/data_r5a2, 3,062 top-5 seat-episodes):
    - Q2 opens on d6 in 99 % of episodes.
    - **Q3 opens on d8 in 71 % and d9 in 29 %**, and 3,059/3,062 hold 3 quadrants at dawn d10.
    - Q4 opens in 28 %, on d10-11.
  - The teacher's dawn cash on d8 is a median of only 241: it buys land intra-day from same-day sales.
  - The model learned this timing. Its 3rd-quadrant order sits on d9, where the cap (built from the older teacher set) has 0.
- **(c) is not the cause.**
  - `decode_market` emits BUY_LAND 2nd in MORDER (after SELL, before HIRE / BUY_ANIMAL / BUY_SEED / BUY_PRODUCT), so land is paid before seeds within a step.
  - Cash is not gone on d9 (2.8k-4.7k peak).
  - The only affordability effect is on d8, where cash reaches 2k only at h16-h23.

## B. The LAND overlay (exact recipe)
`_land()` is in `S/land3q1/agent/main.py`. The identical code goes into the KERNEL2 clone body through `S/land3q1/patch_body.py`, with `os.environ` replaced by `_ENV`.
- **Switches:** `BCB_LAND=D`, `BCB_LAND_T=T`, `BCB_LAND_R=R`, `BCB_LAND_END=E`, where E = 14.
- **When it acts:** from the dawn of day D through day E, hours 0-20, on every step.
- **What it does:** while the unlocked quadrants' non-shed capacity (24 tiles per quadrant) is below T, and cash is at least the next quadrant's price (1,000 / 2,000 / 4,000) + R, it orders BUY_LAND x1.
- **Where it sits:** it runs after the per-day caps, so it bypasses the BUY_LAND cap, and it never removes an order. BUY_LAND is emitted 2nd, after SELL and before HIRE and the other buys.
- **What T means:** T=70 opens the 3rd quadrant (72 tiles); T=90 opens the 4th as well (96 tiles).
- **R = 800:** the teacher's one-day seed + feed order spend, median of d7 810 / d8 490 / d9 1,070 (`teacher_spend.txt`).
  - C = **1,800** for Q2, **2,800** for Q3 and **4,800** for Q4.
- **Exactness (python engine = fast env), 3/3 games money-exact** for L6t90 on r5a2 (`res/ref_L6t90.csv` vs `res/x3_r5a2.csv`): 94,389/4,644, 88,616/125,419, 77,280/93,758.
  - The local GPU fast env reproduces the remote fast env on 6/6 x3 rows, and on 320/320 overlapping r5a3 rows.
  - The control cell equals BCKNOB1 stage C r5a2 `c90_k1_w1_m1_f0` (W29, -2 vs PFS).

## C. Verdict and recipe for BCBODY5
- **CLOSED as a flip lever.**
  - The 116-seat leg is at most +1 flip vs the control. That is +1/-0 for r5a2 L7t70 and +2/-1 for the r5a3 T=70 cells.
  - Live27 gives 1 faithful win and does not keep the controls.
  - Mechanism: more tiles and a little more money (+11 tiles d9, +1.2k..+1.9k on the seats it fires), but the losses are -30k deep and decided from d10 by sale volume and price.
- **Keep as a coin fix, not a training target: the M1 land cap is wrong for the model.**
  - `caps90.json` BUY_LAND is `[0,0,0,0,0,0,2,0,5,0,1,0,...]`, and the model buys the 3rd quadrant on d9 (as 29 % of the teacher episodes do).
  - Cheapest fix: exclude BUY_LAND from `_cap_market`. Equivalent: set LAND=7|LAND_T=70|LAND_R=800|LAND_END=14 in the clone cfg.
  - Also make the cap ledger charge 1,000 / 2,000 / 4,000 by quadrant.
  - Expected value: about +1k/seat on the MELON band (t ~2), 0..+1 flips. Never buy the 4th quadrant (T=90: -3k..-4.6k).
