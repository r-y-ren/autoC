# IDLEOPS — where our 10.4 % idle turns go, and what one of them is worth

2026-09-16. Tools `S/idleops/` (`idle.py`, `report.py`, `probe_hops.py`, `run_all.sh`);
ledger `idleops.csv` + `report.md`. Read off **34 real Kaggle replays, both seats** (68
seats: the 20 M & M & P & Q games of `S/mmpq/quads.csv`, our 14 of `quads_ours.csv`, and
both opponent sets) through the engine's own `_apply_unit_action`. An op is **effective**
only if the engine moved the unit, its tile, its inventory, the seed purse or the shed;
IDLE = PASS + every silently no-op'd order.

## 0. VERDICT

**Our idle is a SCHEDULE artifact, not lost production, and the tail half is removable but
worthless.** 706 idle ops a game (10.5 %) against their 335 (5.1 %), and the excess splits
**dawn 297 vs 76** (the turn-1 BUY-row law — family already closed) and **tail 398 vs 243**
(the block's own end). It buys nothing: the yield our farm actually **loses to decay is 2.2
units a game** (theirs 1.6), 95 idle ops a game stand on work nobody comes back to (theirs
50), and **every ripe tile we idle past is harvested later anyway**. The new default-off
switch `IDLE_TAIL_HOPS_ON` (`plan.py:3411`) does exactly what the census asks — it fires,
it adds ops — and the ENG22 kill gate reads **Δmargin −213, t −1.05, 0 flips of 22 boards:
FAILED, leg chain stopped**. **Idle falls and the margin does not rise.** The 19.3k revenue
gap is volume on land we already own (186 plantings to their 271) and the labour to plant it
is not what we are short of.

## 4. Q4 — what the action interface would have to own
The switch above reaches the tail for nothing because the filler may only touch tiles **the
admit stage already refused** (`idx >= n_tasks`), refused on value. The **95 ops a game that
stand on an *admitted* tile the day never reaches** are the only ones with a claim on coins,
and re-admitting them is not a magnitude: `n_adm` is fixed once, at dawn, by
`_count_le(cum_est0, turns_h)` (`plan.py:6108`) against a *projected* task set, and `_routes`
is then fed the unwidened `d`. An interface would have to own (a) **re-admission mid-day**, so
a unit whose block ends early takes the next-ranked task instead of the best *unadmitted* one;
(b) **the dawn pickup** — which unit waits for the BUY row, per kind rather than per day
(297 ops); (c) **the hand-off** between two units on the same open tile — 232 of our 326
under-the-feet idle ops are deferrals that resolve later the same day, so the interface has to
tell "covered" from "dropped". Bounds: 706 idle ops, **2.2 units of yield actually lost**, and
a paired engine read of **−213 coins** for the one piece a constant can move.

## 5. Dead ends recorded
* `IDLE_TAIL_HOPS = 4` — fires (2 pin boards of 6, +2 / +6 ops), ENG22 −213 t −1.05: the tail
  is real and not worth coins. Idle as a *cause* of the 19.3k revenue gap is falsified with it.
* Harvest in the tail (`TAIL_FILL_ON` excludes it on purpose): 127 idle ops a game stand on a
  ripe tile, but that yield is banked later anyway — a deferral, not units.
* The dawn half: `ROUTE_FREEFIRST_ON` (+23, t 0.18) and `PRESTOCK_V2_ON` (−326, t −1.92)
  closed it before this box; nothing here reopens it.
