## 3. Throughput (games/hour)
* Before: RLLOSS1, 3 workers, lockstep, uncontended: 24 games / 88 s = **980 g/h = 0.27 g/s**. Workers sit at 60 % CPU because they wait on the lockstep.
* After (ppo_fast: G concurrent sub-batches on separate worker pools, rr0 training rollouts). Remote box at load 15-22 on its 8 cores
  (nproc = 8, not 12; RLLOSS1 + ESLOSS1 already use them all). 4 workers: V56 exact 698 g/h, V56 rr0 **860 g/h (0.24 g/s)**.
  Self-play (both seats run our runtime) with 2 workers: 275 g/h, about 1.3x the per-worker cost of a V56 game.
* Ceiling: 8 uncontended cores x 7.4 s/game (rr0) = about 3,900 g/h = **1.1 g/s**, 18x short of the 20 g/s the guide requires.
  Reaching 20 g/s needs the VRP router and the V-family opponents off the Python path (a JAX/C++ port of both). That is a multi-day port.
