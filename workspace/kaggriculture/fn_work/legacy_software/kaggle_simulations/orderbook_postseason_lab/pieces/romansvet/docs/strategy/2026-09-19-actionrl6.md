# ACTIONRL6 — the PPO head gets a LIVE opponent (self-play pool), 2026-09-19

## Why
ACTIONRL5 is the whole motive. flow254 trained against 306 **tapes**, won +9 points of sim win rate (0.826 → 0.918), and on the
real engine out of sample came back +646 coin (gift-free, `dTHEIRS` −58) with **board wins 94.6 → 89.3 %, net flips −3**. A tape is
a replay: it does not re-plan when we take its water, its tiles or its shop, so every coin the head learns to take off it is a coin
a live seat takes back. The head learned to exploit a recording, and the ladder pays wins.

## What was built (master `4272ec4a`)
`S/actionrl/ppo.py` gains **`--pool <npy,npy,…>`** and **`--selfplay-frac F`** (default 0.5). Per episode, with probability `F`,
seat 1 is a theta drawn from the pool and `tape_ctl` carries a **negative seat** — `sim/rollout.py:323` already reads that as "no
tape here", so that seat plans for itself against whatever we do, every dawn. The other `1 − F` stay on tape rungs. Both live in
ONE compiled program (the tape gather is masked by `ctl[0] >= 0`), so the mix costs no compile and no step time.

The pool is **theta-only and fixed** — the shipped centre `S/winjudge/ship7692/theta7659.npy` (self-play proper) is always member
0, and `--pool` adds more. A path that is missing, not 7,692 long, or byte-equal to a member already in is **SKIPPED and named** at
startup, never padded. There are no head snapshots in it and that is deliberate: `plan.RESIDUAL_FN` is module-level with no seat
argument, so a past head could not be seated without a second hook.

**The seat-1 masking bug this exposed.** `rollout.run_day` plans BOTH seats through that one hook, seat 0 first. Under a tape the
opponent's plan was discarded, so nobody noticed; under self-play the opponent would have flown **our head too**. `head.jax_fn` now
hands the second call of a dawn `head.noop_override` — `decode` of each slot's no-op, exactly what a fresh `ZERO_BIAS` head emits —
so seat 1 is the plain shipped planner by construction. Tape episodes are unchanged (that plan was thrown away either way).

`log.tsv` prices the halves apart: `sp_win` / `tape_win` / `sp_n` inserted after `mwin` (so `sec` moved column 13 → 16).
