# lr 0.003 → 3e-4: the queued launchers re-staged (2026-09-11T16:40Z)

Consensus [§59](2026-09-10-consensus.md) measured the B-seeded ES arms as a **noise walk**: every record
loses on its *own* training rungs (flow200 g10 −646 t −3.8, g100p −318 t −2.0; flow201 g10 −511 t −3.4;
flow196 g100p −551 t −3.1; mean −270, Δobj_w < 0 on 5/8), with in-sample/hold-out Pearson 0.66 — so there
is no in/out gap to blame. Adam normalises every coordinate to ±lr regardless of signal, so lr 0.003 ×
5997 coords is an isotropic step of ‖δ‖ ≈ 0.23/generation. §59's prescription is **step length**:
lr 0.003 → 3e-4 at unchanged σ/pop, on every queued launcher.

[§60](2026-09-10-consensus.md) (landed 16:34Z, one minute before this staging) withdraws §56's
"correctly signed but tiny → lower the step" reading — at B the landscape is a plateau plus one
board-independent day-0 switch, and E3's six cells all fail. §60 nonetheless states that **§59 stands and
the lr 3e-4 re-stage proceeds as the direct test of it**, with the expectation of *slower wandering on a
plateau — level records, not gains*. That is the reading rule for these five arms: a level record at
g100-g300 confirms §59; a losing record refutes the step-length account and points back at the estimator.

## Step-length audit (deliverable 5)

Read against `scripts/train.py` and `S/localarm/tree/src/kagg3/es/train.py` (the tree the remote runs;
its `scripts/train.py` carries the same argparse — every flag in all five launchers exists in both).

1. **Adam's lr is the only step-length control.** `delta = cfg.lr * mhat / (jnp.sqrt(vhat) + cfg.eps_adam)`
   (train.py:4974). argparse exposes exactly `--lr`, `--optimizer`, `--sigma`, `--weight-decay` near the
   step; there is **no** lr schedule, lr decay, warmup or step-clip flag anywhere
   (`grep -iE 'lr_decay|lr_schedule|warmup'` → nothing).
2. **Nothing silently re-inflates the step.** `--stall-sigma-mult 1.0` (all five launchers) multiplies
   **σ**, not lr, and at 1.0 is a no-op. `--restart-sigma-on-stall 150` and `--real-gate-recentre 3` both
   call `_restart_from_record()`, which jumps θ back onto `best_abs` and clears `m`, `v` **and**
   `adam_t`; because `adam_t` is reset the bias correction stays correct — train.py:5473-5476 says
   leaving it at `t` is what would make the first step after a clear 3.16× lr, and the code does not.
   **No flag needed neutralising in flow206/flow205/flow203b/flow207.**
3. **The one hatch that survived, neutralised:** flow204's `LR="${LR:-$LR_DEFAULT}"` env override. With
   the map flattened to 3e-4 it is the only remaining path back to 0.003. flow204b adds a 10-line guard
   in the file's own `POP_ANY` idiom: an explicit `LR` above 3e-4 exits 2 unless `LR_ANY=1`.
   Verified: `LR=0.003 DRYRUN=1 SIGMA=0.02 POP=512` → exit 2; `LR=3e-4` → exit 0.
4. **Weight decay 1e-4 at lr 3e-4 — material, and NOT lr-scaled here.** The decay is decoupled and
   lr-independent per generation: `upd = (1 - cfg.weight_decay) * step` (train.py:4994-4996). This is the
   opposite of the AdamW convention the question assumed. `scripts/train.py`'s own `--weight-decay` help
   states the coupling rule: it sets the equilibrium norm `~lr*sqrt(n/(2*WD))`, "*an A/B that moves lr and
   leaves WD alone has moved the norm as well as the step, and the two effects cannot be told apart
   afterwards. Scale it as WD ~ lr^2 to hold the norm.*"
   Arithmetic at wd 1e-4, n_live ≈ 3759: equilibrium ‖θ‖ **13.0 at lr 0.003 → 1.30 at lr 3e-4**, against
   B's ‖θ‖ ≈ 15.8. Per generation the decay pull is `wd*‖θ‖` ≈ 1.6e-3 and is **coherent** (always toward
   the origin); the Adam step is 3e-4·√5997 = 0.023 and is isotropic. Over 300 generations: **0.47 of
   coherent contraction (−3.0 % of the norm) against 0.40 of random walk**, where at lr 0.003 the same
   0.47 sat against 4.0 of walk (12 %). So decay does not re-inflate the step, but at 3e-4 it becomes the
   largest single *systematic* displacement in the arm.
   **Decision: wd left at 1e-4 in all five files**, because §59 asked for a one-variable test and moving
   wd and lr together would reproduce exactly the confound the help text warns about. **Reading rule:** if
   flow206's records look like B uniformly shrunk 1-3 % rather than B moved along a direction, wd is the
   confound, and the companion arm is the same file at `--weight-decay 1e-6` (= 1e-4·(3e-4/0.003)², the
   WD ~ lr² pairing that holds the norm). Never move wd and lr in the same arm.
