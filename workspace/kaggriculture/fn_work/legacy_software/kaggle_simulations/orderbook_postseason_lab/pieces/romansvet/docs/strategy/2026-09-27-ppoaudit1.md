## 1. Reward (per game, what each PPO arm optimises)
`R = sign(ours−theirs) + 0.3·tanh((ours−theirs)/6000)`, divided by the running std, then centred on its K=8 group (ppo_fast.py:769-777). It is placed at dawn 30 only, and GAE λ 0.95 γ 1 runs with the value head (L779-783).
- Shop-draw noise term: **0 by construction**. Training eod runs with `shop_crn=True` (L446 → eod.py:267, `SHOP_CRN_BASE`), so a tile change cannot re-roll the shops.
- Total reward variance: run2s Var R 1.215 (E R 0.229), run3g1 1.257 (0.097). The win term is Var(sign) ≈ 1 − E[s]² ≈ 0.96-0.99, which is **≥ 79 % of Var R**. The margin term is capped at 0.09 (≤ 7 %), and the rest is the covariance between two terms of the same sign.
- Within a group (what the gradient sees): the win term's variance is ≤ 4 × the sampled-vs-greedy flip rate = 4 × 0.05-0.07 = **0.20-0.28**, against ≤ 0.09 for the margin term. The win term is **not drowned**. It is sparse instead: vs V56 the base wins 91 %, so most V groups agree on all 8 games and carry only the margin term ("win bigger"). Old run2 shows the result at u180-190: dO +395..+444 with dT +235..+269, **Δtheirs t 2.3-2.6 = gift**.
