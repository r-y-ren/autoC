# RESALE1 (2026-09-26): late shop-wheat resale — NO SHIP, wheat-resale axis CLOSED

The V "wheat pumpers" buy 56-217k of shop wheat. Can we buy shop wheat late and resell it for a margin?

## 1. Mechanism (read from the engine, `kaggriculture.py` `_commit_unit` / `_town_consume`)
- The "shop" wheat is `BUY_PRODUCT WHEAT` from the SAME market pot we sell to. A buy quotes p(I−1..I−k); a sell quotes
  p(I..). A round trip against an unchanged pot nets exactly 0. The same-turn margin (sell quote − buy price) is
  **mean −0.033, max 0** on every d15-29 turn (20 dev boards).
- Profit only comes from the EXOGENOUS pot change between the buy and the resale:
  `sum_j [p(I+E−j) − p(I−j)]`. Our own lot sales in between cancel (the pot-path identity).
  The town removes wheat without paying for it: 1 unit per unlocked wheat shop every 4 turns (BAKERY, PIZZA, BRUNCH,
  ICE_CREAM, FARMERS_MARKET), plus the town centre 1/day. Reacting V56 sells wheat only at h1 (11.2 u).
- Ledger check (`S/resale1/check.py`, `hours.py`, `byhour.py` on the SELLAUDIT1 per-turn ledger: dev 0-19, exact sim vs
  reacting V56, head_940, SAFETY_S 1e9):
  - Dawn pot p10/50/90: 9,649 / 9,798 / 9,919 (below I0; quote 39).
  - Exogenous drift per day (rival net − town): −37 / −16.5 / +6. It is negative on 82 % of days.
  - Hindsight best window, net of the rival's gift: k=100 +1,192/game intraday. That needs 100 free shed units all day.
  - Best fixed rule h4→h22, k=100: **+954 ± 74 /game** (20/20 boards > 0, d15-28). Buying at h1 is negative (−223), because the rival sells at h1.
  - Our free shed before the market, lowest over h4-h22: median 64, p10 11.
- Verdict of step 1: margin > 0 on most days, so NOT closed by the check. The hindsight size, with the rival and the town fixed, is ~+1k/game.

**Why it loses** (d15 X=0, boards 0-24, paired product ledger):
- The resale fills: +502 wheat sold vs 507 bought.
- The wheat leg itself is flat to negative: Δwheat revenue − resale cost = **−310/game** (median −159). The forecast was ~+0.5 coin/unit, which is ~+250/game at ~35 u/day. The integer price moves 1 coin per ~28 units at pot 9,800, so a 15-22 unit daily drain often moves nothing.
- Stock held in the shared 100-unit shed displaces our own deposits:
  - every other product sells 4-10 fewer units;
  - harvest −6 units, hire bill +172.
- The rival's revenue rises +165..+321 (Δtheirs t 1.1-1.7 in every cell).
- The upside is capped by the shed. Even the open-loop ideal (100 free units all day) is +954/game, and there are only ~35 u/day of room.

## Verdict
**NO SHIP; late wheat resale CLOSED** (with the 80-unit pump, FERTSALE1 and SELLAUDIT1). Buying from and selling to the same pot is value-neutral apart from the town's drain. The drain is worth <1 coin/unit/day. The shared shed makes every resale unit displace our own sale volume. The pumpers' 56-217k is gross turnover (LIVEWATCH16: their net wheat ≤ ours), not a lever. RESALE_ON stays OFF; nothing is packaged.
