# ESFLAT1 — why both BAND ES trainers are flat, and the g10 decision (2026-09-28)

Analysis only. It runs no games and does not touch either trainer. The remote run3 cache was copied read-only by scp.

**Verdict.** Neither trainer has anything to climb. The measured fitness also points the wrong way.

1. **ESBAND1 (eswork genes 0-5).** The shipped point is a local optimum on the quantised grid. For every gene, the shipped level is the best level sampled; levels 0..6 were all covered. There is no sigma that gives +2 flips. This axis is CLOSED.
2. **ESBAND2 (6,779 live theta7659 floats).** The antithetic members show no linear signal: corr F(+e),F(−e) = −0.09.
3. **Both trainers pay the base draw's winner's curse.** The PFS base won 91/142. The chaos expectation of the same policy is 87.2 (z +1.6), so a zero-effect member reads −3.6 flips on the band (half A −4.2, half B +0.4).
   - The members' mean net (−1.7 and −1.9 per 71 seats) is almost exactly this curse.
   - It also drives frac_pos and the sigma schedule, which alternate with half parity.
4. **Most flips are near-tie noise.** 298/333 (ESBAND1) and 247/338 (ESBAND2) are on seats with |base margin| < 3k.
   - ESBAND1 flipped **0** of the 32 MELON losses ≤ −3k in 1,551 member games.
   - ESBAND2's 16 up-flips on those seats are mostly rival-tape breakage: 13 on smackaveli_114119876 at −18.3k, with the rival purse < 95 % of live.
5. **Fitness F is the wrong meter.** Hard flips explain only 42 % of F's variance; the dmargin/1000 term carries the chaos. A soft-win fitness has about 3x the signal-to-noise.
6. **g10 recommendation: prepare a restart recipe for after g10.** Let both run, per the never-kill-for-flat rule.
   - The hold-out of ESBAND1's centre is expected to read 0 ± 3 flips, with P(GATE2 pass) about 3-5 %.
   - ESBAND2 has no centre to judge.

## Method and file formats

- **Job cache.** Both trainers go through `eswork.run_eps` (S/eswork1/eswork.py L158).
  - Episodes run in CHUNK=10 jobs. Each job is cached as `<run>/jobs/<md5(es_stack.float32.tobytes() + repr(boards))>.pkl` = pickle `(ours int64[10], theirs int64[10], secs)`.
  - `es_stack` is the [10, 10] eswork gene rows (ESBAND1) or the [10, 7692] theta rows (ESBAND2).
  - `boards` are the tuples `(seed, our_seat, town tuple, <KAGG3_DATA>/<opp main.py>, latch)`, reordered latched-first by `esband.run_eps`.
- **Reconstruction.** `S/esflat1/reconstruct.py` regenerates each generation's candidates:
  - noise from rng 5000+g (ESBAND1) or 6000+g (ESBAND2);
  - sig_mult taken from the previous es_log line; centre = base until an ACCEPT, then the step candidate; `live()` quantisation;
  - the exact chunks and md5 keys; it then reads the pickles back.
- **Reconstruction check.** 0 missing jobs. The step candidate's full-band net matches every es_log line to the flip:
  - ESBAND1 g1-g6: −4 −1 · −1 +1 −4 (g3 same-policy);
  - ESBAND2 g1-g4: −7 +1 −3 −1;
  - the member_net vector of ESBAND2 g1 matches.
- **Coverage.** ESBAND1 run3 g1-g6: 97 members × 71 seats = 6,887 games, plus 5 step candidates × 142. ESBAND2 run1 g1-g4: 64 × 71 = 4,544 games, plus 4 × 142.
- **Outputs** (`S/esflat1/res/`):
  - `{tr}_seats.tsv`: gen, member (0-7 = +eps, 8-15 = −eps, 16 = centre, `step` = the step candidate on all 142), pair, sign, label, fam, base ours/theirs/margin, ours, theirs, margin, dmargin, flip, dsoft (w = 1/(1+e^(−m/3000)), GATE2), dtheirs.
  - `{tr}_members.tsv`: one row per member, with fit (the trainer's F), net, up, dn, dsoft, dmargin mean, dtheirs mean, ndiff, x0-5 (the gene noise in sigma units; ESBAND1 only).
  - `esband1_qdelta.tsv`, `esband1_slopes.tsv`, `esband1_levels.tsv`, `seat_chaos.tsv` (per seat: base win, p_win under the member re-rolls), `tables.txt`, `curse.txt`, `snr.txt`.
- **Faithful seats.** Uses the JC1 cash proxy: a member game is unfaithful if the rival's final cash is < 95 % of its live cash (`boards_all.json` `live[1]`).
- **Reproduce.** `bash S/esflat1/run.sh`. It scps the remote cache, then runs reconstruct ×2, analyse, levels, curse and snr. It takes about 20 s of CPU and runs no games.

## Q3 — noise floor and what GATE2 needs

| | ESBAND1 (±1 gene unit) | ESBAND2 (theta σ 0.002, ≈ null) |
|---|---|---|
| member games that differ from base | 92 % | 98 % (σ 0.002 already re-rolls every game) |
| per-game dmargin sd | 1,750 | 6,419 (seat median 1,322; MELON 1,935) |
| per-game dsoft sd | 0.076 | 0.149 |
| per-member sd, 71 seats: soft / hard net / F (within gen) | 0.90 / 1.64 / 0.51 | 1.01 / 1.90 / 1.09 |
| soft-sum SE on the full 142 | 0.91 | 1.77 |
| share of F variance explained by hard flips | 42 % | 41 % |
| per-member z of a true +2 flips/100 (+2.8 on 142): F / soft / hard | 0.55 / 1.55 / 0.85 | 0.47 / 1.39 / 0.73 |

- **Seats per member.** Each member is judged on one 71-seat half, on all seats (the JC1 faithful rule is not applied). Only the step candidate sees all 142.
- **What GATE2 needs.** To detect +2 flips/100 (+2.8 on 142) at 80 % power, the soft-sum SE must be ≤ ~1.0 on 142. ESBAND1's per-member noise is at that bar; ESBAND2's is 1.8x above it. On a 71-seat half, both are 1.3x-2.5x above it.
- **Winner's curse** (res/curse.txt, seat_chaos.tsv). Per seat, p_win under the member re-rolls gives:

| seats | base W | E[W] under re-roll (ESBAND2 / ESBAND1) | curse |
|---|---|---|---|
| all 142 | 91 | 87.2 / 87.6 | −3.8 / −3.4 (z +1.6, chaos sd of W 2.37) |
| near-tie < 3k (34) | 21 | 18.7 / 18.3 | −2.3 / −2.7 (V near-ties 15/22 vs 12.5) |
| half A (gens 2, 4, 6) | 46 | 41.8 | **−4.2** |
| half B (gens 1, 3, 5) | 45 | 45.4 | +0.4 |

- **What the curse does.** PFS was selected on these seats, so the ES centre (F ≡ 0) holds a lucky draw that no re-rolled member can keep.
  - On half A, every member starts about −4 flips behind. That explains ESBAND2 g2 and g4 (frac_pos 0.06, member_net −3.4 and −5.1) and ESBAND1 g2, g4 and g6.
  - The rejects feed `sig_schedule` a frac_pos that depends on half parity, not on sigma. That is why ESBAND1 was driven to x0.5 and ESBAND2 to x2.
  - The full-band accept needs band net ≥ 0 against a −3.6 expectation. An accept is therefore a +1.1 sd lottery: ESBAND1 g5's "+1 (+2/−1)" is g3 3 → 2, a flat cell.

## Q4 — the g10 hold-out and the recommendation

| arm at g10 | what is judged | expected hold-out (BAND-HOLD 104 + NEW30, 134 seats) | P(GATE2 pass) |
|---|---|---|---|
| ESBAND1 | centre = cand_g005 (relay 2/5/3/**2**, lead 5), unless a later lottery accept | true effect ≈ 0 flips (g3 = 2 vs 3: −0.42 vs −0.49 soft/71); paired chaos sd ≈ 3.1 flips / 1.7 soft; ~0 ± 150 coins/board | ≈ 3-5 % (false-positive rate); P(net ≥ +2) ≈ 25 % by chance |
| ESBAND2 | nothing yet (best 0.000, no accept in g1-g4) | P(some accept in g5-g10) ≈ 40 % at ≈ 10 %/gen; if it accepts, expect −1..−2 flips (the σ 0.004 cost beyond the curse is −0.9/71) | < 5 % |

**Recommendation: prepare a restart recipe for AFTER g10.** Do not act on it now: both trainers keep running to g10 under the never-kill-for-flat rule.

- Judge only ESBAND1's centre at g10, and only as a cheap formality (about 7 min). The expected result is null.
- Do not spend a hold-out run on ESBAND2 unless it has accepted.
- Expected coins/flips from either trainer at g10: **0 flips, 0 coins** (±3 flips of chaos).

**ESBAND1 gene axis: CLOSED, no restart.**
- The shipped relay/lead point is the best sampled level on every gene, and the 0..6 / lead 3..7 range is covered.
- The ES over genes 0-5 has converged. It is not starved.

**Restart recipe for an ESBAND2-style theta ES** (only if an ES arm must stay in flight):
1. **De-cursed base.**
   - Per seat, use p_win and mean soft-win over ≥ 16 null re-rolls in place of the single PFS draw. `seat_chaos.tsv` already has 32 per seat from ESBAND2 g1+g3 (half B) and g2+g4 (half A).
   - Compute frac_pos and the accept test against that expected base, not against F ≡ 0.
   - This removes the −3.6-flip handicap (−4.2 on half A) and the parity-driven sigma schedule.
2. **Fitness = GATE2 soft-win** (Σ Δw, w = 1/(1+e^(−m/3000)), family shares as now) on **JC1 faithful seats**: drop a member-seat when the rival's cash is < 95 % of live. The soft-win SNR is 1.4-1.6 against 0.5 for F, and the drop removes the 13 broken-rival MELON "flips".
3. **Seats.** All 142 per member with 4 antithetic pairs (8 × 142 = 1,136 episodes, the same cost as 16 × 71 today) in place of alternating halves.
4. **Sigma.** Keep 0.002. At 0.002, 98 % of the games already re-roll, so a larger sigma adds cost (σ 0.004: −0.9 soft/71) and no more signal.
5. **Accept gate** = GATE2 branch A on the de-cursed base, plus the faithful-seat dtheirs gift rule.
   - Even so, the antithetic test shows no measurable gradient at this sigma.
   - Expected result of a restart: under +2 flips by its g10, with P(real +2) < 15 %.
   - The rank-deciding cell (32 MELON losses ≤ −3k) flipped 0 times in 1,551 ESBAND1 games and 16 times in 1,024 ESBAND2 games; 13 of the 16 are on a broken rival tape and 3 on yannikschiffne_113299011. Per BUILDREVIEW1, the melon cell is not a macro-float or relay-gene problem.
