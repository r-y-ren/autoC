- **Judge rows.** PFS vs each rival on boards_m40, 80 games each:
  - S/pricegap1/res/g0cf_pfs (= S/judgerival1/res/g0capsfix/pfs.csv)
  - S/judgerival2/res/jr2_pfs_fl

  - the per-farm cumulative sales ledger (units, coins, spend) at steps 240 (dawn d10), 432 (dawn d18) and at the end
  - the cash at h1 / d10 / d18 / end
- **Judge windows.** So the judge windows are d0-9 / d10-17 / d18-29.
- **Per-day state.** The JR3 diag logs (S/judgerival3/logs/*.diag.jsonl; money, quadrants, crops and animals at h12 every day) add per-day state:
  - big: 80 games on m40 vs PFS
  - g0capsfix: 18 games on l27/tape boards
  - flood: 5 games
- **Real seats.** TOPAUDIT1's fp.jsonl, using table.py's seat selection (current LB sub, newest-10 games per team).
  - Families: P48 = DSM, Victor, Anton, Mother-Goose and akmr (56 seats); PQ4 = M&M&P&Q, DECEM, seek inspiration and My second life (48 seats).
  - OLD = the base.tsv seats of the four switchers (15 seats).
  - The real seats were re-bucketed to the judge windows.
- **Scripts.** S/judgecal1/sig.py, boards.py, boards27.py, traj.py, gaps.py and live.py. Outputs in S/judgecal1/res: sig.md, boards.md, boards27.md, traj.md, gaps.md and live.md.
- **Caveat.** The real seats' coins come from top-10 vs top-10 games, where both seats sell into the same books. The judge rival shares its market with PFS. The same-board read (boards.md) is the like-for-like one.
