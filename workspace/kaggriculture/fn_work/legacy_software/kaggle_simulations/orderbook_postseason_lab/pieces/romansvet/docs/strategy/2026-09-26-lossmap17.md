# LOSSMAP17 (2026-09-26): the Durian analysis over every live loss of vrp5, vrp7 and vrp3

**Question (USER).** "Do the analysis you did for the Durian game on all our losses. What other loss structures do we have? Can we generalise them into one counter-strategy, or do we need several?"

**Answer.** We need two counters, not one. One loss structure is a separate early-game problem: the non-V herd+land engine, with 34 losses and +189 Elo points at stake. The other is a late-conversion problem: the V-plate near-miss plus the late flips, with 27 losses, +124 points, and +58 points from the close games. The two share nothing before d20. The d0 melon plate is the top cell in 59 of the 67 losses, but it is **not** a loss structure of its own. Against V, the plate costs us the same −17.4k in the 82 wins as in the 20 losses.

## Data and method
- **Games.** All live episodes of vrp3 56527550 (233 games, 52 losses), vrp5 56542089 (192, 64 L) and vrp7 56572961 (29, 3 L). That is 454 games and 119 losses. Validation self-play was dropped. Replays: `S/lossmap17/gz/` (rsync of the GAPCENSUS1 cache plus the new vrp7 episodes, fetched by `parse.py`). Episode list: `S/lossmap17/eps.tsv` (`api.py` + `eps.py`, the GAPCENSUS1 copies).
- **Parser.** `S/lossmap17/parse.py` writes one JSON per game to `out/`. For each seat, every step's money delta is split as follows:
  - The exact hire bill: fib over the change in `hires_today`.
  - Land: the step at which the unlocked quadrant count rises (1000/2000/4000).
  - Fixed-price buys: seeds 10/20/50/100/80, animals 300/400/500.
  - Product buys, at the quote from the previous step.
  - Sell revenue, spread over the step's SELL orders by quote × qty.
  - Revenue is clamped to [0, sells at quote]. A larger residual means a fixed buy failed; this came to 136k over 908 seats, about 150 per seat.
  - **The books close exactly in all 908 seats: 3000 + revenue − spend = final money.** Every money move landed on the step of its order, so the lag attribution the dispatch allowed for was never needed (lag 0, unattributed 0).
- **Gap and plantings.** G(d) is the dawn money gap (ours − theirs), and G(30) is the final margin.
  - **Created** = the window with the most negative ΔG. The windows are d0-9, d10-19, d20-27 and d28-29.
  - **Decided** = the window holding the last crossing from G ≥ 0 to G < 0.
  - **Product cells** are net cash (sell − product buy of the same item), ours − theirs, by window. Wheat churn nets out.
  - **Plantings** = PLANT tiles at dusk with `planted_day == d`.
  - **Hands** = the noon count.
  - Durian check: G28 +150, margin −1,251, hands 8/7/7 vs 10/11/11. It matches, except that the dusk-count plantings d23-27 are 40 vs 63 (the dispatch had 44 vs 59, counted another way).
- **Family** comes from the opponent's board at d2 h0 (the LIVEWATCH16 rule):
  - V = 11-13 melon and 6-9 wheat.
  - MELON = a non-V opener with 1-11 melon.
  - ZERO = 0 melon.
  - OTHER = the rest.
- **Rating.** RATINGPATH2 Elo/400 model, theta = MLE over vrp5+vrp7 games with per-sub index ≥ 20 (181 games, 117-64, theta 2,721), opponent = `oppR0`. A structure's cost = theta with its losses flipped to wins − theta. "close" flips only the losses with margin ≥ −3k.
- **Scripts and outputs.** `analyze.py` → `games.csv` / `cells.csv`; `famstats.py` → `famstats_all.txt` / `famstats_r2500.txt` (wins vs losses by family); `drill.py` → `drill.txt` (per-window product / plantings / hands / spend, both seats, oppR ≥ 2500); `structures.py` → `structures.md` / `losses.csv`.

## 3. Verdict: two counters, ranked

1. **Early regime counter for non-V herd engines (S1 + S3: 38 losses, ceiling +208 pts, close games only +29).**
   - One mechanism covers both: the opponent's d0-9 land + herd + crew builds a d10-19 volume lead (102 vs 56 plantings), and our late game cannot close a −16k d20 gap.
   - It must be **gated** at the d2 tell (non-V melon or zero-melon, ≥ 5 animals). Every unconditional version (WHEATMIX1, HERDVRP1, Q4) loses against V, which is 68 % of our ≥ 2,500 games.
   - It must be judged on the non-V bank (NONV1 tape seats and the POOL kernels), not on V56.
   - It is the NVTHETA1 / NONV lineage. MELONCOUNTER2 is a partial piece of it, not a separate counter.
   - The upside is the largest, but the margins are big (median −13k), so each flip needs a whole-farm swing.
2. **Late-conversion counter (S2 + every late flip: 27 + ~7 losses, +124 pts ceiling, +58 close, +53 late flips).**
   - One mechanism: parity to d19, then short d20-27 herd and crop output and d28-29 stock.
   - It is unconditional, or gated on the gap at d23-26, never on family.
   - This is the ENDWAVE1 / ENDGAME1 target, judged on VLOSSBED.
   - Its margins are small (median −2.9k), so it is **the more recoverable of the two per unit of effort**.

A single generalised counter would have to be both early and gated (S1) and late and ungated (S2), so it cannot be one switch. The only shared piece is the constant d23-27 planting and crew deficit (44 vs 62, hands 8 vs 11), and that deficit is the same in the wins. Late-volume fills on it are closed (RELAYFILL1, CREWRELAY1, TOMATOFILL1, GAPFIX1).
