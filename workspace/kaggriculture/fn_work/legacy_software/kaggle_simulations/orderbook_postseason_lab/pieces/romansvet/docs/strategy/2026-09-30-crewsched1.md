## STEP 2: the crew scheduler (PROG_CREW_ON) and the TUNE20 ratio progression

**Rules implemented.** Everything is in `src/kagg3/prog/direct.py` behind `self.crew`. Knob names are the `PROG3_KNOBS` overrides; the defaults are the shipped crew settings.

1. **Water skip** (`c_skip1`, `c_skipoff`): no age-1 water for wheat or carrot, and no strawberry or tomato water on off-phase days. A dry-yesterday plant stays critical.
2. **Fertilise order** (`c_wfert`, `c_fert_pri`):
   - wheat is fertilised at age 2-3 while its yield is below 6;
   - strawberry and tomato on yield-eve;
   - carrot never;
   - fertilise sorts before water on the same tile (below the critical-water priority), so the visit is FERTILIZE+WATER.
3. **Critical-water urgency** (`c_crit`): priority = -0.5 × max(0, h - 8) for any plant with consecutive_unwatered ≥ 1.
4. **Wheat harvest** (`c_unf3`): unfertilised wheat is harvested at age ≥ 3 after its water. Strawberry and tomato are harvested only at yield ≥ 2 (`c_st2`).
5. **Global unit-task matching** (`c_match`): pass 2 is a greedy match over all (free unit, unclaimed task) pairs by distance + priority, with a stick bonus of -1 for the previous target and unique targets. It replaces j4's unit-index-order first-come dispatch.
6. **Feeder role** (`c_feedrole`): in round 0 a unit carrying wheat may only take FEED tiles; if it gets nothing there, round 1 lets it take any task.
7. **Feed pickups and buying** (`c_wq`, `c_wjobs`, `c_wbuy`): feed pickups of 4 wheat, with up to 8 pickup jobs. The day's feed lot is bought from h0, up to 40 units per order (j4: h ≥ 1, 10 units).
8. **Replant in place** (`c_inplace`) and seed anticipation (`c_seedant`):
   - empty tiles under a unit are assigned first;
   - a HARVEST of a one-shot crop this step claims a wheat seed for the next step.
9. **Market seed reserve** (`c_mres_lead`): the seed reserve before a land buy uses the same lead (0) as the tile planner (j4: 1 day). This removes a day-9 deadlock where Q3 tiles are held for strawberries that the market will not buy.
10. **Animal harvest** (`c_aharv`): animals are harvested only when full (goose 4, sheep 4, cow 3). Exceptions are the lot days (d6/8/9/10/12, h < 8) and d ≥ 28.
11. **Feed rule** (`feed_all_until` 15 in crew; j4 26): after d15, feed on yield-eve or when unfed yesterday.

## What the data says the next builder must do

1. **The gap is made in d10-29 operations, not the opening.** The "from day N" decomposition shows this: from MMPQ's own d10 state our executor ends at 0.748 of MMPQ's final.
2. **The executor is labour-bound, and the ~0.78 plateau is zero-sum.** Crew changes move labour between animals and crops, trading one for the other:
   - feeder role: animal value to about 100 % of MMPQ, while wheat falls from 310 to 209 units;
   - feed after d15: wheat back to 278, milk and wool down.
   The coins ratio stays at 0.775-0.785 across 25+ cells. MMPQ does 164 tile ops with 122 moves per game-day on 13 units; we do 141-148 ops with 138-148 moves.
3. **The first missing piece is acreage upkeep, not routing.** MMPQ keeps about 1 empty and 1 weed tile from d12 to d28; we carry 8-15 empty and 3-8 weed tiles, which are mostly far rows (y 8-9) that the distance-greedy matcher never reaches.
   - Cutting ours to MMPQ's level is worth about 15-20 wheat tiles × 1.4 units/day.
   - Critical-first matching and radial ownership were both tried, and both lost.
   - The next builder needs a rule that keeps every tile planted without starving the animals: a per-tile daily service plan (MMPQ's bundles) sized to the crew, not a better greedy score.
4. **Animal service is solvable.** Feeder role plus harvest-when-full puts eggs, milk and wool at 0.95-1.0 of MMPQ units (c8/c12, value 0.834-0.840). Keep this, but fund it from walking saved elsewhere.
5. **Two MMPQ habits we still lack:**
   - Fertiliser volume: 11.4 uses per game-day vs 7.0, because 21.8 are collected in the morning feed lines and carried all day.
   - Strawberry harvests on off-phase days: 19.6 on d20 vs 8.0.
   Either is worth a cell only after point 3 frees labour.
