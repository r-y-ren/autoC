## The switch (worktree `kagg3_wt_programme1`, branch `programme1`; patch S/programme1/switch.diff; built by apply_switch.py, apply_switch2.py, apply_switch3.py)
- **`PROGRAMME_ON`** (plan.py config block, default False) + `PROGRAMME_SCHED` ("median" | "p75") + `PROGRAMME_TABLES` (the two schedules, embedded).
  It drives PFS with the existing PROGRAM_ENGINE executor (`agent/runtime.ProgramEngineState` + the planner's `pe` path). The executor keeps an
  observation-reconciled intent every dawn: plantings per crop with decade debt (the teacher's phase totals), the herd as a cumulative stock
  target (S, C, G) minus the standing head, hands per day, the quadrant count per day, and a cash reserve. Its runtime hooks create the work:
  `repair_land` (buys the overdue quadrant mid-day), `repair_herd` (buys the herd deficit after the sales), `repair_hire` (adds a hand when
  owed plantings exceed free units), `repair_work`, `repair_sales` (d0-9 animal products, d10+ ripe melon), and `repair_decay`. Its ASTRA14
  tables are replaced by the teacher tables (`_tab()` in runtime.py), and the d0-2 opening is the teacher's (melon 6/8/8, wheat 12/12/14).
  `PROGRAMME_FEED_ON` (default True) adds the teacher's daily BUY_PRODUCT WHEAT as a floor on the budget grant (`_rebuy_extra`).
- **`PROGRAMME_MODE`** ("engine" default; the per-element ladder): a MACRO_EXEC mode on PFS's OWN decode with the teacher schedule as data,
  installed by `programme_macro_init()` in `Runtime.__init__`: "hands" (crew row), "prog_hh" (+ herd floor = stock deficit vs the standing
  head, animal priority in the grant), "prog_hhl" (+ quadrant-COUNT calendar), "prog_all" (+ per-crop planting floor).
- **`PROGRAMME_HANDBACK_DAY`** (0 = never): the programme owns the farm on d0..D-1, PFS's own planner from dawn D (the V56 D18 hand-back analog).
- **Lots** (the "teacher small lots" level): TOPMECH1's `TOPMECH_SLICE_ON` (own SELL rows cut to 4 units, the rest queued) + `TOPMECH_DRIP_ON`
  (one extra SELL per product per turn while the quote stays >= 0.6 x the I0 price, at most 4 units; 0.6 = the teacher's p25 quote fraction for
  strawberry/melon), ported verbatim from branch topmech1 (d21882b2 + fa204b38).
- **OFF = byte-identical:** every PROGRAM_ENGINE read became `(PROGRAM_ENGINE_ON or programme_engine())`, a Python-false branch when off.
  Measured: closed-loop ctl (switch off) == REACTCLONE1's PFS rows **80/80 exact** (both purses); live27 ctl == S/bceval1 pfs rows **27/27** and == live 27/27.
