## 1. Switch `KERNEL2_PFS_H0MERGE` (branch `pfsh0merge1` 2791f8c0, on zerogate1 fda8c901; default OFF)
- **Step 0 (no-op):** PFS runs its d0 h0 on the real h0 obs exactly as master does (`Runtime._pfs_act`), so the plan, route_vrp and per-game state are the same as master. The action is buffered, and the engine no-op plus the V56 preload are returned.
- **Step 1, gate fires (V56):** the buffer is dropped and V56 plays as in the candidate.
- **Step 1, gate does not fire (PFS, including ZERO_CASH exclusions):** PFS runs **unshifted** on the real obs. The plan built at step 0 is reused, because it is not h0 and the day has not changed.
  - **Market:** the buffered h0 rows go first, then the plan's h1 rows. The cap is 10, and any excess is carried in order to the next turn, since PFS's h1 row can hold 6 orders. Every later d0 market row plays on time.
  - **Units:** the farmer, whose row 0 never played, and the hands hired by the h0 row, who spawn one hour late at the end of step 1, replay their plan rows one hour late. At a unit's first PASS row it skips that row and is back on schedule, because the master unit idled that hour. Hands hired later are on time.
- **From d1:** master PFS.
- `KERNEL2_PFS_SHIFT` is bypassed on these seats.
- **Refactor:** `act`'s PFS body moved verbatim into `Runtime._pfs_act`.

**Why merged is not PFS.** Trace of team_114081589, d0 and d1 hour by hour: `res/trace_team_d0d1.txt`, from `trace.py`.
1. **Market order.** Our 53-wheat pump and 4 hires play at step 1, after the rival's h0 buys have moved wheat from 25 to 27.
   - We pay +9 over the day (d0-end cash 192 PFS vs 183 merged).
   - The rival, who no longer competes with our h0 buy, pays −3 (2,854 vs 2,857 at h1).
   - Everything else in the h1 row lands at the same cash.
2. **Units cannot recover the idle hour until they idle.**
   - The farmer loses its h0 move. The 4 h0 hands spawn at the end of step 1 and first act at h2 instead of h1.
   - PFS's d0 unit rows are dense: the farmer's first PASS is at h18 and the hands' at h17-21. So d0 units run one hour late for most of the day, as in shifted.
   - A hand with no PASS row on d0 loses its row 23. On team that is the WATER after the last carrot, giving 18 vs 19 plantings at d1 h0.
   - The market is on time and the plan is the h0 plan, which is where merged gains over shifted: ZERO +2 W, and V dtheirs −417 per seat.
3. **Divergence.** From d1 PFS replans on a farm that differs by 1 planting and 9 coins. Tiles and units match PFS again at d4 h0, but cash and the rival's path have diverged, so no seat is byte-identical.
- **What an exact recovery would take:** it is impossible under an idle h0. The farmer hour and 4 hand-hours are gone, and the rival's h0 buys always precede ours.
