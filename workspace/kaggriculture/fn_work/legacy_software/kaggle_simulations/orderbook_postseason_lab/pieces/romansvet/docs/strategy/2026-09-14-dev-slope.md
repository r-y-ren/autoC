# DEV-SLOPE: is the development budget (`n_dev`) a live lever?

**VERDICT: no — it is a LOSS in both directions and B sits on a strict local
optimum of its own coordinate. +1 nat on `gb2[5]` (dev_frac level, +0.70
tiles/day) costs -9,644 coins/board (t -13.1) on the 68 band boards and -10,784
(t -5.8) on the 12 ymg_aq boards; saturating it (dev_frac = 1, the whole
reachable range, +1.97 tiles/day) costs -13,420 (t -14.2) and -9,959 (t -3.3);
the -2 tiles/day control costs -7,042 (t -6.2) and -6,114 (t -2.6). No arm wins
on either set; on the band every arm flips 27 of the 29 boards B won to losses
and flips none back. And the asked-for sizes are not reachable at all: the
entire range of the development budget through its own theta coordinates is
+1.97 tiles/day, so "+4" and "+8" do not exist — `n_dev` is hard-clipped at
`n_free`, and on 48 % of the d1-10 ymg_aq dawns ymg_aq's own `n_dev` is above
our ceiling with `dev_frac` pinned at 1. The loss is made of herd: the extra
seed money is spent on d1-d3, EGG revenue collapses 6,016 -> 1,964 and
FERTILIZER 10,307 -> 6,510, the d10 herd goes 11.1 -> 6.9 head, and the crops
return only +1,736.**

All sim-descriptive: CRN sim, tape-action opponent seat, pinned towns,
`shop_crn`, same runner as MACRO-EXEC (`S/dev_slope/ledger.py` is
`S/macro_exec/run.py` with the output directory changed, one line). No engine
game, no ES arm. Baselines re-run in this tree at the same moment as the arms
(`raw_ymg_B.npz`, `raw_band_B.npz`) rather than reused from `S/macro_exec`,
because `plan.py` is being edited in this working tree by another leg.

Tools: `S/dev_slope/{slope.py,ledger.py,report.py}`; raws
`raw_{ymg,band}_{B,dp1,dmax,m2}.npz`; full output `S/dev_slope/report.md`.

## 1. Reachability — which coordinates, and how far (Q1)

`brain.decide` (`brain.py:1002-1034`):

    n_free      = n_free_slots(obs, land=land_ok)      # land_ok: gene not vetoing AND affordable
    dev_frac    = sig(head[5] + aux[2] * n_free / 25)
    n_dev       = qfloor(dev_frac * n_free)            # <= n_free, hard
    animal_cnt  = qfloor(sig(head[6]) * n_dev)
    plant_total = n_dev - sum(animal_want)             # brain.py:1034

`head = gh @ g2 + gb2`, `aux = gh @ g5 + gb5`, so the coordinates are

| what | theta coordinates | count |
|---|---|---:|
| dev_frac level | `g2[:,5]` + `gb2[5]` (`gb2[5]` = index **3879**) | 33 |
| dev_frac's free-tile slope | `g5[:,2]` + `gb5[2]` (index **4385**) | 33 |
| animal share of `n_dev` | `g2[:,6]` + `gb2[6]` (index **3880**) | 33 |

Everything upstream of `gh` moves them too, but only through every other decode
at once. Measured on the 180 real ymg_aq dawns (`S/macro_extract/extract.py`
`policy_obs`, the same dawns MACRO-EXTRACT and CROP-DAY used), d1-d10:

| perturbation | measured effect on `n_dev` | sd @ sigma 0.02 |
|---|---|---:|
| `gb2[5]` alone, bisected for +1 tile/day | z* = +1.7455 | **87.3 sd** |
| `gb5[2]` alone, bisected for +1 tile/day | z* = +2.0193 | **101.0 sd** |
| `gb2[6]` alone (animal share) | never moves the **total** (it only re-splits it) | infinite |
| whole `g2[:,5]+gb2[5]` block, sigma-0.02 draw | mean -0.032, sd 0.077, p90 0.115 tiles/day | **~25 sd/tile** |
| whole `g5[:,2]+gb5[2]` block, sigma-0.02 draw | mean -0.051, sd 0.072, p90 0.132 | ~20 sd/tile |
| whole `g2[:,6]+gb2[6]` block, sigma-0.02 draw | 0.000 | dead for the total |

**The range itself is the finding.** With `dev_frac` saturated at 1 (`gb2[5] +=
20`), mean `n_dev` over the d1-10 dawns goes 5.77 -> **7.73** tiles/day: +1.97
tiles/day is *all* there is. `n_dev` is clipped by `n_free`, which is the day's
free tiles at **dawn** (plus the next quadrant's 25 locked tiles only when
`land_ok`). B already sits at that ceiling on 21.7 % of the d1-10 dawns, with a
mean headroom of 1.97 tiles/day. So the requested +2 is the ceiling, and +4 and
+8 are not expressible by theta at all, by any value of any coordinate.

1. **The + arms buy crops with the herd's money.** `dp1`'s crop revenue rises
   **+1,736** in all (wheat +602, carrot +587, tomato +570, melon +546,
   strawberry -569) and its animal side falls **-11,715** (EGG -4,052, WOOL
   -3,007, FERT -3,797, MILK -859), for the -9,979 net: the d10 herd is 6.9 head against B's 11.1. `animal_count
   = sig(head[6]) * n_dev` *rises* with `n_dev`, so this is not the decode
   re-splitting — it is coins. Cash at dawn d1 is 172 (dp1) and **39** (dmax)
   against B's 165, and the animals that B buys on d1-d3 are never bought.
2. **At the ceiling the tiles are not even planted.** `dmax` leaves **21.2**
   idle tiles at dawn d10 against B's 2.7 and ends with *fewer* crop tiles
   (37.6 vs 37.7): the seed order is placed at dawn from a budget the day's
   coins cannot fill, the money is gone, and the board sits empty. Same shape as
   MACRO-EXEC's `full` mode (44 idle at d10) one level down the chain.
3. **The - control loses differently, which is what makes B an optimum.** `m2`
   under-develops: 33.2 crop tiles at d10, 10.3 idle, wheat revenue 8,288 vs
   12,002 — but it *also* loses the herd (6.4 head), because `animal_count`
   is a share of the same shrunken `n_dev`. Both directions cost 6-13k
   coins/board: B is a strict local optimum in this coordinate, exactly as
   DOMINANT-STRATEGY 2026-09-03 found for the 41-int lattice.
4. **The opponent seat doubles every number** (`Δmargin ~ 2 x Δcoins`): the pot
   is shared, so seeds we buy and products we fail to sell reappear in their
   prices. Their coins go 104,284 -> 114,778 under `dp1`.

## 5. What this says to do next

The development *fraction* is closed. Two things are not, and the tables above
say which is bigger:

1. **Land timing (the d9-d10 jump).** ymg_aq's tile count goes 39.2 -> 48.8 ->
   55.2 on d8-d10 while ours stays at 38; their dawn `n_free` on those days is
   15.3/16.2 against our 2.2/1.1. `n_free` moves 25 tiles the day a quadrant is
   bought and `decide` already counts the prospective tiles (the M1 comment at
   `brain.py:988`). **The one next experiment: the same paired CRN ledger on
   `gb2[1]` (the land-bias coins, `head[1]`), swept to buy the second and third
   quadrant earlier, everything else B, on the same 12+68 boards.** It is the
   only coordinate measured here that raises `n_free` itself, and it is the one
   the ymg_aq tape actually uses.
2. **The dawn census.** `n_dev` is sized once, from tiles free at dawn; ymg_aq
   plants 11.3 tiles from a 6-tile dawn on d2 by replanting inside the day. No
   theta value can express that — it is a planner change (a second development
   pass after the day's harvests), not a gene.

## UNVERIFIED

* Sim only. No engine game, no ES arm, no promotion gate.
* The +4 and +8 arms were never run — section 1 shows they are unreachable by
  theta; a `setattr` override that forced them would have to over-ask past
  `n_free`, where `plan._wants` clips the seed want anyway.
* The reachability table is decoded on **ymg_aq's** dawn boards (the same dawns
  CROP-DAY used), not on our own; our own board's dawn free tiles are in the
  section 2 table (idle column) and are *smaller* on d1-d3 (0.0), which makes
  the ceiling finding stronger, not weaker.
* Band dawns were not decoded separately: the 68-board evidence here is the
  paired ledger, not a decode slope.
* `t` values are paired over boards with the two seats of a board counted as two
  rows, as in every MACRO-* table; they are not independent games.
