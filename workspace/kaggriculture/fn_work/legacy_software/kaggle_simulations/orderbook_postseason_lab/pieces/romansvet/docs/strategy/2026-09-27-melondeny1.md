## Phase 1: market mechanics (read-only)
**(a) Price function.**
- Price = 250 + 8.76·ln(1+(I0−inv)) below I0 and 250 − 0.01·(inv−I0)² above it (`spec.py:116` MELON row; `spec.market_price`).
- There is ONE inventory, shared by both seats (engine `_process_market` uses `obs0.market`; sim `state.py:65` `mkt_inv[9]`).
- No shop buys melon (`spec._SHOP_DEMAND`). The only drain is the town centre, 1 unit per 24 turns (engine `_town_consume`; `sim/rollout.py:176`).
- So the melon book never resets. Every unit booked earlier lowers every later quote for the rest of the season.

**(b) Order of sales.**
- Within a step, orders resolve by slot index. At the same slot, both seats get the same per-unit quote in lockstep, committed in player order (engine `_process_market`; `sim/market.py` docstring). That is symmetric.
- An earlier slot or an earlier step books first.

**(c) Replays.**
- **V (8/8):** plants 12 melon on d0 h7-h18 and sells 72 u from d10 h8/h9 to d11 h0, in slot 0, in the same step as PLACE. The book goes 9989→10060 and V earns 17,440.
- **Openers:** first sale at d10 h6 (boom, Rogues), h9-h11, and d12 (tine).
- **Us:** hands hold the harvest until dusk and sell at dawn lot h1. So we always sell after V's whole line.
- A d0 melon can be harvested from d10 h0, because age counts in days.

**Answer: yes, units can be booked first.** Placing and selling on d10 h1-h8 puts our units ahead of V on every board.
- Exact cost to V's 72-unit line, per unit we book ahead: −59 coins. By units booked: X=6 −357, 12 −704, 24 −1,437, 36 −2,375, 48 −3,519.
- Our own gain from the same move is +57/u (270 vs 212/u when sold after V).

## Verdict: NO SHIP (no cell meets the bar; every cell loses flips and gifts)
**Sale order works.**
- Early booking doubles the denial per unit: N6 early −1,213 vs default −567.
- On the V bed, the default timing moves V's melon line by exactly 0, which is why MELONVRP1 saw "no denial".

**Reach caps it.**
- The extra hands spawn at h1 on the 4 shed-access tiles. Only plate tiles within access distance 3 can be placed by h8.
- At about 5 u per tile at d10 (unfertilised, 4-5 waterings), that is at most ~20 units ahead of V: −1.1k. The −2k bar needs ≥ 31 units.

**Seed funding caps N and still gifts.**
- After the d0 h1 row, only ~142 coins of purse are left. The whole d0 seed row is 270 coins (8C + 11W), so N ≤ 3 is the most that seed money can fund.
- Even a seed-funded plate removes 4-16 d0 field tiles (−22 carrot, −27 wheat units d0-9 at N3). That costs purse d10 (−0.1k to −0.9k) and animals at d10: sheep 3.7 → 2.6 at N3 and → 1.0 at N8.
- The rival then sells the same units at higher prices, mostly d20-29 MILK/STRAWBERRY/WOOL/WHEAT. On V, N3 gives +7.7k to them against only −708 on melon.

**Self-denial.** Our own late melon line sells deeper in the book: MELON d20-29 −2.4k (N3, V bed).

The axis is closed on mechanism. The ≤ 20-unit reach limit, the 270-coin seed row and the herd/price cascade are all fixed by the engine and by our opening. The switch stays OFF.
