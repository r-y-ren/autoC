# `S/simprobe/` — reading a planner knob on the pinned judge, in the sim

2026-09-09, harness agent. Scripts under `scratchpad/simprobe/`
(`probe.py`, `compare.py`, `run.sh`). Sim runs only; no engine leg was spent.

## Usage

```
S/simprobe/run.sh "<name>" "<knob string>" [worktree] [theta]
```

* knob string: `NAME=VAL,NAME2,brain.NAME=VAL` — `NAME` is a `kagg3.core.plan`
  global, `brain.NAME` a `kagg3.core.brain` one, bare `NAME` means `True`
  (`S/drainpin/on2b.py`'s grammar).
* defaults: worktree `.claude/worktrees/arms-next`, theta
  `artifacts/kagg2_games/thetas/flow135_g350_gpfwdfv_gb028.npy`.
* writes `<name>.csv` (`seed,opponent,seat,mine,theirs` — the `S/lossflip`
  layout, so `S/lossflip/flips.py` and every downstream reader accept it),
  `<name>.log`, `<name>.summary` ending `SIMPROBEDONE`, and the summary line

```
SIMPROBE <name> HELD42: d-margin X (n=84) | LEG20: d-margin Y (n=20) | LOSS12: d-margin Z (n=24) | flips +a/-b
```

The knob-free BASE leg of a `(worktree, theta)` pair is computed once and
cached as `base_<md5-10>.csv` (+ `.meta`), so an arm after the first costs one
leg.

## What a board is

The 61 pinned-town tapes of the three judge sets — the 42 held-out ids
(`S/lossflip/heldout_ids.txt`), the 10 leg-family ids
(`S/lossflip/leg_family.py`) and the 12 `S/loss12/ids.txt` ids — both seats,
122 boards. Each board is *exactly* the board `S/lossflip/run.sh` gives the
engine: opponent index `i` is its position in
`sorted(glob(artifacts/panel_opp_town/opponent_tape_*/main.py))` and its seed is
the first draw of `default_rng(777001 + 1000003 * (i + 1))`. The town is not
drawn — it is the `town` row recorded in `artifacts/tape_actions_town/<id>.npz`
(`sim.eod.unlock_shop`), the same schedule the engine leg pins with
`KAGG3_TOWN_SCHEDULE=.../town_schedules.json`.

Knobs are forced by `setattr` **before** `kagg3.sim.rollout` is imported
(`rollout` reads plan globals at import time to build its static turn sets), and
`kagg3.core.plan` is imported out of `--src` first so `kagg3.__path__` pins the
whole sim at the worktree under test — `S/simswitch/screen_sw.py`'s pattern,
asserted at run time (`rollout.P is P`, both `__file__`s under `--src`).

## Fidelity, measured against the engine

**Levels.** `base_0fea3d58f2.csv` (worktree `agent-a85c50163fb5dcbb7`, theta
`flow135_g350`) vs the engine's `S/lossflip/flow135_g350.csv` on the 98 boards
the two share:

| set | n | coin-exact | mean abs board error | sim set margin | engine set margin |
|-----|---|-----------|----------------------|----------------|-------------------|
| HELD42 | 84 | 32 | 591 | -5,398 | -5,482 |
| LEG20 | 20 | **18** | **13** | +7,738 | +7,725 |
| LOSS12 | 24 | — | — | — | not in the engine csv |

Set-level agreement is 84 coins (HELD42) and 13 (LEG20). Per board the held-out
set carries a tail (max 6,133 on one board); the leg family is essentially
coin-exact. The 2-tape smoke reproduced the engine rows byte-for-byte
(92,926/68,110 and 68,847/61,044, both seats).

**Deltas** — the number the tool is actually for:

| arm | set | engine | sim | gap |
|-----|-----|--------|-----|-----|
| `OPEN_PUMP_ON=True,CARE_FILL_ON=True` (a85c, flow135_g350) | HELD42 d-margin | +54 | **+64** | 10 |
| | LEG20 d-margin | +46 | **+46** | **0** |
| | held-out flips | +0/-0 | **+0/-0** | — |
| `brain.PLANT_MIX_DRAIN_ON=True,GAIN=1.0` (a85c, flow135_g350) | HELD42 d-margin | -6,243 | **-6,109** | 134 |
| | LEG20 d-margin | -4,615 | **-4,616** | **1** |
| | held-out flips | +2/-8 | **+2/-8** | — |

The flips are the *same boards*, not just the same counts: `106933649/0,1`
flipped up; `106459205`, `106512362`, `106558765`, `106606614` (both seats)
dropped — all four are in the engine's dropped list. No set is off by more
than ~500 coins; the largest gap seen is 134.

Caveat on the prompt's second case: `PLANT_MIX_DRAIN_*` are `brain` globals and
do not exist in `arms-next`. The engine read quoted (HELD-OUT +2/-8,
H_dm -6,243, LEG20 -4,615) is `S/drainpin`'s `drain10`, which ran in
`agent-a85c50163fb5dcbb7` on `flow135_g350`; that is what was reproduced.

## Cost

122 boards, one leg, one process, ~1 core. Wall on a box at load ~26/12 cores,
four legs running concurrently: 588 s / 640 s / 696 s / 773 s. Compile
dominates (a 4-board smoke took 308 s), so an arm is ~10-13 min with the base
cached, ~20-25 min for a fresh `(worktree, theta)` pair. The engine equivalent
is ~20 min per arm at **10 workers**; the win is throughput (four arms at once
on a loaded box) and the absence of the shop lottery, not raw single-arm speed.

## Not verified

* LOSS12 has no engine counterpart — those 12 ids post-date
  `S/lossflip/flow135_g350.csv`, so its sim d-margin (+52 / -5,089) is
  unvalidated. It is the same machinery as the two sets that were validated.
* Only two knobs were checked, both from one worktree/theta pair. The
  `arms-next` + `gb028` base leg was computed and cached
  (`base_4d1adb3fef.csv`, byte-identical to the a85c/`flow135_g350` base — the
  `gpfwdfv`/`gb028` genes are inert at their default knobs, which is itself a
  useful sanity read) but no arm was run against it.
* Knobs that change the tape's own seat, the opponent package, or anything
  outside `plan`/`brain` are out of scope: the opponent here is the action tape,
  not a package.
