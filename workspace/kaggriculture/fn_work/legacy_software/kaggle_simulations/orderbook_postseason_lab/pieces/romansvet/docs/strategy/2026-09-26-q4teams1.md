How the Q4 P&L is computed:
- Revenue is Q4 harvest units × the seat's own net realised price for each product.
- The marginal wage is the Q4 hand-day equivalents priced at the top of that day's fib ladder. That is about 4.1-4.5k, i.e. hands #10-12 at 55 + 89 + 144 per day.
- The attributed wage uses each hand's own fib rank × its Q4 share of ops.
- The P&L does not count the own-price hit on Q1-3 wheat, which is about −1 to −2k, or the denial of the rival's wheat.

## 2. What they do on Q4 that we do not
- **DSM, DECEM and FQ always buy Q4, on d10.4-10.6.**
  - Each buys it in the first d10 step whose cash, counting that step's own sales, covers 4,000; the median purse just before is 3-5k.
  - Upstream, Q2 is bought on d6 and Q3 on d8-9. We buy Q2 a day earlier, Q3 on d10.1, and never buy Q4.
- **Q4 is an all-day relay from d10 to d27 and is mostly wheat.**
  - They plant 5-15 tiles on d10 and 8-9 on d11, then 2-8 a day, with the last planting on d27.
  - Wheat is 60-75 % of Q4 plantings and carrot comes second.
  - Watering is survival level, about 3.4-3.8 waters per planting, for 3.7-4.4 wheat units per planting. Fertiliser is light at 25-55 ops, and there are 0-2 animals.
  - About 12-18 % of Q4 tile-days stay empty.
  - Q4 yields 340-400 units and nets about +4.5k to +6.8k at the marginal wage.
- **The Q4 crew is exactly the extra hands they hire over us.**
  - Q4 takes 44-56 hand-day equivalents, about 2.2-2.8 hands/day. They run 11.7 hands on d10-19 against our 9.2, so the difference is +2.5.
  - Their hire bill is 6.2-7.1k against our 3.3k.
  - Their crews almost never PASS on d10-29 (0-3 turns/day). We PASS about 25 turns/day, roughly one idle hand-day every day.
  - They also carry 6.6-6.8 hands on d0-9 against our 3.8, with slack: 7.4-8.0 ops per hand-day, and DSM/DECEM PASS 27-33 turns/day. Alongside that early crew they buy Q2 on d6 and Q3 on d8-9.
- **What they drop in Q1-3 to pay for it:**
  - Late melon: 0.2-0.9 tiles on d10-29 against our 7.3. They sell only their d0 plate (60-72 melons at 202-221 each); we sell 87 at 162.
  - Some strawberry: 14-19 tiles against our 20.
  - They grow more wheat in Q1-3 (17-26 tiles against our 14) and hold no cash reserve.
  - Their total wheat is 380-600 units at 25-33 each; ours is 194 at 37.
  - Their rivals' wheat sells at 24-32; our rivals' sells at 43. Part of that gap is the weaker pool we play in.
- **It is one recipe with two variants, not four.**
  - DSM and DECEM are near twins. Their Q2/Q3/Q4 buy hours, hands per day, tomato and geese, and melon at exactly 60 all match, and so does their Q4 mix of wheat, strawberry, carrot and tomato.
  - FQ runs the same schedule but with a denser wheat-only Q4 (79 of 106 plantings, 15 on d10). It has no tomato or eggs anywhere, the farmer works Q4, it never PASSes, and it has the lowest wheat price on both sides (24.9 / 23.7).
  - FQ's new sub 56521606 reproduces FOURTHQ1's 56521806 row.
- **Boey, rank 2, does not use Q4.**
  - Boey bought Q4 in only 3 of 23 recent games, late on d11-12 and with 14.8k+ in hand. It lost all 3, and its Q4 P&L was −2.4k.
  - In the other 20 games it sits on 14-30k by d11-13, so not buying is not a matter of cash.
  - Boey reaches DSM-level wheat volume (410 units) from 25.7 wheat tiles in Q1-3 alone, with a lower bill of 5.2k.
  - The common denominator of all four teams is therefore not Q4. It is: 11-12 hands on d10-29 with no idle turns, Q3 by d8-9, no late melon, and 380-600 wheat units per game.
