Branch `drydeath1` (from engmix1 75d67e13). Tools: `S/drydeath1/census.py` (replay census, any seat), `run.sh` (V56 leg, `KEEP=1` keeps replays), `eng.py` (ENGINE leg), `summary.py`. Data: `S/drydeath1/deaths.tsv` (one row per death: crop, plant day/hour, death day/hour, cause, units, book price, water and dry days of that plant life, idle PASS turns on the lethal day, saveable, rescuing unit and slack), `summary.txt`.
Engine rules (kaggriculture.py): `_daily_refresh_plants`: `consecutive_unwatered >= 2` at end of day turns the plant into a WEED (DRY). The planting day counts as 1, so an unwatered planting dies that night. `_decay_plants`: after `max_lifespan_step`, a plant loses 1 unit every 2 steps and becomes a WEED at 0 (EXPIRY). SPENT means an exhausted ongoing plant with 0 units, which loses nothing.
Saveable means that on the lethal day some unit had a PASS run long enough to walk to the tile and WATER: dist+1 turns for a trailing run, 2·dist+1 for a mid-day run.

## The contradiction, resolved
(1) The IDLEWORK "85.7 % of extra plants die" was a **projection** made on a double-counted upper bound of tend capacity, for hypothetical idle-tail PLANTs. It was not a measured death rate of executed plantings. The executed fill plants survive: PLANT_FILL_LATE adds +0.11 (V56) and +0.28 (ENGINE) DRY deaths per game while adding ~2 extra EXPIRY and ~2 DECAY units per game. So the fill family did not lose by death. It loses through the n_free/herd channel and price, as ENGGATE1/ENGMIX1 found. The V56 dev100 fill cell, run here for the census, is **53-47 vs 67-33 (net −14, Δours −867, Δtheirs +72)**.
(2) WATERAUDIT's 484 hand PASS/game on days with no dying plant is consistent with this census: there are almost no dying plants.
(3) The occupancy gap (1,178 vs 1,288 tile-days) is **not survival**. ENGINE occupies more tile-days while losing more crops, so the gap is on the planting/admission side, which PLANT_FILL_LATE, PLANT_ASK and REPLANT already rejected.
