# CROP-DAY: a per-crop, day-indexed log-share bias on the plant mix

**VERDICT (2026-09-14).** A per-crop day-indexed softmax bias is *decodable and
cheap* -- one `cd` coordinate moves its own day bucket's tile count by +1 at
**0.17-1.7 sd** of a sigma-0.02 draw (worst live crop 4.8 sd; +8 tiles 1.3-7.2
sd where the board has the tiles) and touches no other day -- but it **does
not** make the ymg_aq block crop programme expressible. With `cd` free and
saturating (+-3.8 to +-10 nats), the macro fit's plant residual does not
improve: mean per-day **share** error 0.314 -> 0.350 (total variation), mean
**volume** error 4.03 -> 4.14 tiles/day, summed plant MAE 3.827 -> 3.902. The
d2 pure-wheat and d3 pure-strawberry blocks are *already pure* in the
shared-softmax fit and are missed on tile **count** (d2 6.00 of 11.33, d3 1.01
of 6.00), not on mixture. So: the shared softmax is not what loses the block
programme -- the day's tile budget is (`n_dev` target 8.56/day, fit 5.10, B
7.78).

Tools in `S/crop_day/`; the gene ships OFF (`brain.CROP_DAY_ON = False`).

## 1. Design

`cd`, appended last: `N_CROPS x N_CROP_DAY_BUCKETS` = 5 x 9 = 45 params,
**7,020 -> 7,065**, zero-init, read straight off theta (`params.cd`) rather
than emitted by the head -- the quantity is a proportion, the head already
reaches the mix through `cm`, and a 32-wide matmul per bucket would be 288
coordinates to say what 45 say. ON branch, inside the existing
`theta.shape[0] > PO.offset("cm")` branch and after the drain term:
`crop_logits += clip(CROP_DAY_GAIN * cd[:, bucket(day)], +-CROP_MIX_CLIP)`.

**Buckets.** `HIRE_BIAS_BUCKETS = (6, 11, 21)` is *not* reusable here, and
neither is the "6 buckets of 5 days" fallback: both put ymg_aq's pure-wheat day
2 and pure-strawberry day 3 in one bucket, which is the season-constant failure
one level down -- the experiment would have been decided by the edges rather
than by the hypothesis. `CROP_DAY_BUCKETS = (1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 11, 21)`: one
bucket per day through the opening (where the block programmes live), then the
hire bias's own joints, of which the hire edges are a **subset** -- so the
four-bucket scheme is this block with coordinates tied.

**Gain.** `CROP_DAY_GAIN = 64.0`: at the training sigma 0.02 one sd on one
coordinate is `64 * 0.02 = 1.28` nats of log-share -- O(1) on the scale the
softmax reads, and 1.6 sd against the 2.0487-nat melon first-tile boundary
measured at B. `CROP_MIX_GAIN`'s 256 would be 5.12 nats a draw (a multi-tile
jump every step); a raw gain of 1 would be 0.02 nats -- the ~100x-dead gene the
2026-09-09 slope rule exists to catch.

## 3. Slope (gene-slope rule, 2026-09-09)

`S/crop_day/slope.py` -- the six real ymg_aq dawns, mean decoded
`plant_target`, bisection on one `cd` coordinate ("leak" = max change on the
other day).

| day (bucket) | crop | z* for +1 tile | sd @ 0.02 | sd @ 0.01 | leak |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 10 (6) | WHEAT / STRAWBERRY | 0.00864 / 0.01046 | **0.43 / 0.52** | 0.86 / 1.05 | 0 |
| 10 (6) | MELON / CARROT / TOMATO | 0.021 / 0.023 / 0.034 | 1.05 / 1.14 / 1.68 | 2.1 / 2.3 / 3.4 | 0 |
| 20 (7) | TOMATO / CARROT | 0.0265 / 0.0328 | 1.33 / 1.64 | 2.65 / 3.28 | 0 |

"+8 tiles" is unreachable *on these boards* for any gene value: the ymg_aq
dawns plant 7.50 tiles in all at d10 and 4.00 at d20 and a saturated bias takes
all of them (WHEAT d10 4.67 -> 7.50); d20 STRAWBERRY/MELON never move because
`can_mature` masks them -- an engine fact, not a dead gene.
`S/crop_day/slope_wide.py` repeats it on wide dawns (nquad 3-4, 20k-60k coins,
19.75-26.75 tiles a day):

| day | crop | +1 tile sd @0.02 | +8 tiles sd @0.02 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 10 | MELON / STRAWBERRY | 0.21 / 0.32 | 1.32 / (holds the whole budget) |
| 10 | WHEAT / CARROT / TOMATO | 1.65 / 2.31 / 4.83 | 3.79 / 4.31 / 7.19 |
| 20 | WHEAT / CARROT / TOMATO | 0.17 / 0.18 / 1.31 | (budget) / 1.39 / 3.40 |

**Rule: PASS.** Every plantable crop's first tile is under the 5-sd threshold
(0.17-4.83 sd at sigma 0.02, median ~1.1); the eight-tile jump is 1.3-7.2 sd --
cheap for crops already in the mix, expensive for one the grow score puts last
(TOMATO 7.2 sd): the gene tilts, the grow score still prices.

## 4. Expressibility -- the measurement that answers the question

`S/macro_extract/fit.py --crop-day` (new flag, defaults unchanged). Same 180
ymg_aq dawns, same 1,500 steps at lr 0.01, Adam over all coordinates; the only
difference is the `cd` term.

| channel | ymg_aq target | B | fit (shared softmax) | fit (+ `cd`) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| plant MAE, WHEAT / STRAWBERRY | 5.21 / 1.04 | 3.89 / 0.98 | 1.185 / 1.012 | 1.101 / 1.012 |
| plant MAE, CARROT / TOMATO / MELON | 1.01 / 0.32 / 0.47 | 1.26 / 0.30 / 0.74 | 0.912 / 0.251 / 0.467 | 1.000 / 0.322 / 0.467 |
| **sum plant MAE** | | **7.17** | **3.827** | **3.902** |
| plant loss term at step 1,500 | | 7.696 (step 1) | 4.493 | 4.848 |
| mean per-day share error (TV) | | | 0.314 | 0.350 |
| mean per-day volume error (tiles) | | | 4.03 | 4.14 |
| `n_dev` tiles/day | 8.56 | 7.78 | 5.10 | 5.10 |

Per-day the two fits are the same programme (board-mean tiles):

| day | target | shared softmax | + `cd` |
|---|---|---|---|
| d2 | WHEAT 11.33, rest 0 | WHEAT **6.00**, rest 0 | WHEAT **6.00**, rest 0 |
| d3 | STRAWBERRY 6.00, rest 0 | SB 0.80 / WHEAT 0.19 | SB 0.80 / WHEAT 0.21 |
| d4 / d6 | WHEAT 6.83 + SB 3.00 / SB 10.00 + WHEAT 6.33 | nothing / WHEAT 6.69 | nothing / WHEAT 6.47 |

**The d2 block is reproduced in mixture and missed in volume, by both fits.**
The shared softmax already puts 100 % of day 2 on wheat -- 6.00 tiles where
ymg_aq plants 11.33; day 3 is 79 % strawberry in both fits, 1.01 tiles against
6.00. The fitted `cd` block is not idle -- it saturates (WHEAT +3.84 nats on
the d2 bucket, STRAWBERRY +3.86 on d3, -10.03 on the d11-20 bucket, 40 of 45
coordinates non-zero) -- and buys nothing, because the mixture was never the
binding constraint. Day 4's bucket stays exactly zero: the fit plants nothing
that day, so the share has no gradient at all.

`S/crop_day/capacity.py` says where the tiles go missing -- decoded
`plant_target` totals on the same dawns against the free tiles we would have on
their board (mean over the 6 boards):

| day | ymg_aq planted | our free tiles | B `plant_target` | fitted seed |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| d2 | 11.33 | 31.00 | **5.00** | **5.00** |
| d3 / d4 | 6.00 / 9.83 | 31.67 / 25.00 | 5.67 / 0.00 | 0.83 / 0.00 |
| d6 | 20.17 | 44.33 | 14.33 | 7.00 |
| season | 241.3 | 1073.3 | 158.8 | 116.8 |

The board is never out of tiles -- 25-50 free every day against a 5-tile want,
so the limit is the decode's own `dev_frac * n_free - animal_count`, the chain
the crop softmax sits *downstream* of. MACRO-EXTRACT section 4 item 5's
"shared-softmax capacity limit" reading was half right: the limit is a
**capacity** limit and it is not in the softmax.

## 7. What this says to do next

1. The crop mixture is **not** the ymg_aq gap and a per-crop softmax is not the
   lever. `cd` is cheap (45 params, PASS on the slope rule, exactly inert at
   zero) and is now available to ES as a day-indexed tilt; it is not a
   promotion candidate on this evidence, and no arm should be launched off the
   imitation fit alone.
2. The measured wall is tiles per day: `n_dev = dev_frac * n_free` minus the
   animal share, fitted at 5.10 against ymg_aq's 8.56 and B's 7.78 with the fit
   free to move `head[5]`, `head[6]`, `aux[2]` and 25-50 free tiles on the
   board. That is the block to take apart next -- decode saturation, the
   planner's admit/afford path, or the coins.
3. Two gates are red at HEAD and were before this leg -- `[legacy]` in
   `test_backend_agreement.py`, five crew/hire tests in
   `test_crew_and_herd_mix.py` -- so neither file can fail loudly right now.
