## 1. The switch (`plan.MELON_PQ4_ON`, default OFF; overlay `src/kagg3/agent/melon_pq4.py`, hook in `runtime.make_agent`)
**OFF.** The runtime is never wrapped.

**Latch.** The switch reads the rival's cash at d0 h1 and fires only if it is in `MELON_PQ4_CASH = "2438|2464"`.
- These are the two PQ4 codes: h0 [COW 1, WHEAT 5] in either order.
- Census of the rival's cash at h1:
  - PQ4TAPE24: 24/24 (2438 ×18, 2464 ×6).
  - HOLD52: 7/52 are PQ4-coded, and all 7 are PQ4TAPE24 games. The other 45 carry P48/other codes.
  - OTH56: 0/56.
  - V56: codes 2630+, 0 fired.
- On any other code, every call returns the inner runtime's action untouched.

**d0 h1, right after the latch.** Our h1 row spends the whole purse on these boards. For example: SELL WHEAT 48, SEED W11 C8, GOOSE 1, COW 4, SHEEP 1, leaving about 187 coins. The overlay changes the row as follows:
- It adds BUY_SEED MELON N.
- It cuts N WHEAT seeds (the filler; CARROT only if WHEAT runs out; strawberry never).
- The rest of the cost comes from the purse.
- Animal lots are split into single-unit orders, so a short purse drops the last animal(s) rather than a whole lot.
- The d0-1 PLANTs of the cut crop nearest the shed (access distance <= 3, or any from h12) become PLANT MELON.

**d10.** The plate is ripe at d10 h0 (melon's first-yield age is 10 days).
- At h0 the overlay adds one extra HIRE per ripe plate tile, up to the 10-order cap. These hands are spliced out of the planner's observation.
- Each extra hand walks to the nearest unclaimed ripe plate tile, harvests, walks back, and PLACEs. It repeats while the trip fits before h22.
- Each hour it sells SELL MELON min(L, shed) in slot 0. L = 0 means sell all.
- From d11 the tiles belong to the planner again.

**Grid:** N in {4, 6, 8, 12} × L in {6, all}.
- **Sale start {ripeness, d10 h0} collapses.** A d0 plate is ripe exactly at d10 h0. The first unit reaches the shed at h8, because the nearest plantable d0 tiles are at access distance 3. So the named 16 cells are 8 distinct cells, plus the control N = 0 (= OFF).
- Added outside the named grid: N = 2 and N = 3. These are the plates the leftover purse can nearly fund without losing an animal.

**v1 bug, fixed in v2.** v1 converted CARROT PLANTs while only WHEAT seeds had been cut. On each fired seat, one later wheat PLANT was PASSed and one carrot seed was left unplanted.
- The v2 fix converts only PLANTs of the cut crop. It is in the committed code.
- The grid ran on v1; v2 was read on n4_l6.
- In H mode v2 n4_l6 gives -4,364, t -3.01, net -4, against v1's -3,254, t -3.20, net -3. The fix does not change the verdict.

## 6. Why it cannot work in this shape
**Reach.** The nearest plantable d0 tiles are at access distance 3, so the first melons reach the shed at d10 h8. That is after their h6 lot and alongside their h9 12-lot.
- On most boards the planner's own d10 h0 row leaves only 0-4 order slots for extra HIREs (k_req 0 on 2/24 boards at N=4).
- The rest of the plate is harvested by planner hands that hold until dusk and sell at the d11 dawn.
- So we book ahead of only part of their line, and their d10-14 melon falls just 0.5k-2.6k.

**Purse.** The plate is bought out of the d0 h1 row, which already spends everything.
- N=4 drops the sheep; N>=8 also drops cows.
- The d10 cash is about -0.9k (n4) and the d0-9 herd products fall.
- The rival's late milk, strawberry and wool prices recover: +3.3k to +15.8k d15-29.

**Self-denial.** Our own late melon line sells deeper in the book: -3.2k to -11.4k d15-29.

**Summary.** The same three costs were found against V (MELONDENY1), against the P48 clone (D10WAVE1 B4/B8) and in the melon counters (MELONCOUNTER2), and they now hold on the faithful PQ4 judge. The d10-14 window gap is the programme's d0-9 body (crew, herd and melon together), not a plate bolted onto PFS.
