## 4. The pooled honest picture

- On all 12 boards, **only `flow167_g150` clears 2 SE** (t 2.05, and only just). Every other
  candidate — g170 at +2,467 included — is inside its own SE band.
- On the 10 byte-exact boards, four clear it (g130, g150, g170, 167_g150) — but that is a
  post-hoc subset chosen after seeing the deltas; treat it as a *sensitivity check*, not a result.
- **Jackknife** (drop one board, `an3.py`): dropping **107079367** — the base's worst loss,
  −22,234 — is the worst case for *every single candidate*. flow167_g150 falls to +1,668 / t 1.73,
  g170 to +1,176 / t 0.72, g167_g60 to −27. **No candidate survives the loss of one board at
  t ≥ 2.** The whole leg's positive signal is one tape deep.
- **Candidate-vs-candidate differences are not resolvable at n = 12** (paired over boards):
  g170 − g150 = +588 (t 1.04, p 0.32); 167_g150 − g170 = **+88** (t 0.08, p 0.93);
  167_g150 − g150 = +677 (t 0.89, p 0.39); g170 − g50 = +1,745 (t 1.09).
  The ordering g50 < g130 < g150 < g170 < 167_g150 in the headline numbers is **noise**.
  Only two contrasts resolve, and both are against the one candidate that lost ground:
  g170 − g80 = +2,976 (t 3.88, p 0.003) and 167_g150 − g167_g60 = +1,879 (t 2.61, p 0.024).

**Reading: the leg says "flow166/167 late generations are not worse than the live theta, and are
probably +1.5–3k on fresh losses". It cannot rank them, and it cannot certify any one of them.**
