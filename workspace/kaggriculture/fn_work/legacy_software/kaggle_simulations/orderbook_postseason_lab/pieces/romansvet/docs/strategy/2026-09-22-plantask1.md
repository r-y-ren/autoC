# PLANTASK1 — raise the Macro ask (2026-09-22)

## Implementation and parity

`plan.PLANT_ASK_ON` floors the Macro plant ask during `PLANT_ASK_DAYS` at
`PLANT_ASK_FRAC * n_free`.  It derives separate pre-land and post-land asks, assigns each extra tile
to `_candidates`' current best marginal seed ratio, and stops at the seed bill affordable from the
purse after the hire bill and existing crew reserve (and after the land gap on the post-land leg).
Thus `_wants`, land valuation, the shared grant, hiring, `plant_eff`, routing and servicing all see one
ask; there is no leftover-tile fill.  `PLANT_ASK_QUAD` admits quadrant four only when `land_reach`
saturates at 25 and the post-reserve purse holds its full 4,000 coins.

OFF whole-plan parity is byte-identical to pre-switch master `6a24864c` on three pinned boards (opening,
d14 window, d24 late).  `tests/test_plant_ask.py` also pins the window, fraction, funding, marginal crop
choice and quadrant cash/reach gates.

## Mechanism

The ask rose and executed: only 0/0/14/29 of the added requested plantings failed across the four
cells.  They were serviced and monetized, not merely queued.  Per board, the cells added 94.92/154.81/
155.62/163.89 WATER commands, 23.42/44.06/39.81/43.42 HARVEST commands, and 49.37/69.13/61.24/65.04
crop sale units.  But the added load also killed 3.15/6.42/2.87/3.35 plantings per board and displaced
the incumbent programme.

The direct extra seed bills were 768.60/1,262.30/1,194.20/1,292.60 coins per board.  Fourth land added
another 1,960/4,000/3,840/4,000 coins per board.  Those two cash sinks explain most of the first two
own-purse losses; at FRAC=1 the larger servicing workload additionally displaced more valuable work and
the shared market moved against us (Δtheirs positive).  The planner did fund, service and sell the
volume; the marginal volume simply cost more than it returned.

No cell met `net >= +3`, so the held-out 100 and exact LIVE302 tape gate were not run.  Default remains
OFF; the last unfalsified direct volume lever is closed.
