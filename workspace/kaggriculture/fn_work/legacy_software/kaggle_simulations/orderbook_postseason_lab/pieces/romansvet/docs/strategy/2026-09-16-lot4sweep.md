## 3. Verdict

**Turn 17 is the optimum.** The switch has one legal interior maximum: 14, 15
and 16 all pay the same flat ≈ +112 band margin, 17 pays +689 (6.2×), 18 is an
unguarded collision with shipped lot 3 that wipes 29 % of our sales, and 19 and
21 are negative. 17 is the only turn that sits immediately in front of lot 3
without landing on it, which is the whole mechanism — it splits the day's
largest lot while `ticks_before(17) == ticks_before(18)`, so it costs zero town
ticks. There is nothing left to sweep: the legal set {14,16,17,19,21,22,23} is
now read except for 22 and 23, both strictly later than the losing 21.

**PUMP_OFF adds rather than overlaps — and still does not pay.** LOT4@17 alone
is +791 and PUMP_OFF alone is +410; their sum is +1,201 and the measured combo
is **+1,158**, so the two mechanisms overlap by only **43 coins (3.6 %)** — an
opening-round-trip switch and an afternoon lot-depth row are genuinely
independent levers. The directly measured increment (+367, both purses moving
the same way as standalone PUMP_OFF: +204 / −164 vs +244 / −167) confirms it.
But independence is not significance: PUMP_OFF's own sd is 2,215 against
LOT4's 780, so bolting it on triples the pooled sd (780 → 2,398) and **collapses
the t from +13.19 to +6.28** while raising the mean 46 %, and the increment
loses 8 boards to win 2. On the §115b bar that governs the increment it is a
REJECT, exactly as it was standalone. Ship LOT4@17 on its own; PUMP_OFF stays
where `2026-09-16-v45leg.md` left it.
