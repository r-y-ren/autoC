## 2. What it is (class)
It is a **V-series route-replay stack, not an ENGINE/DSM-class program.** The header reads "public V39 + v9 layers". The base
is yhay81 shop-router tapes: a precomputed 719-step action tape per shop sequence. On top sit about 40 wrapper layers from
ahmedberatozer V31-V44/V53, tetsutani, aurax7, prvsiyan V221B/V224, Dmitrii Gluzdov E182, lucifer19 Nocturne,
pipe18, "elo_2615" and "2695" ports, plus 6 "Claude … layer: own implementation" layers (CARROT2, ORDERPRI2, CAPHARV,
SHEDROOM, HERD2, COWSWAP). Line-set Jaccard is 0.817 to thomastschinkel metav4 and 0.808 to V55; it contains 93-96 % of their
lines. That is under the 0.85 family cut, so it gets a new family **F32_abhinav0370**, a superset of F04. The notebook's own README
claims 1,337-31 against 57 public opponents on the author's local simulator and ties in self-play. It describes its ladder
deficit as "late-game conversion d18-29 … the same-turn sell race".

Day by day (engine replays against us, e.g. board 0 and the 5 losses):
* **d0**: step 0 wheat pump `BUY_PRODUCT WHEAT 13, BUY 30, SELL 30`. Plate d1 = **12 melon + 8 wheat** (fingerprint
  12m/8w/$101-105, same as V55-V57). Herd starts at d1 with 2 cows and 2 sheep, 4 cows by d4, geese from d7.
* **Land**: Q2 d6, Q3 d11-12, Q4 d18 on some boards (smoke 6/11/18; 1 of the 5 losses has Q4).
* **Crops**: strawberry 20→33 tiles d6-12, then 25 wheat and a carrot swap late (d25+, CARROT2/V9_CARROT). No tomato.
* **Melon**: the whole 12-tile plate is harvested and sold on **d10-11, 72 u at 245-249** (first-mover rent). This is the one
  place it out-prices us in every loss.
* **Herd**: 6 cows, 3-5 geese and 5-15 sheep by d11 (shop/price-conditioned species), fed through d29 (0.35 escapes/game).
* **Hires**: about 11 `HIRE` orders on d10 and later (the tape's roster). **Sales**: tape schedule plus RACE reservation (below). It sells
  about 350 fertilizer/game against our ~270, and dumps the shed at the end (T_TERMINAL_CLEAR, step 718).
