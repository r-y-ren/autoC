On a town-pinned rung the action-seat sim IS the engine -- 85 coins of error on margins of +-20k,

Seat and board are ruled out: `real_gate.csv` gives byte-identical `mine`/`theirs` for seat 0 and

gate uses seed base 20260904 -- different seeds, and the levels still agree to <100 coins.

## Verdict: (a), population mix -- but the mix is training WEIGHT vs GATE COVERAGE

Not "the sim lies about the family": on the family the sim is engine-exact and it *reports the loss*.
Not a seat/board effect. The run is doing exactly what it is paid to do:

* weighted in-sim margin over the 121 tape rungs: **-5,850 -> -3,271** (gens 10..50), a real +2,579 gain,
  **94 % of it on the 99 rungs the gate never plays**;
* the 22 rungs the gate does play move +590 over the same span (and **-400** from gen 20 to gen 40);
* the 10 tapes the verdict is taken on are weight 0, by design.

Held-out transfer is poor and negative: the 27 gate tapes that are no rung at all read -2,280 /
-1,847 / -700 per board at gens 10 / 20 / 40. Training on 99 boards of the same Kaggle top-tier
population moved the held-out 27 the wrong way for 40 generations.

(b) is real but *secondary and localised*: it is the whole of the 5.6 % of training weight that is
gate-visible (the 13 open-loop w=1 rungs), and it is aimed through a measurement whose sign agrees
with the pinned engine 47 % of the time.

## The change the evidence supports

**Swap the 15 open-loop rungs for their `artifacts/tape_actions_town/<id>.npz` twins** -- all 15
files exist locally; the config currently points them at `artifacts/tape_actions/<id>.npz`. This is
the only rung set with a *measured* discrepancy (bias +12.1k/board, sign 47 %), it is 100 % of the
gate-visible weighted signal, and the fix is a path edit. Expected size: those 13 w=1 rungs stop
rewarding play the pinned engine scores at -2,393/board; on the fidelity numbers the corrected
signal is worth ~12k/board of bias removed on 5.6 % of the weight, i.e. ~+680 coins/board of honest
gradient, plus the sign errors on 7 of 15 rungs.

Then, the larger lever the same data implies: put weight on the gate's own distribution. The gate's
42 non-family tapes carry 13/231 = **5.6 %** of the training weight; the family carries 0. Raising
the 42 to weight 2 (84/231 = 36 %) is what makes the fitness's +2.6k of sim gain land where the
verdict is taken. Free bonus: because the sim is engine-exact on pinned rungs, **the leg20 verdict
itself can be computed in-sim every generation** (7 of its 10 tapes are already pinned rungs, read
to 85 coins) -- an in-sim leg20 screen would have refused the gen-10 record without spending a
173 s engine leg.

## The corrected recipe

`scratchpad/simleg/launch_flow166.sh` (derived from the remote `~/launch_flow164.sh`, not launched).
147 tape rungs + 4 archetypes = 151: **w3 20**, **w2 117**, **w0 10** (the leg family). All 147 are
`artifacts/tape_actions_town/<id>.npz`; the 15 open-loop rungs are repointed, the 27 gate tapes that
were no rung are added, and the byte-identical duplicate `106581754` is dropped from `--tape-actions`
and `--real-gate-opponent` (gate 49 -> 48 opponents, 96 games). All 38 remaining non-family gate
tapes carry w2 (5.6 % -> 36 % of the training weight). Every npz verified present on the remote
(`~/stage_leg20/artifacts/tape_actions_town/`, 173 files) and locally; every `--rung-weight` key
matches a `--tape-actions` entry; `bash -n` clean.

`--abs-select` selects fixed **seed pairs** (`sel`/`hold`/`all`), not rungs -- there is no flag that
restricts the abs probe to named rungs, and none is needed: `AbsReport` is a rung-**weighted** mean
(`src/kagg3/es/train.py:1342`, `wm = np.average(..., weights=w)` at `:5807`), so the w2/w0 change
already moves record nomination onto the gate's boards and already keeps the family out of it.
