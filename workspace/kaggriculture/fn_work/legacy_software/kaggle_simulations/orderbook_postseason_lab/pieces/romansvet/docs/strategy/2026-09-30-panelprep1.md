# PANELPREP1: one-command paired panel for a deterministic programme body; crew and crewF4Sbc body reads

**Date:** 2026-09-30, 09:08Z to 11:20Z.

**Questions:**
1. Build ONE command that judges any deterministic body against the field in under 90 minutes, paired with the live PFS agent on identical boards and scoring both purses.
2. Run it on the best existing bodies to learn how the loss to the field scales with the HOLD20 coins ratio. Three bodies:
   - PROGRAMME3R j4 (0.805);
   - the CREWSCHED1 crew executor (crewsched1_0930 cf1b9790, `PROG_RB_DIRECT_ON=True,PROG_CREW_ON=True`, HOLD20 0.847);
   - on the coordinator's request, CREWCOMBO1 crewF4Sbc (e68700d1, HOLD20 0.904 coins / 0.787 value).

**Verdict:** numbers only. No body is judged here.

## The panel

**Command.** Both scripts live in S/panelprep1:

```
bash S/panelprep1/panel.sh <tree_src> "<switch string>" <tag>     # launch; returns at once
bash S/panelprep1/panel_sum.sh <tag>                                # fetch + print the table (partial reads are fine)
```

- **Body knobs:** env knobs such as `PROG3_KNOBS` and `CREWBC_W`, plus any variable named in `PANEL_ENV_VARS`, are exported to the local worker and forwarded to the remote chain.
- **Files:** anything the body needs, such as BC weights, must sit inside `<tree_src>`, because only the tree is copied to the remote.
- **`PANEL_NOVENDOR=1`:** plays the game without `ev._vendored_imports`. A body that imports kagg3 lazily in-game needs it (see slips). The V56 bank agent and the tapes seat no packaged kagg3 agent.
- **Other env options:**
  - `NO_LOCAL=1` skips the local worker;
  - `JOBS_R` / `JOBS_L` override the job lists, and `JOBS_R=""` means local only;
  - a job `m40:3` or `p48:3` runs only the first 3 games.

**Sets.** All are paired with the live PFS agent on the same board and seat.

| set | runner | worker | games | control (PFS) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| V56 m40 + v21, reacting | S/panelprep1/pvr.py, a copy of S/vband1/vr.py with `PANEL_NOVENDOR` added; seat 0; bank agent ahmedberatozer v56 | local, 1 | 40 + 21 | S/vband1 v56_ctl_m40_w0/w1 and v56_ctl_v21 rows (PFS anchor + OFF string) |
| P48TAPE142, gate-reactive | remote ptape.py `PANEL_REACT=1` = the GENETAPE1 grun.py GT1_REACT rule (P48 rule 5.4 re-decided on the sim WOOL price, lot adjusted by the rival's wool-stock delta) | remote, 2 | 142 (REFRESH6 p48tape142.txt) | anchor PFS sim rows = the live record, 142/142 exact |
| PQ4TAPE24, taped | ptape.py | remote | 24 (pq4tape1r pq4eps.txt) | anchor sim, 23/24 exact (115319134 is the known non-exact game; the control uses the anchor sim) |
| OTH56, taped | ptape.py | remote | 56 (p48sweep1r oth56.txt) | anchor sim = live, 56/56 exact |

- **Pooling:** by unique (episode, seat), with priority P48 > PQ4 > OTH. 18/24 PQ4 games and 10/56 OTH games are inside the P48 list; SWITCHSWEEP1R's count of 2 for OTH is off by 8. So the pooled n is 255: 61 V56 + 194 unique tape games.
- **Which reactive runner:** GENETAPE1 grun.py GT1_REACT. It is verified exact: GENETAPE1 offr == off == live 51/51, and here 3/3 P48 plus the 91-game ctl build. It is also more reactive than RSTUNE1R's G25, because it adjusts the lot by the stock delta as well as by price.
- **Loaders:** ptape.py loads games from pq4tape1r, livehealth1r, p48loss1r, refresh5 and refresh6 (REFRESH6 run6.py). It records:
  - our sales ledger at steps 360, 432 and the end;
  - wool audit counters;
  - reactive order changes;
  - per-call p99 and max;
  - runtime errors.
- **Control ledgers** (for the unit columns) are cached once: rstune1r h_ctl + t_ctl (103 games) plus a new 91-game `pfs_ctlrest` build on the anchor tree (programme2r/tree/src). A future body pays only for its own games.
- **Load gate:** a remote launch happens only when the 1-min load is under 12, read in the same ssh call, and every job is re-gated in pchain.sh. At most 2 remote workers and 1 local worker, all under `nice -n 19 ionice -c 3`.
- **Table:** panel_sum.sh prints the RSTUNE1R pooled_ref100 format with these columns:
  - per set: n, own, rival and margin with t, W off vs on, net flips;
  - strawberry d15-17 and milk/wool/egg d18-29 units vs the control;
  - wool-gate changes and divergences;
  - body own coins, win score off vs on, ctl exact, apply p99, errors;
  - pooled rows by unique game, including the paired win-score delta with its 95 % lower bound;
  - a BAR block with the astra bs15 numbers.

**Line fits:**
- **m40 (4 points):** the least-squares line gives 1.38k dmargin per +0.01 of ratio and 1.30 margin coins per body own coin. Zero own margin vs V56 needs ratio 1.065, or own coins of 110.0k. Per step: 0.805->0.847 +2.00k/0.01, 0.847->0.904 +0.70k/0.01, 0.904->0.920 +3.40k/0.01. At the last-step slope, zero margin needs ratio 0.98.
- **v21 (4 points):** the least-squares line gives 1.34k dmargin per +0.01 of ratio and 1.26 margin coins per body own coin. Zero own margin vs V56 needs ratio 1.079, or own coins of 112.7k. Per step: 0.805->0.847 +2.46k/0.01, 0.847->0.904 +0.39k/0.01, 0.904->0.920 +3.40k/0.01. At the last-step slope, zero margin needs ratio 0.98.

**The per-step slope is not monotone.** 0.847 -> 0.904 (F4S + BC) bought only 0.70k (m40) and 0.39k (v21) per +0.01. 0.904 -> 0.920 (`feed_all_until=26`) bought 3.4k per 0.01 on both legs. It is also the only step that cut V56's coins (m40 rival -2.4k). It restores late milk: m40 100.5 vs PFS 101.5, v21 114.7 vs 97.7. That is the late animal-volume half of the invariant. The other half, strawberry d15-17, stays at 14-23 units against PFS's 48-51 for every body.

**The rival term barely moves with the ratio.** V56 earns +15.4k to +20.6k more against every executor body than against PFS: m40 rival +19,994 / +17,516 / +17,807 / +15,376 for j4 / crew / crewF4Sbc / crewF4Sbcfa. The body sells 14-23 strawberries d15-17 against PFS's 48-51, so V56's strawberry and wool prices are not denied. Almost all of the ratio gain goes to our own coins.

**vs P48:**
- **p48c clone** (PROGRAMME3R): j4 dmargin -29.8k (W 12/32 vs 32).
- **P48TAPE142 gate-reactive:** the crew body reads -1.4k (t -0.45); crewF4Sbc margin +3167 sd 36239 t 1.04; crewF4Sbcfa margin +7607 sd 36168 t 2.51. But the tape rival cannot react to a body that diverges from step 0. Tape margins carry sd 35-48k per game, against 5-7k for switch reads, so the tape sets are close to noise for a full-divergence body. The reacting V56 is the only closed-loop read in this panel.
