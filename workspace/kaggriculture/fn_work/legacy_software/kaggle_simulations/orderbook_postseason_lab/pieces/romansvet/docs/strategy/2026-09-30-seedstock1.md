# SEEDSTOCK1: is seed stock the cause of the executor's acreage loss?

**Date:** 2026-09-30, 08:39Z to ~09:40Z. Opus, xhigh, time box 75 min.

**Question:** CREWSCHED1 found that our deterministic MMPQ executor (tree_j4 + PROG_CREW_ON) keeps 8-15 empty and 3-8 weed tiles on d12-28 against MMPQ's ~1+1, and that its teacher-forced PLANT match stays at 0.15, with "PLANT -> PASS for want of a seed" as a top mismatch. Is the seed the cause, and what purchase rule closes it?

**Verdict: NO. The seed is not the cause.**
- Seed-out-of-stock explains 0.3 empty tile-days per game out of 216 (MMPQ 0.2 of 125).
- 80 % of our empty tile-steps are tiles with a PLANT task and no hand heading there. The executor is labour-bound.
- MMPQ-level seed stock (c_wbuf 6 / 12) is noise: HOLD20 +0.005 (t 0.64) and +0.009 (t 0.94).
- A labour rule that brings our empty tile-days down to MMPQ's level (216 -> 119-124) LOSES coins: -0.012 (t -1.54) to -0.061 (t -5.85).
- Acreage upkeep is therefore not the missing piece either. The wheat gap sits in plant deaths and yield per harvest on the same number of plantings.

  - the emitted market list;
  - for our executor, the orders dropped by the 10-order cap and the seed claims passed to `market()`.
- **Reproduction:** all finals match CREWSCHED1 exactly (HOLD20 j4 0.805, crew 0.847).
- **Code read** (/mnt/e/_work/kagg3_wt_crewsched1, cf1b9790):
  - `src/kagg3/prog/direct.py` assigns every empty tile a PLANT task: demand crops first, then wheat filler while cash allows.
  - Units walk to those tasks regardless of seed stock.
  - Seeds are claimed just in time, when a unit is assigned to walk to a tile (pass 2) or stands on it without a seed (pass 1: PASS + claim). They are bought in that step's market list, in the order SELL -> HIRE -> ANIMAL -> LAND -> SEED -> PRODUCT, then truncated at 10 orders.
- **Engine order** (sim.hpp `step`): unit actions run BEFORE the market. A seed bought at step t is plantable at t+1. If PLANT requests for a crop exceed the seeds held, all of that turn's PLANTs of that crop are dropped. Seed prices are fixed (wheat 10).

## (2) The seed rule, MMPQ vs our executor

**MMPQ** (truth records; logs/mmpqseed_h20.txt and mmpqseed2_truth.txt; d10-26):
- **Stock:** holds a standing seed stock of 9.0 seeds at h0 (6.2 wheat), against 3.3 empty tiles. Wheat stock peaks at 9.3 on d16 and runs down to 0.5 by d28. During h6-21 it sits at 2-12 and hits 0 in only 10 of ~6,700 steps.
- **Wheat buying:** 1 seed per order (74 % of orders) or 2 (17 %), in any hour h5-h20 (peak h12-h16).
  - P(buy) is about 0.4 whatever the stock level (0.39-0.49 for stock 2-8), so it is not a reorder point.
  - Only 26 % of MMPQ plantings (61.9 of 241 a game) follow a same-crop buy in the step before. The rest plant from stock.
- **Carrot:** bought in lots of 6 (142 orders), sometimes 12 or 18, mostly h12-h16, spread over d10-26.
- **Order position:** seeds are never cut by the 10-order cap. No MMPQ list on d8-28 exceeds 10 orders. Its h0 list is SELL + HIRE = 10, and it buys only 1.5 seed units a game at h0-h4.

**Our executor (crew):**
- **Stock:** 3.1 seeds at h0 (2.3 wheat) against 8.9 empty tiles.
- **Buying rule:** just in time, n = claimed tiles - stock, per crop, cash-checked. Wheat is bought only when stock ≤ 2-3: P(buy) is 0.69 at stock 2, 0.08 at 4 and 0 at ≥ 6.
- **Order position:** seed orders come after sells and hires, so the 10-order cap drops 25.9 seed units a game (29.1 in j4): 24.7 at h0 (behind the dawn SELL + HIRE list) and 1.2 at h1. They are re-bought at h1, which is the 25 seed units a game our executor buys at h1.
- **Cash blocks:** 2.7 seed units a game in crew. j4 has 97, all on d8-9: its land-reserve lead of 1 day holds back demand-crop seeds, which crew rule 9 already fixes.

**Difference in one paragraph:** MMPQ keeps a standing seed stock of about 6 wheat plus carrot lots of 6, and tops it up 1-2 seeds per step all day, so its hands plant from stock. Our executor buys each seed in the step a hand is dispatched to the tile, holds about 2 in stock, and loses about 26 seed units a game to the 10-order cap. Under our own play both rules deliver the seed before the hand arrives. The seed-out delay costs 0.3 tile-days a game, and cap drops are re-bought one step later while the hand is still walking. The rule difference is real but not binding. What differs is that 35 of our tiles a game wait more than 24 h (117 tile-days), mostly in rows 8-9, because no hand is free to walk there.

## (3) The fix, and its reads

**Seed-emission rule (MMPQ-faithful).** This is the concrete version of the rule, but it does not close the gap:
- at d ≤ 27, every step from h1: `BUY_SEED WHEAT max(0, 6 + plants_this_step - stock_wheat)`;
- `BUY_SEED CARROT 6` whenever carrot stock is below the carrot tiles assigned;
- cap handling: at h0, emit BUY_SEED before the HIRE fill, so it takes the slot of the last hire (that hire moves to h1 as MMPQ does), and the 24.7 h0-dropped seed units a game are never cut.

The existing knob `c_wbuf` (wheat claim = plants + buffer) implements the stock part. HOLD20, paired vs crew (logs/cells1.txt):

**Labour test: does acreage upkeep pay?** New knob `c_eage` (worktree kagg3_wt_seedstock1, branch seedstock1_0930, default 0; OFF reproduces crew's per-step records exactly on HOLD20):
- the executor remembers the first step each empty or weed tile was seen;
- the tile's PLANT and DIG priority drops by `c_eage` per hour after `c_eage_h0` = 3 h, capped at `c_eage_cap` = 4.

- **Where the labour goes:** the walk to rows 8-9 is paid for with WATER and HARVEST elsewhere. Weeds rise to 63-70 and carrot falls to 71-87.
- **What we get back:** wheat moves only +9-11 units.

  - about 25 plantings a game die to weeds (28 vs 8 deaths);

  Acreage count and seed are not the gap.

## What the next builder should take

1. **Close the seed axis.** Stock level, JIT vs buffer and cap drops explain at most 0.3 tile-days a game. The MMPQ stock rule reads +0.005 (t 0.64).
2. **Close the acreage axis too.** Empty tiles are a symptom of a labour-bound crew. Forcing MMPQ-level acreage costs 0.012-0.061 of the ratio.
3. **The next test is survival and yield per planting**, not count:
   - the 20 extra wheat deaths a game;
   - 3.91 vs 4.68 units per harvest.
   Both are the water/fertilise service of the tiles we already plant, i.e. CREWSCHED1's "bundles" (WATER+HARVEST+PLANT+WATER in one visit). A cell must show harvests ≥ 165 and deaths ≤ 12 on HOLD20 before any coins read.
