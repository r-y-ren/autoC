## 2. Which subs play in the final, and how the final LB is made
- Evaluation: "only the latest 2 submissions are tracked. The latest 2 submissions are also used for final leaderboard evaluation."
- Evaluation: "Every bot submitted will continue to play episodes until the end of the competition, with newer bots playing a much more frequent number of episodes. On the leaderboard, only your best-scoring bot will be shown".
- Evaluation, Final Evaluation section: "Games will continue to run for approximately two weeks to continue to reduce uncertainty, especially for new agents. A final Bradley-Terry tournament will be run on those episodes to produce the final leaderboard."
- Timeline: "October 1, 2026 to (approx) October 15, 2026 - We will continue to run games, or until the leaderboard has reached convergence. At the conclusion of this period, the leaderboard is final."
- Admin post by María Cruz, 2026-07-31 (https://www.kaggle.com/competitions/kaggriculture/discussion/731587): "At the end of those two weeks, we will be running a single Bradley-Terry Tournament, which will determine the final leaderboard rankings. ... we believe this change reduces any "hot streaks""
- Open question: a user asked on 09-15 whether the BT fit uses only the Oct 1-15 episodes or the whole history, and how ties count. The organisers have **not answered**, so the episode window is not settled. The phrase "those episodes" most plausibly means the post-deadline games.
- A team's rank most plausibly comes from its best of the 2 latest subs ("only your best-scoring bot will be shown"). No text describes a manual choice, and no text describes a rating reset. The live Elo carries on, but it does not decide the final ranking: the BT fit does.

## 3. Active slots and retirement
- Evaluation: "only the latest 2 submissions are tracked". FAQ: "Only your most recent **${competition.NumScoredSubmissions}** are active", with API `numScoredSubmissions = 2`. This means the slots work FIFO: the newest 2 uploads are the active ones, and we cannot pick which 2.
- The boilerplate rules §2.2b say "You may select up to two (2) Final Submissions for judging." This conflicts with the competition-specific Evaluation text ("latest 2"). Assume the latest 2 play.
- Evaluation: "Each day your team is able to submit up to 5 agents". Rules §2.2a say the same (5/day), and the API gives `maxDailySubmissions = 5`.

## 4. Time and resource limits
- The pages do not state actTimeout, runTimeout or overage. The FAQ variables `${competition.AgentRam}`, `AgentCpuCores` and `AgentDisk` are unresolved, and GetCompetition does not return them.

- Size: API `submissionSizeLimitMb = 20480`, so the only stated limit is 20 GB. Getting Started: "Your submission must have a `main.py` at the root with an `agent` function"; for multi-file agents, "bundle into a tar.gz with `main.py` at the root". Files live in `/kaggle_simulations/agent/`.

## Where memory is WRONG or incomplete
1. **The final ranking is a Bradley-Terry tournament, not the Elo rating.** ratingpath2 §4 ("final decided entirely by theta"; the plateau is reached within about 200 games) holds only if BT reflects true strength. Under BT, the episodes matter, not the live rating path. Early games at a low rating are not "lost" rating, but a new sub gets far fewer episodes in the BT sample if it arrives late: "especially for new agents", "newer bots playing a much more frequent number".
2. "Retired subs stop playing": the text says every bot keeps playing, but only the latest 2 are "tracked" and "used for final leaderboard evaluation". What we observed (a retired sub's episodes stop) fits "tracked", so the practical rule is unchanged.
3. 09-23 was also the entry deadline and the date notebook publishing was disabled, not only the team-merger deadline.
4. Do not assume the "2 final selections" boilerplate lets us pick. The last 2 uploads before 09-30 23:59Z are the final pair.

Timing implication: make the last upload well before 2026-09-30 23:59Z, and make sure both final slots hold the two strongest files. A 3rd upload retires the older of the two.
