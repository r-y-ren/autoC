## 6. What this changes

* **The lever is live and it is not new code.** `OPEN_PUMP_ON=False` is a one-word switch
  already in `plan.py`. Its value rises with V45 adoption: at today's 2/26 it reads +414
  (t +1.16) on the live leg; at full adoption the controlled measurement says ~+1.1k a game.
  **Re-run `run_v45.sh` on a fresh cut in 24-48 h** — the same 30-board recipe, one `dl.sh`
  and one `cut_all.sh` — and the same arm becomes a promotion-grade read or it does not.
  That is the cheapest calibrated experiment on the board.
* **`OPEN_PUMP_UNITS` must never be raised.** 70 is −779 live / −5,344 synthetic; 80 is
  −4,030 live. V45 sized *its* trip to 70 against a population that does not draw at hour 0;
  we do, so the same number is a gift.
* **`OPEN_PUMP_TELL_KEEP0` and `OPEN_PUMP_TELL_MIN` are closed.** The tell is blind (§3.1)
  and the fix is worth +2 coins (§4.2). No switch was written. nbintel §6.1's two probes are
  both discharged: probe 1 says resize *down to zero*, probe 2 says the turn-reading tell is
  not worth building.
* **The judge's blind spot is now bounded, not just named.** nbintel §6.2 warned that
  replayed tapes understate the live band on the opening. §4.2 puts a number on it: on the
  one axis V45 changed, a pre-09-15 tape misprices a shipped switch by **1,062 coins a
  game**. `_RACE` (V44) remains un-firing against us for the reason nbintel gives — it needs
  our worker positions to equal theirs — so **§115b's bar should not be loosened.**
* **Nothing about the melon story survives.** Neither seat's day-1 melon count moves in any
  arm on any board. V45's own claim ("their funded first-day plan drops a melon") is true
  against *another clone*, not against us.

## 7. What was NOT done

* No `OPEN_PUMP` change is shipped. Nothing under `src/` was touched; every arm is a
  run-time switch on the existing constants, and the constant reads are reported as reads.
* Only `OPEN_PUMP_ON=False` was taken to the engine on the other families; the other five
  arms are sim-only. A §115 candidate table was not assembled (no LOSS10 / TOPB3R9 legs),
  and no promotion is claimed on any statistic.
* The synthetic V45 set was measured in the sim (18 boards) and the engine (18 boards) but
  **not** registered in any judge family; its ids carry a `V45` suffix precisely so they can
  never be mistaken for a live tape.
* `OPEN_PUMP_MIN_MONEY`, `OPEN_PUMP_DAY` and the `B_WHEAT` slot choice were not re-priced.
* **Pre-existing, not touched here:** `tests/test_open_pump.py::test_off_plan_is_byte_identical_to_the_pre_switch_planner`
  and two `tests/test_open_pump_racer.py` cases fail on master with `src/` unmodified (the
  OFF-plan digest is `d43120eca5443f9b` against a pinned `e086a25a8c18fb6f`). They are
  digest pins of the same family as the nine failures triaged in
  `2026-09-14-test-triage.md` §4 and are outside this leg's scope, but a future
  `OPEN_PUMP` change must re-pin them rather than inherit a red gate.
