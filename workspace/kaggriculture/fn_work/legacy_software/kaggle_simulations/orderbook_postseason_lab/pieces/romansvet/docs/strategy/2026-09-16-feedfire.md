# FEEDFIRE — the unfed-fire care bank is NOT +1,660/board, it is +204/+50

**VERDICT: STOP BAR HIT on both legs. Nothing built, no switch, no gate run.**
CARETIMING's headline "care bank destroyed at an UNFED fire = 1,660 coins/board
ENG22, 1,524 V45" does not survive two corrections. Priced the way the two-purse
rule requires, the whole ceiling — *every* unfed fire fed, wheat paid for at the
day's quote — is **+204/board on ENG22 and +50/board on V45**. The oracle
variant that feeds only the fires whose gain beats the wheat (perfect hindsight
selection, which no plan can do) is **+359 / +191**. The bar was +450 on both
legs. The feed family is CLOSED.

## Q1 — the two corrections, and the cause table

**(1) Half the unfed fires are on day 29 and produce nothing at all.**
A fire lands at the *end* of its day. A day-29 fire's units would need a day-30
HARVEST, and there is no day 30, so they are never harvested and never sold.
8.5 of our 22.2 unfed fires/game (ENG22) and 10.4 of 19.2 (V45) are day-29
fires. `plan.py:7373` refuses every feed there for exactly this reason
(`survival_pays = day < VAL.pay_day()`), and it is right. They carry **0 coins**.
They also carry 6.2 / 7.6 of the 7.6 / 9.2 "escapes" per board: deliberate
end-of-game abandonment with a mean 1.2 / 0.2 days of production left.

**(2) The burned units were priced at the game-MEAN realised sale price.**
8.5 of our 18.9 burned units per game are WOOL. At the game mean WOOL fetches
67; at the **spot quote on the day the fire lands** it is at or near the floor.
Those 8.5 units are 985 coins at the mean and **136 at spot**. The plan reads
the spot quote (`view.price[an_prod]`), so the mean price is not a price it
could ever have acted on, and selling 15 more animal units would push the quote
further down, not up. Ledger below is spot-priced; the mean-priced column is
kept so the gap is visible.

Reach is not the constraint: 0.0 unfed fires/board are "crew never got there",
and on the refused ones a unit was standing on the tile (usually for
COLLECT_FERTILIZER) with wheat already in its bag about half the time. The
opponent classes leak far more — ENG22 +1,009/board at spot, V45 +171 — so this
is a place the engine class bleeds, but that is their loss, not our gain.

## Q2 — the mechanism (measured, not built)

Engine: `FEED` takes 1 WHEAT from the *acting unit's own inventory*
(`kaggriculture.py:505-514`, `_farmer_inventory`), not from the shed — a unit
must withdraw at the shed first, so shed wheat is a necessary and not a
sufficient condition. `_daily_refresh_animals` (`:820-833`) pays
`min(max_held, y + 1 + pop(pending_care_bonus))` only when `fed_today`, and
zeroes the bank either way.

`src/kagg3/core/plan.py`, the whole feed decision:

- `:7373` `survival_pays = day < VAL.pay_day()` — the day-29 cut-off. **This is
  the line that produces 38 % / 54 % of the "unfed fires", and it is correct.**
- `:7468-7470` `fires_tonight` / `must_feed` (`t_cons >= 1`) / `bank_feed`
  (`fires_tonight & t_bank > 0`).
- `:7513` `feed_want = has_animal & (t_water == 0) & (must_feed | bank_feed | care_ok)`.
- `:7557-7558` `feed_value = where(must_feed, keep_val + bank_val, bank_val) + care_val`
  with `bank_val` (`:7551`) `= min(t_bank, max_held - 1) * view.price[an_prod]`
  — the **spot** quote, and the one comparison a one-line change could move.
- `:7562` `feed_price` = the hour-0 wheat quote for shed wheat, the buy curve
  above it. `:7564` `feed_pass = feed_want & (feed_value > feed_price)`.
- `:7611-7612` `feed_need` / `wheat_short` → the wheat BUY candidate.
- `:8057` `wheat_avail = shed_wheat + wheat_buy`, `:8065`
  `want_feed = feed_pass & (feed_rank < wheat_avail)` — the ration.
- Executor: `:8317` `v_feed` → `:8372` `tile_value`, plus the mandatory tier
  `mand_feed` at `:8381` (`FEED_MANDATORY_ON`, already ON).

**Shape verdict: shape-static.** The only movable comparison is `bank_val`'s
price — a `CARE_HOLD_ON`-style forward/floored price in place of the spot quote
(module constant, no gene, layout 7,428 untouched). It is not worth building:
raising the price the bank is valued at only buys the WOOL fires, which are the
ones whose units are genuinely worth ~nothing when they land, and the forward
price is the sell-timing family, CLOSED (SPREAD6, PRICEVOL).

## Dead ends / what this closes

- "Unfed fires are a 1,660-coin bucket" — an artifact of mean-price valuation
  plus day-29 fires. Any future ledger that prices a counterfactual unit must
  use the **spot quote on the day it lands**; the two-purse rule already said so.
- Feed reach / crew labour: 0.0 fires per board. Not a lever.
- Feed rationing by shed wheat: 2.2 / 1.0 fires, +83 / +10 coins. Not a lever.
- The **feed family is CLOSED** alongside wool retarget, fertilizer hold/denial,
  sell cadence, market rows, crop mix, tiles+hands, care/harvest timing.
