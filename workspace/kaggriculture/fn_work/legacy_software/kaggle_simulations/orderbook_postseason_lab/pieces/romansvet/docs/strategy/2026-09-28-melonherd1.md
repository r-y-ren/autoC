# MELONHERD1 (2026-09-27 21:25Z-23:05Z): a latched d2-9 herd lift on the 51 BAND142 MELON seats -> NO SHIP (every cell gifts)

**Verdict: NO SHIP.** All six grid cells lose flips and gift the rival (Δtheirs t 3.7-8.3; each was killed by the rule at n = 27). No cell reached +3 flips, so GATE2 and the full-band run were not triggered. The latch is exact: with it OFF, 2/2 seats are byte-identical to `pfv1`. With it ON, the 10/10 V seats are byte-identical to `pfv1` (it never fires there). The src stays on the worktree branch `melonherd1` (aab86e58, unmerged).

**Mechanism (in one line).** A bigger herd without the crew to feed it escapes. The lift spends the d5-9 purse on animals, and the day's hires drop:
- hires 3/4 vs 5/7 on d7/d8;
- FEED ops 1-2/day vs 6-9.

As a result, 7 animals go unfed 2 days and escape on d8 (the whole herd except 3 cows). The planner then re-buys cows, and they escape again. At d10 the herd is fewer geese and sheep, not more. Our EGG/FERT/WOOL (or MILK) sales into the shared books fall by 40-70 units each, and the rival sells its unchanged volume at higher prices.

- **What happens.** The lift turns cash into animals (d5 purse) at VALUE_CAP priority. The same days lose the hires that carry FEED, so the herd goes unfed for 2 days and escapes (engine `consecutive_unfed >= 2`). The re-bought stock escapes again.
- **Where the herd ends up.** Across the 27-34 seats of every cell, the lifted lane is flat to +1 and the other lanes fall:
  - +2s cells: cows 5.9→3.0 (d9 cell) and 6.0→4.9 (d5 cell);
  - +c cells: sheep 3.4→0.8;
  - geese ≈1.6→0.3-1.3 in every cell.
- **How it matches the prior evidence.** This is the HERDTILT/MELONHYBRID1 finding seen from the other side: V56's d10 herd of 12.9 rides on 51 hires d1-9 vs our 19.6. The herd is downstream of the crew, and the crew is downstream of a different d0-1 purse plan (V56 d1 cash 18 vs PFS 214).
- **What does not work.** A herd ask added on a PFS body without the crew is not a smaller version of V56. It is a herd PFS cannot feed.
- **What would be needed.** Any further herd lever needs the feed labour first. The crew/hire axis is CLOSED, and HIRE levers gifted on every leg (TRAINFIX/crew routing = trained solution), so this family closes here.
