## 3. Design (shipped form, OFF by default)
`Solver.mode2_ii`: on a failed insertion, if `ROUTE_VRP_REPAIR_ON` and the hand has stops, `_repair(base, pend, trial,
_CLOCK()+REPAIR_MS/1000)`; None -> break as before. `_unit_best` (exact cheapest FEASIBLE slot; base route must be feasible
— a first draft accepted INF-INF=0 deltas after an ejection broke a give->need chain and cascaded; caught on dawn 263),
`_eject`, `_repair`. Constants REPAIR_MS=100, REPAIR_EJECT_K=4. Every step calls `_check()` so the apply() deadline raises
_Timeout and the best checkpoint is restored; ctx/checkpoint of the repaired crew is recorded exactly as a plain drop.
Stats `rep_try`/`rep_ok` only when ON. mode2_opt (ROUTE_VRP_OPT_ON, off) untouched.
