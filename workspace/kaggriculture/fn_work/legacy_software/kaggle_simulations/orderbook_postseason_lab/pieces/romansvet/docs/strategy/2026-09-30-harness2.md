## 1. Milk soft gate (P48 rule 5.5)

**Fit, P48 rival seats, d10-28, steps with milk stock > 0 (n 55,168):**

| slot | price < 50 | 50-150 | ≥ 150 |
|---|---|---|---|
| cadence h ≡ 1 mod 4 (n 13,945, 54.7 % of milk sales) | 0.317 | 0.323 | 0.304 |
| other hours (n 41,223) | 0.079 | 0.086 | 0.103 |

- Logistic fit (`fit/milkfit.py`, `milkfit.json`) on const, cadence, price/100, cadence×price/100 and log1p(stock):
  - weights -2.946, +1.743, **+0.121 (t 5.3)**, **-0.183 (t -5.5)**, +0.242 (t 11.5);
  - implied cadence sell probability at stock 6 falls from 0.323 at price 10 to 0.285 at price 300.
  - Off-cadence it rises from 0.08 to 0.11.
- **Milk is stock- and cadence-driven, not price-gated, on the programme tapes.** The P48RULES1R figure of 0.28 → 0.64 (r 0.13) does not reproduce per step. Lots: median 3, mean 4.65.
- The same fit on wool is also shallow (cadence 0.26 → 0.31). The G25 hard wool gate stays as RSTUNE1R fitted it; it was not refitted here.

**Rule in v4:** coupled thinning on the counterfactual price, with stock held at the live value so that only the price reacts.
- A recorded milk sale is withheld with probability 1 − P(pc)/P(pl) when P(pc) < P(pl).
- A sale of min(stock, 3) is added with probability (P(pc) − P(pl))/(1 − P(pl)) when the tape holds and P(pc) > P(pl).
- u is deterministic per (seed, step, seat).
- At equal prices it is the identity.
- It fired on 10 P48 games as a hold (10 sales) and on 10 as an add (11 sales), and on 1 PQ4 hold. It is a near no-op, as the fit says.

## 2. The cash rule (replacing the 50-coin loan)

Measured with `fit/cash1.py` and `fit/cash2.py` on the recorded acquisitions (live twin: units executed per order slot, purse before and after).

| | P48 | PQ4 |
|---|---|---|
| buy slots | 98,718 | 15,127 |
| cash-short slots (executed < ordered and purse < unit price) | 4,641 | 601 |
| same order re-issued at the **next step** | **76 %** | 63 % |
| re-issued within 2-3 / 4-24 / > 24 steps | 11 / 10 / 3 % | 11 / 19 / 6 % |
| never re-issued | 0.9 % | 1.2 % |
| re-issued with the same quantity (≤ 3 steps) | 61 % | 97 % |
| SELL of milk/wool at the next step (baseline at any step) | 0.045 (0.119) | 0.023 (0.141) |
| live d0-2 **hire** shorts followed by a successful hire within 1-3 steps | **88/98** | **157/164** |

The measured behaviour is retry until affordable, with the same order at the next step. There is no sell-first (sales after a short are rarer than baseline) and orders are almost never dropped. A live hire that is short on d0-2 goes through 1-3 steps later, once the programme's own farmer earns the coin.

**Rule in v4** (`harness_v4.py` block 4b):
1. **Retry.** Kept from v3: the ledger re-issues the deficit every step, with a 48-step window that covers 96-97 % of the measured re-issues. Hire deficits expire at dawn.
2. **Wheat-first deferral at the purse edge.** If the counterfactual purse cannot fund the step's live spend (shortfall = live before − sim before − live after > 0), the step's WHEAT/FERTILIZER product and wheat-seed units are cut by ceil(shortfall / price), last slot first, and re-issued later. Following MMPQ, the wheat count is the only cash-flexible count.
3. **Hire credit.** If the live rival hired at this step and a shortfall remains, the hire goes through on credit (at most 50 coins outstanding), repaid from the first counterfactual income above live. Any balance left at the end is deducted.
   - This stands in for the adaptive farmer the tape cannot replay. The hand's taped actions are the rival's only d1-2 income, so a hire delayed by 1-3 steps would lose that income entirely.

**Development.** Two earlier cuts of the rule collapsed games, and are the reason for the final form:
- "Cut wheat whenever poorer than live" collapsed 115508416: rival 76.6k against 154.1k live. It cut 6,798 wheat units and the animals starved.
- "Purse-edge cut without the hire credit" collapsed 115271256 by -75.8 %. The d2 h0 hire failed by 1 coin, since vrp26's d1 h20 wheat buy moved the price by 1.
- Both were fixed before any row was kept. The superseded rows are in res_remote/v26HNC_* and v26HW0_*.

**Loan vs rule.**
- The v4 rule fired the hire credit on 7 P48 + 4 PQ4 games (246 + 4 coins, 0 outstanding at the end). The v3 loan fired on 139/149 P48 + 25/25 PQ4 games (11,519 + 2,132 coins; 63 + 14 games with a balance deducted at the end).
- Identity is the same under both: 172/172 and 26/26, because nothing fires.
- Faithfulness counts are in §4. v4 with the loan in place of the rule (`H:CL`) was read on PQ4TAPE26 only; the P48 chunks were dropped for compute.
  - It is **identical in faithfulness** to v4: 23/25 within 5 %, 0 below -20 %, and the 3 PQ4 collapse games at the same values.
  - Its read is nearly the same: vrp26 − vrp24 +1,050 (t 2.86), against +1,074 (t 2.93) with the rule.
- The cash rule therefore replaces the loan at equal faithfulness, with 1/50th of the injected coins.

## 3. The 1/149 unit-action drift: weed-follow

- **Trace.** Traced with `trace2.py` on 115243853, where vrp26 is seat 0 and the rival seat 1. The rival's tiles first diverge at d10 h23: tile (6,8) is empty in the sim and a weed live, and tile (6,9) is the reverse.
- **Cause.** `end_of_day` draws the weeds of farm 0 and then farm 1 from **one per-day RNG**, one draw per EMPTY tile. When our farm's empty-tile count changes, the rival in seat 1 gets its weeds on other tiles. Its taped clear/PLANT actions then miss, and tile_div grows to 8.
- **Repair.** At each dawn, the rival's EMPTY/WEED tiles are set to the live twin's EMPTY/WEED pattern; other tile kinds are untouched. This needs one new engine hook, `kag_set_weed` (EMPTY ↔ WEED only), in S/harness2/fastenv.
- **Result.** On 115243853, tile_div 8 → 0 and the rival goes 95.5k → 95.9k (live 98.6k). Over the vrp26 H run it fired on 15 P48 + 6 PQ4 games.
  - Games with tile_div > 0 at the end: 3 P48 + 1 PQ4, max 2. v3 had 13 + 3, max 8.
