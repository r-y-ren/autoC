## 1. Ledger (ON - OFF per game; t in brackets)

### 1.1 Where the switch acts: three planner sites
The switch feeds `day` into `projector.inv_at_day` / `projected_inv` at three sites:
- **C, crop-seed values:** `_candidates` `inv_h`, the sales-window curve of the k-th seed.
- **A, animal values:** the same `inv_h` walked by `_stream_rev` for the k-th goose / cow / sheep, plus the shared fertilizer stream.
- **S, sale lots:** `SELL.lot_inventories`, the forced-overflow rank, and the provisional land-revenue lots.
- **F, wheat/feed buy quotes:** `TURN_BUY` / `TURN_PRESTOCK`.

The OFF valuation says of itself "opponent supply is absent by construction; `press`/`grow_mult` carry it". `grow_mult`, `press` and the residual are trained against the FIELD, so the full family curve counts the average rival's supply a second time.

**When the first decision differs:** most first divergences are at **d5 h0/h1 (step 120/121)**. That is the first dawn whose `_FIRST_P` window reaches the d10+ family supply.
- **Hires:** 3 vs 4 HIRE.
- **Feed pickup:** PICKUP WHEAT 1 vs 4.
- **Seeds for a cow:** BUY_SEED STRAWBERRY 12 vs 8 and no BUY_ANIMAL COW (115344778, 115191203, 115464139).
