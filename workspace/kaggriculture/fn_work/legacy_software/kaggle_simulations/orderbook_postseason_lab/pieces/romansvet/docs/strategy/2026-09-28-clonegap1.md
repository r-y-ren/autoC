- **Accounting identity (per game):** final gap = the sum over products of the revenue gap, minus the sum of spend gaps. The residual is 0.0.
- **Revenue gap per product x phase** = VOLUME ((u_c - u_t) x teacher realised price) + quote level + lot slippage (TOPMECH1's method).
- **Families** = volume + quote, net of their own seeds, animals and feed. Lot slippage is shown separately.
- **Realised price** = the engine price table walked from the pre-trade town inventory.
- **Units** come from the shed rule, since the engine sells from the shed after the unit actions. On sell-only and fully funded steps, the units are topped up so that revenue equals the money delta.
- **Spend** = revenue - money delta, allocated to that step's orders by nominal cost.
