  The live27 seats against rivals >= 2,450 (n 26) agree: mean -7.3k, sd 8.7k, 5 wins.
- **What +10 points of P(win) costs (+4 wins of 42).** Empirically, shifting every margin: +2k -> 11 wins, +3k -> 14. Scaling the spread
  around the same mean: x1.5 -> 13 wins, x2 -> 15. An independent, zero-cost, fair +-b coin bet: b 5k -> 10.0 expected wins,
  b 10k -> 11.5, b 15k -> 15.5. The same bet costing 2k of mean: b 10k -> 9.5, b 15k -> 14.0. Normal model: +10 points needs +2.9k of mean
  at fixed sd, or +4.8k of sd (8.5k -> 13.3k) at fixed mean, i.e. an independent +-10k draw bet at zero cost.
- **Rule of thumb: 1k of mean ~ 2 wins of 42 (~5 points); a variance bet is worth it only if its cost is under ~1k per +-5k of swing.**
- **Other teams vs the top-4 (BEATPROG1 vsprog_all, 16 teams with >= 40 games).** Across teams, P(win) follows the mean (r +0.90) far more

## 2. The draw ledger (S/riskwin1/res/draw.md; 64 WINANATOMY1 live games, exact per-day prices and per-seat revenue)

**How the draw works (engine kaggriculture.py `_end_of_day`, kaggle-environments 1.32.7).**
- One shop is appended to `town.unlocked_shops` at the end of day 3k-1, so it is visible from the dawn of d3, d6, d9, d12, d15, d18, d21 and d24.
  MAX_SHOP_INSTANCES = 8, so every game gets exactly 8 (64/64 games). Each draw is `rng.choice(sorted(SHOPS))`, uniform over the 8 types, with
  replacement (observed 56-77 per type over 512 slots). A shop drains its products every 4 turns from its unlock to the end (single-product shops
  YARN_STORE and PET_CAFE drain 2 units, i.e. 12/day; the others 1 unit per product, 6/day); the town center drains 1 of each product per day.
- **The rng is `random.Random((seed * 1_000_003) ^ day)` with the hidden episode seed** (resolve_episode_seed scrubs it from the configuration;
  live seeds are 31-bit, e.g. 963612233), and on the SAME rng `_spawn_weeds` first draws one `random()` per EMPTY unlocked tile of farm 0, then
  farm 1, before the shop choice. So the shop drawn is a function of (hidden seed, day, empty-tile count of BOTH farms that night): identical for
  both seats (one shared town), not visible before it unlocks, and re-rolled by any change in either farm's empty-tile count. Without the seed
  (2^31 candidates; the agent has 1 s per turn and 60 s of overage) a re-roll is another uniform draw, so steering the count has zero value.
- **Observable early enough?** Each shop is known from its unlock dawn and drains for the rest of the game, so a body can condition on it from
  d3/d6/.../d24 on. Nothing about the draw is known at d0 (town.unlocked_shops is empty), so a d0 decision (the herd, the plate) can only be a bet.

over 10k never wins (0/18). So a bet must be placed by d18-21 and must swing ~10k to flip a typical loss.

## 3. The bets (switch family HOLD_SHOP, worktree riskwin1_0929) and the grid

Every cell is a switch-set that the step-1 rival identification (KERNEL2_FIRE_SWITCHES, MELONTRIAL1 f1cbd8c2, ported to riskwin1_0929 as a
src patch) fires on programme seats only; unfired seats stay PFS (9/9 exact on the live27 tapes). Closed-loop cells are the same strings set
statically (every judge board is a programme-clone seat).

- **New switch `HOLD_SHOP_ON` (default False; OFF byte-identical: remote OFF gate 6/6 exact vs the banked JUDGERIVAL1 g0capsfix PFS rows on both
  trees, unfired tapes exact to live).** For each product in `HOLD_SHOP_ITEMS`, from `HOLD_SHOP_FROM` while the day is < `HOLD_SHOP_END` (27)
  and no unlocked shop eats it, our voluntary sale is withheld; once a shop that eats it unlocks, PFS's own sell side runs; from d27 PFS
  liquidates as always. `HOLD_SHOP_MODE`:
  - `"render"`: a runtime clamp on the rendered SELL orders keeps `HOLD_SHOP_CAP` units in the shed. The plan still counts on the sale
    (funding) and the forced-overflow sale is cut too.
  - `"plan"`: the sell reservation of a held product is set to COIN_CAP in `plan._sell_hold` (both the provisional funding sale and the real
    sale, the MELON_DENY pattern), so the plan knows the stock stays and the forced-overflow sale still runs.
- Existing switches reused: `WOOL_FIRST_ON` (DSM's opening herd through d2: exact cows / sheep / geese ask; `WOOL_FIRST_SELL=False` keeps
  PFS's wool reservation) and `ENDGAME_TOMATO_ON` (d10-18 crop mix to tomato up to `ENDGAME_TOMATO_TILES` held tiles once
  `ENDGAME_TOMATO_SHOPS` tomato-eating shops are unlocked).

## 5. Why every bet loses: in a shared market a draw bet is a transfer to the rival

- **Holding is a public good.** Without a shop that eats it, a product's price sits on its glut, and OUR supply is part of the glut. Withheld
  wool (or carrot / tomato / egg) lifts the price the rival sells its own identical book into: hw40 rival +3.9k closed loop / +2.5k tapes,
  hpw +2.0k / +1.3k, hpa +7.2k / +6.4k. On the no-YARN boards of the tapes (capitaalgain, shunkikyoya, madmax0404, c0nrad) the render hold
  pays the rival +4.7k..+12.3k. When the YARN_STORE does come (vvs: 4 YARNs from d9) the hold earns +4.3k, but the programme holds as many
  sheep as PFS (d18-29 wool 88 vs 87 units), so it rides the same draw.
- **The render clamp is worse than the plan hold** (hw40 own -3.6k vs hpw -0.3k): it also cuts PFS's forced-overflow sale and the plan still
  spends the wool cash it expected.
- **Draw-react adds supply to the draw product and crashes our own hinge** (drt12: own -2.2k, t -4.4; tomato is where PFS already out-earns
  the rival by holding, +4.7k per +6 units/day of tomato drain; adding 12 tiles of supply spends that edge; ENDGAME_TOMATO's own 2026-09-04
  note measured the same at 34 tiles).
- **The sheep bet swaps our cows/goose for sheep at h1 and hands the rival milk and eggs** (sb33: rival +14.9k, W 38 -> 26 of 40; var25p
  +9.7k; faithful tapes +9.4k..+15.2k), the REALLOC1 / HERD1 mechanism: our h1 cows hold the shared milk price down.
- **Structural reason.** The draw lifts both purses by the same amount because the programme and PFS hold the same late book; variance in the
  MARGIN can only come from a book that differs from the rival's, and every way to make it differ at d0-18 either costs our own coins (herd,
  supply) or lifts the rival's price (hold). With mean -8.1k and sd 8.5k, +10 points of P(win) needs +2.9k of mean or an independent, free
  +-10k swing; the cheapest asymmetric exposure found costs the margin 2.2k (hpw) for no measurable swing (W 75 = 75; margin sd 13.2k vs 13.7k).
