## 5. Verdict — NO SHIP; DAgger into head_940's last layer does not transfer
The oracle is real on every searched board (+3.9k/board, 5/5 losses flipped, gift-filtered) but it is selection over
~260 CRN games: at 30 labels/board and 2-6 kept deviations/board the choices are not predictable from the head's 64
features (0/6 held-out-board accuracy, < 45 % in-sample), and the policy that half-follows them loses its own training
boards (−533/board) and 1-4 held-out wins. Round 2 did not fix it (r2_ep6 −2, r2_ep1 −3). What would be needed: ~50×
more boards (≈ 20 min/board at 3 cores → a GPU-free 2-day farm), a richer input than the 64 head features
(day one-hot, rival state), and a trainable trunk; or accept search at test time (not possible in the 2 s/turn budget).
