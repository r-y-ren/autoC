# Re-slope-checking the drain-mix gene on the flow166_g170 record init

*2026-09-09/10, worktree `.claude/worktrees/drain-gene` at `d4317f5`
(leg20 trainer + drain-mix gene + shed fix). CPU only, no engine games.*

The drain-mix gene (`gd`/`gbd`, `docs/strategy/2026-09-09-drain-gene.md`) was
sized against **one** init: `flow135_g350_gpfwdfv_gb028`, the live champion,
which read **24.4 %** pooled / 23.8 % per-board median at
`MIX_DRAIN_GAIN = 5.0`, sigma 0.02 -- the middle of the 15-35 % band.

flow168 will not start there. It starts from the record theta
`artifacts/kagg2_games/thetas/flow166_g170.npy` (6,789 params, md5
`05a59c1b712dd34cb9bdb2bad8360d94`). The gene rule
(memory `gene-slope-check`) is about *this* init at *this* sigma, not about
the gene in the abstract: the decoded term is `GAIN * z * share`, and `z` is
`gh @ gd + gbd` off a **trained** global hidden layer. A different init is a
different `gh`, hence a different slope for the same constant.

## 3. The slope, 256 members x the same 12 recorded boards

Method verbatim from `test_one_es_step_moves_the_mix_for_a_minority_of_the_
population`, widened from 64 to the launch measurement's 256 members: isotropic
draws off the padded init, `_traj(12, seed=3)` recorded hour-0 boards, each
member decided twice -- once at `MIX_DRAIN_GAIN` and once at 0.0 -- and counted
as moved when its own gene changed its own `plant_target` or `animal_want`.

**Harness validated first**: `gb028` at sigma 0.02, gain 5.0 reproduces
`0.2438` pooled / `0.2383` median, i.e. the recorded 24.4 % / 23.8 % to the
digit. The numbers below are therefore comparable to the launch measurement.

| init | sigma | gain | pooled | per-board median | boards live |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `gb028` (recorded arm) | 0.02 | 5.0 | 24.4 % | 23.8 % | 11/12 |
| **`g170`** | **0.02** | **5.0** | **32.9 %** | **40.2 %** | 10/12 |
| **`g170`** | **0.015** | **5.0** | **26.5 %** | **31.1 %** | 10/12 |
| `g170` | 0.02 | 4.0 | 27.9 % | 33.0 % | 10/12 |
| `g170` | 0.02 | 3.5 | 24.8 % | 29.3 % | 10/12 |

Per-board at sigma 0.02: `[.58 .00 .40 .56 .01 .29 .18 .41 .57 .50 .00 .44]`;
at 0.015: `[.54 .00 .31 .48 .00 .23 .13 .31 .45 .39 .00 .35]`. Two boards never
move at either sigma (the no-free-tile day the shipped test already documents,
plus one more), so `boards_live` = 0.833 -- still above the 0.75 bar.

**Why g170 is hotter.** Not the gene, the hidden layer. 170 generations of ES
have grown `gh`, so the same zero block decodes a larger pre-activation off the
same sigma:

| init | sigma | `mixd` sd | mean \|z\| | decoded term mean \|t\| | fraction clipped |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `gb028` | 0.02 | 0.0482 | 0.0381 | 0.352 | 0.2 % |
| `g170` | 0.02 | 0.0580 | 0.0462 | 0.414 | 0.5 % |
| `g170` | 0.015 | 0.0431 | 0.0343 | 0.309 | 0.1 % |

`share` is identical across inits (it is a board feature, mean
`[1.86 1.37 1.50 1.51 -4.00 1.16 0.11 0.08]`, mean magnitude 1.72), so the
whole 20 % rise is `gh`. Clipping is still negligible either way -- the gene is
not saturating, it is simply louder.

## 4. Verdict on the gain constant

`MIX_DRAIN_GAIN = 5.0` does **not** need changing, provided the arm is launched
at sigma 0.015.

* At **sigma 0.02** the pooled 32.9 % is inside the 15-35 % band but the
  **per-board median 40.2 % is above its ceiling**. The band is checked on both
  statistics for a reason -- the pooled mean is dragged down by the two dead
  boards. On the boards that can move, two members in five have their mix
  rewritten by a gene drawn from *zero*; that is the far side of "a minority",
  and it is the mix becoming noise rather than the mix becoming selectable.
* At **sigma 0.015** both statistics land inside the band (26.5 % / 31.1 %),
  slightly hotter than the recorded arm and comfortably clear of dead.
* If flow168 must run at sigma 0.02, the equivalent fix is `MIX_DRAIN_GAIN`
  3.5 (24.8 % / 29.3 %, the gb028 arm's numbers) -- a code change, measured
  here but **not made**.

No dead-gene risk exists on this init in any configuration measured; the only
live question was the top of the band.

## 5. Go / no-go

**GO at sigma 0.015** from `flow166_g170_gd.npy`
(md5 `8dbc99641e24f80094969ab5df95c6d7`), gain unchanged at 5.0 -- which is also
flow173's lineage sigma, so the two arms stay comparable.

**Conditional no-go at sigma 0.02** with gain 5.0: nothing is broken and the
identity is exact, but the gene is measurably out of the band it was sized for
on the per-board median. Launch it there only alongside a drop to gain 3.5.

## 6. Reproducing

The measurement is ~8 s on 12 cores per row; the scripts are throwaway
(`slope_g170.py`, `sweep.py`, `diag.py` in the session scratchpad) and reuse
`tests/test_drain_mix_gene.py`'s own `_traj`, `_obs_of`, `_share` helpers so
that nothing about the board set or the observation construction is re-derived
here. Nothing in `src/` or `tests/` was modified.
