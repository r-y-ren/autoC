By channel: crops (wheat+carrot+tomato+strawberry+melon) **+10,981** to B;
animal products (egg+milk+wool+fertilizer) **-14,236** to B. Sum -3,255.

By band the loss is *not* where the swing is. The d10-14 melon tax costs B

against B's 8, which is worth -11,322 fertilizer and -7,326 milk to B, only
partly offset by B's 6 geese against 3 (+8,181 egg). B's strawberry lead
(+21,639, 361 units against 247) very nearly pays for the melon opening and the
animal mix together, and falls 1,275 short.

in items 1-6 of section 7 - both cash paths, all per-product units/coins/prices,
the band split, the spend lines, the closed identity -3,255 + 1,980 = -1,275,

sheep, the 14 unsold wheat units and 5 shed fertilizer, and the PASS counts.

FACT: the opponent opened 12 melon tiles on day 0 and banked 17,440 melon coins
on d10-d14 - the band-clone opening signature.

INFERENCE (single replay, outcome-selected, not a promotion signal): that the
1,275 residual is the animal-mix channel (opponent 14 pasture animals and 67
bought fertilizer units against B's 9 and 8) rather than the melon opening,
because 18,373 of the 22,148-coin d10-14 melon deficit (83%) is recovered in
d15-29, and 520 more in d0-9. This is one board.

UNVERIFIED: whether the opponent reacts to the shop draw or to our seat; the
coin value of the starved sheep and of the unsold end-state (the ~687 coins is a
mark-to-final-price figure, not a realisable counterfactual); and whether any of
the section-7 items would still be defects under a paired engine test. Nothing
here justifies a forced-melon, fertilizer-volume or feed-cadence lever - the
fertilizer and sell-timing families are recorded closed, and this episode is
selection, not evidence.
