## 2 — REACTING seats in fitness (was: 374 tapes + self-play against theta only)

There is **no `--pool` flag in `scripts/train.py`**. The existing mechanism for seating a frozen theta as a
re-planning opponent is `--rung-theta NAME=PATH.npy` + `--rung-weight NAME=W`, so four rungs are added at
weight 1: `clone_m4`, `clone_m8`, `clone_m10` (`S/actionrl/pool/clone_m*.npy`, ACTIONRL10's melon-plate
clones) and `af3_60` (`S/alphafarm/theta_af3_60.npy`). No code change.

`--episodes 376 → 520` so the residual block is large enough to sample them: 374 pinned rungs × 1 episode
leaves **146 carried = 73 pairs**, `--arch-frac 0.9` sends **66 pairs = 132 episodes = 25.4 %** of 520 to the
four clones (the four stock archetypes stay at `--rung-weight 0`), 7 pairs to self-play against theta.
`--chunk 128 → 96` keeps the device footprint (`chunk × episodes`) at flow258's; it is a batching knob only.

> **LIMITATION — episodes ≠ objective weight.** The pinned block keeps flow258's `--rung-weight tape_act_*=4`,
> and `pinned_weights()` scores a pinned episode at `2 × 4 = 8` against `1.0` for an unpinned one, so the four
> reacting seats are 25 % of the **episodes** but only **~4.4 % of the fitness weight**. If the arm reads flat,
> lowering the tape rung weight is the lever, not more episodes.

**GATE260 LAW respected:** the 112 loss tapes are the GATE only; none was added to `--tape-actions`.
