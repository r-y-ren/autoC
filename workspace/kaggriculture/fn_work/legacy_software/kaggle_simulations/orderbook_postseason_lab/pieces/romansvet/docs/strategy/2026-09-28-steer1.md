## Verdict
- **NO steering move is worth >= +2 net flips per 20 MELON seats. Nothing to implement; no steer1 worktree was opened.**
- **The engine leaves almost nothing to steer.**
  - Seeds, animals, hires and land are unlimited, fixed-price (hires fib-priced per farm) and per player. "Buy the rival out" does not exist.
  - The only shared object is the 9-product market book (sell prices; buy prices for WHEAT and FERT).
  - The shop draw is coupled to our empty tiles, but the seed is hidden, so steering it is blind.
  - The rival sees our money, tiles, quadrants and hands, never our shed or orders.
- **Every market move that bites HELPS the rival.** Per reacting rival, n = 10 paired seats each (dtheirs t):
  - wheat0 (buy 40 wheat at d0): dtheirs +10.9k / +14.6k / +12.1k / +12.9k (t +3.8..+7.3); dmargin -11.9k..-20.3k.
  - wheat5: rival +2.9k..+6.4k on 3 of 4 rivals (t +3.2..+4.8).
  - wheathold: rival +5.1k..+8.0k on 3 of 4 rivals (t +3.2..+6.2).
  - wheatdump (sell our wheat at once d1-4): rival +6.6k..+14.5k on all 4 rivals (t +2.4..+7.4), dmargin -8.0k..-17.3k.
  - land8: rival +1.2k..+8.8k (t +1.2..+1.9); dmargin -11.6k on prvs, noise elsewhere.
  - The d10 cash of the rival barely moves (|Δ| <= 0.6k). The rival's gain appears in d10-29, and it mirrors OUR lost late volume: what a d0-9 move costs our plan, the rival collects in the shared late books. This is the two-purse coupling seen in earlier denial tests (MILKSHOP1, FERTDENY).
- **The cheap moves are noise.** fert0: dmargin -4.4k..+0.7k. front: -0.7k..+2.0k, all |t| <= 1.5 except prvs fert0 at -5.2.
- **The only positive cell** is clone x wheat5, +3.9k (t 1.47). It reverses on web3 (-10.7k, t -3.0), prvs (-6.0k, t -3.9) and kitex (-3.7k, t -3.7). That is chaos, not a lever.
- **Measurement caveat.** Kaggle ListKernels (read-only, 698 kernels at ~18:00Z, `S/steer1/kernels_now.tsv`) finds no public kernel by any of the 16 loss-rival teams, so none of them can be banked as a reacting rival.   - The BC top-5 clone r4c (`S/bcbody1/agent`, BCSIM1 config), the only reacting top-5-programme body we have.
  - The three closest MELON-family public agents: web3cainiao v21_tact (7m/11w, reads our tiles via `_opponent_exposure`), prvsiyan frontier_lab (7m/11w) and hboyang kitex_v0 (7m/7w).

## 4. Reading
- **The wheat book is where the programme lives in d0-9, and both directions pay the rival.**
  - Making wheat dearer (wheat0, wheat5, wheathold) and making it cheaper (wheatdump) all RAISE the rival's final coins by +2.9k..+14.6k.
  - The rival's d10 cash moves by at most ±0.6k in every cell.
  - So the rival's gain is not the d0-9 price channel. It is the mirror image of our disrupted plan: less or later volume from us in d10-29 leaves the shared late books (milk / strawberry / tomato / wool / eggs) to the rival.
  - A d0-9 market move has to pay for itself in OUR plan first. None does, because the curves are T = 100-450 units deep, the d0-9 books are tens of units, and our d0-5 cash is the binding constraint of the opening.
- **Signal moves do not exist at the cash we hold.**
  - land1 cannot fire (PFS < 1,000 at d1 h0).
  - land8 (the rival's own q3 day) is noise on clone / web3 / kitex (dmargin -0.9k..+0.3k, |t| <= 1.9) and loses on prvs (-11.6k, t -2.1).
  - No reacting rival we can run changes its plan when our farm looks bigger.
- **The lever that remains is ours, not theirs.**
  - This confirms LOSSBODY1: the 21 MELON live losses are a body gap (49 vs ~70 productive tiles at d9, q3 at d10 vs d8, no d2 strawberries).
  - The rival is deterministic, but its inputs from us (market book and our public farm) carry tens of units in d0-9, and it already beats us on its own production.
  - The top-5 gap is closed by producing more, not by steering: the BCBODYn clone stream and body changes.
