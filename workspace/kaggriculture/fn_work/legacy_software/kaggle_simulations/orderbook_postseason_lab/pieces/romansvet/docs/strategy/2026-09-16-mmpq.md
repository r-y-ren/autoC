# MMPQ — M & M & P & Q buy all four quadrants. What does the fourth one earn?

2026-09-16. Tools `S/mmpq/`. Read off **their own replays**, to the coin: 20 recent games of
submission **56254996** (rank 2, 3,086-3,116), 2026-09-16 11:34Z-15:14Z, 12 WIN / 8 LOSS by

## 0. VERDICT

**Yes, they buy the fourth quadrant — 16 of 20 games, day 15.3, out of a 22,024-coin cash
pile — and no, it is not what makes them rank 2.** It returns **12.1 coins per unit-op**
against **33.6** on the quadrant they start with, stays under water from the day it is
bought until **day 28.2 of a 30-day game**, and clears **+4,482** in a 122,674-coin purse
— *before* any charge for the 836 crew-ops it eats. Charge those at what the rest of their
own farm realises (21.9/op) and it is **−13,875** and never pays back.

**The decisive evidence is in the same replays: none of their 20 opponents buys it** —
including **Majkel1337, rank 1, 3181-3211, in 9 of the 20 games**. Every opponent seat stops
at three quadrants, exactly as we do; M & M & P & Q are the only build on the board that
takes a fourth, they are rank 2, and the team above them does not. Our claim survives with
one correction: it is not a *loss* in their purse, it is a **low-yield sink for cash that
had nothing better to buy**.

## 1. Method (`S/mmpq/quad.py`, `S/mmpq/ledger.py`)

Nothing re-implements the rules. Every recorded action is replayed through the **engine's
own `_apply_unit_action`** on a copy of the previous step's farm/private, one unit at a
time, so each op's effect is read from the engine, not guessed from a state diff (a diff
conflates the unit phase with `_decay_plants` / `_daily_refresh_*`, which run in the same
recorded transition). Market phase = `scripts/replay_profile._simulate_market` verbatim:
**reconstruction error 0 coins on all 20 of their seats** (3 on ours, 594 worst opponent
seat). A unit-op is charged to the quadrant the unit stands on (`DROP`/`PICKUP` to a
shared `SHED` bucket: the four shed-access tiles are one per quadrant and serve the whole
farm); production to the tile's own quadrant. **Revenue is money actually banked** — the
seat's real per-product sell revenue split by each quadrant's share of the units of that
product harvested to date, so the columns sum to the real revenue, no invented coins —
less land price, seed cost of every plant on it, purchase cost of every animal on it.
Internal consumption (fertilizer applied, wheat fed) is not charged twice: its output
already lands in the quadrant that grew it.

Reproduce: `pick.py --feed <ListEpisodes.json> --out $S` → `OUT=$S P=4 bash dl.sh` →
`PYTHONPATH=src quad.py --dir $S -j 3` → `ledger.py --all` (per-game rows `--per-game
--pergame-net`; the tables below are `--crops --pay --se --three`).

## 3. Why it works for them at all, and why it is marginal

* **A late ongoing-crop annex, not a farm.** Mean planting day on SE **d18.6** (NW d10.5);
  8.8 tiles are TOMATO — *ongoing*: plant once, harvest for days — against 2.9-4.5 elsewhere,
  and no herd (0.1 goose, 0.0 cow, 0.2 sheep). **71 % of everything SE earns arrives d27-29,
  87 % d25-29**: it is still filling up at the whistle.
* **Bought with money, not with a plan.** 22,024 coins on hand the turn before, five times
  the 4,543 before the 3rd. The 4,000 price is 3.3 % of their end purse; crew time is the bill.
* **The crops are the scarce ones.** Realised vs base: STRAWBERRY 182/120, WHEAT 35.6/25,
  TOMATO 68.2/60, CARROT 40.0/35 — against MELON 164/250, WOOL 151/200, MILK 123/160,
  FERTILIZER 61/100. SE carries the short four and none of the glutted: a **crop-mix**
  lesson that needs no fourth quadrant.
* **Labour slack pays for it and they have almost none.** 5.1 % PASS overall, **3.7 % on
  SE**. The 836 ops SE eats came out of the first three quadrants.

**The claim survives in both readings, and gets sharper.**
* *"Uneconomic for OUR build"* — **holds, a fortiori.** Land is not our binding constraint:
  we own three quadrants and run **twice their idle rate** (10.4 % vs 5.1 %), plant **85
  fewer tiles**, earn **18.4 coins/op to their 23.3**; our SW already yields 13.3 and a
  fourth sits below it. The planner is not missing the option — `src/kagg3/core/plan.py:7530`
  prices a 4th quadrant every turn (`view.nquad < 4 & … & (land_value + macro.land_bias >
  0)`) and **declines it in 92/92 profiled games and all 14 fresh ones**: a valuation our
  build already ran and rejected, not a gap.
* *"Uneconomic in general"* — **holds as stated, but it is not a loss.** In the only build
  that takes it it is +4,482 and pays back d28.2. Precisely: **the 4th quadrant is the worst
  land on the board, a third of the first per crew-op, and every top-five build but one declines it.**

**Ceiling in OUR purse, under the two-purse rule.** Our idle is 695 PASS ops a game. Redeploy **every one** onto a 4th quadrant at their SE
rate: 695 × 12.1 = 8,411 gross, −4,000 land, −~1,400 seeds ⇒ **≈ +3.0k — a hard ceiling,
not an estimate of the gain.** It assumes idle ops can be steered to new land, which
`2026-09-09-board-fill.md` §1 says they cannot: **99.4 % of our idle tile-days are the plan
cap not asking for a tile we already own**, not a labour shortage. **Ledger counterfactuals
overstate; only paired engine runs count** — and the nearest paired measurement we own
points the other way: `2026-09-16-jointlift.md`, tiles + hands, **−4,835 margin, t −13.1,
24 flips, all to the opponent** ("the extra plantings are wheat never watered to maturity").
**No arm is proposed here.** What is worth testing is not land: it is our **10.4 % idle and
186 plantings on land we already own**, and the crop mix (23 tomato units sold to their 122).
