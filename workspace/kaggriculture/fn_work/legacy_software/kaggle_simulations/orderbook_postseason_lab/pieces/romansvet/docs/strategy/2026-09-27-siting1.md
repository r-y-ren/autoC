## Rule under test
`src/kagg3/core/plan.py:11132-11148` — free coop/pasture slots and new-build slots are ranked by
`_rank_near(xp, mask, dev_key)` (`plan.py:7105`, exclusive rank by ascending bucket `key`, ties to lower serpentine index),
`dev_key = _dev_key(xp, compact_eff) = DIST_SHED * compact // DIST_MAX` (`plan.py:7131`). `compact` is the ES gene
`relu(tanh(dev[0]))`; `ROUTE_EFF_ON = False` (`plan.py:5971`). The near-shed siting switch therefore **already exists**:
`ROUTE_EFF_ON` with floor `DIST_MAX` = "rank every build by DIST_SHED" — ROUTEEFF 09-16 REJECT −111 (t −0.21), RESWEEP1 09-26 −2 flips.

## Method (engine events, no game runs)
`S/siting1/siting.py` imports `S/crewaudit1/audit.py` (unmodified), wraps `_apply_unit_action`, and replays the 16-game
CREWAUDIT1 bed (8 V near-misses + 8 matched V wins, `S/crewaudit1/bed.tsv`). Every BUILD_COOP/BUILD_PASTURE: tile, day,
Manhattan distance to the nearest shed-access tile (market orders are a separate channel off the shed, so shed access
== market access; one number), then every successful PLACE/FEED/CARE/HARVEST/COLLECT_FERTILIZER on it.
walk = Σ ops × distance (one-way). Value = own revenue / own hand-turns (CREWAUDIT1 games.tsv), one-sided, TWO-PURSE caveat.
Outputs: `S/siting1/out/structures.tsv` (580 structures), `S/siting1/out/pergame.tsv`.
