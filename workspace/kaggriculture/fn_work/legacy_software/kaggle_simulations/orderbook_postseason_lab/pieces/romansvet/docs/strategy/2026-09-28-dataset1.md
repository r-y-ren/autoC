## Dataset (what it really is)
- `kaggle/kaggriculture-episodes-index` (v60) = one `manifest.csv` row per day -> `kaggle/kaggriculture-episodes-<date>`, 07-30..09-27 (60 days).
- Each daily dataset = one `<episode>.json` per episode (the full replay, 33-38 MB, stored identity) + `manifest.csv` (episode_id, create_time, avg_score, min_score, sum_score, agent_count, size_bytes). **No team or submission column.**
- Size: **40,914 episode files, 1,280 GB** (21.4-21.5 GB/day, 576-928 eps/day); full list with sizes in `S/dataset1/files.tsv`.
- Access is credential-free (no kaggle.json on this box): `GET https://www.kaggle.com/api/v1/datasets/list/kaggle/<slug>?pageSize=200` (file list, paged) and `GET .../api/v1/datasets/download/kaggle/<slug>/<file>` (302 to a signed GCS URL).
- Teams / sub ids per episode: batched `ListEpisodes {"ids": [200 ids]}` (0.5 s per 200 eps; 429 after ~40 calls, back-off 10-80 s). `S/dataset1/idx_all.tsv` = 51,562 seat rows, 40 days (08-19..09-27).

## Pipeline and throughput
- `indexer.py` (manifests -> ListEpisodes ids -> `idx.tsv`), `sel.tsv` = core4 seats not in the corpus, newest first.
- `pipe.py` = ONE process: 4 download threads + 1 conversion thread running the **unchanged** `S/bcbody1/extract.py` `one()` (only `OUT` overridden and its `json.load` handed the already-parsed dict; the gate covers that path). Raw replays are deleted right after conversion.
- The official dataset files are bandwidth-bound: 9.2 MB/s = 0.25 eps/s (34 MB each). The bulk therefore used the byte-identical episode URL with gzip (2.45 MB/ep):
  - local (nice 19, ionice idle) 0.55-0.57 eps/s, newest first;
  - remote (1 process, nice 19) 0.16-0.23 eps/s, oldest first; the two met at 09-17 at 22:59Z and both were stopped.
- Converted 3,236 + 47 double seats in 63 min; about 8.5 GB moved (dataset files 1.1 GB for the gate, gzip replays 7.4 GB). Conversion errors 0.
