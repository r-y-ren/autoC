This stream re-read every staged switch that was runnable on BRAINSTORM7's faithful programme-class judge, P48TAPE103. It also built the OTH tape set. All files are at user@remote-host:/home/user/stage_r/p48sweep1r and are mirrored to S/p48sweep1r.

## Method
- **Judge:** our seat is the candidate tree with LE.SWITCHES plus the arm. The P48 opponent is replayed from its tape over the 103 real games: 34 from 56687235/774 and 69 from 56652418/56686302/56686308.
- **Pairing:** every cell is paired against the live game, which equals OFF because identity is exact 103/103.
- **Tree identity:** each candidate tree with its switch OFF was byte-identical on 3 games (ndiff 0): revfix1, rf2_off, pricedask1, tilelease1, rs2, herd1, land1, clsearch1, clsearch1b, combo1, ms1, d10_master, ra_wt and earlyland1.
- **Bars:** margin >= +1k, t >= 2, net flips >= +3, own >= 0. A cell that passes then needs the V56 m40/v21 read.
