## Verdict
The COST AXIS is CLOSED as a lever family. Our expenses are already 11.9k per game below DSM's. The identifiable waste is about 1.1k per game, and the only line above 150 (animal payback, about 500) cannot be cut without gifting: cap 7 gives −11 flips and +4.8k to theirs. The only non-gift cost saving left is the hire bill, which is VRP crew size (ROUTERAUDIT1 oracle +722 bill per game → RRDEPTH1/RRSPEED1). Nothing else deserves a follow-up probe: the fert buy, uncared feed and cap-waste lines are each under 150 per game, with no knob that avoids a volume change.
