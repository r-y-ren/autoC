## 4. Are the two subs' BT fits independent?
Yes, as far as the text and our model go. Each submission is its own agent in the tournament, and the team is scored by its best agent.
- Rules (docs/strategy/2026-09-26-rules1.md, lines 11-17):
  - "The latest 2 submissions are also used for final leaderboard evaluation"
  - "On the leaderboard, only your best-scoring bot will be shown"
  - "A final Bradley-Terry tournament will be run on those episodes"
- FINALSLOT1 §2 fits each sub's BT theta on its own October games, with opponents fixed at their rating. It then takes team = max of the two.
- The games of sub A do not enter sub B's likelihood. The only coupling is second-order: in a joint BT fit, A's games move the thetas of shared opponents. With ~500 games per opponent, that effect is negligible.
- **Not specified anywhere:** whether our two subs can be paired against each other (never observed in our lists), and whether pre-October games count (the 09-15 forum question is unanswered).

## Caveats
- **tau:** one scalar estimated from 8 games on 2 values (2438, 26), and V56 without hand-back. Top-5-programme rivals at 1/564/938 may transfer differently. That is the only reason the bold option has any value.
- **Linear mixing:** the per-family live delta mixes fired and unfired MELON games linearly in theta, not in win probability.
- **Pure V56 on V seats:** there are no rows; I assumed a 50 % mirror. Its conclusion (dominated) holds for any theta_V below PFS's 2,788.

- **Live base:** vrp18 has no live games in lw23 yet (uploaded 16:35Z); its live theta is modelled as base + its band delta, times tau.
