## Method (`S/mixscore1/score.py`, zero new games, 1 CPU, ~1.5 min)
- **Machinery.** `S/bandbank2/mixtrack.py` is exec'd without result files. It supplies the boards_band2 seat table, the GW/LB mixes and the per-100 bin composition, plus `octmix`, `wmle` and `weights`.
  - PFS alone reproduces mixtrack exactly: raw 2,686, GW 2,559, Oct GW 2,637, Oct LB 2,651.
- **Arm seat set.** Each arm uses the seats it actually ran. Missing seats are filled from the arm's own base only inside BAND142, and only where the arm is gated so those seats are identical by construction.
  - Where that does not hold, the fill is flagged FILL-ASSUMED: the V56 d0 hybrid, MELONVETO partial, MELON_DUMP (it fires on V seats), RELAY_FILL and CREW_RELAY (ungated). For these the non-MELON cells are assumed equal to PFS.
- **Paired estimator.** θ_arm(mix) = θ_PFS,276(mix) + [θ_arm − θ_PFS](mix) on the arm's seat set. SE comes from a family-stratified paired bootstrap (200).
  - Oct = the fixed point θ = θ_arm(octmix(θ)), reported as θ at the fixed-point mix (mixtrack convention).
  - A root scan over 2,200-3,100 finds **one** root for every arm except the FILL-ASSUMED V56 d0 hybrid, so the V56 kernel has no high equilibrium.
- **BT.** `S/btsim1/sim.py` is exec'd up to its package block (R 2,000, same seeds).
  - Family θ per arm = PFS 276-seat family θ + the paired family shift on the arm's seats.
  - PFS 276: V 2,867, MELON 2,480, ZERO 2,739, OTHER 2,763. BTSIM1 used the 142-seat values, V 2,954 and MELON 2,495.
  - Repro: PFS alone on the 142-seat θ gives rank **66**, the same as BTSIM1. On the 276-seat θ it gives **75**, because the HOLD/NEW V seats are harder. The current pair therefore reads **75**, not 66, under the enlarged leg.
- **Snapshot.** In-flight files were read at 20:18Z: bandrejudge1 a / e, melonvol1 rf / cr.
