## Reading

- **PFS's planner already prices the draw.** brain.py reads `obs.shops @ _SHOP_DAILY` into its demand projection. The slopes show it acts
  on it within 0-3 days of the unlock and as strongly as the programme.
- **The CREWLOSS1 price gap is both seats' supply meeting the same new demand.** The late price still rises +43..+56 per strawberry shop
  after both seats' +8 tiles. A strawberry shop drains 6 units a day (126 units if visible by d9); the two seats together add
  ~+90..+120 late units per shop.
- **Extra strawberry on top moves the price, not the purse.** Our late strawberry units rise +65..+105, the price falls -25..-38 and our
  purse falls. Where the town also has a milk buyer, the strawberry mix also takes the wheat/tomato tiles.
- **Extra cows are at best a denial tool, not an income tool.**
  - On m40 they lower the milk price the rival sells into (98 -> 85), and the rival loses what we lose.
  - On m76 the rival does not lose (+0.3k) while we still pay (-2.0k).
  - Same class as HERD1's c2 cell (+0.4k ns).
- **What is left.** The draw sets who wins a close game, but it is already cashed. A stack-rich town is won by the seat that makes more of
  everything, not by the seat that shifts more into the drawn product. CREWLOSS1's lever does not exist as a volume switch.
