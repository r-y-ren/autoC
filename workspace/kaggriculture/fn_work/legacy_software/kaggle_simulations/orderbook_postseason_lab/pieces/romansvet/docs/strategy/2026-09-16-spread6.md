### 4.2 The identity that kills the bound

    gross taken  =  Σ (n × first-unit quote)  −  forfeit

| 68 band, our seat, frozen volume | first-unit value | forfeit | gross taken |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| B (off) | 136,821 | −8,409 | 128,413 |
| 6 rows, tick cap, clean | 127,956 | −1,811 | 126,146 |
| **Δ** | **−8,865** | **+6,598** | **−2,267** |

**The forfeit falls by 6,598 and the quote it is measured against falls by
8,865.** The engine's price is a function of the *cumulative* market inventory
(`sim/market.py` `_quotes`, `off = +j` on top of `mkt_inv`), so the n-th unit of
the day is quoted off the inventory the first n−1 left, whether they went out in
one order or in six. Splitting the order changes which unit is called "first"
and nothing about the path. What a split genuinely buys is the **town
consumption that fires between two rows** — and `LOT-DEPTH` §2 measured that
directly: **+46 coins on a 65-unit wheat liquidation in an 8-shop town, +163 in
a 32-shop town.** Six rows a day for thirty days at that rate is a few hundred
coins, which is the order of magnitude §4.3's price column actually shows, and
it is swamped by the hour the rows are moved to.

So pricevol §4.3's **+5,382 is not an upper bound that is merely optimistic; it
is a quantity of the wrong kind.** The census statement it rests on — that
TOP-NEW forfeits 2,554 where B forfeits 7,936 — remains true and remains
descriptive: **it says the top five's orders are smaller, not that their coins
come from the smallness.**

## 5. THE TWO CONFOUNDS, MEASURED OUT

### 5.1 The land row is funded by lot 1 — and the spread starves it

`ops.py:TURN_PRESTOCK`'s own comment says it outright: **"land is funded by the
morning's own lot."** The day's BUY_LAND rides the spare tenth slot of
`O.SELL_TURNS[0]` (turn 3), *behind* lot 1's row at turn 1, and the planner sizes
it against dawn money without knowing the engine will drop an order the purse
cannot pay for. Cut the turn-1 row down to the town's per-tick appetite and the
purse is short at turn 3. On the 10 TOP5NOW boards it costs the **second
quadrant on day 5**:

| day | `nquad` OFF → ON | dawn purse OFF → ON | planted tiles OFF → ON |
|---:|---|---:|---|
| 5 | 1.00 → 1.00 | 1,388 → 1,384 | — |
| **6** | **2.00 → 1.00** | 887 → **1,899** | 27.6 → **15.5** |
| 7 | 2.00 → 1.30 | 683 → 1,176 | — |
| 10 | 2.10 → 1.80 | 5,863 → 5,929 | 38.5 → **27.1** |
| 12 | 3.00 → 2.70 | 3,819 → 3,892 | 51.5 → 41.6 |

The ON purse is **+1,012 richer at d6 — the land bill, unspent** — and eleven
tiles short for the rest of the season. `SPREAD_ROWS_SKIP_LAND_DAYS` keeps the
shipped three-lot layout on any day whose plan buys land (a traced `where`, since
`d.buy_land` is a traced int and a row's turn cannot be), and with it the band
volume gap closes from **−80 units (t −17.6) to −1 unit (t −0.56)** and the
engine control from **−5,260 to +179**.

**This is a defect of B's own layout, not of the arm.** A day whose BUY_LAND
depends on the size of the turn-1 row is a day one bad shop draw can cost a
quadrant, and nothing in the planner prices that. It is worth its own look,
independently of any spread.

### 5.2 The shed door

`LOT-DEPTH` warned that a tight cap destroys units at the shed door and
`SHED-CLIP` has B's shed sitting at 99 % of its 100-unit cap with 21.6 units a
game-seat already clipped, so the switch is written never to leave a unit

## 7. VERDICT AND WHAT IT CLOSES

**CLOSED.** Six variants over three board sets (68 band + 44 ENG22 + 20 TOP5NOW
= 132 paired game-seats per variant, both seats, CRN, action-replay opponent), and
the least-bad reading of the mechanism is **−1,622 of margin at t −8.40** and
**−1,882 of coins at t −10.15** on the 68-board band. The gate for spending an
engine leg was band MARGIN ≥ +300 at t ≥ 2; the §115b bar (POOLED180 ≥ +450 at
t ≥ 3) was never approached and no engine judge leg is warranted.

**What this closes:**

1. **pricevol §4.3's +5,382 — "the single largest reachable line" — is
   falsified as a coin lever.** The burst size is reachable (2.83, past the top
   five's 3.73) and reaching it costs 1,882-2,752 coins on frozen volume. §4.2 gives
   the one-line reason, §4.3 shows the bound's own four products losing 87 % of
   the money, and `LOT-DEPTH` §2's +46-to-+163 was the right size all along.
2. **The sell clock is now measured at every door, and this was the last one.**
   `EARLY_SELL` moved whole lots earlier (+2,184, shipped); `LOT4` added a
   fourth row late (−831 margin) and early (+112); `LOT_SPLIT` capped within the
   three turns (≤ +69); `SELL21` moved lot 3 to turn 21 and handed the opponent
   +1,383 (consensus §90); and **`SELL5` (consensus §91) offered the allocator
   two extra rows at turns 7 and 14 — and it declined them**, taking only 52 of
   1,441 season units (3.6 %) through them, level in 320 paired engine games.
   §91's own words were *"the row count was never the constraint"*, and it
   closed the family with the allocator's discretion intact. **SPREAD6 is that
   experiment with the discretion removed**: six rows, the volume forced onto
   them, the burst capped at the town's own appetite. The allocator was right.
   Forcing what it declined costs **−1,882 to −2,752 of coins and −1,622 to
   −1,802 of margin on the band**.
   **Every door on the sell clock is now closed from both sides, and the only
   one that ever paid paid by selling EARLIER and BIGGER.**
3. **The expressibility census's 87.3 % (pricevol §4.6) is confirmed harmless.**
   Trading on 8 hours instead of 3 is now built, shipped behind a switch, proven
   in the engine, and worth −1,882 to −2,752. The empty hours were empty for a
   reason.
4. **The channel that IS live is our VOLUME, and it moves the other purse 3.5×
   more than ours.** The difference between §3.1's tick-cap row and §3.2's is
   79 units a game-seat, and it is worth **−2,806 to us and +9,794 to them**
   (+6,695 of the as-specified reading is strawberry alone, on frozen opponent
   volume). That is **159 coins of margin per unit of our production, against a
   realised price of 91** — the denial trade
   `docs/strategy/2026-09-08-loss-anatomy.md` §4 named as the d15-29
   discriminator, now priced from our own side and by accident. It is the one
   number in this study that points somewhere, and it points at production,
   which `docs/strategy/2026-09-09-board-fill.md` has already priced at
   −5.6 to −6.9k because labour binds.
