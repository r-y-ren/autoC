# LAND: is buying a quadrant EARLIER a live lever?

**VERDICT: NO — earlier land is a LOSS, and on the closest-to-clean arm it buys
literally nothing.** Copying ymg_aq's own quadrant calendar (quadrant 3 on day 8
instead of B's day 10, quadrant 2 unchanged on day 5) costs **-285 coins/board
(t -0.2) on the 12 ymg_aq boards and -881 (t -2.2) on the 68 band boards**, and
because the pot is shared it costs **-3,428 (t -3.1) and -3,867 (t -8.2) of
margin**, flipping 13 of the 29 band boards B wins and flipping none back. The
quadrant is bought and then **not used**: crop tiles at d10 are 37.8 against B's
38.1 while idle tiles go 1.1 -> **29.6**, and the two lines have converged again
by d13. Pulling it earlier still through the gene (`gb2[1]`, the land-bias
coins) is far worse — **-12,739 (t -3.5)** on ymg and **-16,855 (t -8.8)** on
the band — because the same coordinate also drags quadrant 2 from day 5 to day
0-3, where the 1,000 coins *are* the opening ramp. There is no positive arm on
either set. The delta is made of land cost displacing the herd (d10 herd 11.1 ->
7.6, animal-product revenue -2,466) with **zero** extra tiles planted, and the
coins we hand the market early come back as the opponent's price (+2,986).

**And B's day-10 purchase is GATE-bound, not cash-bound.** At dawn d9 our purse
is 2,144 against the third quadrant's 2,000 and both of `_derive`'s hard laws
pass outright; what refuses is the gene, whose `tanh` is saturated at -1 so
`land_bias = -land_price` and the planner is told to demand a whole extra
quadrant price of margin. The earlier-land question is therefore *answered*, not
*blocked*: B can buy earlier, the arms make it buy earlier, and it loses.

All sim-descriptive: CRN sim, tape-action opponent seat, pinned towns,
`shop_crn`; the same runner as MACRO-EXEC and DEV-SLOPE (`S/land/ledger.py` =
`S/macro_exec/run.py` with the output directory changed plus two flags,
`--gb2 i:delta` and `--site2`). No engine game, no ES arm, no promotion gate.
Baselines were re-run in this tree against a frozen copy of `src/` (other legs
are editing `plan.py` live) and reproduce MACRO-RAMP / DEV-SLOPE **to the coin**
(ymg 93,127 / margin -13,751; band 104,092 / margin -192), which is the
cross-check that the OFF path has not moved.

Tools: `S/land/{ledger.py,slope.py,report.py}`; raws
`raw_{ymg,band}_{B,p4,p6,p10,sched}.npz`; full output `S/land/report.md`,
reachability `S/land/slope.log`.

**The first divergence is quadrant 3, and it is two days.** Quadrant 2 is not a
divergence at all: both sides buy it on day 5, on every board. B is not behind
on land until d10, and through d8 B has *more* cash at every dawn but d7 and
more crop tiles than ymg_aq on d1-d3.

**Is B's day-10 purchase cash-bound or gate-bound? Gate-bound.** At dawn d9 our
purse is **2,144** against the 2,000 the third quadrant costs, and `_derive`'s
two hard laws (`money >= land_gap = max(2000 - rev1, 0)` and
`money >= land_cost // 2`) are both satisfied outright before a single sale.
What refuses is the valuation gate, `land_value + macro.land_bias > 0`, and the
gene's half of it is pinned:

    land_frac = qfloor(tanh(head[1] + aux[1]*afford) * 256)     # brain.py:983
    land_bias = land_price * land_frac // 256

B's decoded `land_frac` is **-255.8 of -256** averaged over the 180 ymg_aq
dawns -- `tanh` is **saturated at -1**, so `land_bias = -land_price` and the
planner is being told *"do not buy a quadrant unless it is worth more than a
whole extra quadrant price"*. On d0-d12, 25.6 % of dawns are affordable and
`land_ok` (the decode's own "the gene has not vetoed it and it is affordable")
is True on only **7.7 %**: on two of every three affordable dawns the gene, not
the purse, is what says no. B is late because its land gene sits at the most
conservative value the transform can express.

A side effect worth recording for DEV-SLOPE: because `land_ok` is almost always
False, `n_free = n_free_slots(obs, land=land_ok)` almost never counts the next
quadrant's 25 locked tiles. DEV-SLOPE's "n_free +land" column is a hypothetical
B never takes.

## 2. Reachability of an earlier quadrant through theta (Q2)

`gb2[1]` is index **3875** (`gb2` offset 3874); B's value is **-2.7018**.
Measured on the same 180 ymg_aq dawns, `land_bias` as a fraction of the
quadrant's own price:

| gb2[1] delta | mean land_bias / price (d0-12) | dawns the gene says yes | dawns affordable | dawns BOTH |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| -20.0 | -1.000 | 0.000 | 0.256 | 0.000 |
| -2.0 | -1.000 | 0.000 | 0.256 | 0.000 |
| -0.5 | -1.000 | 0.077 | 0.256 | 0.077 |
| **B (0)** | **-0.999** | **0.077** | 0.256 | 0.077 |
| +0.5 | -0.997 | 0.154 | 0.256 | 0.115 |
| +1.0 | -0.991 | 0.385 | 0.256 | 0.231 |
| +2.0 | -0.934 | 0.974 | 0.256 | 0.256 |
| +4.0 | -0.143 | 1.000 | 0.256 | 0.256 |
| +6.0 | +0.925 | 1.000 | 0.256 | 0.256 |
| +10.0 | +0.996 | 1.000 | 0.256 | 0.256 |
| +20.0 | +1.000 | 1.000 | 0.256 | 0.256 |

Two facts fall out.

1. **No later arm exists.** B is already at the negative saturation of `tanh`;
   -2, -4 and -20 all decode to exactly `-land_price`, the same bias B has.
   The asked-for "later by 2 days" control is **not expressible by theta**, by
   any value of any coordinate on this line. That is why this report's control
   is the schedule arm in the other direction instead.
2. **The sd cost is enormous.** One sigma-0.02 draw of `gb2[1]` moves
   `land_bias` by 0.00000 of a price to five places; a sigma-0.02 draw of the
   whole `g2[:,1] + gb2[1]` block (33 coordinates) moves the mean bias by
   **0.0001** of a price (sd over 24 draws), against a B level of -0.9992. The
   deltas that do anything are +2 = **100 sd**, +4 = **200 sd**, +6 = **300 sd**
   at the training sigma.

**ymg_aq's purchase day is not reachable within 5 sd — it is about 300 sd away**
(5 sd = a delta of 0.1, which does not move `land_frac` off -256 on a single
dawn). The arms below therefore over-ask deliberately: they are a measurement of
what earlier land is *worth*, not a proposal for an ES step.

## 4. Anatomy: what the delta is made of (Q4)

Season sell revenue, our seat, board mean, 68 band boards:

| arm | WHEAT | CARROT | TOMATO | STRAWB | MELON | EGG | MILK | WOOL | FERT | total | buy spend |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| B | 12,002 | 6,914 | 3,988 | 34,503 | 13,701 | **6,016** | **24,695** | **16,287** | **10,307** | **128,413** | 12,701 |
| sched | 12,262 | 7,062 | 4,302 | 34,965 | 13,829 | 5,619 | 24,132 | 15,597 | 9,491 | 127,257 | 12,409 |
| p6 | 11,161 | 6,017 | 6,885 | 33,673 | 12,608 | 4,058 | 22,620 | 11,362 | 6,482 | 114,865 | 10,464 |

1. **The quadrant is bought and then not planted.** Under `sched` the third
   quadrant arrives at dawn d9 and crop tiles on d9 and d10 are **38.2 / 37.8**
   against B's **38.2 / 38.1** — identical to a tile. What changes is *idle*:
   2.2 -> 29.2 at d9 and 1.1 -> 29.6 at d10, and by d13 both arms are back at
   56. Two days of land, zero days of crop. The development budget is sized once
   at dawn from `n_dev = qfloor(dev_frac * n_free)` out of a purse the purchase
   has just emptied, so 25 new tiles arrive with no money to seed them — the
   same failure MACRO-EXEC's `"full"` mode showed (44 idle at d10) and
   DEV-SLOPE's `dmax` showed (21.2 idle at d10), one level up the chain.
2. **The 2,000 coins come out of the herd.** d10 herd 11.1 -> 7.6 head;
   EGG -397, MILK -563, WOOL -690, FERTILIZER -816 = **-2,466 of animal
   product** against +1,312 of crop (total sell revenue -1,156), for the -881 net. Crop revenue does rise
   (the tiles do eventually get planted), but not by the herd's worth.
3. **Seed cash, not land, is the binding resource.** Cash at dawn d9 falls
   1,664 -> 661 (band) and 2,144 -> 416 (ymg) under `sched` — the purchase is
   affordable and is still the difference between a funded and an unfunded seed
   order the next morning.
4. **The gene arms fail on quadrant 2, not quadrant 3.** `gb2[1]` is one bias on
   *every* quadrant decision, so raising it past B's saturation pulls quadrant 2
   from d5 to d0-d3 as well. On the band that means 1,000 coins gone on d0-d1,
   d1 crop tiles 19.0 -> 14.9 and idle 0.0 -> 17.7-27.2 for the whole first
   week; the opening ramp never happens and the arm never recovers (d15 crop
   tiles 51.0 against B's 56.2, still 21.9 idle). Quadrant 3 *does* move to
   d7-d8 on the band — which is ymg_aq's day — and it does not help.
5. **The opponent doubles it.** Δmargin ≈ 4 x Δcoins on `sched`: our early
   2,000 coins are their price. Their coins go 104,284 -> 107,270.
6. **Owning the land earlier is not what ymg_aq's d9-d10 jump is.** With their
   calendar copied exactly, our tile count does not move at all. Their 48.8 and
   55.2 crop tiles at d9-d10 come from planting **11.3 tiles out of a 6-tile
   dawn** (d2) and 20.2 out of 19.3 (d6) — replanting *inside* the day as
   one-time crops clear. That is the dawn-census defect DEV-SLOPE §5.2 named,
   and this report is its second independent confirmation.

## 5. What this says to do next

The land family is closed: the purchase day is reachable (the schedule arm makes
it exactly ymg_aq's), it is gate-bound rather than cash-bound, and moving it
earlier is a wash on coins and a loss on margin on both sets. The gene
coordinate is worse still and is 300 sd away from any ES step.

**The one next experiment: INTRADAY-REPLANT.** Both DEV-SLOPE and LAND now point
at the same planner defect from opposite sides — the day's development is sized
once, at dawn, from tiles that are free *at dawn*, so neither more budget
(DEV-SLOPE: -9,644) nor more land (LAND: -881) can be used. Measure a second
development pass at a mid-day turn, after the day's harvests clear tiles: same
`_derive` machinery, same paired CRN ledger, the 68 band + 12 ymg boards, with
the first pass unchanged so the arm is purely additive. The observable to
predict it: B's own idle count the morning after a purchase (11.6 at d11) and
ymg_aq's 11.3-tiles-from-a-6-tile-dawn on d2.

## UNVERIFIED

* Sim only. No engine game, no ES arm, no promotion gate. Every number is
  CRN-sim, tape-action opponent seat, pinned towns.
* The asked-for "earlier by 1 / 2 / 4 days, bisected" ladder was not run as
  such: section 2 shows the gene is a saturating `tanh` with only three distinct
  reachable points on this line, and it moves quadrants 2, 3 and 4 together, so
  a bisect to a named day-shift does not exist. `sched` delivers the exact
  2-day shift on quadrant 3 with quadrant 2 held at B's day, which is the clean
  version of the requested arm.
* The asked-for "later by 2 days" control does not exist either — B already sits
  at the negative saturation (`land_frac` -256 of -256).
* `t` values are paired over boards with the two seats of a board counted as two
  rows, as in every MACRO-* table; they are not independent games.
* The reachability decode (section 2) is on **ymg_aq's** 180 dawn boards, the
  same dawns DEV-SLOPE and CROP-DAY used, not on our own; our own dawn purses
  are in the section 1 table and are larger through d8, which makes the
  gate-bound finding stronger, not weaker.
* `sched` also *forbids* a purchase on any day the schedule does not name. B and
  ymg_aq both stop at three quadrants on these boards, so this bit never binds;
  under the p-arms a fourth quadrant does appear (band dawn 15.2), which is one
  more reason those arms are not a clean land-timing measurement.
