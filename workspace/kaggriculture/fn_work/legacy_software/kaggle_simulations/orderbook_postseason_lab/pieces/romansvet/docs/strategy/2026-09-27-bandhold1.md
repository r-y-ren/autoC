## How a trainer candidate is judged on BAND-HOLD
Run from S/bandleg1. leg.py could not take a boards file, so **`BL_BOARDS` was added**. `BL_HEAD` and `BL_THETA` were also added
for head/theta candidates. All three default to no-op, and a smoke run with explicit head_940/theta7659 gave the vrp12 seat
margin exact.

```
cd S/bandleg1
BL_BOARDS=../bandhold1/boards_hold.json KAGG3_SRC=<candidate tree>/src [BL_HEAD=<head.npz>] [BL_THETA=<theta.npy>] \
  nice -n 10 ../../.venv/bin/python leg.py <arm> "PLACEFEED_ON=True,PF_PUMPSAFE_ON=True[,<switches>]" ../bandhold1/res/<arm>.csv band <workers>
python3 ../bandhold1/pair_hold.py ../bandhold1/res/<arm>.csv     # flips, Δours/Δtheirs (t), per family and per sub vs res/pfs_hold.csv
```

- **ES (eswork_theta) candidates:** copy the base tree and drop the candidate's genes into `kagg3/core/eswork_theta.npy`.
- **Cost:** about 20 s per game at 3 workers on the loaded box, so 104 seats take about 35 min.
- **Hold-out law:** the POOL7-BANDHOLD rows and boards_hold.json must never enter a trainer's train or accept set.
  S/bandbank1/build_xr.py and esband.py read POOL6-BAND / boards_all.json only.
