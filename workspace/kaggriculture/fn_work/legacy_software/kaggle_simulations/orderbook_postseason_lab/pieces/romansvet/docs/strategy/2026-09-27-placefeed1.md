## 1. Design (≤ 10 lines) and cost
- `PLACEFEED_ON` gates `pf_ok` = placement tile (`m_place`, standing structure or new build), kind sheep/goose, first-fire harvest day ≤ `pay_day()`, product quote > wheat quote, not terminal.
- Chain: `... BUILD, PLACE, FEED, CARE` on the same tile, so the same unit does it inside its route. Two cands are inserted right after the PLACE row; CHAIN_MAX 6 still holds (DIG+BUILD+PLACE+FEED+CARE = 5).
- Wheat: the tiles join `Prefix.want_feed`, which carries the feed pickup mask, the wheat reservation against the sale, the prestock forecast and the VRP stop `need`.
- Any shortfall versus `wheat_avail - Σwant_feed` is re-bought on the turn-1 row (`_pf_wheat`: the grant's leftover room and purse, walked up the opponent-free buy quotes). `purse_left` is charged for it.
- Tile value: `+ max(price[prod] - price[wheat], 1)`.
- **Cost per placement:** 1 wheat (≈ 25-30 coins) + 2 hand-turns (FEED, CARE). No extra move, since the unit is already on the tile. Possibly 1 wheat PICKUP turn per block that had no feed pickup.

## 5. Verdict: NO SHIP (criterion Δours > 0 fails; gift-free and flips pass)
**What the mechanism does:**
- The fix works in the engine. Fed+cared on the placement night goes 0 → 98 %. First-fire units: sheep 5.0 → 5.95, goose 2.7 → 3.7.

**Why our purse does not follow:**
- **The +1 unit does not become coins for us.** Pooled, sheep produce ≈ +7.5 extra first-fire wool a game (7.9 sheep placements × +0.95; geese 3.0 × +1.0 egg), yet wool sold rises only +1.7 a game (−3.3 on sheep-heavy boards). Egg sold +2.9, milk −2.3.
  - Wool's above-I0 price curve is `sq` (target 3.2 over T = 105), so extra early wool lowers every later wool quote.
  - The sale-hold logic keeps wool volume ≈ flat, so the first-fire unit mostly moves wool earlier and cheapens the rest. Milk −2.3 is likely the labour and wheat the extra feeds displace (not traced).
- **Sheep-heavy boards (≥ 10 sheep) lose −759 own**, and the rival loses −2,169 there. That is price denial, not our purse.

**Where the value lands:**
- Rival purse −921 a game (t −4.86), margin +868, flips +6/−2 over 123 games. This is the CREWAUDIT1 TWO-PURSE caveat measured: one-sided "+1.3k/g" → own −53 (t −0.28).
