# 2026-09-16 — SHED-CLIP: the route-side lever (`CLIP_CAP_ON`)

**VERDICT: §115b REJECT — increment +131, t +2.00 on POOLED180 (bar +450 / t ≥ 3).
`CLIP_CAP_ON` ships default OFF and OFF is the shipped program byte for byte.**
The mechanism is real, the sign is positive on every set that matters and the
clip falls by two thirds; the size is a third of the bar.

## 1. WHAT IS CLIPPED, AND WHAT IT IS WORTH

`_end_of_day` → `_drop_inventories_to_shed` walks the seat's units in order and,
inside a unit, in first-acquisition order; `take = min(n, max(0, cap - cur))` and
**the tail past 100 is destroyed**. `S/shedclip/census.py` instruments that walk,
whole, on the shipped pair's own plan (LOT4@17 + SELL_SLOT_PRIORITY), 30 V45LEG
boards, real engine, real opponent tapes: the clip is **17.80 u / 1,415 coins a
board** (sd 1,486) over **2.90 clipping nights**, and on one of those 104.9 u
come home into ~98.8 of room and 6.14 die.

By item, u/board / coins/board / coins per unit: WHEAT 7.13/281/39,
MELON 2.43/420/**173**, CARROT 2.00/111/56, STRAWBERRY 1.60/109/68,
WOOL 1.60/322/**202**, FERTILIZER 1.43/37/**26**, TOMATO 0.97/89/92,
EGG 0.47/27/57, MILK 0.17/19/114.

**The composition is the whole finding.** Which units die is an accident of the
serpentine: the clip lands on **1.47 of the night's 11.8 units** and takes their
late, dear ranks. Price the *same count* of units at the cheapest the seat
carried home that night and the clip costs **277 coins instead of 1,415** — a
**1,138 coins/board ceiling that costs no labour at all**.

## 2. WHY THE OTHER TWO ROUTE LEVERS ARE NOT IT

* **(a) bank earlier** is measured twice and lost twice: `MIDDAY_DROP_ON`
  (crew-wide return leg) −1,157 a game t −11.3, and `BANK_BEFORE_LOT`'s
  `BANK_LOT = 2` (the same excursion against lot 3's turn) −1,257 on 192 pairs.
* **(c) re-order the evening**, priced here for the first time, is small:
  re-ordering **inside the clipped units** is worth **212** coins/board, inside
  the **last** unit **475**. The 1,138 needs a *cross-unit* permutation, and
  unit index is the serpentine block cut, not a free relabelling.
* **(b) carry less** reaches the ceiling from the other end: the shed's room is
  the scarce good, so spend it on the dear units and defer the cheap yield.

## 3. THE SWITCH (worktree `kaggriculture3-shedclip`, branch `shedclip`)

`CLIP_CAP_ON = False` at `src/kagg3/core/plan.py:663` (doc block `605-662`),
`CLIP_CAP_ROOM = spec.SHED_CAPACITY` at `:670` (the measured night shed is 0.8 u
— lot 3 and LOT4@17 clear it — so the room the haul comes home to IS the
capacity), the cap itself at `plan.py:7186-7229` inside `_derive`, **before** the
chain is packed: a suppressed tile must lose the HARVEST op itself and not merely
its value, or it is admitted for a harvest it will not take. `tests/test_shedclip.py`, 6.

Mechanism: rank every HARVEST (crop and animal) by its **unit price**, ties to
the lower serpentine index — `_rank_by`'s pairwise ordering, summed over
*quantities* instead of counted, which a rank cannot do. A tile is suppressed
iff the harvests strictly ahead of it already fill the budget; the straddling
tile is **kept**, so the cap is a floor on the carry. The deferred yield is not
lost: `sim/eod.refresh_plants` keeps `t_yield`, and only a plant already at
`CROP_MAX_YIELD` decays, from the day *after* it tops out.

**The budget is `CLIP_CAP_ROOM` minus one unit per `COLLECT_FERT` the day
takes**, and that correction is the difference between a live switch and a dead
one: of the 104.9 u that come home on a clipping night **18.80 are FERTILIZER**,
one per collection. The first arm counted harvests alone, sat at 86 of 100,
never bound, and read the clip 17.80 u / 1,419 coins against 17.80 / 1,415 OFF —
inert. The collections are *charged*, not suppressed: `_candidates` may source a
FERTILIZE from the very animal the COLLECT stands on.
