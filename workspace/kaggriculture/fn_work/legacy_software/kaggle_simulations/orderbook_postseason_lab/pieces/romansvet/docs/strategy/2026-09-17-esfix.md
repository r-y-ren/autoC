## 2. Mechanism — two flags (`src/kagg3/es/train.py`)
1. **Saturated margin.** `fitness_components` (:585-602) scores the relative half as
   `sigmoid((mine−theirs)/margin_scale)`; both arms run `--margin-scale 3000` on 110k games,
   so every margin is in the flat tail and the term **is the win bit** — a +14,400 gift to
   the opponent moves it by ~0. `shaped_advantage`'s docstring (:628) names this exact
   degeneration. Default 100,000.
2. **A fifth of the gradient is a mirror.** `--arch-frac 0.5` (`scripts/train.py`:1718) spends
   half the non-pinned slots on the **self-play pool** = the run's own frozen lineage. With
   `--pinned-once` the 52 tapes hold 416 of 524 weight units (79 %, `rung_episodes` :4530);
   the residual 108 episodes are 21 % of the weight but **34 % of the episode count**, and
   `mean_win` is an *unweighted* mean over episodes (:5133). The module docstring (:14) knows
   it: "mean_win saturates … the pool is the policy's own lineage, so 'beat your past selves'
   is satisfiable at any absolute level". That is the meter that rose 0.46 → 0.54.

`--pinned-fixed-seed` (`pinned_seed_word` :1324 over the per-generation draw :4974) does freeze
52 boards for the run's life; the `train` row above is the direct test of it and it is worth
**0.6 %**, so it is **KEPT** (with `--with-town` the shops are pinned by the tape anyway, and
rotating the word only puts noise back into 79 % of the signal). This is the one deviation
from the brief and the table is why.
