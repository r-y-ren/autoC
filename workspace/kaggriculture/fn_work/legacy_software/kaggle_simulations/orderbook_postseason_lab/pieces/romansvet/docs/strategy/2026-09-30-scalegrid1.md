## 3. What the rows say
- **V56 scale (a):** the only axis with signal, and it is a trade.
  - Raising the V curve (a075/a100) loses one m40 win: 38 -> 37, incremental m40 margin -216 / -448.
  - It gains on the V-coded tape seats: a100 OTH56 +1,335/row on 4 changed seats, +1 flip.
  - Lowering it (a025) loses both v21 flips (15 -> 13, own -354).
  - 0.5 sits on the V56-faithful optimum: it is the only point that keeps m40 38 and v21 15 together.
- **Programme scale (b):** monotone loss away from 1.0 on the 79 MEL-coded seats, with zero flips moved.

- **PQ4 curve (c):** it reaches 25 latched seats, moves no win and gives -4/-6 per row. The 1-10 live PQ4 record is not a curve-scale problem.
