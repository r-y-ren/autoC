# WHEATLATE1 — the dosed, water-gated wheat fill, d10-19

2026-09-19, branch `wheatlate` off `master` 9616aca8. The cell [[volumehi]]/[[opscensus]] left
standing: their d10-19 surplus is **cheap wheat** (83 % of their plantings, 3.82 water turns each)
funded out of our idle turns, and the ungated fill that reaches it (`PLANT_FILL_LATE_ON`, every tile
from d12 on, no turn accounting) is −871 t −3.01 here. This is that fill, **dosed** and **priced**.

## The float (`plan.WHEAT_LATE_ASK`, 0.0, free, layout-free)

On d10-19 (`WHEAT_LATE_DAY0/DAY1`) the fill may add at most `floor(WHEAT_LATE_ASK * n_free)` wheat
tiles to `macro.plant_target`, and no more than the day's **spare crew turns** can water at
`WHEAT_LATE_TURNS` (6 = 1 PLANT + the 4.06 WATER turns/planting OPSCENSUS measured + `EST_MOVES`).
The spare-turn estimate is the planner's own — `land_reach`'s numerator verbatim, `n_units * (TPD −
ROUTE_BASE − LAND_PICKUPS) − worked_pre * (1 + EST_MOVES)` (`plan._wheat_late_cap`, call site
`plan.py:9144`). The tiles ride `_fill_wheat` + the PLANT_FILL second seed grant, so seed is bought
at dawn from the coins the day's own grant left. The window mask carries `extra > 0`, so **zero
extra tiles is the OFF program** and no day buys seed it cannot plant. Not a gene (`SWITCH_GENES`
stays 18, layout 7,692): `SW_EXTRA=,WHEAT_LATE_ASK=…`.

## Verdict

REJECT — dose-monotone, losing at the gate, gift-positive live, flips negative on every leg. POOLED
never run (precondition HIBAND Δours > 0 and Δtheirs ≤ 0 fails on all three arms). The gate halved
the ungated fill's −871 and kept the damage off the shared curve on tape, but a turn-priced wheat
tile is still a losing tile here, and the knob *above* the gate is inert — every dose between this
and `PLANT_FILL_LATE` is now measured. The idle turns OPSCENSUS counts are turn-1 BUY-row waits and
position-bound turns that a labour estimate prices as free and the route cannot convert
([[idlework]] 85.7 %). `WHEAT_LATE_ASK` stays 0.0, byte-identical, layout-free, unmerged. The shape
left standing is OPSCENSUS's own: a **turn-cost term in the crop score** (`brain.decide` prices no
crop by the water turns it will eat), not a bigger ask.
