**Cause: the ask itself.** `brain.decide` sizes `n_dev = dev_frac * n_free` with `dev_frac = sig(head[5] + aux[2]*n_free/25)` ≈ 0.53 (theta gene), and
`plant_total = n_dev - animals` (animals 0 after d12). `_derive` funds 97 % of the ask (seed 70 of 72); seeds never bind, the purse never binds
(tens of thousands idle), crop eligibility only binds from d28 (`can_mature`: nothing on d28-29; tomato after d21, strawberry/melon after d19).
So ~half the free slots are never asked for — a gene fraction, not a constraint.

## 5. VERDICT
**NO SHIP / REJECT** (`RELAY_FILL_ON=False`). The empty tiles are unasked because `dev_frac` ≈ 0.53, not because of seed, cash or eligibility; filling
them is gift-free but costs more crew (hires, moves, herd care, fertilizer) than the relay's ~95 coin/planting returns. Dev +3 on both doses is noise
(held-out −6 / −1, tapes −4, faithful −1). DSM's relay pays because its whole farm and crew are built for it from d6 (11.5 hands, 0 PASS, no herd care
competing); on our crew-limited seat the marginal tile is a walk. Anything that re-opens this must first cut the per-tile turn cost (route locality:
relay tiles adjacent to the existing wheat block) or free herd-care turns; volume by ask (PLANT_ASK / ASK_FILL / PLANT_FILL_LATE / WHEATLATE1 /
RELAY_FILL) is now closed on both halves of the season.
