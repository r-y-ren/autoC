## Verdict
- **Fewer hands: yes. Less work because of fewer hands: no.** We run 42 % fewer hand-days in d0-9 and 14 % fewer in d10-19. In d20-29 we
  run 5 % fewer. Our hands are never the bottleneck: 0 labour-bound game-days out of 1,218 loss game-days. From d10 on they idle 2.3-2.5x
  more than the rival's. Each hand does about the same work per day as the rival's hands (19-21 vs 19-22 effective actions per hand-day).
- **The smaller crew is the CONSEQUENCE of a smaller job.** The rival has 92.5 vs 11.0 melon tile-days in d0-9, 15 % more animal tile-days
  and 58.5 vs 1.8 seed unit-days in stock in d10-19. Its extra d0-9 hand-days go to walking (+419 moves) and hauling or building (+83).
  They do not go to planting (68 vs 67) or harvesting (102 vs 97). In d0-9 we harvest 216 units to its 157.
- **Where the user is right:** in d10-19 we do less work: -11 % productive actions and -16 % units harvested (701 vs 831). We sell 455 units
  to the rival's 820. Net of market buys our revenue is -16.7k. That is the smaller job (melon -12.8k, fertilizer -3.2k, milk/wool -3.1k),
  not missing hands.
- **Crew does not separate wins from losses.** Against the MELON family (7-28 vs rivals >= 2,340), our crew, work and units are the same in
  wins and losses (hand-days 37/94/106 vs 35/94/108; units 215/713/1,043 vs 205/713/1,036). The swing is our late prices: strawberry
  141 vs 81, milk 142 vs 79 in d20-29. Those follow the shop draw (town strawberry/milk/wool demand +18/+28/+31 % in the wins).
- **Lever:** the ledger points at the d10-19 melon wave (-12.8k/game). That is a d0 cash and land allocation, already tested and closed:
  D10WAVE1, MELONTRIAL1, MELONSHIFT1, COMBO1, PLATENOLAND1. Adding crew was closed by CREW1 (-2.9k..-9.5k). The only crew coin left points
  the other way: at most 0.7k/game of last-hire wages, on ~9 d10-28 days with a full idle hand-day. CANDIDATE none.

## 3. Cause or consequence? CONSEQUENCE: our hands are never the bottleneck
- **Labour-bound days** (`res/extra.md` (a)). A labour-bound day has a labour sign AND 0 idle hand-turns. A labour sign is a ripe non-ongoing
  tile standing at dusk, an at-cap tile/animal, or an empty tile with seeds in stock.

|---|---|---|---|---|

| rival | 1,218 | 580 | 30 | 41 |

- **Ripe tiles and late harvests.** Ripe non-ongoing tile-days standing at dusk: 0.2 / 0.1 / 2.2 per window (rival 0.0 / 0.9 / 2.4).
  Harvests >= 1 day late: **0.0 in every window, both seats, all 42 losses**. At-cap: 0.0 / 0.4 / 0.1 (rival 0.0 / 1.1 / 6.4).
- **Idle hand-turns.** From d10 we idle 2.3-2.5x the rival (226 / 255 vs 97 / 102 per window; 23.6 vs 9.3 per day in d10-28), and 74-78 % of it
  is PASS at h18-23: the day's work is done. Hour profile (d10-19, per game): h0-5 20, h6-11 4, h12-17 26, h18-23 176.
- **Planting.** Planting is never delayed for lack of hands. Our seeds in stock at dusk are 1.6 / 1.8 / 1.6 unit-days per window (rival
  15 / 59 / 48): we buy seed when we plant it. Our empty tiles at dusk (41.7 / 20.4 / 42.7) sit on the land-buy days (d5 15.4, d6 8.6, d10 11.8)
  with 0 seeds and idle hands, and at the end of the game (d25-28, both seats). That is the purse and the seed buy (CREW1, YIELD1 M7), not labour. d1-2 purse at dawn: 191 / 104 coins.
- **The job each crew carries.** The rival's larger crew serves a larger job:
  - Melon tile-days d0-9: 92.5 vs 11.0.
  - Animal tile-days: 77 / 190 / 183 vs 66 / 165 / 164.
  - Owned tile-days in d10-19: 785 vs 746 (Q3 d8-9 vs our d10; Q4 in some games).
  - Plus a market relay. Per hand-day both crews do the same work.
- **Within the MELON family** (35 games), our d0-9 hand-days correlate +0.25 with margin. Our d0-9 crew tracks our own d0-9 job: PLANT r +0.64,
  seed+animal spend r +0.61, units harvested d0-9 r +0.84 (`res/extra.md` (j)). The rival is also stronger in the low-crew games (rating r -0.24).
  - The pooled r +0.41 (all 88 games) is a family confound: V games give us both a bigger early job (hand-days 40 vs 35) and more wins.
  - Forcing the crew exogenously (CREW1 `CREW_PLAN`, 7-10 hands at d9) bought no units and lost 2.9-9.5k in the closed loop.

## 5. The lever the ledger points at, its bound, and what already tested it
- **The one block the ledger isolates is the d10-19 melon wave: -12.8k/game revenue in all losses (-13.5k in the MELON losses).**
  - The rival sells 65-66 melons in d10-19 against our 12-18, from 88.6 vs 5.5 melon tile-days planted in d0-9.
  - The bound is the full block, ~13k/game, if the plate could be planted without cost.
  - It is not a labour lever: the plate needs d0 cash and tiles. PFS's d0 purse has zero slack (BRAINSTORM1 invariant) and goes to the herd.
- **Already tested, all NONE:**
  - D10WAVE1 (docs/strategy/2026-09-29-d10wave1.md): B4/B8 plate -15.3k/-17.1k margin.
  - MELONTRIAL1: own +0.2..+0.6k, rival +12..+18k.
  - MELONSHIFT1: P8d3 own +3.2k, rival +4.6k.
  - COMBO1: plate + herd floors.
  - PLATENOLAND1: plate on the starting tiles, keeps the cows but not the herd.
  - Each plate costs the d0-9 herd, and the rival takes the late milk/wool/strawberry prices.
- **The crew lever itself:** CREW1 (docs/strategy/2026-09-29-crew1.md) floored the crew at 7-10 hands by d9: -2.9k..-9.5k vs g0capsfix. The
  extra hands buy no units and the rival gains +3.3-5.4k. This ledger shows why: there is no labour-bound day to relieve.
- **What CREW1 missed (small; it points the other way):**
  - CREW1 counted d1-17 on older live27 seats. On vrp20's losses, d18-29 is not labour-bound either (idle 255 vs 102, ripe at dusk 2.2 vs 2.4, late harvests 0).
  - In d10-28 we carry a full idle hand-day (>= 24 idle hand-turns) on 8.9 days per game. The last hire on those days costs 673 coins/game (fib 55-89).
  - That is the upper bound for hiring ONE FEWER hand, if the other hands can absorb its route. The VRP's mode-ii drop already tries this, so the real gain is below 0.7k.
  - CREW1 also said the programme's crew "carries a bigger job". Refinement: in d0-9 that job is walking and hauling, not plant/harvest. Its productive actions are only +28 above ours.
  - Uncared animal-days at dusk in d20-29 are 62.8 ours vs 43.5 rival. But hands are idle on those days, and a care bonus is paid only on a later
    fed production day, so late care is worth little. It is not labour and not tested; the bound is below 1k.

CANDIDATE: NONE. PROMISING: NONE. The answer to the user: we hire fewer hands because we have less to do, not the reverse. The work gap is
the d10-19 melon wave and herd size (a d0 cash allocation, closed by five arms). The live W/L swing against the same family is the late price board.
