- No arm met the escalation rule (net >= +5 flips with faithful dtheirs not a gift), so the 9-leg JUDGEALL1 was not run.

## 1. The switch and the foreign-state check
- **The switch.** `KERNEL2_HANDBACK_DAY=<D>` sits in the plan.py switch block; the default 0 means never.
  - In `Runtime._kernel2`, on a seat the step-1 latch gave to V56, the first turn with step >= D*24 (the dawn of D) sets the latch to `"back"`. As ZERO_BACK does, it sets `k2_fn=None`, resets the VRP bank (`vrp_saved=0`) and the fill ledger (`vrp_fill={}`), and returns None, so `_pfs_act` plays from D h0 to the end.
  - At D = 0 the new block is one `int(...) > 0` test, and only on V56 seats.
- **PFS replans sanely from a foreign state.** The planner is stateless across dawns.
  - `build_day` reads only the parsed obs view and the macro.
  - The plan module's globals are config only (RESIDUAL_FN, _DEFAULT_TABLE, MACRO_SCHEDULE, _ENGINE_GATE_BASE).
  - There is no cached plan, no day-0 seed table and no herd or hold quota keyed to its own history. The herd, seed and hire asks are recomputed from the observed farm at every dawn.
- **Runtime-level history audited:**
  - **Found and fixed: `prev_mkt_inv`.** It feeds the brain.market_momentum feature; production runs with `pass_prev_mkt_inv=True` (main.py, eval_vs_baselines).
    - PFS last rotated the dawn market inventory at d0. Without a fix, its first dawn at D would read the whole d0 -> dD market draw as "yesterday's" momentum.
    - The minimal fix is in runtime.py, inside the HANDBACK block. Every kernel turn at hour 0 of d1..D-1 rotates `prev_mkt_inv/dawn_mkt_inv` exactly as `_pfs_act` does, so PFS's own rotation at D gives prev = the d(D-1) dawn.
    - The plan logs confirm it: at D=10, prev = the d9 dawn. So do the tests.
  - **VRP bank and fill ledger:** PFS's d0 h0 VRP state is stale after V56's days, so both are reset at the hand-back, as ZERO_BACK does.
  - **ENGINE_GATE:** it latches on PFS's first dawn >= d1, so it now latches at D on the rival's dD field. This is harmless.
    - Its set (MELON_PLATE_TILES=20, MELON_PLATE_DAY=1, NONV_PLATE_LAST=6) only rewrites days 1..6, because `_melon_plate` acts on `DAY <= view.day <= LAST`. All of those days fall before D=8.
    - opp_engine was False on both logged boards.
  - **Other switches:** RIVAL_TELL, SELL_SLOT_RIVALRANK, PROGRAM_ENGINE and ROUTE_NN are OFF in the gene block, so none of them reads history.
  - **Hands** are hired daily (there are none at dawn), so the crew is always PFS's own.
- **First-day plans at D=10** (`S/handback1/planlog.py`, output `res/planlog_*_D10.json`).
  - The V56 body at d10 h0 was the same on both boards: 5 wheat, 20 strawberry and 12 melon tiles, 6 cows and 7 sheep, about 3.7k cash.

  - **tape_seekinspiratio_114103638 (h1 2438):**
