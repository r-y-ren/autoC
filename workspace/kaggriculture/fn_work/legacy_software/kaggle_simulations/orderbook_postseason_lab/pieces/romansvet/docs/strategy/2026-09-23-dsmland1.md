# DSMLAND1 — does DSM (rank 2, 3,096.8, 100-0) work all four quadrants? (2026-09-23) — **NO: DSM owns 3 quadrants in 68/68 games**

Branch `dsmland1` (worktree `kagg3_wt_dsmland`; from progfidelity1 = b281e68f tree). READ-ONLY. Tools `S/dsmland1/{ledger.py,report.py,run_ledgers.sh,run_dev.sh}`; out `report.txt`, `quad_econ.tsv`, `per_game.tsv`.
Method: every replay step is diffed (tiles `[y][x]`, `unlocked_quadrants`, hires, shed + carried stock). Harvest = once-crop PLANT→empty or ongoing/animal yield→0 on that tile. Revenue = the seat's own SELL orders priced on `spec.market_price` at the pre-step book, capped by stock (DSM seat 111814429: 143.6k gross vs 113.5k final). Each quadrant is credited with its harvested units × the seat's revenue per harvested unit, plus fertilizer revenue split by animal-product share. Costs: land (`LAND_PRICES` 1,000/2,000/4,000), seed per PLANT on the quadrant, animals placed there, and the day's hire bill (fib) split by the share of hand-turns spent standing on the quadrant. Payback = first day cumulative value ≥ cumulative cost. This is an average (not marginal) attribution, so every owned quadrant nets positive; the marginal verdicts are the paired reads at the end.
