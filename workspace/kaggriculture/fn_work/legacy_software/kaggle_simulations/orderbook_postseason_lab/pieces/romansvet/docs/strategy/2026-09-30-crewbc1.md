## Integration (`prog/crewbc.py` numpy inference, knobs in `prog/direct.py`, switch PROG_CREW_BC_ON default OFF)
- `bc=1`: the pass-2 (free) hands pick among the rulebook's LEGAL task tiles (+ shed squares) by `logp_net(tile) - bclam*(dist + prio)`.
- `bc=2`: the net's primitive-action head drives every hand (legal mask: in-bounds moves, own-tile op present in the menu and
  feasible, DROP/PICKUP only at the shed; args from the rulebook), falling back to the rulebook action.
- `bcani=1/2`: animal chains (feed/care/collect/animal harvest/pickups, carrying an animal, shed jobs) and structure builds stay with
  the rulebook (without the build guard coops are never built: geese d9 0.1 vs 2.85, eggs 32 vs 231).
