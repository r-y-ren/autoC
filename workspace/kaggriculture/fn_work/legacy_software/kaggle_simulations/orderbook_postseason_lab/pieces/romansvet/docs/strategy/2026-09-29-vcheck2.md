## Mechanism: this loss is the herd, not the strawberry lot
- **Strawberry is not the lever here.** vrp21_clsearch lost V by moving about 30 d15-17 strawberries late (VCHECK1). This candidate keeps 46/41 of PFS's 51/48:
  - the V's 208 late strawberries still sell @91 / @73 (PFS @89 / @71);
  - rival strawberry moves only +0.4k / +0.6k.
- **The offsets cut the animal books.** The d1-4 COW -1, the d5-9 GOOSE -1 and the d10-14 GOOSE -1 / SHEEP -2 all shrink the herd. Geese at d9 h12 are 1.0 vs 2.0.
  - Our d10-29 volume falls: eggs 56 vs 122u, fertilizer 92 vs 142u, wool 89 vs 117u (v21). m40 is the same shape.
  - The V56 agent sells the same units: wool 138 vs 133u, fertilizer 269 vs 270u. It gets better prices: wool +3.6k / +4.0k, fertilizer +1.4k (t 9.8 / 12.0).
- **This is the VBAND1 rule again, "our volume is denial", on the animal books.** At d18 we are +0.5k / +1.2k ahead (o432). The own loss and the whole rival gain come in d18-29, once the smaller herd's output stops draining the wool, egg and fertilizer curves.
- **Against p48c the extra strawberry pays; against V56 it does not.**
  - vs p48c: our late strawberry sells @161 on top of +27u, strawberry +6.1k (t 13.1). That covers the -5.7k on eggs, fertilizer and wool, so own is +3.7k.
  - The rival still gains the same +3.6k on those three books (+2.3k wool, +1.3k eggs, +1.4k fertilizer). So the margin is +26.
  - vs V56: our late strawberries meet the V's 208-unit wave (@108 / @99). The strawberry gain is only +0.5k / +0.8k, and own goes negative.
- **The clone reads did not see this.** ESHEADCL1's fitness paid own coins only, with no denial term. In the p48c cell the candidate is what ESHEADCL1 measured: own up, rival up by the same amount.
