## 2. Cost per generation and update signal-to-noise

* Plain generation **55.5 s** (median 55.4) = 512 candidates × 160 episodes = 81,920 episodes → 1,476 episodes/s, **65 gens/h**. Abs report every 10 gens adds ~17 s (72.3 s) → 3 % of wall. JIT at gens 1 and 10 (552 + 944 s) = 25 min per launch; gen 1200 spent 474 s (other-GPU launch contention). Real gate runs on 10 CPU workers *off* the GPU: 0 % of generation time; verdict lands 8 gens (~7.5 min) after nomination.
* Episode budget: 147 of 160 episodes (92 %) are the pinned rungs, 13 for the 4 unpinned rungs + pool (the launcher's own warning).
* Estimator (`train.py` 3749–3786): `grad = (adv⁺ − adv⁻) @ eps / (pop·σ)` on rank-normalised, CRN-paired antithetic fitness, then Adam at lr 0.003. Reproducibility of that direction is already high at this setting: half-split Spearman 0.93/0.95 at E_half 128/256 with `--shop-crn` (build-story §9), 0.96 with `--pinned-once` at 160 episodes (memory, es-noise-floor). Doubling pop or E buys ≤ 0.03 Spearman for 2× time. Within a generation the population spread is pure measurement noise: median best−mean win 0.116 ≈ 3 sd of the binomial sd at 160 episodes (0.039); only the paired difference carries signal.
* What Adam does with it — measured on the record thetas (rms displacement per coordinate vs the pure-noise walk `lr·√G`): g0→g60 0.0237 vs 0.0232; g60→g300 0.0443 vs 0.0465; g300→g940 0.0731 vs 0.0759; g940→g1000 0.0209 vs 0.0232; g1000→mean g1230 0.0469 vs 0.0455. Every interval sits **on** the noise-walk line: each coordinate moves ≈ lr per generation regardless of signal (the code's own comment on Adam's `|mhat|≈√vhat` floor). A consistent drift would scale with G (0.003×640 = 1.9/coord), not √G. ‖θ‖ inflated 15.78 → 17.96 (g1000) → 18.51 (g1230) with weight decay 0 — the saturation mechanism the wall audit found (land_bias pinned at clip). So the per-generation update is ~all diffusion; the gain is the tiny bias of that walk, which is why 640 gens buy +2 pts/100.
* Gate SNR: leg20 delta SE ≈ 18k on 24 games (family-volatility doc); accepted deltas +1.5k…+41.9k, refusals −17k…+5k → the gate is a lottery at these n, and the last three refusals are the only ones consistently < −15k.
