## 1. Engine truth: how yield is made
- **Non-ongoing crops (wheat, carrot, melon)**, `_new_plant` L215-226: the plant starts at `yield_units = 1`. WATER (L431-444) adds +1 (or +2 if `fertilized_until_day >= day`) only when `(max_yield_day+1)//2 <= age <= max_yield_day`, capped at `max_yield`. Windows: wheat ages 2-4, giving 4 without fertiliser or 6 with it. Carrot ages 2-3, giving 3 or 4. Melon ages 6-12, giving 6 (7 waters, capped). HARVEST (L446-472) takes all `yield_units` and clears the tile.
- **Ongoing crops (tomato, strawberry)**, `_daily_refresh_plants` L769-802: `yield_units` starts at 0. On nights where `(d+1-planted-first_yield_day) % interval == 0`, up to `max_yield` = 4 productions, the plant gets +1, or +2 if the tile was watered that day and `fertilized_until_day >= day` (L797). Held units are capped at 4. After the 4th production the plant decays (`_decay_plants` L752-766: −1 every 2 steps, then it becomes a weed). Maximum = 8 per plant; unfertilised = 4.
- **Death:** `consecutive_unwatered` starts at 1 on the planting day (L221). Two unwatered nights turn the tile into a WEED (L782). FERTILIZE (L475-481) costs 1 FERTILIZER and covers day, day+1 and day+2.
- **Animals**, `_daily_refresh_animals` L805-833: `pending_care_bonus += 1` on every night where the animal was CARED **and** FED (L829-830; CARE L524-530 is free, FEED L505-513 costs 1 wheat). On a production night (`(d+1-placed-first_yield_day) % interval == 0`), `yield += 1 + (pending if fed_today else 0)`, capped at `max_held` (goose 4, cow and sheep 6). Then pending resets to 0, so an unfed production night **loses** the bonus (L826-828). The ceiling per production is 1+interval: goose 2, cow 3, sheep 4. The first production also carries the pre-yield days (placement to `first_yield_day`), so up to 1+first_yield_day. Two unfed nights in a row and the animal escapes (L817-820). Age, happiness and weeds play no part. Harvest timing does not change output unless `max_held` binds.
- **Yield state variables, complete list:** watering inside the window (non-ongoing), fertiliser active on watered production nights (ongoing), days cared and fed since the last production, fed on the production night, and held units against the cap.
