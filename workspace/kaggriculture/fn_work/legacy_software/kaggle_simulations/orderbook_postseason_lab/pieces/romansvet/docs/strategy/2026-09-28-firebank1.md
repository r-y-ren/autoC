## 2. Arms and runs (run.sh; res/off.csv, res/on.csv)
- **OFF** = vrp18_k2hb2046 as shipped: `KERNEL2_FIRE_CASH=26|29|2046|2338|2438`, HANDBACK_DAY=18.
- **ON** = OFF with FIRE_CASH `20|26|29|190|938|2046|2338|2438|2464|2854`. A seat fires only when its sim rival h1 is listed, so on each seat ON is exactly OFF plus that seat's value.
- **Compute:** 1 worker when the 1-min load was above 14 (arms in turn), otherwise 2 (arms side by side), all at nice 10. Wall time was 16:33-17:35Z.
- **Batch order:**
  - Round 1 took a slice of every value in live-share order: 938, 20, 2854, 2464, 190.
  - After round 1, 190 was already a CANDIDATE. The other four values already had at least 3 combined faithful losses, so they could no longer meet the "at most 1 faithful loss" rule.

- **Judge:** JC1 faithful (|rival units - live| <= 5 % and rival purse >= 0.5 live, in both arms), soft-win t (s=3000), two-purse rule (paired seats only).
- **LIVEVAL1 seats are pooled directly:** their OFF was vrp17, which equals vrp18 everywhere except on 2046 seats, and their ON listed these same values.
