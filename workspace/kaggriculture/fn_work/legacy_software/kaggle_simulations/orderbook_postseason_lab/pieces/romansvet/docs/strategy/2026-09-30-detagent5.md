## 3. Reading

1. **No spend item is waste on V56.** Every cut loses margin; hands 10-12 earn 2.1-3.5 coins of V56 margin per coin of wages (hand 12:
   5,255 / 2,538), and the 20 % feed-lot cut saves ~0 coins (the shortfall is re-bought) yet costs 4.1k. Mechanism (two purses): the item's own revenue exceeds its price (hand 12: 3.7k revenue for 2.1k wages) AND V56 is ~open
   loop on units, so each unit we do not sell raises V56's price on the same product (hand 12: V56 +3.6k, all d18-29 price: strawberry
   +1.6k, milk +0.8k, wool +0.5k, wheat +0.5k). On V56 a marginal unit of our output is worth ~2x its own price in margin; the MMPQ tapes
   (fixed opponent) see only the own half, which is why H11 costs -1.6k on the tapes and -5.3k on V56.
2. **Exception = the 4th quadrant**: L3 loses 7.3k own but V56 LOSES 5.8k too: without Q4 the crew moves from Q4 wheat to strawberries/
   animals, and our earlier S/M/W sales cost V56 more than our missing wheat gives it (V56 wheat +4.4k vs strawberry -2.4k, milk -2.3k,
   wool -3.0k). Net Q4 still earns +1.5k margin on V56 and +8.0k on the tapes: keep it.
3. **The crew is sized right at 12.** The 12th hand earns +5.3k margin; the 13th loses 0.5k (full) / 0.2k (peak). Earlier hands (d4-9) are
   cash-limited and return noise (+906 n40 for +1 hand d6-9; the +2 dose is -1.4k).
4. **So the -10.5k own purse vs vrp26 is not a spend problem.** The body spends 10.5k more and each of those items pays for itself on V56;
   the gap is what vrp26 does with LESS (its strawberry/milk supply timing d0-17, DETAGENT4's top-3), not what the body buys.

Against the user's rule (any size when paired t >= 2 on V56 m40 + v21 vs det2_place, no win regression, tapes within 0.010): every cut,
every defer and every add MISSES (table above; the ledger of each miss is in its row and in §2), including h69 (§4).
