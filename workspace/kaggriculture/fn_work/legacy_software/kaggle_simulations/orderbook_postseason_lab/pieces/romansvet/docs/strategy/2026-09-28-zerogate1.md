## 2. d1-dawn switch-back `KERNEL2_ZERO_BACK` (`res/zback.csv`, `res/summary.md`)
**Rule.** At our first d1 turn of a V56-latched game, if the rival has 0 MELON plantings and at least `KERNEL2_ZERO_MIN_PLANTS` = 12 plantings, PFS takes the farm for the rest of the game.
- PFS runs unshifted from d1 h0 with fresh per-game state. Its ENGINE_GATE NONV2 latch then fires at d1.
- Why the plant count matters: the shipped ENGINE_GATE tell (0 melon, ≥ 1 plant) would also switch 2 flagged MELON seats that are 0m at d1. Those are tineshagent (5 carrot + 1 wheat) and wewannabetomat (8 plants), and V56 wins both.
