## 3. H2 survives -- where the objective loses it

`shaped_advantage` reads the judge's margin on the pinned block, whose weight in flow226 is 52
episodes at `EPISODES_PER_PAIR * rung_weight` = 8 vs 160-52 = 108 residual at 1 -> **416/524 =
79.4 %**; the other 20.6 % is 54 pairs, 90 % archetypes (`--arch-frac 0.9`). Line scan of the
realised displacement on that block (`rows/linescan_train52.txt`, t = fraction of the
displacement, L2 0.0609, 104 boards):

    t          0.25    0.50    0.75    1.00
    d_margin   -569    -409    -501  -2,224   (t -1.18 -0.63 -0.85 -3.59)
    d_theirs   +723    +981  +1,728  +2,659
    win       52.9%   54.8%   55.8%   49.0%

`d_theirs` is monotone -- the gift is priced at every point -- but for three quarters of the walk
the block's margin is flat inside noise while its **win rate rises**; at `--margin-scale 100000`
the rel term is linear in that margin, so the objective's dominant term was near-silent over most
of the trajectory and the collapse is all in the last quarter. The fault is not the simulator,
the tape seat, the market model or the purse accounting: the 79.4 % term is flat along the
search direction and the 20.6 % residual sets it.
