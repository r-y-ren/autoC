## 1. Reward spec (`route_nn.observe` / `worth` / `finish`) — USER law: dense route-quality reward, purse/wins = gate only
W(s) = cash + projected value of OUR farm, evaluated at the first observation of every day:
* field: each plant = expected harvest units (current yield, or the crop's growth window reached within the days left;
  0 if it cannot yield before d29) × the planner's own projected price at that harvest day
  (`projector.inv_at_day` on the engine price table, `spec.build_price_table`; no new pricer);
* animal yield on the tile, shed + bags at spot price; seeds at seed cost.
DAYR[d] = W(dawn d+1) − W(dawn d); the last day closes on the final purse (`finish`, nothing is worth anything after the last turn).
So: (a) a planting is credited projected sale value − the seed it spent, a dry death / rot debits the same value;
(c) the hire bill is the engine's own cash debit (farmHandCostMult·fib(n)); (d) shed-overflow destruction (goods vanish)
and h23 harvests (field → shed value) are inside W. Σ_d DAYR = final purse − W(first tracked dawn) exactly (telescoping).
(b) Paired baseline: the trainer subtracts the SAME board's OFF DAYR (planner alone, crew head disarmed) day by day —
training pool of fixed boards (V56: LIVE250 rows 100-149 + 50 fresh seeds with LIVE250 towns, never gate boards;
DSM prefix: first 60 of the 131 DSMFULL1 boards × K ∈ {10,15,20}); the first visit of a key also plays OFF, cached in
`runs/<run>/baselines.json`. Reward is 0 on every day the policy changes nothing.
