## 3. Rule diff against DSMLOGIC1 (18 rules)
- **Hold unchanged (15):** R1-2 opening; R3 Q2/Q3 timing; R7 crew start and shed pickups; R8 animal triple; R9 water classes; R10 fertilize ages; R11 harvest ages; R12 relay (163 replants, next turn 0.89); R13 crop tree (small tomato tilt only); R14 JIT seeds; R15 flush (EGG flush 0.64 -> 0.77, otherwise the same); R16 morning wheat/fert sale (fert morning 0.76 = 0.75); R17 drip on ticks (MILK 0.58 / 0.56, WOOL 0.43 / 0.47, STRAWBERRY 0.41 / 0.43); R18 endgame harvest and drop.
- **Changed (4):**
  - R3: Q4 fills on d10 in 40/40 games (was 101/131), and BUY_LAND is no longer re-issued every turn.
  - **R4 herd:** the sheep gate has loosened. With WOOL >= 200 at d6 it buys 0 sheep in 2/22 games (was 35/83); with WOOL < 200 it buys sheep in 8/18 (was 11/48). The price correlations are unchanged (COW-MILK 0.69, SHEEP-WOOL 0.65, GOOSE-EGG 0.49), so the mix keeps the same price shape at a higher level: +2.5 COW, +2.6 SHEEP and -1.0 GOOSE, with the d6 geese deferred to d9-12.
  - R5: feed wheat d10-19 is 95 (was 111), and 84 fewer wheat units are sold d10-29. With a herd 1.9 animals larger, more of its own wheat goes to feed.
  - R6: the 12th hand is present on 86 % of d14-24 hand-days (was 61 %).

## 4. New mechanisms (checked against the CLOSED index in MEMORY.md)
- **OPEN: none.** Every change is a parameter shift inside a family we have already tested:
  1. Larger cow+sheep herd with fewer geese at d6-12. CLOSED: HERDRAMP, HERDTILT2 (gift), ENGHERD1 ("more animals, defer gifts"), WOOLFIRST1 (milk gift), EGGDOSE1, and wool/eggs in DSMGAP2.
  2. Tomato over wheat tilt, +4 tiles. CLOSED: CARROTBID tile reallocation and the OPSCENSUS crop mix.
  3. Keeping own wheat for feed rather than buying it. CLOSED: FEEDKEEP1.
  4. Q4 always on d10. CLOSED: Q4PROG1, Q4VRP1 (4th closure).
  5. More 12th hands. CLOSED: HIRELEVEL1, CREW24, KNOBVRP1.
  6. d29 idle hands. CLOSED: ENDFAMILY and ENDTURNS.
- The shape of the change (spend ~1.3k more on herd by d12 and get +4.7k purse by d20) is the ENGHERD1 "more animals" lever. We rejected that lever for us on the two-purse rule: extra milk and wool units gift the rival's price. DSM wins 109-0 with it, but it has no paired run, so the gift cannot be read from live data.

## 5. Verdict
**No port candidate.** DSM's new sub is the same 18-rule program with a heavier, sheep-first herd (+1.9 animals at d13), a denser 12-hand crew and a small tomato tilt. The live gain (+1.6k mean margin vs opp >= 2,300, 109-0) is confounded by the change of period and pool. All changed axes are CLOSED. The only thing not yet run is a re-test of the herd level under the current VRP router (ENGHERD1 predates VRP). That belongs to the coordinator as a separate dispatch; it is not a new mechanism.
