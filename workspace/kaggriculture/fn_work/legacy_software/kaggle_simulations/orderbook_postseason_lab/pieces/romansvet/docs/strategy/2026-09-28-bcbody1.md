# BCBODY1/2 — closed-loop behaviour clone of the top-5 body (2026-09-28)

**Verdict: NO-GO.** The clone reproduces the top-5 d0 opening exactly. It then collapses: on the 10 teacher seats it ends at **0.50x** the teacher's own final money. It wins **2 of 40** BAND142 seats (PFS 20, V56 body 14, KERNEL2-gated 27).
It is not a KERNEL2 handoff target. No BCBODY3.

## Encoding and model
See `S/bcbody1/feasibility.md` for the full spec.
- **Unit head** (shared net for the farmer and every hand): 44 tokens (15 simple + PLANT x5 + PICKUP x12 + PLACE x12), plus a 45-class quantity head for PICKUP and PLACE. Input is a 19x19 egocentric crop of the 28-channel own grid, 154 global features, the unit index and the unit's inventory.
- **Market head:** 21 keys (SELL x9, BUY_SEED x5, BUY_PRODUCT x2, BUY_ANIMAL x3, HIRE, BUY_LAND), each a 45-class quantity (0 = none).
- **Model:** conv net, 3.2M params, JAX. Trained with AdamW, warmup-cosine lr 1e-3, 2,048 unit + 512 market samples per step, 30k steps. The best checkpoint is selected on held-out tok_nonpass + mkt_nz.
- **Agent:** `S/bcbody1/agent/main.py`, a numpy mirror of the net with argmax decoding.
  - **BCB_MASK=1** (default, v2) adds an engine-legality mask and a per-step de-dup of tile claims. The engine drops ALL PLANTs of a crop when requests exceed seeds, and the raw clone looped 4 units on PLANT MELON.
  - **v1** of the mask had a bug: the PICKUP budget was zeroed after the first unit, which broke the opening. **v2** decrements by 1.

## d0 / d1 behaviour (fresh boards)
**Teacher d0 plan** (all 10 teacher seats, `d0/teacher_summary.txt`):
- h0: 1 cow, 3 sheep and 5 wheat.
- h1: 5 hires and a second cow. Pastures are built at h2-h8.
- 8 melon seeds and 12-15 wheat seeds are bought and 8 wheat products; 5 WHEAT are sold.
- State at d1 h0: **6 melon + 12 wheat tiles, 2 cow + 3 sheep**, money $2-16, hands 5 on d0. At d1 dawn it hires 3.
- MMPQ's variant: 7 melon, 13 wheat, 3 cow + 2 sheep.
- There is no 12-melon plate on d0. The melon count grows to 9-13 by d2 and d9.

**Clone v2** on the 40 eval seats is deterministic on d0: every seat gets the same plan.
- 2 cow, 3 sheep, 9 bought wheat, 8 melon seeds, 15 wheat seeds, 6 wheat sold, 4 hires on d0 (teacher 5).
- **mel_d1 = 6 on 40/40**, 21 productive tiles at d1 h0, mel_d2 9.1.
- On tape_decem_114310214 at d1 h0: 2 cow + 3 sheep + 6 melon + 10 wheat, $9. The teacher had 12 wheat and $2.
- At d1 dawn it orders 8 HIRE with $9 (teacher: 3 hires with $2). Most of these orders fail for lack of cash.

The opening is reproduced; the body is not.

## Verdict and next step
**NO-GO for a BCBODY3 KERNEL2 handoff.** Pure behaviour cloning from 264 public episodes gets the scripted d0 opening right: d0 unit accuracy 0.955, market 0.80, a 6/6-melon opening on every seat. It does not get the body right:
- mid- and late-game market accuracy is 0.2-0.3;
- the unit policy is 0.75 per decision per unit with no coordination, so errors compound in closed loop;
- the clone ends at half of the teacher's purse.

A handoff at any hour would inherit the collapse, which starts on d2-d6.
The only reusable piece is the opening. It is already known and scripted (MELONSWAP1 / top-team recipe), so the clone adds nothing there.

If this family is ever revisited:
- The next steps would be DAgger-style correction, using our planner as an online labeller of the clone's states, and a per-farm joint unit decoder.
- Neither fits the 09-30 deadline.
