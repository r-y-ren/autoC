## 3. Mechanism — the optimiser trains a sampled policy but the package flies greedy

The constraint did what its one-sided definition permits, but did not preserve the incumbent's edge.
Mean gift stayed slightly positive at about +0.02..+0.05 while adaptive `eta` rose to about 1.9;
positive and negative discordance remained flat near 200/200. Meanwhile entropy rose from 10.15 to
11.46 and sampled win rate rose from 49.3% over the first 50 updates to 53.1% over the last 50.

That training improvement does not survive argmax deployment. PPO assigns credit to sampled actions,
while the gate flies the greedy head; increasing entropy can improve the sampled objective without
moving the deployed decisions in the same direction. The reward also differs from the intended signed
gift objective: `max(g, 0)` gives no reward for reducing the opponent purse below the reference. The
two checkpoints' near-identical gate failures despite continued sampled improvement point to this
objective/decode mismatch rather than insufficient head capacity.
