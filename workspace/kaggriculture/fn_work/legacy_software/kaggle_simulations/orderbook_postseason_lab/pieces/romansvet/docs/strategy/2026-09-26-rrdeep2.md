# RRDEEP2 (2026-09-26 17:00Z–17:35Z): deeper VRP search on the compiled router → NO SHIP; the depth axis is saturated at 150/10/ON

**Question.** vrp8_jit (sub 56580780) runs ruin-recreate at 150 iterations / destroy size 10 / re-solve ON, which RRDEPTH1
chose when the Python router was the limit. The compiled kernels leave most of the 0.65 s bar unused. Is there more gain
in more depth?

**Answer: no.** Every cell that fits the clock is net 0 to −2 on dev100. More search saves a real 84-164 coins/game of
hire bill (t −2.5 to −4.0) and drops 1.9-4.0 more hands/game. It moves our purse by only +85 to +147 (t 1.7-2.1), and
the rival gains about +100 in cells a and c. No cell reaches the stage-2 gate (net ≥ +2, Δtheirs t < 2), so no
held/FRESH/tapes/faithful legs were run and there is no package.

## Verdict — NO SHIP, depth axis CLOSED at 150/10/ON
- No cell passes the stage-2 gate. Doubling the RR iterations, widening the destroy, or adding restarts saves hire bill
  (~150/game), but it does not flip games. The extra saving goes partly to the rival: the cells with more search effort
  (a, c) show Δtheirs of about +100.
- RRDEPTH1's 30→150 step gave dev100 +3 and Δours +255. The 150→300 step gives −1 and +113. The router search is on its
  plateau. ROUTERAUDIT1's +722/game oracle is mostly the 30→150 step already shipped.
- The restarts knob stays in the code, OFF by default and byte-identical. vrp8_jit (ad9b5b2f) is unchanged and remains
  the package. head_940 and theta7659 are untouched.
