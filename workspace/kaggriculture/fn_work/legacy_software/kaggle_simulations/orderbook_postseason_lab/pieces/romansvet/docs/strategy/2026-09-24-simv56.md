# SIMV56 (2026-09-24, 23:43-03:13Z) — reacting V56 as a sim rival seat + PPO against it

## 1. What was built (`S/simv56/`, branch `simv56`)
* `v56sim.py` — the V56 notebook agent (`S/v56leg/main.py`, the pool wrapper's callable via `get_last_callable`, one fresh exec per
  episode) plays a seat of the fast sim. The sim is stepped from the host: `dawn` (jit+vmap) plans BOTH seats exactly as
  `rollout.run_day` does (our seat with the residual head, V56's plan discarded), `turn` applies both seats' rows for one hour on the
  engine-faithful FULL market path (the tape seat's path: raw V56 rows, compacted planner rows), `eodf` = `eod.end_of_day`.
  Per turn the host pulls the state, `build_obs` writes the engine observation for the V56 seat (tiles as engine dicts, float money,
  hands/farmer positions, per-unit inventories in insertion order via `inv_seq`, shed/seeds, market inventory + engine prices, the
  ordered `unlocked_shops` list recovered from the per-day shop-count diff, step/day/hour), V56 acts, `encode` = the tape encoder
  (`tape_actions._unit_row/_market_row`). `V56Pool` = spawn workers (JAX_PLATFORMS=cpu) that own the V56 instances.
* `fidelity.py` — sim-vs-V56 against retained ENGINE replays, per-step obs diff for the V56 seat.
* `screen.py` / `spair.py` — greedy heads vs V56-in-sim on the LIVE250 boards (exact seeds, seats, pinned towns), paired flips.
* `ppo_v56.py` — PPO (ppo.py's `_sgd_step`/GAE/KL-stop) with a `--bv` V56-in-sim half + `--bf` flow257 half (tapes + af3 theta pool);
  `--panel N --gift-lam L`: fixed panel of N boards with the greedy resume head's (ours0, theirs0) cached once, V56 reward
  `(win - win0) + ((ours - ours0) - L (theirs - theirs0))/1e4`, halves normalised separately, shop CRN on the V56 half.

## 5. Verdict: NO SHIP
* **Fidelity: YES for V56.** The V56 seat's observation is byte-exact until our own seat diverges (d15-19 on 3/3 boards), every
  divergence is OUR planner's sim-vs-engine-agent gap; V56 purse error 0.3 % median over 100 dev boards, ours −1.2 % (agent-runtime
  layer the sim does not model → sim wins 59 where the engine wins 73; board agreement 86-90 %). ~14× engine speed per core.
  The sim screen is a usable fast V56 gate: its sign matched the engine on both engine-gated checkpoints.
* **Training: flat.** Two PPO arms (plain flow257 reward; paired cached-baseline reward with gift tax 1.5 and shop CRN), ~70 updates /
  2,800 V56 games each, found no gradient toward V56 wins: sampled V56 win and paired flips flat, every greedy checkpoint −1..−4
  on the sim dev screen vs its own start (head_940+fix). b u50 read +2 vs master on engine dev (below the untrained fix's +4, Δours
  −250 t −3.5) and failed held-out −3 with a gift +151 t 2.0 — RIVALPURSE1 u580's pattern again. The residual head's 18 clamped slot
  nudges are not where the V56 flips are (BESTRESP: plan-level, purse-conditioned moves); the new opponent seat makes that lesson
  cheap to re-test, it does not change it.
