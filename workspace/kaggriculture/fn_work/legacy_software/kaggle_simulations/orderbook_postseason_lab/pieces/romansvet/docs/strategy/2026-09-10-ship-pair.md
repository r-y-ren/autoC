# Shipping the tail pair: TAIL_FILL_ON + BANK_BEFORE_LOT_ON with flow166 gen 170

*2026-09-10. Built in the `ship-pair` worktree off `45f8217` (arms-next, the
delegating packager). The package is built and verified here; **nothing has
been uploaded.***

## 1. What ships

Two planner switches that have sat in the tree, correct and inert, since
2026-09-03, flipped from `False` to `True` in `src/kagg3/core/plan.py`:

| switch | line | what it does |
|---|---|---|
| `TAIL_FILL_ON` | plan:2589 | spends the PASS turns between the end of a unit's block and the end of the day on up to two hops to the nearest free carry-free task (COLLECT_FERTILIZER / WATER / DIG-a-weed) at a rank no block can hold |
| `BANK_BEFORE_LOT_ON` | plan:1858 | lets a block already carrying >= `BANK_MIN_VALUE` coins of harvest take a <= `BANK_MAX_TURNS` excursion to the shed and DROP it in front of the day's second sell lot |

They ship **together** and they get withdrawn together. That is not a
preference: the pair is what was measured, and the halves are lopsided in
opposite directions (§2).

The packager (`scripts/package_submission.py`) has no switch flag -- it vendors
`plan.py` verbatim -- so shipping the pair *is* flipping the two module
defaults. That is what this commit does.

## 2. The evidence, in the order it was taken

**Sim sweep** (`docs/strategy/2026-09-09-switch-sweep.md`), 128 pinned
held-out boards, both seats, paired against the shipped default:

| arm | HELD42 d-margin (t) flips | LEG20 | LOSS12 |
|---|---|---|---|
| `TAIL_FILL_ON` alone | +775 (t 3.31) +2/-0 | +1,366 | **-354** |
| `BANK_BEFORE_LOT_ON` alone | +165 | +209 | +166 |
| **the pair** | **+817 (t 3.50) +2/-0** | **+1,404 (t 2.70)** | **+575** |

The pair is almost exactly the sum of its halves, so the two do not interfere
-- and the bank half, inside the noise on its own, is what turns the filler's
LOSS12 loss into a gain. That is why the pair and not the filler is the
candidate.

**Engine confirmation**
(`docs/strategy/2026-09-09-plateau-review-verdicts.md` §32): HELD-OUT **+2/-0**,
H_dm **+801**, LEG20 **+1,403 a game** (filler alone +748 / +1,365). The sim
predicted +817 / +1,404 -- the two agree to about twenty coins a game. First
planner switch to pass the leg-family rule with a full standard error of
margin.

**Composed with the candidate theta**, on the 55 held-out live boards
(`S/live55/flips.py`, engine, paired against the LIVE theta `flow135_g350`):

|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `flow166_g170` alone | 34.5 -> 52.7 % | +22 | -2 | +3,145 | 645 | 4.88 |

not merely re-cut the same win.

**Still open: the drawn legs (veto).** The pair's drawn-board legs were still

## 5. Tests

Each switch owns a test module whose OFF half pins the plan byte-identical to
the planner the switch was cut into. Flipping the defaults does not invalidate
those pins; it means the *other* switch is now in the baseline they read.
Following `854b86b` (the same problem when `OPEN_PUMP_ON` was promoted), five
modules get an autouse fixture pinning the newly-on switch(es) off, with the
reason on the fixture:

| module | pinned off | why |
|---|---|---|
| `tests/test_tail_fill.py` | `BANK_BEFORE_LOT_ON` | its PIN digests predate the excursion, and `_worked` counts `OP_DROP` as working the tile the unit stands on -- the excursion banks into the shed from a farm tile another block owns |
| `tests/test_bank_before_lot.py` | `TAIL_FILL_ON` | its PIN digests were taken at `bdcf5f9`, before the filler existed |
| `tests/test_tail_care.py` | both | pre-pair OFF digests |
| `tests/test_care_fill.py` | both | pre-pair OFF digests |
| `tests/test_care_hold.py` | both | pre-pair OFF digests |

A tree-wide sweep of every `*_byte_identical_*` / `*_is_the_off_plan` test then
found **twenty-three more failures in twenty-one further modules**, all the same
one thing, and all fixed the same way (commit `b9b732b`): `test_animal_defer`,
`test_early_sell`, `test_endgame_tomato`, `test_feed_mandatory`,
`test_fert_volume`, `test_harvest_first`, `test_hire_row`,
`test_late_straw_cap`, `test_market_pack`, `test_melon_open`,
`test_midday_place`, `test_open_deny`, `test_open_pump`,
`test_open_pump_racer`, `test_opp_mix`, `test_plant_fill2`, `test_route_early`,
`test_route_split`, `test_same_day_fert`, `test_survival_water`,
`test_wheat_volume`. Thirty-four modules in `tests/` carry a plan digest; **all
43 digest tests pass with the pair on**, and the same filter run against the
pre-flip tree at `45f8217` fails none of them, which is what says these are the
flip's doing and not something already broken.

**No digest was re-pinned and no assertion was weakened.** Every PIN tuple in
the tree still says what it always said: "OFF, this switch's planner is the
planner it was cut into."

The one failure that looked like behaviour --
`test_tail_fill.py::test_on_never_lets_two_units_work_the_same_tile` -- was
run down rather than papered over. It is the excursion's DROP, not the filler:
the same collision appears with `TAIL_FILL_ON` **off** and
`BANK_BEFORE_LOT_ON` on (probe: `S/pkgchk/collide2.py`), and on seed 1 it is
unit 4 emitting `OP_DROP` on tile 44 at turn 10 -- banking into the shed from
where it stands -- while unit 7 legitimately DIGs, PLANTs and WATERs that
tile at turns 9-11. `_worked` treats any non-move, non-PASS, non-PICKUP op as
*working* the tile, so a DROP taken from someone else's admitted tile reads as
two units on one tile. Nothing double-works a tile; the blocks are still
disjoint rank ranges.

## 7. A pre-existing gate failure the pair makes slightly worse

`tests/test_gates.py::test_no_tile_write_collisions` fails -- and it **fails at
`45f8217` too, before the flip**. It is the release gate on the simulator's
parallel scatter: no two units may take a tile op on the same tile in the same
turn. Isolating the two switches over the test's own 60 trials
(`S/pkgchk/gate.py`):

| arm | collisions |
|---|---|
| pair OFF (the shipped tree) | **7** |
| `TAIL_FILL_ON` only | 7 |
| `BANK_BEFORE_LOT_ON` only | **8** |
| the pair | **8** |

So the invariant is already violated in the tree we ship today, seven times
over, and the excursion adds an eighth: trial 5, turn 11, units 3 and 4 both
acting on tile 33 (FEED and CARE). The filler adds none.

This is **not** the `_worked` artifact from §5 -- `OP_DROP` is not in the
gate's `TILE_OPS`, so the shed DROP cannot cause it. It is a real collision
between two units' admitted work, and it wants a separate investigation: either
the disjoint-block guarantee has a hole the animal ops (FEED/CARE) walk
through, or the gate's `n_units` reconstruction over-counts. Nothing here was
changed to make it pass -- papering over a live release gate would be the wrong
move, and the honest statement is that the pair inherits a broken invariant and
nudges it from 7 to 8.
