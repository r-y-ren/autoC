## 1. The arm

A wide day (`n_hire > ops.MO` = 10) hires a second row at turn 2, so no unit may step
before turn 3 [LAW, `ops.ROUTE_BASE_WIDE`] — a unit that has walked off its shed-access
tile re-scatters every hand `_spawn_hand` places afterwards. `ROUTE_SPLIT_ON`'s wide branch
(`plan.py`, `wide_ok = wide & (d_pick[e1] > 0) & (u <= MO)`) already buys the one turn that
law leaves: a pickup-owing block collects at turn 2, standing still.

Turn 1 is the same kind of turn and it is still empty: **143 of 143 turn-0 unit-days PASS
at turn 1** on every wide day of the three HOURTRACE tapes. **45.7 unit-days a game** own a
block owing **two or more** pickup kinds (`d_pick >= 2`) — kind A at turn 2, kind B at turn
3, walk at turn 4. Such a block can take kind A at turn 1 instead: stationary, so the
occupancy counts the turn-2 HIRE row reads are bit-identical, and its walk then starts at
`ROUTE_BASE_WIDE` = 3, the first turn the law allows.

What makes turn 1 different from turn 2 is the BUY row: turn 1's market phase runs **after**
turn 1's unit phase, so the pulled pickup draws last night's shed close. That is FREEFIRST's
per-kind test, and it has to hold for the kind that *actually lands on turn 1* — `pk_turn`
stamps the rows in `PICK_ITEM` order (wheat, fertilizer, one per animal), so that is the
block's **lowest-indexed owed kind** and no other.

## 3. Legality (deliverable 2)

* **Spawn law.** Over all 90 board-days of both arms, **zero** MOVE ops are emitted at
  turns 0, 1 or 2 — the crew is stationary right through the turn-2 HIRE row, so
  `_spawn_hand`'s occupancy counts, and therefore every hand's tile, are unchanged by
  construction. `tests/test_widepick.py` asserts the same two properties on the fixture:
  nothing but PASS/PICKUP below `ROUTE_BASE_WIDE`, and the turn-2 occupancy vector is the
  OFF vector exactly.
* **Hire law.** The converted units are turn-0 hires (`u <= MO`), whose first legal turn is
  `O.TURN_BUY` = 1 — this reaches it and never goes below it.
* **BUY-row law.** The kind on turn 1 is the block's lowest-indexed owed kind and it is
  absent from today's BUY row (`test_on_pulls_only_a_kind_the_buy_row_does_not_deliver`).
* **No quantity moves.** Every market row of every board is byte-identical ON and OFF
  (`test_on_moves_no_quantity`); `blk` is the same vector, only the turn each row sits on
  changes.
* **Cut against `bud + 2`.** The test is asked of `e2 = _cut(bud + 2)`, the block that
  actually runs when the switch grants two turns, and not of `e1`: a longer block owes a
  superset of kinds and asking at `e1` could put a bought kind on turn 1.
* NOT done in this box: a `kaggle_environments` replay of the ON plan. The engine-side
  claim rests on the sim, which `tests/test_widespawn.py` pins against the engine's
  `_spawn_hand` on 2,812 crews with 0 mismatches, plus the zero-MOVE census above.
