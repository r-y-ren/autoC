## D. Reading
1. **Hands are not the lever.** The shipped HIRE_ROW hires only hands the router loads. H2 (+2 affordable hands d1-9) moves the crew 3.42 → 3.45, as E1 did in NONV1.
2. **Early land is not the lever.** Q2 on d3 (every board) drains the d3-4 seed purse. Hands fall to 3.00 and plantings to 4.86, and margin moves −4.3k. The tiles arrive without the coin to plant them.
3. **The plate is a gift against melon rivals.** M20 and QM20H2 give theirs +17k (t 5.5-6.9) on the 10 melon-rival tapes. The rewrite takes the day's tiles off strawberry and wheat (`_MELON_PAY_RANK`), crops these rivals also sell (18 strawberry tiles by d9). I did not ledger by product whether their gain is our missing strawberry supply or shop seed stock they now get, so that part is unmeasured.
4. **Against zero-melon rivals the plate pays** (Δours +7-11k, t 2.1-2.8, every dose). The wins mostly come from our own harvest, not from denial: Δtheirs is between −0.4k and +2.7k.
5. **Limits of the plate.**
   - The window must end by d6. M20Lz (to d9) turns atif into a loss on both seats.
   - Tomatos swings non-monotonically with dose (open-loop tape).
   - atif's margin falls 12.8k under M20z.
   - Live frequency is 4/275 games, so the rating value is about 2 flips per 275 games, if the tapes transfer.
