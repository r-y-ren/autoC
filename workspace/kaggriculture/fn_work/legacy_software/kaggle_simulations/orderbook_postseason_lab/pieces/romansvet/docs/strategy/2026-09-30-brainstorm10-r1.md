## 3. The pair (max-of-two, per the coordinator's live-LB evidence)
**Mechanics.** Each sub gets its own BT θ over its October games, and the team takes the maximum.
- Per-sub sampling SE: σ ≈ (400/ln 10)/√(n·p(1−p)) with n ≈ 470 October games (FINALSLOT1) and p 0.66. That gives σ ≈ 17 θ.
- **Twins** (vrp25 + vrp26, H +113/game, 0 flips) are worth E[max] − θ = σ/√π ≈ **9.5 θ**. At 470 games this is less than the coordinator's 20-30, which were quoted at 80-300 games.

**Is there value in two different files?** Yes, but not from seat-mix complementarity. The max picks one sub's θ, and each θ is fit on that sub's own games, so "wins a different seat mix" adds nothing by itself. The value comes from the second file's mean shift δ and from its genuine uncertainty τ. With s = √(2σ² + τ²):

  g(δ, τ) = δ·Φ(δ/s) + s·φ(δ/s),  against the twin value g(0, 0) = 9.5 θ.

A different file beats the twin when δ > δ*(τ):

| τ | δ* | in live win rate |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | 0 | 0 |
| 20 θ | ≈ -6.5 θ | ≈ -0.85 points |
| 40 θ | ≈ -22 θ | ≈ -2.9 points |

- **The downside of slot 1 is capped:** a hopeless file costs at most the twin option, 9.5 θ.
- **M3's numbers:** δ ≈ +5 θ (programme +3/180 × 41 %, V 0/61), τ ≈ 3 θ. That gives g ≈ 12.5 θ, **+3.0 θ over keeping vrp25**. If M3 were as much worse as it reads better (δ = -5 θ), the result would be -2.2 θ.

**What difference, and what read.**
- The difference worth buying is a file with δ ≥ δ*(τ) and a real τ.
- The read is per-family paired win-probability deltas:
  - V56 m40 + v21 + fresh 167 (exact);
  - programme H-180 split by provenance (F2's method);
  - OTH56.
- Weight them by the live mix 48/35/6/11 to get δ ± SE, then compare g(δ, SE) with 9.5 θ.
- **No tonight's cell has τ large with δ near 0.** det2_place (-23k on V56) has δ ≪ 0.

**FIFO trap.**
- **Only slot 1 is a free option.** After upload X the pair is {vrp26, X}. The next upload retires **vrp26**, not X, so upload 2 must pass the full F_stress/beat gate.
- **Upload 3 is the only repair path.** Removing a bad X after that takes two uploads.
- Recommendation:
  - slot 1: M3-all if fresh 167 ≥ 160, else M3-P (idea 1), else vrp26-COPY (idea 5);
  - hold slots 2-3 unless a cell passes the beat gate against vrp26 itself.
