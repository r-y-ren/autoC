# Seed-309 judge: next-mechanism review

## Decision

The completed seed-309 evidence does **not** justify another policy or training
experiment. Its repaired-simulator final centre loses margin to B in every one
of the seven separately reported engine families: TOPB2 -124.7, LIVEC-H30
-395.2, LIVEC-H30B -40.2, LIVE62 -215.0, LOSS10 -911.5, NEXT30 -109.0, and
NEXTHIGH -713.5 coins per board. Own coins rise in six of seven families, but
opponent coins rise in all seven. These effects must remain separate; their
shared sign is a mechanism clue, not a pooled estimate.

The two tempting responses are already covered. Changing the own/margin blend
would repeat the closed objective-reweighting family: the older population
audit shows that the configured objective follows own coins on 622 of 855
component-conflict pairs, but does not identify a better coefficient. Adding
an opponent-price heuristic would repeat sell timing/volume/denial work, where
multiple interventions raised our purse while raising the opponent's more.
Fresh fields, forced melon, block-SNR, dither, radius-1/2 ISEARCH, and the
maturity feature premise also have direct negative or falsifying evidence.

## Highest-priority missing measurement

One unresolved measurement concerns **population-to-complete-plan attenuation**,
and it warrants a no-game census rather than a new arm. On the thirty fixed
episode-108450468 dawns, seed 309's 1,191-coordinate displacement changes at
least one decoded macro on 30/30 days but changes the complete plan on only
1/30. Conversely, the two generation-zero populations change the day-0 crop
target in about 56% of members, but that macro-only probe never built complete
plans. The missing link is whether the actual sigma-0.01 population routinely
crosses executable planner boundaries.

Use the already specified fixed sample in
`2026-09-13-es-action-threshold-measurement.md`: the first 128 antithetic pairs
on each of twelve pre-existing H30 B dawns (three fixed captures, days 0, 5,
15, and 25). Compare all six action arrays against the centre and report each
dawn separately: changed-plan incidence, pair crossing pattern,
macro-changed/plan-identical incidence, changed cells by array, and distinct
plan hashes. This is about 3,084 CPU planner builds and no simulation or engine
games.

Frequent complete-plan changes across these dawns would count against local
plan invariance; rare changes would show limited variation on these states.
Neither result identifies the cause of failed training, measures action value,
or establishes a global bottleneck. No numeric pass/fail threshold is specified.
The three day-0 observations are identical, leaving ten unique observations
among the twelve fixed cases; those repetitions are not independent evidence.
This diagnostic does not justify changing sigma, decoding or the mask. Block
sigma, dithering and local integer search retain their earlier negative evidence.

This measurement is not redundant with ISEARCH (outcomes of 24 fixed integer
edits), dithering (a held-out gradient intervention), block-SNR (gradient
support), or the melon probe (one macro field on one dawn). None measured the
complete plans expressed by the current antithetic population. Seed 310 is an
already-running independent replicate and should finish under its frozen
protocol; it is not a reason to launch a third arm.
