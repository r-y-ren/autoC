Engine rules (`_daily_refresh_animals`, the same in `src/kagg3/spec.py`):
- **Production fires on schedule whether or not the animal ate.** It gives 1 unit plus the pending care bank, and the bank pays only if the animal is fed on the fire night. So "produced" is 100 % of scheduled fires for every living animal. The per-unit difference is the bonus.
- **A CARE banks +1 only on a day the animal is also FED.** An unfed fire night wipes the bank.
- The ceiling is (1 + interval) / interval units a day: goose 2, cow 1.5, sheep 1.33.

## Verdict
- **Audit answer.** No: our animals do not get CARE on all days.
  - **Given a feed, we care 91 % of cow and sheep days and 96 % of goose days.** The misses are the `care_pays` price veto (cow 7.7, sheep 6.9 days a game), the correct pay-day horizon (cow 4.0, sheep 2.6) and router drops (cow 0.8).
  - **The bigger gap is FEED.** Unfed days: cow 42, sheep 36 a game, and 3.8 sheep run-offs. That axis is closed.
  - The rival's near-100 % care includes 36 cow-days and 27 sheep-days a game of CAREs on unfed animals, which are worthless.
  - Geese are at parity. The goose gate bug is fixed and inert.
- **`NONV_WOOL_CARE=True` (ungated) passes the letter on both beds and is the sheep ship-leg candidate:**
  - bed net +3, Δtheirs t −2.88.
  - dev net +2, Δtheirs t −1.96.
  - Caveats: Δours is negative (t −1.9 / −1.6), and the flips are the boards RIDE and SLIVER already flip: herd_saf@16, cha22@9, v56_9.
- **`CARE_FED_ON` passes the letter (bed +1, dev +2, Δtheirs t −4.4 / −5.2) but is dominated by `CARE_RIDE_ON`.** It is RIDE's superset. On the same bed it scores +1 against RIDE's +4, and its own-purse loss is 3× RIDE's. The cares it adds beyond RIDE mostly bank a sheep unit that an unfed fire night then wipes (+7 cares for +1.9 units). **Keep it OFF.**
- **Stack.** RIDE + WOOL passes both beds: bed +4 (the same net as RIDE alone, Δtheirs −478, t −3.98) and dev100 +2 (Δours −234, t −2.62; Δtheirs −401, t −4.96). Adding FED kills it: bed net 0, Δours −601 (t −3.82).
