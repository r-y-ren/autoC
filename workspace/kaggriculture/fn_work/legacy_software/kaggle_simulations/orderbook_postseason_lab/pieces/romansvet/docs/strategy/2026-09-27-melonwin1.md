# MELONWIN1 (2026-09-27 17:14Z-17:30Z): why the rival under-produces in our 14 MELON wins, and whether we cause it

**Verdict: BOARD-driven, i.e. OPPONENT-driven.** The win/loss split on the 51 BAND-leg MELON seats (PFS master vs the rival tape, 14-37) is set by the size of the rival's own d0-9 melon plate.
- **Plate size decides the seat.** Rival ≤ 8 melon tiles: **5-0**. 9-11 tiles: 8-14. ≥ 12 tiles: **1-23**, and the 1 is the 36-tile melon mirror.
- **The plate is locked in before we can touch it.** It is planted d0-9. That is before any melon unit enters the shared book: the first melon sale is the rival's, d10 h11-12, in 51/51 seats, and we sell 0 melon before it in 51/51.
- **Our side is the same in wins and losses** on every shared-book channel.
- **Nothing to probe.** There is no player-driven mechanism, so no probe is designed.
- **Denominator correction.** The 82.6k/67.9k in the dispatch is CREWAUDIT1's 8-vs-8 live V bed and covers all products. On these 51 seats the rival's d10-19 sales are **79.7k (losses) vs 59.2k (wins)**, and its MELON-only sales are **17.0k vs 12.9k**.

## Causality: which state is shared (engine `kaggriculture.py`)
- **Shared: the market books, one inventory per product.**
  - `_new_market` 178-185.
  - A SELL adds +1 to inventory when the price is > 1 (`_commit_unit` 652-661).
  - Buys from the book are WHEAT/FERT only (598-601).
  - MELON is in no shop (`SHOPS` 103). Only the town centre drains it, 1 unit/day (745).
  - So the melon book is a first-come pool, about 158 units above I0 before the floor.
- **Not shared: seed and animal prices.** They are constants (`CROPS[item]["seed"]` 603, `ANIMALS[item]["cost"]` 605).
- **Not shared: the hire ladder.** It is per farm (`hires_today`, `_do_hire` 702-710, reset per farm in `_end_of_day`).
- **Town draw:** `rng = Random(seed*1_000_003 ^ day)` (871, 891). It is independent of both players.

**Our one side variable, our melon tiles (p 0.017), is a consequence and not a cause.**
- It correlates negatively with the rival's plate (Spearman −0.31, p 0.03).
- Inside the rival 9-11 stratum it vanishes and reverses: wins 4 vs losses 6 tiles, p 0.62.
- The only shared-book effect runs in the other direction. The rival's plate is dumped first, from d10 h11. That pushes our melon price down to a mean of **141 in losses vs 176 in wins**.
- Our wheat and carrot sales into the shared d4-12 books are identical across groups. They cannot explain a rival plate that is fixed before d10.
- Our melon line is already a probed knob, and every probe gifted: P10 gated plate on this leg Δtheirs +2.8k, MELONVRP1, MELONCOUNTER2.

## What we can never win, and the rating cap
- **Seats:** a rival with ≥ 12 melon tiles in d0-9 is 1-23 under PFS (the single win is the mirror) and 1-23 live (there the single win is hamedvakili +1.8k, which PFS turns into −0.7k).
  - That is 24/51 band MELON seats. On vrp10's own seats it is **16/26, all lost**.
- **Share of vrp10 band games:** 57 % MELON (BANDLEG1) × 16/26 ≈ **35 %**.
- **Cap:** at a mean oppR of about 2,670, even with every other band game won, the band-implied θ is ≤ 2,670 + 400·log10(0.65/0.35) ≈ **2,778**.
  - Rank 5 (2,954) needs ≤ 16 % band losses.
- **Caveats:** tapes do not react, and the vrp10 sample is n = 26.
- **Hence:** top 5 is out of reach on this cell without a counter to the large melon plate. All counters tried so far (plate, counter crops, herd, Q4, fill) are CLOSED.
