**Conventions.** These are the same as MMPQ's. (d,h) is the decision state, the observation at step 24d+h. "h0 of dN" is the observation at step 24N. Every number is n, mean±sd.
- **FOUND**: sd 0, or sd ≤ 0.3 for counts, pooled or in every key group with n ≥ 5.
- **PARTIAL**: the key cuts sd by at least 2x, but groups stay above 0.3.
- **NOT FOUND**: the best correlate is given instead.

Keys tested: m1, h0 code, team variant, q4, yarn day, milk/egg shops at d9, the shop added at d3/d6/d9, and per-product demand sequences (open consumer shops at d3/d6/d9/d12/d15).
