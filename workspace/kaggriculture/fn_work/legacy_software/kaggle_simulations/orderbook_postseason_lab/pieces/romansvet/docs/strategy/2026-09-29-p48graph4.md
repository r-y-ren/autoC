## Gate 0: PASS
- **What the real seat does on d1-5** (16 boards; `realdays.py`, `realharv.py`, `realsum.py`; res/realdays.json, realsum.md):
  - **Cow before any free tile.** P48 buys the d2 cow at h8 while all 25 Q1 tiles are still occupied. It harvests d0 wheat later the same day, h12-18:
    - d2: (2,1) (1,0) (3,1) (0,1) (0,0), 16/16 boards;
    - d3: (4,0) (3,0) (1,1) (0,2), plus (1,2) 7/16 and (2,0) 5/16.
    - The cows then stand on (3,1) d2, (4,0) d3 and (2,2) d4.
    - The port's `repair_fund` needed a free tile (`room`). That is why it bought no cow on d2-4.
  - **Wheat.** The harvested wheat is sold at the next h0: d3 7.0 u, d4 8.1 u, 495 coins over d2-4. The rest feeds the herd. The port harvested its d0 wheat at saturation (age 4, d4-5) and sold 21 u at d5 h0.
  - **Fertilizer.** Fertilizer = one unit per animal per day, whether fed or not. P48 sells it as it is banked: h2, h5 and h8. All 5 d2 units are gone by the h8 cow.
    - The port banked from h7 (fert sale steps d2 h7/h8/h9/h12).
  - **Seeds.** P48 buys seeds at h11-20, after the cow.
- **Switches** (all default off; built by apply_d14.py, apply_d14b/c/d.py, d13ok.py):
  - `P48G_D14_ON`:
    - (a) The dawn plan harvests the day's P48 wheat tiles at age 2 (`P48G_D14_HARV`, d2 and d3; `harvest_age` in `_derive`).
    - (b) `d14_fert`: d1-5 shed fertilizer, plus this step's DROP bags, is sold the step it is banked.
    - (c) `d14_defer` + `repair_fund`: on d2-4, until the day's first animal buy (or `P48G_D14_HOLD_H`=12):
      - no BUY_SEED / BUY_PRODUCT WHEAT rows; the plan's future seed and feed buys are not netted from the cow cash;
      - the feed stock shrinks to today's unfed shortfall;
      - no feed hold; herd first, seeds after the cow;
      - `room` +1 while the day's cow tile (`P48G_D14_COWTILE`) still stands as wheat.
    - `repair_place` puts the animal on that tile even when the plan claims it.
  - Sub-switches (under D14):
    - `P48G_D14_FERT_FIRST`: collect first on the fertilizer tiles, d1..`P48G_D14_FERT_LAST`=5 only (the ROUTE_SPLIT=2 mechanism without its d6-9 feed loss);
    - `P48G_D14_FEED_KEEP`: the hold defers seeds only;
    - `P48G_D14_WHEAT_SELL`: at d3-5 h0-1, shed wheat above today's herd feed is sold.
  - `P48G_D13_REPLAY_GUARD` (needs D0_ON): d1-3 play the canonical rows 24-95 of `P48G_D0_SCRIPT`.
    - The market rows the real purse did not fill are dropped (`P48G_D13_OK`, 4 of the 72 rows; `ProgramEngineState.p48_fill` run on the real observation + the unit commands).
    - On the first step where our purse cannot fill a row the real seat filled, the scheduler takes over for the rest of the game.
    - On the 16 boards the replay never fell back (d1-3 cash d2 h8 475 + 194, d3 537 + 190).
- **Identity:** OFF == P48GRAPH3 g1e rows, **6/6 exact** on the first code (res/id0.md) and on the final code with all sub-switches (res/id1.md).
