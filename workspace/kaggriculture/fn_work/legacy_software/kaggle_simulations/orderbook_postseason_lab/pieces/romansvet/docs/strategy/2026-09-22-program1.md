# PROGRAM1 — frozen-head complete-program selector

PROGRAM1 enumerated all 2,100 exact real-engine outcomes for the chronological
150-board LIVE250 training panel: abstain plus the 13 distinct successful
LIVEEXPERT schedules.  Every board used its recorded seed, original learner
seat, pinned town, and taped opponent.  The resumable enumeration took 9,250.4
seconds with four workers and one OpenMP thread per worker.

The selector's 115 dawn-10 inputs are the unchanged legacy 64-vector followed
by actual opponent purse, purse difference, both farms' 5 crop × 3 age-bin
histograms, both farms' nine standing-product yields, and learner seat.  Money,
count, and yield scales are 1e4, 20, and 50.  The new channel is made only from
the public dawn observation using whole-board reductions; it contains no
episode, seed, game index, future town/tape entry, or final outcome.  In
particular, the frozen head still receives zero in its legacy opponent-purse
slot.

## Provenance-safe CV and verdict

A one-hidden-layer 115→16→14 tanh MLP was fit for 100 full-batch updates per
fold with seed 0, learning rate 1e-3, L2 1e-3, and a separate nonnegative dual
updated at 0.1.  Its objective was exactly expected paired win reward
`W(a)-W(0)` subject to expected mean `(theirs(a)-theirs(0))/1e4 <= 0`; there
was no margin term, entropy bonus, critic, or GAE.  Exact logit ties choose
option 0.

Folds were `game_index mod 5`.  A held-out fold's rows were excluded from fit,
and every expert option whose source game belonged to that fold was unavailable
both in training and held-out selection.  All five held-out models selected
abstain on all 30 boards:

Item 17 kills before dev when pooled OOF net is not positive.  Therefore the
all-150 fit was not performed, dev was not run, sealed was not opened, no
selector artifact was promoted, and production runtime/packaging were not
edited.  Complete-program enumeration removed exploration and credit-assignment
failure, but the dawn state still did not generalize the board-specific
rescues out of fold.
