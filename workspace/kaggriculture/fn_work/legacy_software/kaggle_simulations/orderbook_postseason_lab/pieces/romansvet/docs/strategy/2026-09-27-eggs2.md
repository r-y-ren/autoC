## 3. The mechanism, in numbers
**Line items that make geese negative for us (T6 d6, per game):**

1. **Our own purse wins; the eggs do pay.**
   - Eggs +3.7k and manure +0.7k, against feed wheat −0.9k, crops displaced −0.7k (plantings −7.6), birds −0.5k, and hires plus feed buy −0.9k.
   - Net Δours +2.1k (t 5.5). About +40 coins per extra goose-day.
2. **The loss is entirely the rival's purse (+4.0k), and it is PRICE on books where the rival is the big seller.**
   - The rival's units barely change. Our displaced units leave the shared book, and V56 sells its whole volume at the higher quote.
   - Rival gain per unit of ours that goes missing:
     - wheat 31 coins (932 / 30 u)
     - milk 139 (1,641 / 11.8 u)
     - wool 129 (1,070 / 8.3 u)
     - straw 202 (1,492 / 7.4 u)
   - Those same units cost US almost nothing, because our remaining milk, wool and straw sell dearer (milk +92, wool −76, straw +331).
   - The eggs we add sit on a book the rival hardly uses: rival egg −185. The manure we add is our only denial: rival fert −802.
3. **Where the displaced units come from:**
   - **(a) The d9-11 herd purse.** The floor's geese take the coins of the cow and sheep build: −0.4 cow and −0.8 sheep standing from d12 to d29. That is −12 milk, −8 wool, and less cow/sheep FEED (−9 / −11).
   - **(b) Feed wheat.** Each goose-day eats one wheat. +44 feeds means −30 u of our wheat sold and a rival wheat gain of +0.9k. This is intrinsic to eggs.
   - **(c) Plantings −7.6** (wheat and carrot), which cuts straw and wheat harvest.
   - Channel (a) is about 2/3 of the gift. Cell `hfb6` (§4) removes it and the rival's milk + wool + straw gain falls from +4.2k to +0.95k.
4. **Why Boey's 7 geese are +6.8k and ours are not: the dose is funded from different money.**
   - Boey's geese are bought d2-9 out of a zero-cash opening, with 11 hands, bought feed wheat, and a d10-14 plate paying the cow and sheep build. Its cows and sheep lose nothing, so its geese are additive and the rival's shared books do not rise.
   - On our body, the same birds come out of the d9-11 herd purse, so the dose converts shared-book volume (milk/wool/straw/wheat, where every unit we drop is worth 30-200 coins to V56) into thin-book volume (eggs, where the rival loses 2 coins per unit we add).
   - Later geese avoid the displacement but do not pay: a d12+ bird lays about 20 eggs for 300 coins plus feed.
5. **The plan.py:9514 gate is not misfiring.**
   - EGGDOSE1's fix holds: the floor is a stock, and the geese sit at 6.0 against target 6.
   - The gated want is the path most floor birds take anyway: `_geese_floor` raises `macro.animal_want`, and `acquire_ok` is open. That is why the `_wants`-only herd-first gate (`hf6`) is inert.

- Late geese avoid the herd but cannot repay 300 coins plus feed.
