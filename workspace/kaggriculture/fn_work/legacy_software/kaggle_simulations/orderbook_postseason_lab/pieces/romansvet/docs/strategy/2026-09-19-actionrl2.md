## (b) Read the log
`S/actionrl/<run>/log.tsv` — `update, games, mean_reward, win_rate, mean_margin, entropy, loss, sec, hist`. `hist` is the
per-slot action histogram (18 groups, `;`-separated): the first diagnostic is whether the no-op column is still ~100 % — a head
that has not left the frozen planner cannot have learned anything, and one that has left it everywhere is entropy-collapsed.
`mean_margin` is the honest number; `mean_reward` mixes in the ±1 win term.

## ACTIONRL3 — the three gaps closed (same day, later)
* **Value baseline.** `head.py` grew `wv`/`bv` (hidden → scalar) on the SAME trunk and `forward_value` returns
  `(logits, V)` off one pass; `ppo.gae(v, reward, lam=0.95, gamma=1)` replaces the constant per-episode advantage.
  `gamma = 1` because a coin on day 3 and a coin on day 29 are the same coin at the final bank. `wv`/`bv` are zero at
  init and cannot reach the logits, so `tests/test_residual.py` stays 20/20 and the identity contract is untouched —
  and the 5-update smoke's update 0 is byte-identical to `smoke1`'s (2.4002 / 0.906 / +15,876.5). Honest caveat: at
  `V = 0` GAE is NOT flat, it is `lambda^(29-t) * R`, so dawn 0 starts with 23 % of the credit. That tilt is GAE's bias
  half and it unwinds only as V learns. `log.tsv` gained a `vloss` column; stdout gained `vloss` and `V|sd`.
* **`jax_fn` now takes `jrandom` as an argument** instead of importing jax inside the function. Not cosmetic: the
  packager's `forbidden_imports` is an AST scan and it (correctly) failed the build on that nested import, since
  `head.py` ships into the archive byte-identical. Passing the module keeps the one-module contract without weakening
  the gate.
* **Packaging.** `scripts/package_submission.py --residual <head.npz>` copies `S/actionrl/head.py` →
  `kagg3/core/residual_head.py` and the weights → `residual_head.npz`, and splices three lines into `main.py` at the
  `_THETA` anchor (`plan.RESIDUAL_ON = True`, `RESIDUAL_HEAD = <abs npz>`). It composes with `--opening`. VERIFIED:
  without the flag the tarball md5 is `3fb115736293feb84d7fc5dcef0deb33` both before and after the change; with the
  identity head the tarball smokes **720 steps / 0 bad / 184,456 / 129,457** — the shipped coins, no jax, no es/sim.
