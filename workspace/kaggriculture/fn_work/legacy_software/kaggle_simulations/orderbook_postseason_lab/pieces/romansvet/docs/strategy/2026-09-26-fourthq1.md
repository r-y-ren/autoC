## 2. Mechanism: why their Q4 pays and ours did not
1. **Labour is not the difference. Yield per Q4 tile is.**
   - Our Q4VRP1 arm paid 7,012 in hires (base 3,247, +3,765). That is the same bill as FQ (6,455) and DSM (7,115) pay on four quadrants.
   - For that bill our arm got **+82 harvest units** (tomato +50, melon +20, wheat +30; deaths 5→12.5). FQ gets **399 Q4 units** and DSM 353.
   - Q4 gross at FQ's own prices is ≈ wheat 8.6k + straw 2.2k + milk 1.6k + carrot 1.3k + wool 1.3k ≈ **15k**.
   - Costs: land 4k + seed 1.2k + animals 0.6k + the hire bill above the 3-quadrant top teams' 5,115 (FQ opponents, +1.3k) ≈ 7.1k.
   - So FQ nets **≈ +8k**, the same as DSMLAND2's +8.2k.
2. **Q4 is a dense low-input wheat relay.**
   - 15 of 25 tiles are wheat. It plants about 5 per day on a 4-day cycle and waters ~3.5 per planting, so ~3.6 wheat units per planting (cap 6).
   - It fertilises almost nothing (28 ops), runs almost no animals and no tomato, and the farmer stays out.
   - That is 1.73 ops per worked tile-day, about 32 Q4 ops per day, or 2.6 hand-days at 12 ops per hand-day.
3. **The purse is spent as soon as it arrives, so the crew and the land come early.**
   - Dawn purse is about 10 coins from d4 to d9. Hands reach 10 by d8. Q3 is bought d8.3 and Q4 at d10.3, both paid out of the same step's sales.
   - We hold ~2k at dawn on d8-9, run 3.9 hands/day on d0-9 and buy Q3 on d10 with 4.8k in hand.
4. **Wheat volume doubles as denial.**
   - FQ sells 695 net wheat at 26.8 each, a lower price than ours, and its rivals' wheat fetches 24.7 per unit. Rivals of DSM get 31.9 and rivals of ours 42.4.
   - FQ carries fewer products (no tomato, no eggs). Its net wheat of 18.6k is 2.2× ours.

## 3. Portable rules (no port of code; none are shipped knobs today)
- R1 **Q4 = wheat relay, planted to density.**
  - ≥ 5 wheat plantings/day on Q4 from d10 to ~d25. Fill 13 tiles on d10 and 10 on d11, then relay. Stop planting on d26.
  - Survival-level water (~3.5/planting). No fertiliser and no animals on Q4. No tomato or melon on Q4.
  - This is `Q4_RELAY_ON` (Q4RELAY1, not merged).
- R2 **Crew 12 on d10-19 is the price of the ticket, not the cause of the loss.** Budget ~3.6k hire bill over d10-19, about 360 per day. The Q4 hands must come with R1's plantings.
  - Adding hands without dense Q4 plantings = Q4VRP1 (+3.8k bill, +82 units).
  - Adding plantings without hands = Q4PROG1: labour is taken from Q1-3.
- R3 **Buy Q4 on d10 in the step whose sales cover 4,000.** The FQ median purse before that step is 2.9k. No reserve (`Q4_RELAY_RESERVE`=0, `Q4_RELAY_BUY_DAY`=10). Q3 on d8-9 is an upstream lever. Our Q3 lands d10 and ties up the purse, and that is not tested here.
- R4 **Accept a ~27 wheat quote.** FQ's average net wheat price is 26.8. The volume pays, and it also depresses the rival's wheat.

## 4. What Q4RELAY1 should change
- **Default to `Q4_RELAY_WATER="min"`.** FQ runs ~3.5 waters/planting and ~3.6 u/planting. Keep `Q4_RELAY_FERT=False`.
- **The per-day ROI gate is too strict as priced.**
  - `breakeven.txt` puts break-even at a 27.4-28.0 quote (h9, OPH 12) and 44-46 (h11). FQ realises 26.8 and still nets ~+8k/game.
  - The gate charges Q4 with the full marginal Fibonacci hands. The rival-class evidence is a marginal bill of only +1.3k over 3-quadrant top teams at the same crew size.
  - Set `Q4_RELAY_ROI` ≤ 0.8, or gate on the dawn plan's fill (≥ 5 plantings/day) rather than on the quote. A gate that throttles the relay to a few plantings per day reproduces Q4VRP1's +82 units.
- **Use a fixed cadence rather than an ask.** 13 / 10 / then 5-6 per day to d25 and 0 from d26. Keep crew at 12 through d19; FQ runs 11.9 then, and 10.8 on d20-29.
- **Keep the relay wheat-only (`Q4_RELAY_TILES`=25, ~15-18 wheat tiles), with no animal placement on Q4.** Most of DSM's and our Q4 losses were tomato, geese and deaths.
- **Legs to watch:** theirs (Δ rival wheat price) and dev flips. The denial effect (R4) should show as Δtheirs < 0, not as a gift.
