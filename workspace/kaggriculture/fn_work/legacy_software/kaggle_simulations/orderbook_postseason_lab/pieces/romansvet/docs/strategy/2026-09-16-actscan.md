## 2. Why each one is state-forced or already right

Engine rules that decide whether a day-choice can exist at all
(`kaggle_environments/envs/kaggriculture/kaggriculture.py`):

* **WATER, one-time crop** `:431-443` — inside ages `[(mxd+1)//2 .. mxd]` a
  watering adds 2 covered / 1 not, clamped to `max_yield`; **outside that window
  it adds nothing**. So the day-choice is real — and `plan.py:7425`
  `bonus_water = (c_ongoing == 0) & (age >= c_ws) & (age <= c_mxd)` *is* that
  window, so 87.9 % of our one-time waters already land in it and 0.0 land on a
  clamped tile. The 54.7 out-of-window waters are `must_water` survival
  (`:7409`, two unwatered days WEED the tile, `:782-784`). Same-op-count oracle
  (move a wasted water onto an unwatered in-window day, `k <= 3`,
  spot-priced on the harvest day, day-29 tiles excluded): **+44 / +53**.
* **WATER, ongoing crop** `:769-802` — a fire pays `2 if (was_watered and
  fertilized) else 1`, so an **unwatered** fire still pays 1. Watering only buys
  the fertilizer bonus, which is FERT_TIMING's lever, already shipped: 41.5 % of
  our ongoing waters are fire-eve AND fertilized against the class's 36.5 %.
* **COLLECT_FERTILIZER** `:505-514`, `:832` — `fertilizer_available` is a
  **boolean** re-armed once a day; it never accumulates. A missed day is gone, so
  this is reach, not day-choice, and `plan.py:7566`
  `want_collect = has_animal & (view.t_favail == 1)` takes it on sight: 98.2 %.
* **HIRE** `:698-710`, `:881` — `_end_of_day` **clears `farm["hands"]`**, so no
  hand persists: every hand is re-hired each morning and the `mult*fib(n)` ladder
  restarts daily. There is no "hire earlier" choice at all; the only choice is
  how many hands to buy today, which is the crew-volume family (JOINT-LIFT,
  CLOSED). Our last hand of the day costs **62.5 coins** (max 144), against
  ~159 coins per unit of production (`2026-09-16-pricevol.md`), so the ladder
  never binds. The +2,680 "flatten the ladder" number is cost-only: it moves
  hand-days off days 15-29 onto days 0-5, which is exactly MACRO-RAMP, measured
  **−4,860**. `plan.py:8848` `n_hire`.
* **BUY_LAND** `:712-726` — the price is fixed by purchase ORDER
  (`LAND_PRICES`), not by day, and both seats buy exactly 2 extra quadrants
  (nobody buys the 3rd — `2026-09-16-mmpq.md`) within half a day of each other.
* **BUY_SEED / BUY_ANIMAL** `:668-688` — quoted at `CROPS[c]["seed"]` /
  `ANIMALS[a]["cost"]`, **constants**. The day cannot change the price; only the
  ramp can, and the animal ramp is CLOSED (WOOLPRICE, ANIMAL_BUY_FWD_ON inert).
* **DIG / DROP / PICKUP / MOVE / PASS** — state-forced (a DIG exists to free a
  tile for the next PLANT; a PICKUP exists because `FEED` takes wheat from the
  acting unit's own bag, `:505-514`). No independent day.

## 3. The one number that is not zero, and why it is not a lever

`BUY_PRODUCT WHEAT` quotes `market_price("WHEAT", inv - 1)` (`:660-668`), which
does move day to day, and buying each of our 160 units at the cheapest dawn
quote of `[d-3, d]` would save **422 coins on ENG22 / 316 on V45** (unrestricted,
any earlier day: 1,316 / 1,109). Both are under the +450 bar on their own leg,
and the measurement is an **upper bound with the SPREAD6 defect**
(`2026-09-16-pricevol.md`): the quote is priced against a *cumulative*
inventory, and we ourselves take 160 units out of it, so concentrating the
purchases onto the cheapest day raises that day's quote. The engine class leaks
more on the same read (814 / 538), as with fertilizer. Not a box.
