# PQ4TAIL1: MMPQ's late game as quotas after the tape, read against V56 (2026-09-29)

**Verdict: NONE.** Both grid cells I ran lose to V56 at t <= -5 on both board sets, so the stop rule fired. The grid is stopped, and there is no package, no `_pin` row and no upload slot.

The one lasting result is gate 0. MMPQ's opening now survives the opponent's first hour. A one-line fix, `:o`, puts the tape's h0 feed purchase first in its market list. With it, the realized tape reproduces MMPQ's d0-10 timetable exactly against PFS on 16/16 programme boards and against V56 on 4/4 boards.

What loses the games is the handover. PFS takes over an MMPQ farm with 3 sheep, so it gives up our wool denial, and the tape's melon/Q4 cash is spent on d10-17 work that repays only part of its cost by d29.

  - PQ4EXEC1's loader change is not needed, because the tape JSON keeps a `rows` key. Its `:g` purse guard is not used.
- **Gate-0 fix: `PROG_AGENT_ON=D:label:o`.** At step 0 the tape's `BUY_PRODUCT` rows move to the front of its market list.
  - The market fills orders in lockstep by list index. The tape's `BUY_PRODUCT WHEAT 5` sits at index 1, so PFS's bulk h0 wheat buy (53 units) filled first and raised our price by 26 coins.
  - MMPQ runs with 0-5 coins of slack, so those 26 coins had knock-on effects:
    - 2 wheat seeds were short at d0 h10-21, so 2 PLANTs were lost.
    - The d3 h2 wheat sale was 53 coins short.
    - Hire and animal orders failed on d5.
  - The engine already fills every BUY one unit at a time up to the purse. Sizing orders from the purse was never the missing piece; the lost coins had to be stopped at their source.
- **Quota switches (plan.py, all default OFF):**
  - `Q4_PRESETS["MMPQ"]`: MMPQ's own Q4 census at h23 of each day, read from ep 115275275 seat 1:
    - wheat 8/14/14/12/10/8 on d10-15, then 6/4/4/9/6 from d17;
    - tomato 6, rising to 9 at d17 and 11 at d18, then 5 from d20;
    - strawberry 1/3/6/7/9 on d11-15;
    - carrot 4 from d23.
  - `Q4_HIRE_FLOOR=<n>`: a minimum number of hands per day while Q4 is owned, raised only while the next hand is affordable (the same `affords` walk as `Q4_EXTRA_HANDS`).
  - `Q4_HIRE_TO=<day>`: the end of the crew window as a scalar. The switch string splits on commas, so the tuple `Q4_HIRE_DAYS` cannot be passed through it.
- **Body.** `PQ4T(H, n)` = `PROG_AGENT_ON=H:pq4r_115275275_1:o,Q4_PROG_ON=True,Q4_TARGETS=MMPQ,Q4_HIRE_FLOOR=n,Q4_HIRE_TO=29`.

## 5. Why it loses

1. **Wool.** MMPQ's farm (this tape was recorded against DSM) holds 3 sheep. PFS on its own farm holds 5.3 sheep at d10 and 8.0 by d15 (smoke, PFS seat). Sheep bought after d10 barely produce: first wool at +6 days, then one every 3 days. Against V56 that costs 4.5-12.7k of our wool coins plus 2.7-6.6k of rival wool coins, which is most of the loss. The eggs (+3.2k at H=15), tomatoes (+3.5-4.2k) and wheat (+1.3-2.0k) that MMPQ wins with do come through, but they do not cover the wool.
2. **The Q4 quota is paid out of d10-17 cash and repays only part of it by d29.**
   - PQ4T(10, 12) is 6.6k behind the control at d18 but 6.7k ahead of t0h10 over d18-29.
   - Net, it is still -7.7k in margin against no quota.
   - Across d18-29, the MMPQ body gains 71.3k against the control's 75.4k.
3. **The herd is not kept.** After the handover PFS lets 2-3 cows and 1-3 geese escape (two unfed days) at d19-22. The 8 geese MMPQ holds from d10 are 5.0 by d29 at H=15 and 3.2 at H=10.
4. **Early strawberries collapse** to 15-31 u on d15-17. The tape's strawberries come on d12-15, so they are not on the d15-17 market.
