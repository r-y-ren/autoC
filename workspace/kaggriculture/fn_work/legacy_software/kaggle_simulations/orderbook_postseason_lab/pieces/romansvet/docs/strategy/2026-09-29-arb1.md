## 1. What the engine lets idle cash do (kaggle_environments/envs/kaggriculture/kaggriculture.py, sha bc8a5487)

- **Only two products can be bought.** `_process_market` quotes `BUY_PRODUCT` only for `item in ("WHEAT", "FERTILIZER")` (L598); any other item aborts the order.
  - So variants 1, 2 and 4 cannot be run as trades on MELON, STRAWBERRY, TOMATO, MILK, WOOL, EGG or CARROT. There is no buy-back, no "hold in the market" and no pre-wave melon stock.
  - The only market lever on those books is the timing of units we grew ourselves (the V4 rows).
- **A position is capped at 100 units.** A bought unit lands in the shed, and `_commit_unit` refuses a buy once the shed holds 100 items (L665-668).
  - At the recorded wheat quotes (30-40), a full shed of wheat is worth at most 4k.
  - The 15.8k (d10-19) and 69.6k (d20-29) of idle overnight cash cannot be deployed.
- **A round trip nets zero by construction.** A BUY is quoted at `p(inv-1)`, a SELL at `p(inv)` (L596-601).
  - The only exogenous profit on a held unit is what other flows do to the book between the two legs.
  - Those flows are the town drain and the rival's net trades. Every rival trade inside the hold is re-quoted against our missing units: a gift when it sells, a cost to it when it buys.
- **Drain schedule.** Drain comes from two sources (`_town_consume` L728-749, run after every market phase):
  - Each unlocked shop instance removes 1 unit of each of its products every 4 steps. The single-product shops, YARN_STORE and PET_CAFE, remove 2.
  - The town centre removes 1 unit of every product except FERTILIZER every 24 steps.
  - MELON is in no shop, so it drains 1 unit/day. FERTILIZER is never drained.
- **Realised drain d18-29** (units/day, mean of the 21 live27 books):
  - wheat 27.8, carrot 17.3, strawberry 18.6, milk 15.9, egg 13.7, wool 12.3, tomato 11.3, melon 1.0, fertilizer 0.
  - Both seats' net sales run faster than the drain in every product except TOMATO (drain 11.3 vs sales 8.1/day).
  - The strawberry book goes from +46 at d24 to +7 at the end (price 16 -> 104). It recovers because the flood ENDS, not because the drain outruns it.

**Why PFS's h0 wheat relay pays.** The relay is OPEN_PUMP: d0 h0 `BUY_PRODUCT WHEAT 53`, d0 h1 `SELL WHEAT 48`, keep 5.
- The 53 units walk the quote from 26 to 32 (`25 + sqrt(10000 - inv)`, amp exactly 1). They cost 1,596.
- After the market phase of step 0, the town centre drains 1 wheat (step 0 % 24 == 0). The rival's h0 wheat buys lower the book further.
- The h1 resale therefore starts at 32-33 instead of 32 and returns 1,474 for 48 units. The 5 kept feed units cost 122, i.e. 24.4 each against 26-27 on an untouched book.
- The rest is the lockstep. At h1, `_process_market` quotes both seats unit by unit off one pre-commit inventory. An opponent whose first h1 order is `BUY_PRODUCT WHEAT 5` pays the lifted quote, and that cash-critical opening loses its second sheep (plan.py OPEN_PUMP block).
- On the 21 live27 programme books the relay is worth **+17/game to us and -11 to the rival** on the recorded trades. The programme runs the same pump, so there is little left to deny.

## 2. Exact arithmetic on the 21 live27 programme books (S/arb1/arith.py -> arith.txt; extract.py re-applies every recorded step with the native engine: cash and market mismatch 0 on all 42 seats)

Method:
- Every counterfactual re-quotes EVERY recorded SELL/BUY_PRODUCT unit of both seats on the recorded book, shifted by our inserted or removed units.
- Our orders go before the recorded commits of their step.
- Tape assumption: recorded quantities do not react. A rival that would sell more into a book we lifted is therefore UNDER-read (the gift is a lower bound).
- (b) The m40 closed-loop books were NOT path-logged: the judge harness does not keep replays, and the remote had no free worker for a logging rerun. The closed-loop reads of section 4 are the m40 measurement.
- Cash rule for the holds: our recorded purse is >= the buy cost at every step of the hold. Shed rule: recorded fill + n <= 100 at every step. Otherwise the cell is not executed and scores 0.

## 3. MARKET_MAKER switch family (worktree `/mnt/e/_work/kagg3_wt_arb1`, branch `arb1_0929` from master 8d670dad)

- **Constants (`plan.py`, default off):** `MARKET_MAKER_ON`, `MM_BUY_STEP`, `MM_SELL_STEP`, `MM_UNITS`, `MM_RESERVE` (1,000), `MM_ROOM` (20).
- **Hook:** `runtime.Runtime.act` calls `_mm_act` when the switch is on; otherwise it calls the unchanged body (`_act_body`).
- **Buy.** One `BUY_PRODUCT WHEAT n` is appended to our market row in the first hour of `[MM_BUY_STEP, MM_BUY_STEP + 24)` where all of these hold:
  - the row has room (< 10 orders);
  - the body places no land, animal or hire order that hour;
  - money minus the body's seed spend is >= a walk-up cost bound + 1,000;
  - shed fill + n <= 80.
- **Hold.** The held units are hidden from the body's observation (shed WHEAT - held), so it can neither sell nor feed them.
- **Sell.** At `MM_SELL_STEP` (or the first later hour with room), one `SELL WHEAT <held>` row closes the position.
- **OFF = master:** `arb1_id` (switch off, g0capsfix, 3 m40 boards x 2 seats) reproduces `jr_g0capsfix_pfs` 6/6 exactly.

**Named grid (complete).** product WHEAT x window {A = buy d7 h2 -> sell d13 h2 (the tape-best fixed cell), B = buy d10 h0 -> sell d14 h0 (the tape-best cell inside the idle-cash window)} x n {25, 50}, all four vs g0capsfix (80 m40 games).
- Flood reads: A25 and B25.
- **Named extras (the truly idle window):**
  - C25 = buy d17 h2 -> sell d23 h2, n 25; live27 tapes only.
  - C25x = C25 + `MM_CAPFIX` (the body runs with `spec.SHED_CAPACITY` lowered by the held count; worktree 8c11771c); live27 tapes + vs g0capsfix on the 38 games of one worker half.
  - Both were added after C25's tape read showed own-unit losses.
- The optional big read was dropped for C25x.
- FERTILIZER is priced out exactly (oracle +9/game, best fixed cell +2 in 1 game) and was not judged. No other product is buyable.
