# ACTIONRL12 — win-only reward on hard boards (`flow265_winonly`) — 2026-09-20

**Why.** flow260-263 all trained with sim `mwin` FLAT at 0.72-0.78 while the margin term moved by thousands:
the gradient came from the coin, and the ladder pays `P(win)` alone. GATE261/263 then said the same thing
twice — every head that suppresses the gift does so out of our own purse, and none beat `head_940` on
margin. So stop paying for coin: reward = WIN only, and spend the batch on boards that can still flip.

## Diff (`S/actionrl/ppo.py`, two flags, ~70 lines incl. docstring)

* **`--margin-weight W`** (default **1.0** = every earlier run byte for byte). `terminal_reward` (new, pure,
  tested) is `win + W * coin / 1e4`, `coin` = the paired `d_ours - LAMBDA*d_theirs` when `--paired-baseline`
  is on, else the raw `margin`. `W = 0` leaves the `+-1` win term and nothing else.
* **`--hard-boards P`** (default **0** = the old uniform draw, RNG stream included). Before training, ONE
  head-off pass over the tape rungs — the `make_baseline` program (head off on BOTH seats, i.e. the shipped
  planner), in chunks of exactly `--batch` so it reuses that program's single compile, seat 1 on the shipped
  theta — measures each board's head-off win rate `p0` over `HARD_K = 4` weather draws (one draw is a
  Bernoulli sample, not a probability; the board is deterministic given the word). Boards are then drawn
  with `board_weights` = `max(P, 4 p0 (1-p0))` normalised: 1.0 at a coin flip, 0.64 at p0 = 0.2/0.8, `P` at
  a sure win or sure loss, never 0. Summary once, to stdout and as a `#HARD` row in `log.tsv`; **log columns
  unchanged**.
* The head-off program is now built when `paired OR hard-boards > 0`, but only CALLED per update when
  `paired` — so the pre-pass costs one compile and ~20 s, not 2x every update.

**`--paired-baseline` is DROPPED from the launch.** With `W = 0` it cannot touch the reward (`W` multiplies
the whole coin term), so it would only buy the `d_ours`/`d_theirs` diagnostic columns at 2x rollout time
(10.7 vs 5.7 s/update, i.e. 6 h vs 3.2 h for 2,000 updates). The gift is measured out of sample at the gate.
