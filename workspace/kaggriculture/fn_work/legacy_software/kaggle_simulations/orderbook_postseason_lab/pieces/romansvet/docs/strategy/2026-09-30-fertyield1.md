# FERTYIELD1: is the MMPQ executor's wheat gap fertilizer yield? (2026-09-30)

Verdict: **KILLED**. Fertilizer yield explains about a third of the wheat gap. With the yield fixed to MMPQ's level, the executor still sells 313-368 wheat against MMPQ's 580, and no arm gets past a 0.823 coins ratio on HOLD20 (the bar is 0.90). The rest of the gap is the number of harvests: fewer plantings, and 16-20 % of plantings are lost before harvest. That is crew labour, not fertilizer.

  - TUNE20: +129.9 u = 3.2k / 4.5k coins. Needed: 0.90 x 131,621 - 97,405 = 21.1k.
  - So "fertilizer alone reaches 0.90" is impossible.

## Verdict
- **Bar ≥ 0.90:** no arm; the best is 0.823. No CANDIDATE BODY.
- **Hypothesis (wheat within 20 % of MMPQ):** KILLED. The best arm sells 368 wheat against 580 (-37 %), and the fertilizer ceiling (+140 u, ≤ 4.9k coins) is below the 10.7k needed.
- **What drives the gap:** harvest count (plantings 157 vs 176, harvest/planting 0.80-0.84 vs 0.98). This is CREWSCHED1's labour-bound finding (empty and weed tiles).
- **Worth folding into any later executor body:** the fertilize-timing rule (+0.4-0.5 u/harvest) and the strawberry off-phase skip (+1.6k HOLD20, t 3.5).
