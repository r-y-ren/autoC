## 1. Codec + baseline (step 1)

## 4. Diagnosis: what the head still gets wrong, and why
- **Feature missing? Mostly no.** Adding the units' carried inventory and this step's unit tokens (mkx) moves non-zero exact +1.5 pts and train loss 0.156 -> 0.144.
  The clock and state inputs already explain the teacher's market orders.
- **Loss? Yes, for the late products.** Class-balanced x4 moves every SELL units ratio from 0.57-0.74 to 0.89-1.03 and adds 13-15 detect points. In closed loop, seed 1 adds
  tomato 1.0 -> 8.4 u, egg 90 -> 105 u and melon d10-17 51 -> 54 u; seed 2 keeps only the tomato gain (3.3 u). x8 overshoots (units ratios 1.04-1.23, false-sell 4-5.5 %): closed-loop melon 47.3 u, rival back to 88.0k.
- **Codec?** The 45-class greedy argmax is the calibration problem (uncertain rows round to 0). A loss weight moves the threshold; sampling or an expected-quantity
  decode would be the codec-side fix. Not tried here.
- **The d2 melon knife-edge.** d2 melon tiles are 7.00 or 8.00 in all 80 games for every clone: the d2 plant is a deterministic, all-or-nothing
  output of the trained weights, and it flips between seeds. Wave fidelity is decided by that one action, so a seed or a loss weight can flip it either way.
- **The wave: neither.** The head fires the wave offline (0.995 at step 246). In closed loop the clone lacks the melons: d2-9 plantings (-3 tiles by d9) and the d10
  morning harvest (2.8-3.3 of 6 tiles by h12). Offline PLANT_MELON recall is 0.997, so this is compounding state drift of the unit head, and per-step BC loss
  weighting cannot see it. The lever is on-policy data (DAgger-style relabelling of the clone's own closed-loop states: DAGGER1's line), not the market head.
