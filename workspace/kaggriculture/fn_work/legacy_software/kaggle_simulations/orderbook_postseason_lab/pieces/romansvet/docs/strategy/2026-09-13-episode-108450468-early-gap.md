# Episode 108450468: the dawn-day-15 cash gap

This is a one-replay, outcome-selected diagnostic. It describes the observed
cash path in public episode 108450468; it is neither a promotion estimate nor a
counterfactual. `S/unitorder/early_gap_108450468.py` reads actions only through
day 14 hour 23 and replays the market phase with the existing verified
`scripts/replay_profile.py` walker. Its market reconstruction matches every
observed money transition exactly (absolute error 0 for both seats).

## The gap appears with the first melon sale

At dawn day 10 B still led 7,046 to 2,958. The opponent's twelve melon tiles,
planted from day 0 hour 7, reached six units each and were harvested from day 10
hour 5. Its first executed melon sale was day 10 hour 9. By dawn day 11 the
opponent led 18,037 to 5,114, a 17,011-coin reversal in one day. At dawn day 15
the score was 31,101 to 11,660.

| successful melon tile entries | 12 | 12 |

| first carrot plant / harvest / sale | none | d0h9 / d3h10 / d4h1 (88-hour lag) |
| carrot units / receipts | 0 / 0 | 27 / 870 |

Both seats sold bought wheat before their first planted wheat matured, so a
wheat plant-to-sale lag would mix opening inventory arbitrage with production
and is left undefined. Neither seat planted tomato before dawn day 15.

The opponent's first melon planting-to-sale lag was 242 turns, ten days and two
hours. B had committed the same 960 coins of melon seed resource by the cutoff,
but its crop had not matured. The measurable difference is therefore timing of
the cash return, not a lower melon seed bill.

The full observed cash identity is exact:

| through day 14 hour 23 | opponent | B | opponent minus B |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| sale receipts | 50,522 | 28,694 | +21,828 |
| direct purchases, land, and hire spending | 22,421 | 20,034 | +2,387 |
| cash at dawn d15 | 31,101 | 11,660 | **+19,441** |

Thus 21,828 gross-revenue advantage minus 2,387 extra spending equals the
19,441 cash gap. Melon receipts contribute 17,440, or 89.7% of the cash-gap
magnitude. This ratio is descriptive: melon receipts are gross revenue, and
17,440 - 960 is not crop profit. It does not allocate watering, fertilizer,
feed, labor, land sharing, displaced output, or the price change imposed on the
other seat.

The other early channels are smaller. Animal-product receipts were 18,719
against 15,183 (+3,536 opponent) while animal purchases cost 7,000 against
6,800. Fertilizer receipts were 10,658 against 7,162 (+3,496 opponent). The
opponent made 103 successful hires for 862 coins; B made 97 for 1,186 because
hire cost resets daily and depends on each day's sequence. Land timing points
the other way: B unlocked quadrant two at d5h3 and quadrant three at d10h3;
the opponent did so at d6h6 and d11h1.

## Mechanism judgment and stop boundary

The most likely mechanism for this *early cash gap* is the opponent's day-0
melon cycle reaching the high-price day-10 pot before B's day-9 melon cycle.
The replay does not show that copying the cycle improves B. That distinction is
already material in this repository: `MELON_OPEN_ON` lost 19,557 coins on the
pinned 130-tape judge, and the one-tile `m0p1` perturbation lost 14,435 on TOPB2
and 15,117 on LIVE-C in its paired simulator development screen (engine
confirmation was not run), while transferring about 11,000 coins to the opponent
(`2026-09-09-switch-sweep.md:25`, `2026-09-11-integer-search.md:146,219`). This
episode supplies mechanism evidence for the known day-10 tax; it does not
reopen the forced-melon, additive-melon, route, or forward-admit families.

The current 1,191-coordinate training mask is not structurally unable to move
this decision. Its frozen config names `gp,dh,ds,g5,gb5,w3,b3,b1`
(`S/snr/flow215_w00/g10/config.json:7`). `b1` and `dh` change the per-product
hidden activation and `ds` changes each product's grow score directly
(`src/kagg3/core/policy.py:633-639`); those grow scores are the inputs to the crop softmax
and largest-remainder `plant_target` (`src/kagg3/core/brain.py:949-1026`). Both population
perturbations and updates are restricted to the mask (`src/kagg3/es/train.py:3566-3577,
4785-4789,4912-4919`). The B dawn-day-0 target in
`root_macro_snapshot.json` is `[11 WHEAT, 11 CARROT, 0 TOMATO, 0 STRAWBERRY,
0 MELON]`, so this remains an integer-boundary reachability question at the
actual training sigma, not a missing feature claim.

The cheapest successor test is a macro-only stop test on this already fixed
dawn observation: decode the exact frozen generation-0 perturbations under the
1,191 mask and report how many change day-0 melon target from zero, together
with the displaced crop targets. Zero such candidates falsifies practical
reachability at the current sigma. A positive count establishes reachability
only; it does not override the paired engine evidence that adding melon has
been harmful, and it must not trigger another melon pilot.
