## 2. Throughput (`S/actionrl/probe_throughput.py`, self-play, DRAWN shops, shipped theta)

but every gate here is the CPU engine.

## 5. Risks, ranked
1. **Shared-book gift** — fatal to this action space. REACTIVITY: every knob proposed here is a QUANTITY and in a shared pot
   every quantity direction gifts (ESJUDGE6 theirs/ours 2.35; 93 % of transfer is PRICE). JOINT-LIFT, CARROTBID, HERDTILT,
   VOLUMEHI, MELONGENES1 = five rejections of quantity reallocation.
2. **The head is already ES-trained and already failed.** `brain.decide` *is* an obs→quantity net; a residual on its outputs is
   head ES, killed flat by ALPHAFARM4 (HEADJUDGE1 +29 t 0.7, both gates); the win-rate objective is separately rejected
   (WINJUDGE1/2, flow238/240).
3. **Credit assignment** — 30 dawns share one ±8 k terminal reward; per-dawn SNR ≈ 1/√30 of what the judge only just resolves.
4. **Untested branches = shop lottery.** Any override moves a tile and re-rolls every later shop (±25 k); ALPHAPROBE3 measured
   **68 % of an in-episode gain as shop hindsight**, transfer 0.04. The pin test (`shop_crn=True`, pinned towns) keeps this
   honest — DRAWN-shop PPO gives it up.
5. **Sim-vs-engine drift** on branches the frozen theta never exercises: unquantified; cure = the engine gate.

## 6. GO/NO-GO
**NO-GO on PPO.** Cost to a judged +50 rating equivalent: 33 k-333 k sim games plus a 278-board engine gate per checkpoint,
against five quantity-family rejections, a killed head ES, a killed win-rate objective and a 300 k-game public RL run that only
reached silver. Negative EV inside the 09-30 deadline. **GO on the smallest experiment, which is not RL:** ship the two proposed
overrides as free module floats exactly as MELONGENES1 shipped `MELON_PLATE_TILES/DAY` (0 = byte-identical, plate-tested) —
