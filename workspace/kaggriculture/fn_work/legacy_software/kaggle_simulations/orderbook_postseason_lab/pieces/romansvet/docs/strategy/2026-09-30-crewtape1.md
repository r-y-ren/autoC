## Router
Each unit is handled in one of three ways, in order:
1. **Sync:** if our unit stands where the recorded unit stood at this step, it issues the recorded command. A move is issued only while the next recorded task is still valid on our farm.
2. **Repair:** otherwise a pointer walks the unit's recorded task list inside [si-back, si+ahead]. The unit takes the first task that is valid now and unclaimed, within `look` tasks and `near` tiles. It walks there or does the task, and stays committed to it until the task is done or becomes invalid.
3. **Fallback:** otherwise the rulebook dispatcher decides.

Validity checks per op:
- Tile kind, crop and yield: HARVEST needs at least the recorded yield on one-time crops.
- watered, fed, cared and fert flags.
- The unit's inventory: WHEAT for FEED, FERTILIZER for FERTILIZE, a fitting animal for PLACE.
- Shed stock for PICKUP.
- The rulebook's structure budget for BUILD.

Tape plantings add seed claims, so the market still buys the seeds. The nearest game is the one with the longest exact shop-sequence prefix known so far, with the higher final as the tie-break. Apply p99 is 0.008-0.012 s; the max is 0.26 s, from the first library load.

## Why the premise fails (route transfer)
**Premise test** (`sim.py`, no simulation): how many unit-steps two MMPQ games share, as exactly equal (position, command) and as equal position.

| pairs | n | equal (pos, cmd) d0-9 / d10-19 / d20-29 | equal position d0-9 / d10-19 / d20-29 |
|---|---|---|---|
| same key group | 187 | 30.4 % / 14.6 % / 6.5 % | 40 % / 25 % / 16 % |
| random pairs | 200 | 25.7 % / 9.7 % / 5.2 % | 36 % / 20 % / 14 % |

Same-group routes are barely more alike than random ones. The per-unit first difference is the same in both, and every hired hand differs from its first steps. The shop draw fixes MMPQ's decisions, not its hand routes, and hand routes follow the tile state step by step.

**How routes transfer when our state diverges from the recorded one:** they do not. Even the game's own routes, replayed against our rulebook market, fall out of sync by d0 h19-21:
- The d0 h9 and h14 market branches change the tile state.
- After that, units almost never stand on the recorded square at the recorded step (sync 4-7 % from d5).
- The recorded tasks are 80 % invalid.
- Repair walks inflate moves to 172/day, against 122 for MMPQ and 131 for the base.
- Wheat falls to 174-186 units, against 286 for the base.

Literal replay is therefore not a ceiling above the dispatcher. The own-route "ceiling" (0.508 HOLD20) sits below the plain dispatcher (0.805). Every softer use also reads below the base: sync-only, near-limited repair, and a dispatcher affinity bonus. The labour-schedule gap to MMPQ (wheat 286 vs 580 u, cycle 4.06 vs 3.58 d) has to come from a state-reactive dispatcher: a learned one (CREWBC1) or a fitted rule set (CREWSCHED1), not from the recorded routes.
