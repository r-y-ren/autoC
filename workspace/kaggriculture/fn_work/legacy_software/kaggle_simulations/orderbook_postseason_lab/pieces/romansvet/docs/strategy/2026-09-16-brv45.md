## 1. PLANT CANCELLATION — the engine rule, and what it costs us (nothing)

### 1.1 The rule, exactly

`/root/.cache/uv/archive-v0/tWqbmruwqm--ie1i/kaggle_environments/envs/kaggriculture/kaggriculture.py`

| line | fact |
|---|---|
| 920-928 | per turn, per seat, `plant_demand[crop]` counts every `PLANT` across **farmer + hands**; `seeds` is the seat's **pre-turn** `private["seeds"]` |
| 928 | `blocked = {crop for crop, n in plant_demand.items() if n > seeds.get(crop, 0)}` |
| 929-932 | `_allowed` rewrites **every** PLANT of a blocked crop to `["PASS"]` — including the ones the seed count could have paid for. One seed short of six requests costs **all six** |
| 941 | `_process_market` runs **after** the unit ops, so seeds bought by **this turn's** market row cannot fund this turn's PLANT |
| 414 | a non-blocked PLANT still no-ops on a `"LOCKED"` tile — **no seed consumed** |
| 424 | …and on a tile that is `not None` (occupied, incl. by an earlier unit's PLANT in the same turn) — **no seed consumed** |
| 417-429 | the surviving path: `seeds[crop] -= 1`, `_new_plant(crop, day, tpd)` |

So there are four distinct ways a PLANT dies and only the first is the
all-or-nothing one. `S/brv45/plant_audit.py` separates them.

### 3.2 The caveat, stated before the table

**This is a ceiling, not a prize.** Memory rule `counterfactuals-overstate` /
the two-purse rule. Every mechanism re-prices **one** of our lots at a time
against the **actual recorded** inventory trajectory, so it ignores (a) what the
moved lot does to every later quote of ours, (b) that the town drain and our own
later lots would move under it, (c) that our route still has to produce the
goods. Only a paired CRN run counts. The table's job is to **rank**.

### 3.4 **D is the bias gauge, and it reads ~600 coins/board**

`2026-09-14-wheat-rebuy.md` proves a buy/sell round trip through the shared pot
is **exactly zero coins, by construction of the quote walk** — the engine even
quotes a BUY at the post-buy inventory "so a buy/sell round-trip against an
unchanged market nets zero" (`kaggriculture.py:719`). Mechanism D is therefore a
**known zero**, and this oracle prices it at **+612 a board at t 13.6**. That is
the size of the one-lot-at-a-time bias: the counterfactual re-sells into a shelf
that the counterfactual's own buy never lowered.

**So read the whole table net of ~600.** A falls from 1,850 to ~1,250, B from
857 to ~260, and C's 11k is mostly the same artefact compounded over 30 days.
