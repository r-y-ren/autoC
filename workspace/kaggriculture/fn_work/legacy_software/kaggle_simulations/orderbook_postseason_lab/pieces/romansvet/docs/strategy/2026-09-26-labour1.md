## 2. Why (d20-27 crew census, `S/labour1/diag.py`: hires planned pre-VRP / post-VRP, hand work ops, PASS, plantings per day)
- **EST_LEAD is not an over-charge in net.** base 11.77 / 10.30 hires, 125.9 work, 27.8 PASS. LEAD 3: 11.34 / 10.12, work 123.9;
  LEAD 1: 11.06 / 9.90, work 120.9. The hire scan buys a smaller crew and the day does less of its OWN work over the whole game
  (LEAD 3 per game: collect-fert -13, water -7, harvest -7, care -7, feed -6; our units wheat -16, fert -17; LEAD 1 wheat -40, egg -21,
  fert -32). The rival takes the market (milk/fert/wool/wheat), so it is a gift and not a saving. The router's 20 active turns per
  hand already include the moves that `cum_est` under-charges per task (ADMITSLACK: 2.86 est vs 4.00 real steps a task,
  `EST_MOVES` 1 vs 1.85). EST_LEAD 5 is what makes the crew size right. It is a compensating error, not a stale constant.
- **The admit credit is inert** (d): n_tasks binds (EMPTY1: dawn plan == ask on 140/145 day-rows), so +3 turns/unit only admits
  optional tail work. +0.08 hires/day, +66 bill, one board-24 price-gift flip. The same result as CREW24/STACK2.
- **The ask floor does what it should, and the work it adds buys hands.** (e) +2.41 plantings/day (8.76 -> 11.16), deaths -3.0/game,
  our wheat +23 u and carrot +10 u. But post-VRP hires rise 10.30 -> 10.98 per day, work +8.7 ops/day, PASS 27.8 -> 27.3, bill +937/game.
  The idle turns are IDLE1's packing residue (2.95 turns per hand at the end of each route, at different tiles and hours). A new
  planting's 5-6 ops do not land in them; the VRP keeps another hand instead. Fert sales -18 u (the new wheat takes fertiliser).
  Adding the admit credit (f) admits more optional tail: +1,129 bill, PASS rises to 28.9, -4 flips.
- **The unhire is a clean bug fix, and small.** It fires on 11/50 dev boards (0.34 hand-days/game). -37 bill/game, no side effects.

## 5. Verdict
- **LABOUR AXIS CLOSED.** The labour model is not stale in net. Pricing a hand at the router's 20 turns shrinks the crew and gifts
  +3.1k/+6.4k (-11/-28 flips). The admit credit is inert. The late ask floor adds +2.4 plantings/day, but they cost +0.7 hands/day;
  they do not ride the idle turns (PASS unchanged), so it is net -1 and -4 with the credit. The residue cannot be filled by
  planner-side pricing. Only a packing change could use it (RRDEPTH1/RRSPEED1).
- **EMPTY_ROUTE_UNHIRE_ON: SHIP candidate under the small-gains rule** (bug fix, gift-free, dev100 +42 t 3.6, held +67 t 5.3,
  0 flips; FRESH300 +58 t 9.3, +1 flip; tapes not run). Every switch stays OFF on the branch. It is not in the gene/switch string.
