## 2. Distillation — recording, not reimplementation

`_qfloor` and `_largest_remainder` are patched to append their argument while the ORIGINAL `decide` runs, so the
network in the loss is the one the sim flies; the loss is the design's in-bin Huber on those floats (zero anywhere
inside `[n, n+1)` — exact for a `_largest_remainder` vector: every share in its own bin ⇒ `left = 0` and the argsorts
never run). The 18 switch-gene columns are frozen. Two measurements shaped the fit:

* **`grow_mult` is chaotic in theta**: Gaussian noise of **1e-4 per column** flips 65 % of its bins, 1e-3 flips all —
  it is a 64x fixed point of `_unit_ratio`, and it shares the `grow` logits with the crop and animal weights, so any
  whole-theta fit destroys it as collateral.
* **Block map (perturbation probe, per field):** `cm`/`cb`/`cd` move **only** `plant_target`, `g8`/`gb8` **only**
  `hire_bias`. Fitting those cannot touch `grow_mult`, `animal_want`, `land_bias`, `crew_target` — and `cd`
  (crop x day-bucket log-share, 45 params, ZERO at ship) is where a searched crop edit belongs by construction.

**No configuration beats the shipped theta's own label accuracy.** Strict all-field agreement with the committed
macro, on 24 held-out boards / 619 labels: **0.7351 at ship**; over 12 runs (Adam 3e-7…2e-5, SGD 1e-5…3e-2 ± global
clip; whole theta / `cm,cb,cd,g8,gb8` / `cd` alone) the best is **0.7189**. Per field at ship: plant 0.830, animal
0.945, crew 0.973, hire 0.987, grow 1.000, land 1.000 — the fit only ever lowers plant and hire. **Not overfitting**:
train loss falls 8.4 → 1.9 while **train** accuracy falls too. The labels CONFLICT — a ±1-tile edit on one board's
dawn is asked of the decode that must stay put on the 62 % of dawns where cand-0 won, and nothing readable at that
resolution separates them. [[planselect]] / [[mpcfeas]], now on the distillation side: the searched macro is
board-specific and a theta is not.
