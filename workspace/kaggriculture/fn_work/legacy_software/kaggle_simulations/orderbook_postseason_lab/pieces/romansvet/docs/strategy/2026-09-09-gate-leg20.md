# The leg-family gate (`--real-gate-metric leg20`)

Working note, 2026-09-09. Build agent, worktree `.claude/worktrees/gate-leg20`
off `bdec0e9`.

## Why

`docs/strategy/2026-09-09-judge-calibration.md`: over 24 arms the pinned gate's
net-flip count had zero true positives against the 1,800-game drawn legs, and
every flip it ever counted landed in the same 15 outcome-unstable boards. The
one statistic that ordered the arms the way the drawn legs do is the **paired
coin-margin delta over the 20 games against the ten leg-family tapes** -- the
pinned tapes that are also played in the six drawn legs. Those 20 games are
already inside every pinned round at zero extra cost; only the arithmetic on
top of them changes.

## What was added

`--real-gate-metric leg20` (`scripts/train.py`), with
`--real-gate-leg-family <comma list of opponent main.py paths or tape ids>`.

**The rule.** Over the pinned round's own rows -- nothing extra is played --
the gate takes the paired candidate-minus-incumbent coin margin and sums it
twice:

* `leg20 delta` = the sum over the games whose opponent is in the leg family;
* `all-games delta` = the sum over every shared pinned game (the H_dm floor).

The candidate is accepted iff **`leg20 delta > 0` AND `all-games delta >= 0`**.
A round that cannot produce the statistic -- no shared game, or a family tape
the round never played -- is REFUSED, for the reason `_significant` refuses an
unpairable significance test.

`--real-gate-min-flips` is **not read** under this metric. Flips and drops are
still counted, still named by tape id in `real_gate.log`, and still carried in
the verdict as an informational screen; the log says so once at the top of the
run and again on every decision line. Everything an acceptance moves -- the
record theta, `best_abs.npy`, the `--real-gate-recentre` counter,
`real_gate.json` -- reads the same `res["accepted"]` it always did, so
re-centring and the record bookkeeping follow the leg20 decision without a
second rule.

**Where it is written down.** `real_gate.log` gets the once-banner and a
`leg20:` decision line per round (leg delta, all-games delta, net flips as a
screen, any MISSING family tape, the verdict); `log.jsonl` gets
`real_gate_leg20_delta`, `real_gate_leg20_all_delta`, `real_gate_leg20_games`
and `real_gate_leg20_missing` beside the flip columns; `real_gate.json` records
`leg_family`, and a resume that changes it says so.

**Backward compatibility.** `metric` still defaults to `win`, `leg_family`
defaults to empty, and the family is refused outside `leg20` (an unapplied
filter is worse than an error). The pinned determinism notes, the seat handling
(`--real-gate-pinned-seats 1|2`) and the unpinned path are untouched: with the
flags off the argv, the log and the verdicts are byte-identical to `bdec0e9`.

**Refused on the command line, before the run starts:** `leg20` without
`--real-gate-pinned`; `leg20` without a family; a family member that is not in
`--real-gate-opponent` (named, with the field listed); a family without
`leg20`; the family without `--real-gate`.

  105228357,105232167,105268279,105269242,105400600,105441481,105441843,105442685,105443859,105592028`;
* **seven tapes added to `--real-gate-opponent`** (105228357, 105232167,
  105269242, 105400600, 105441843, 105442685, 105592028). flow158's gate field
  is the 42 held-out boards and seats only three of the ten family tapes; the
  delta is summed over games the gate actually plays, so without them the run
  would not start (the command line refuses it by name). The field is 49 tapes.
* flow158 runs `--real-gate-pinned-seats 1`, so the family is **10 boards**,
  not the 20 games the calibration named -- the same statistic on one row per
  board instead of two, half the total and the identical sign. The doubled
  count only comes back at `--real-gate-pinned-seats 2`.
* `--real-gate-min-flips 3` is left on the line and is INERT under this metric
  -- deliberately, so the log's "NOT read" note is exercised on a real run.

**Contamination to decide before launching.** Nine of the ten family tapes are
also in flow158's *training* rung (`--tape-actions .../105228357.npz` ... at
`--rung-weight tape_act_...=2`, and 105443859 / 105441481 at 1); only 105268279
is not. The gate is therefore no longer held out from the training set. Two
honest options, both one edit:

1. leave it, and read the leg20 delta as a *training-set* statistic (it is
   still the statistic that ordered arms like the drawn legs, but it can now
   be optimised into directly);
2. zero those rungs -- append `--rung-weight tape_act_<id>=0` for the nine ids
   -- and keep the gate held out, at the cost of dropping nine tapes from
   training.

I have NOT chosen; the block below is option 1 (flow158's rung weights
untouched). Nothing was rsynced or launched.
